4 οἰκέτης, -ου, ὁ (< οἰκέω), [in LXX for עֶבֶד;] a house-servant: Lk 16:13, Ro 14:4; pl., Ac 10:7, I Pe 2:18. (In Plat., Hdt., Si 4:30 6:11, the pl. includes all the inmates of the house, the familia, οἰκετεία..)† SYN.: v.s. διάκονος.
