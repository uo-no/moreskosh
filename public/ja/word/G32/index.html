語義（IGSD）

  1. 天使・御使い (169)
    神のもとから遣わされる天の使い。
  2. 使者 (6)
    人の使者（洗礼者ヨハネへの預言の適用や、人の使いを指す場合）。

Abbott-Smith 辞書本文

原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

176 ἄγγελος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for מַלְאָךְ;] a messenger, one sent: Mt 11:10, Ja 22:25. As in LXX, in the special sense of angel, a spiritual, heavenly being, attendant upon God and employed as his messenger to men, to make known his purposes, as Lk 1:11, or to execute them, as Mt 4:6. The ἄ. in Re 1:20 2:1, al., is variously understood as a messenger or delegate, a bishop or ruler, a guardian angel, the prevailing spirit of each church, i.e. the Church itself. (Cf. Swete, Ap)., in l.; DB, iv, 991; Thayer, s.v.; Cremer, 18; MM, VGT, s.v.)
