6 ** στοιχεῖον, -ου, τό [in LXX: Wi 7:17 19:18, IV Mac 12:13 * ;] prop., one of a row (στοῖχος) or series, hence, the shadow-line of a dial (Aristoph.). an elementary sound or letter of the alphabet (Anth., Plut., π.). the elements or rudiments of knowledge (Arist., al.) He 5:12; πτωχὰ σ., Ga 4:9; σ. τοῦ κόσμου, ib. 3, Col 2:8, 20 (but v. infr.). The material elements of the universe (Plat.; LXX, ll. c.): II Pe 3:10, 12. The heavenly bodies (Diog. Laert.). The demons or tutelary spirits of nature (Enoch., Test., Sol., al.; for this sense in Ga, Col, ll. c., v. ICC on Col 2:8; Enc. Bibl., s.v. " Elements ").†

出現形

  • στοιχεῖα — 対格・中性・複数 4
  • στοιχεῖα — 主格・中性・複数 2
  • στοιχείων — 属格・中性・複数 1
