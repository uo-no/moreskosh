Abbott-Smith 辞書本文（引用）

原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

1 * φυλακτήριον, -ου, τό an outpost, fortification (Thuc., al.). a safeguard (Plat.). an amulet (Plut., al.); in NT for the Talmudic תְּפִלִּין, a prayer fillet, a phylactery, a small strip of parchment on which portions of the law were written and worn on the forehead and next the heart (cf. Ex 13:16): Mt 23:5.†
