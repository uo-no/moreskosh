2 στρατιά, -ᾶς, ἡ [in LXX chiefly for צָבָא ;] = στρατός, an army, a host: of angels (III Ki 22:19, al.), Lk 2:13; of the stars of heaven (Je 8:2, II Ch 33:3), Ac 7:42. As sometimes in cl. (poët.), = στρατεία: II Co 10:4, T (WH, RV, στρατεία).†
