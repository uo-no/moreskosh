52 * σπείρω [in LXX chiefly for זרע ;] to sow (seed): absol., Mt 6:26 13:3, 4, 18 25:24, 26, Mk 4:3, 4, Lk 8:5 12:24, Jo 4:36, II Co 9:10; c. acc. rei, Mt 13:24, 27, 37, 39, Mk 4:32, Lk 8:5, I Co 15:36, 37; seq. εἰς, Mt 13:22, Mk 4:18; ἐν, Mt 13:24, 31; ἐπί, c. gen., Mk 4:31; ἐπί, c. acc, Mt 13:20, 23, Mk 4:16, 20; παρά, c. acc., Mt 13:19. Metaph.: I Co 9:11 15:42-44, Ga 6:7, 8, Ja 3:18; in proverbial sayings, Mt 25:24, 26, Lk 19:21, 22, Jo 4:37, II Co 9:6, Ga 6:7; in interpretation of parables, Mt 13:19-23, Mk 4:14-20 †
