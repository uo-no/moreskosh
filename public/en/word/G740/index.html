ἄρτος artos G740

Gloss

bread; a loaf

Occurrences in the New Testament

97

Senses (IGSD)

  1. bread (90)
    Bread as food. Includes daily sustenance and the sense of a meal.
  2. bread of the Presence (4)
    The bread offered in the temple (MAT 12:4, etc.).
  3. breaking of bread (3)
    The fellowship meal in ACT 2:46 and 20:7.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

97 ἄρτος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for לֶחֶם;] bread, a loaf: Mt 4:3, 4, Mk 3:20, al.; ἄρτοι τ. προθέσεως, bread of the setting forth, i.e. the shewbread. Metaph., ὁ ἄ. τ. θεοῦ, τ. ζωῆς, ref. to Christ, Jo 6:33, 35; in general, food: Mt 6:11, al.; ἄ. φαγεῖν (Heb. אֲכֹל לֶחֶם), to eat (MM, s.v.), Lu 14:1, al.
