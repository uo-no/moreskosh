Senses (NGSD)

  1. how great (3)
    Asking about size or degree: how great. “how great a struggle I am having”, “how small a fire sets how great a forest”. The reading ‘small / great’ in James 3:5 is not decided.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Colossians 2:1

      Θέλω γὰρ ὑμᾶς εἰδέναι ἡλίκον ἀγῶνα ἔχω ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν καὶ τῶν ἐν Λαοδικείᾳ καὶ ὅσοι οὐχ ἑόρακαν τὸ πρόσωπόν μου ἐν σαρκί,

      For I desire to have you know how greatly I struggle for you and for those at Laodicea, and for as many as have not seen my face in the flesh;

    • James 3:5

      οὕτως καὶ ἡ γλῶσσα μικρὸν μέλος ἐστὶν καὶ μεγάλα αὐχεῖ. Ἰδοὺ ἡλίκον πῦρ ἡλίκην ὕλην ἀνάπτει·

      So the tongue is also a little member, and boasts great things. See how a small fire can spread to a large forest!

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 * ἡλίκος, -η, -ον, prop., as big as, as old as. As indirect interrog., what sized, what, how great, how small (the sense to be determined by the context): Col 2:1, Ja 3:5, Ga 6:11 (WH, mg.; πηλ-, WH, txt., RV).†
