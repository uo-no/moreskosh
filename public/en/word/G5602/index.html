Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

101 ὧδε, adv., [in LXX for הֲֲלֹם ,פֹּה ,הִנֶּה, etc. ;] prop., of manner, so (Hom., al.). In poets (rarely) and late writers, of place, hither (Bl, §25, 2): Mt 8:29, Mk 11:3, Lk 9:41, Jo 6:25, al.; ἕως ὧδε, Lk 23:5; here: Mt 12:6, Mk 9:1, Lk 9:33, Jo 6:9, al.; τὰ ὧδε, Col 4:9; opp. to ἐκεῖ, He 7:8; ὧδε . . . ἢ ὧδε (ἐκεῖ), Mt 24:23, Mk 13:21; metaph., here (i.e. in this circumstance or connection), I Co 4:2, Re 13:10, 18 14:12 17:9.
