ἀγαπητός agapētos G27

Gloss

beloved

Occurrences in the New Testament

61

Senses (IGSD)

  1. beloved (one) (55)
    One who is loved.
  2. beloved son (6)
    "My beloved Son" in the voice from heaven.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

61 ἀγαπητός, -ή, -όν (< ἀγαπάω), [in LXX chiefly for יָתִיד, יָדִיד;] beloved (v. M, Pr., 221); by God: of Christ, Mt 3:17; of men, Ro 1:7; by Christians, of one another: I Co 4:14; freq. as form of address, I Co 10:14; opp. to ἐχθρός, Ro 11:28 (v. AR, Eph., 229; Cremer, 17; MM, VGT, s.v.).
