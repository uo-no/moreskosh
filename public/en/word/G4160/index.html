ποιέω poieō G4160

Gloss

to make; produce; create

Occurrences in the New Testament

572

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to do (438)
    Acting in general. To do what was commanded, what is good, what is evil. To do God's will, to do the word, etc.
  2. to make (39)
    To form, build, or prepare things. Includes God's creation.
  3. to make / appoint (73)
    To put a person into a state or role; to appoint.
  4. to bear fruit / produce (17)
    A tree bearing fruit. Figuratively, producing fruit or profit.
  5. to spend (time) (1)
    To pass a period of time (only JAS 4:13).
