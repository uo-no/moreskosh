Bible Green

προσκυνέω proskyneō G4352

Gloss

to make obeisance; do reverence to; worship

Occurrences in the New Testament

60

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to worship (50)
    To worship God.
  2. to fall prostrate (10)
    To prostrate oneself before a person in homage or supplication.
