Senses (NGSD)

  1. barely, with difficulty (6)
    With difficulty, barely, scarcely: “if the righteous is scarcely saved”, “with difficulty restrained”, “arrived with difficulty off Cnidus”, “came with difficulty to a place called Fair Havens”, “with difficulty secured the small boat”, “one will scarcely die for a righteous person”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 14:18

      καὶ ταῦτα λέγοντες μόλις κατέπαυσαν τοὺς ὄχλους τοῦ μὴ θύειν αὐτοῖς.

      Even saying these things, they hardly stopped the multitudes from making a sacrifice to them.

    • Acts 27:8

      μόλις τε παραλεγόμενοι αὐτὴν ἤλθομεν εἰς τόπον τινὰ καλούμενον Καλοὺς Λιμένας ᾧ ἐγγὺς ἦν πόλις Λασαία.

      With difficulty sailing along it we came to a certain place called Fair Havens, near the city of Lasea.

      Read Acts 27:8 in the app

    • Romans 5:7

      μόλις γὰρ ὑπὲρ δικαίου τις ἀποθανεῖται· ὑπὲρ γὰρ τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ τάχα τις καὶ τολμᾷ ἀποθανεῖν·

      For one will hardly die for a righteous man. Yet perhaps for a good person someone would even dare to die.
