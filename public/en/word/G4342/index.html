Senses (NGSD)

  1. to devote oneself / persist (7)
    To devote oneself, to apply oneself earnestly, to hold firmly: “with one accord devoted themselves to prayer”, “devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching”, “we will devote ourselves to prayer and the ministry of the word”, “be constant in prayer”, “continue steadfastly in prayer”, “continued with Philip”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 1:14

      οὗτοι πάντες ἦσαν προσκαρτεροῦντες ὁμοθυμαδὸν τῇ προσευχῇ σὺν γυναιξὶν καὶ Μαριὰμ τῇ μητρὶ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ καὶ σὺν τοῖς ἀδελφοῖς αὐτοῦ.

      All these with one accord continued steadfastly in prayer and supplication, along with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brothers.

      Day by day, continuing steadfastly with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread at home, they took their food with gladness and singleness of heart,

    • Acts 8:13

      Simon himself also believed. Being baptized, he continued with Philip. Seeing signs and great miracles occurring, he was amazed.

  2. to attend / wait on (3)
    To attend on, to be in readiness, to perform one's duty: “soldiers who attended him”, “to have a small boat ready”, “attending to this very thing”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 3:9

      καὶ εἶπεν τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ ἵνα πλοιάριον προσκαρτερῇ αὐτῷ διὰ τὸν ὄχλον ἵνα μὴ θλίβωσιν αὐτόν·

      He spoke to his disciples that a little boat should stay near him because of the crowd, so that they wouldn’t press on him.

      When the angel who spoke to him had departed, Cornelius called two of his household servants and a devout soldier of those who waited on him continually.
