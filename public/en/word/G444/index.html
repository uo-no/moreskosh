ἄνθρωπος anthrōpos G444

Gloss

man; a human being

Occurrences in the New Testament

550

Senses (IGSD)

  1. human being (humankind) (314)
    A human being in general, regardless of sex or status. Used in contexts where being human is the theme, in contrast with God, angels, or animals, and in an indefinite pronoun-like way.
  2. person / a certain man (specific or indefinite) (146)
    A specific or unspecified person appearing in the narrative. Often accompanied by a modifier of occupation or status.
  3. "man" in the title Son of Man (83)
    ἄνθρωπος within the title expression ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου (Son of Man). Derived from the "one like a son of man" of Daniel 7, it is part of the title Jesus uses of himself.
  4. old man / new man (personal dimension) (3)
    Appears as ὁ παλαιὸς / καινὸς / νέος ἄνθρωπος; a theological expression for the former personal mode of existence ruled by sin (old man) or the new mode created anew by God (new man).
  5. inner man (inner dimension of the person) (4)
    Appears as ὁ ἔσω / ἔξω ἄνθρωπος; refers to the inner, spiritual dimension of a person (inner man) or the outer, bodily dimension (outer man).
