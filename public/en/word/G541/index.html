ἀπαύγασμα apaugasma G541

Gloss

of light beaming from a luminous body; radiance; effulgence

Occurrences in the New Testament

1

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

1 **† ἀπ-αύγασμα, -τος, τό (< αὐγή, brightness, whence ἀπαυγάζω, to radiate or reflect), [in LXX: Wi 7:26*;] of light beaming from a luminous body, radiance, effulgence: He 1:3.†

Forms in the text

  • ἀπαύγασμα — nominative · neuter · singular 1

Examples (verses)
