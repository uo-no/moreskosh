Senses (NGSD)

  1. such as / what sort of (14)
    A relative expressing quality or manner, “like”, “what sort”: “as is the man of heaven, so are those of heaven”, “what sort of people we were”, “tribulation such as has not been from the beginning”, “whiter than any bleacher on earth could make them”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 24:21

      ἔσται γὰρ τότε θλῖψις μεγάλη οἵα οὐ γέγονεν ἀπ’ ἀρχῆς κόσμου ἕως τοῦ νῦν οὐδ’ οὐ μὴ γένηται.

      for then there will be great suffering, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now, no, nor ever will be.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 οἷος, -α, -ον, relat. pron., qualitative (related to ὅς as qualis to qui), what sort or manner of, such as, in NT usually without its correl. τοιοῦτος: Mt 24:21, Mk 9:3, II Co 12:20, I Th 1:5, II Ti 3:11, Re 16:18; seq. τοιοῦτος, I Co 15:48, II Co 10:11; id. redundant, Mk 13:19; τ. αὐτὸν . . . σἷον, Phl 1:30; οὐχ οἷον δὲ ὅτι, elliptically, but it is not as though (RV), Ro 9:6.†
