Senses (NGSD)

  1. dove (10)
    A dove: “innocent as doves”, “those who sold pigeons”, “the Spirit like a dove”, “remained like a dove”, “two young pigeons”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 3:16

      βαπτισθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εὐθὺς ἀνέβη ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕδατος· καὶ ἰδοὺ ἠνεῴχθησαν οἱ οὐρανοί, καὶ εἶδεν πνεῦμα θεοῦ καταβαῖνον ὡσεὶ περιστερὰν ἐρχόμενον ἐπ’ αὐτόν·

      Jesus, when he was baptized, went up directly from the water: and behold, the heavens were opened to him. He saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming on him.

      Immediately coming up from the water, he saw the heavens parting and the Spirit descending on him like a dove.
