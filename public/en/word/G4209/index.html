Senses (NGSD)

  1. purple cloth, purple (4)
    Purple cloth, a purple garment: “purple and fine linen”, “dressed him in a purple robe”, “took the purple robe off him”, “the purple shellfish”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 15:17

      καὶ ἐνδιδύσκουσιν αὐτὸν πορφύραν καὶ περιτιθέασιν αὐτῷ πλέξαντες ἀκάνθινον στέφανον·

      They clothed him with purple; and weaving a crown of thorns, they put it on him.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 πορφύρα, -ας, ἡ, [in LXX for אַרְגָּמָן, אַרְגְּוָן;] the purple-fish (cf. I Mac 4:23). purple dye. Later, = πορφυρίς, a purple garment: Mk 15:17, 20, Lk 16:19, Re 18:12.†
