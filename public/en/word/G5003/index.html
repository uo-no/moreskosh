Senses (NGSD)

  1. to lament (1)
    To grieve and wail, to undergo hardship: “grieve and mourn”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • James 4:9

      ταλαιπωρήσατε καὶ πενθήσατε καὶ κλαύσατε· ὁ γέλως ὑμῶν εἰς πένθος μετατραπήτω καὶ ἡ χαρὰ εἰς κατήφειαν·

      Lament, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ταλαιπωρέω, -ῶ (< ταλαίπωρος), [in LXX Chiefly for שָׁדַד pu. ;] to do hard labour, suffer hardship or distress: Ja 4:9. In cl. occasionally trans., to weary, distress (so Ps 16 (17):9, Is 33:1).†
