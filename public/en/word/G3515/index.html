Senses (NGSD)

  1. to behave like an infant (1)
    To act like a little child: “be infants”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 14:20

      Brothers, don’t be children in thoughts, yet in malice be babies, but in thoughts be mature.
