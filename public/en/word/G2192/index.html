Gloss

to have; in various senses and constructions

707

Senses (IGSD)

  1. possession (289)
    Possession: having things, property, authority, opportunity, etc. as one's own.
  2. holding / carrying (33)
    Holding in the hand, wearing, bearing, containing (of vessels), holding captive.
  3. relationship / bodily condition (91)
    Having a person as a relation (father, wife, friend, king, etc.), having a body part, being pregnant, having a share.
  4. state / condition (203)
    Bearing abstract things such as faith, love, peace, need, or conditions such as illness or spirit possession, as a state of body and mind.
  5. to keep (11)
    Keeping words, teaching, faith, etc., holding on to them.
  6. to regard as (9)
    Holding a person to be something: "to regard as." With ὡς or εἰς, and the form ἔχε με παρῃτημένον, "have me excused."
  7. ability / necessity (41)
    With an infinitive, expresses "be able to," "have something to," "be obliged to."
  8. manner (24)
    With καλῶς, κακῶς, οὕτως, ἑτοίμως, etc., an intransitive use expressing "be in a certain condition." Includes expressing a span of time.
  9. middle voice (next / adjoining) (6)
    In the form ἔχομαι, the middle voice, expresses "next" in time or place, and "associated" with things.
