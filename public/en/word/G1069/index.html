Senses (NGSD)

  1. neighbor (4)
    A neighbor, one living nearby: “a neighbour”, “calls together friends and neighbours”, “calls her neighbours”, “do not invite your neighbours”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 15:6

      καὶ ἐλθὼν εἰς τὸν οἶκον συγκαλεῖ τοὺς φίλους καὶ τοὺς γείτονας, λέγων αὐτοῖς· Συγχάρητέ μοι ὅτι εὗρον τὸ πρόβατόν μου τὸ ἀπολωλός.

      When he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost!’
