Senses (NGSD)

  1. parable / illustration (44)
    A coherent illustration told as a story or comparison. Centered on the parables Jesus tells (the sower, the weeds, the vineyard); also appears in the form ἐν παραβολαῖς λαλεῖν.

    • Matthew 13:3

      καὶ ἐλάλησεν αὐτοῖς πολλὰ ἐν παραβολαῖς λέγων· Ἰδοὺ ἐξῆλθεν ὁ σπείρων τοῦ σπείρειν.

      He spoke to them many things in parables, saying, “Behold, a farmer went out to sow.

    • Matthew 22:1

      Καὶ ἀποκριθεὶς ὁ Ἰησοῦς πάλιν εἶπεν ἐν παραβολαῖς αὐτοῖς λέγων·

      Jesus answered and spoke to them again in parables, saying,

      Read Matthew 22:1 in the app

    • Luke 8:9

      Ἐπηρώτων δὲ αὐτὸν οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ τίς αὕτη εἴη ἡ παραβολή.

      Then his disciples asked him, “What does this parable mean?”

      Read Luke 8:9 in the app

  2. proverb / short figurative saying (4)
    A use naming a proverb-like saying or short figurative remark "parable", without the form of a story.

    • Matthew 15:15

      Peter answered him, “Explain the parable to us.”

      When he had entered into a house away from the multitude, his disciples asked him about the parable.

  3. type / symbol (2)
    A use in which something is spoken of as a symbol or type for the present time. Appears in Hebrews.

    • Hebrews 9:9

      ἥτις παραβολὴ εἰς τὸν καιρὸν τὸν ἐνεστηκότα, καθ’ ἣν δῶρά τε καὶ θυσίαι προσφέρονται μὴ δυνάμεναι κατὰ συνείδησιν τελειῶσαι τὸν λατρεύοντα,

      This is a symbol of the present age, where gifts and sacrifices are offered that are incapable, concerning the conscience, of making the worshiper perfect,

      concluding that God is able to raise up even from the dead. Figuratively speaking, he also did receive him back from the dead.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

50 παραβολή, -ῆς, ἡ (< παραβάλλω), [in LXX (cf. McNeile, Mt., 185): Nu 23:7, De 28:37, Ps 43 (44):14, Pr 1:6, Ez 12:22, al. (מָשָׁל), Si 47:17 (חִידָה), al. mutt. in Si, Wi 5:3;] a placing beside, juxtaposition (Polyb., al.). a comparing, comparison (Plat., Arist., al.). a comparison, illustration, analogy, figure (Arist., al.): Mt 24:32, Mk 3:23, He 9:9 11:19; specif. of the pictures and narratives drawn from nature and human life which are characteristic of the synoptic teaching of our Lord, a parable: Mt 13:3, 10, Mk 4:2, 10, Lk 8:4, 9-11, al.; c. gen. ref., Mt 13:18, 36. Like Heb. מָשָׁל (I Ki 10:12, Pr 1:6, Si 3:29, al.), = παροιμία, a proverb or gnomic saying: Lk 4:23 5:36 6:39. SYN.: παροιμία (v. Abbott, Essays, 82 ff.).
