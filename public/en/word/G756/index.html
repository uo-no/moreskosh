ἄρχω archō G756

Gloss

begin

Occurrences in the New Testament

89

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to begin (87)
    In the middle voice, the beginning of an action.
  2. to rule (2)
    Only the sense of ruling in ROM 15:12 and MRK 10:42.
