Senses (NGSD)

  1. rest / place of rest (9)
    Rest, repose, a resting place: “they shall not enter my rest”, “while the promise of entering his rest still stands”, “we who have believed enter that rest”, “whoever has entered his rest”, “what is the place of my rest?”, “let us strive to enter that rest”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 7:49

      Ὁ οὐρανός μοι θρόνος, ἡ δὲ γῆ ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν μου· ποῖον οἶκον οἰκοδομήσετέ μοι, λέγει κύριος, ἢ τίς τόπος τῆς καταπαύσεώς μου;

      ‘heaven is my throne, and the earth a footstool for my feet. What kind of house will you build me?’ says the Lord. ‘Or what is the place of my rest?

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 κατά-παυσις, -εως, ἡ (< καταπαύω), [in LXX chiefly for מְנוּחָה, Ps 94 (95):11, Is 66:1, al.; ἡμέρα τῆς κ., II Mac 15:1;] in cl., a putting to rest, causing to cease. In LXX and NT, rest, repose: Ac 7:49(LXX), He 3:11, 18 4:1, 3, 5, 10, 11.†
