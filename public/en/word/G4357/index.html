Senses (NGSD)

  1. to stay, to remain (4)
    To stay in a place, to remain there: “to remain at Ephesus”, “stayed there many days longer”, “have been with me three days”, “have stayed with me three days”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 8:2

      Σπλαγχνίζομαι ἐπὶ τὸν ὄχλον ὅτι ἤδη ἡμέραι τρεῖς προσμένουσίν μοι καὶ οὐκ ἔχουσιν τί φάγωσιν·

      “I have compassion on the multitude, because they have stayed with me now three days and have nothing to eat.

      Read Mark 8:2 in the app

    • Acts 18:18

      Ὁ δὲ Παῦλος ἔτι προσμείνας ἡμέρας ἱκανὰς τοῖς ἀδελφοῖς ἀποταξάμενος ἐξέπλει εἰς τὴν Συρίαν, καὶ σὺν αὐτῷ Πρίσκιλλα καὶ Ἀκύλας, κειράμενος ἐν Κεγχρεαῖς τὴν κεφαλήν, εἶχεν γὰρ εὐχήν.

      Paul, having stayed after this many more days, took his leave of the brothers, and sailed from there for Syria, together with Priscilla and Aquila. He shaved his head in Cenchreae, for he had a vow.

      Read Acts 18:18 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 1:3

      Καθὼς παρεκάλεσά σε προσμεῖναι ἐν Ἐφέσῳ, πορευόμενος εἰς Μακεδονίαν, ἵνα παραγγείλῃς τισὶν μὴ ἑτεροδιδασκαλεῖν

      As I urged you when I was going into Macedonia, stay at Ephesus that you might command certain men not to teach a different doctrine,

      Read 1 Timothy 1:3 in the app

  2. to continue in (faith, prayer) (3)
    To remain steadfast in the Lord, in grace, in prayer and the like: “to remain faithful to the Lord”, “to continue in the grace of God”, “continues in supplications and prayers night and day”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 11:23

      ὃς παραγενόμενος καὶ ἰδὼν τὴν χάριν τὴν τοῦ θεοῦ, ἐχάρη καὶ παρεκάλει πάντας τῇ προθέσει τῆς καρδίας προσμένειν τῷ κυρίῳ,

      who, when he had come, and had seen the grace of God, was glad. He exhorted them all, that with purpose of heart they should remain near to the Lord.

      Now when the synagogue broke up, many of the Jews and of the devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas; who, speaking to them, urged them to continue in the grace of God.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 προσ-μένω, [in LXX: Jg 3:25 A (חוּל hi.), To 2:2 א, Wi 3:9, III Mac 7:17*;] to wait longer, continue, remain still: Ac 18:18; seq. ἐν, I Ti 1:3. C. dat., to remain with: Mt 15:32, Mk 8:32. Metaph., to remain attached to, cleave unto, abide in: τ. κυρίῳ, Ac 11:23 (R, txt.; ἐν τ. κ., R, mg., v. supr.); τ. χάριτι τ. θεοῦ, Ac 13:43; τ. δεήσεσιν, I Ti 5:5.†
