Senses (NGSD)

  1. registration (2)
    A registration, a census: “the registration”, “the census”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 2:2

      αὕτη ἀπογραφὴ πρώτη ἐγένετο ἡγεμονεύοντος τῆς Συρίας Κυρηνίου·

      This was the first enrollment made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ἀπο-γραφή, -ῆς, ἡ (ἀπογράφω), [in LXX: Da LXX 10:21 (כְּתָב), I Es 8:30, AB, II Mac 2:1, III Mac 2:32 4:15, 17 7:22*;] a written copy. As law term, a deposition (Demos.). In late writers, a register, enrolment, census (MM, s.v.; Deiss., LAE, 160, 268 f.): Lk 2:2, Ac 5:37.†
