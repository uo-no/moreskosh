Senses (NGSD)

  1. violent person (2)
    A striker, a violent person: “not a striker” (twice).
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Timothy 3:3

      not a drinker, not violent, not greedy for money, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not covetous;
