Senses (NGSD)

  1. to touch (3)
    To touch, to handle: “do not handle”, “so that the destroyer of the firstborn might not touch the Israelites”, “whatever touches”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Colossians 2:21

      Μὴ ἅψῃ μηδὲ γεύσῃ μηδὲ θίγῃς,

      “Don’t handle, nor taste, nor touch”

      Read Colossians 2:21 in the app

    • Hebrews 11:28

      πίστει πεποίηκεν τὸ πάσχα καὶ τὴν πρόσχυσιν τοῦ αἵματος, ἵνα μὴ ὁ ὀλοθρεύων τὰ πρωτότοκα θίγῃ αὐτῶν.

      By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, that the destroyer of the firstborn should not touch them.

      Read Hebrews 11:28 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:20

      οὐκ ἔφερον γὰρ τὸ διαστελλόμενον· Κἂν θηρίον θίγῃ τοῦ ὄρους, λιθοβοληθήσεται·

      for they could not stand that which was commanded, “If even an animal touches the mountain, it shall be stoned”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 θιγγάνω, [in LXX for נגע, Ex 19:12*;] to touch, handle: Col 2:21, He 12:20(LXX). to injure (like Heb. נגע, and as in Eur., Iph. Aul., 1351): c. gen., He 11:28.† SYN.: v.s. ἅπτω.
