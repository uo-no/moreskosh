Bible Green

ἀκούω akouō G191

Gloss

to hear; listen; attend

Occurrences in the New Testament

428

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to hear (listen) (219)
    Sound, voice, or words reaching the ear. Includes the ability to hear being given.
  2. to hear news (110)
    To learn of events or of a person as news.
  3. to obey / heed (72)
    To listen attentively, accept, and obey. Includes the call "Listen!"
  4. to hear and understand (19)
    To understand what was heard. Appears often in negative contexts of hearing without understanding.
  5. to hear (God answering) (4)
    God hearing and answering prayers and requests.
  6. to hear (a case) (4)
    To hear a person's account in a trial or official setting.
