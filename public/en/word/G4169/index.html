Senses (NGSD)

  1. which / what kind of (question) (28)
    An interrogative adjective asking which, or what kind or quality.

    • Matthew 19:18

      He said to him, “Which ones?” Jesus said, “‘You shall not murder.’ ‘You shall not commit adultery.’ ‘You shall not steal.’ ‘You shall not offer false testimony.’

      “If you love those who love you, what credit is that to you? For even sinners love those who love them.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

33 ποῖος, -α, -ον, interrog. pronom. adj. (corresponding to the demonstr. τοῖος and the relat. οἷος), [in LXX chiefly for אֵי־זֶה;] of what quality or sort: absol., n. pl., Lk 24:19; c. subst.; prop., in direct questions: Mt 19:18 21:23 22:36 24:42, Mk 11:28 12:28, Lk 6:32-34, Jo 10:32, Ac 4:7 7:49, Ro 3:27, I Co 15:35, Ja 4:14 (but v.s. ποία), I Pe 2:20; in indirect questions = ὁποῖος: Mt 21:24, 27 24:43, Mk 11:29, 33, Lk 12:39 20:2, 8, Jo 12:33 18:32 21:19, Ac 23:34, I Pe 1:11, Re 3:3; ποίας (sc. ὁδοῦ), Lk 5:19.†
