Senses (NGSD)

  1. imitator (6)
    One who follows or imitates: “become imitators of me”, “as I am an imitator of Christ”, “you became imitators of the Lord”, “became imitators of the churches of God”, “be imitators of God, as beloved children”, “imitators of those who inherit the promises”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 4:16

      παρακαλῶ οὖν ὑμᾶς, μιμηταί μου γίνεσθε.

      I beg you therefore, be imitators of me.

      Be therefore imitators of God, as beloved children.
