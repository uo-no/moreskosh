Senses (NGSD)

  1. bad, worthless (6)
    Bad, worthless (with a moral nuance): “whether good or evil”, “every evil deed”, “everyone who does evil hates the light”, “those who have done evil”, “before they had done anything good or evil”, “so that an opponent has nothing bad to say”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 3:20

      πᾶς γὰρ ὁ φαῦλα πράσσων μισεῖ τὸ φῶς καὶ οὐκ ἔρχεται πρὸς τὸ φῶς, ἵνα μὴ ἐλεγχθῇ τὰ ἔργα αὐτοῦ·

      For everyone who does evil hates the light and doesn’t come to the light, lest his works would be exposed.

    • Romans 9:11

      μήπω γὰρ γεννηθέντων μηδὲ πραξάντων τι ἀγαθὸν ἢ φαῦλον, ἵνα ἡ κατ’ ἐκλογὴν πρόθεσις τοῦ θεοῦ μένῃ,

      For being not yet born, neither having done anything good or bad, that the purpose of God according to election might stand, not of works, but of him who calls,

      Read Romans 9:11 in the app

    • Titus 2:8

      λόγον ὑγιῆ ἀκατάγνωστον, ἵνα ὁ ἐξ ἐναντίας ἐντραπῇ μηδὲν ἔχων λέγειν περὶ ἡμῶν φαῦλον.

      and soundness of speech that can’t be condemned, that he who opposes you may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say about us.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 φαῦλος, -η, -ον [in LXX: Pr 22:8 (עַוְלָה) 29:9 (אֳבִיל), etc. ;] slight, worthless, of no account, both of persons and things in various shades of meaning (v. LS, s.v.), in NT, as freq. in cl., always with distinct moral reference (v. Ellic., Past. Epp., 203; Hort, Ja., 85; Tr., Syn., lxxxiv), worthless, bad: Jo 3:20, Tit 2:8, Ja 3:16; opp. to ἀγαθός (q.v.), Jo 5:29, Ro 9:11, II Co 5:10.† SYN.: v.s. ἄθεσμος
