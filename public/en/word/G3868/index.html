Senses (NGSD)

  1. to reject / refuse / avoid (7)
    To refuse, reject, avoid, decline: “have nothing to do with irreverent myths”, “refuse to enrol younger widows”, “avoid foolish controversies”, “I do not seek to escape death”, “do not refuse him who is speaking”, “have nothing to do with a divisive person”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 25:11

      εἰ μὲν οὖν ἀδικῶ καὶ ἄξιον θανάτου πέπραχά τι, οὐ παραιτοῦμαι τὸ ἀποθανεῖν· εἰ δὲ οὐδέν ἐστιν ὧν οὗτοι κατηγοροῦσίν μου, οὐδείς με δύναται αὐτοῖς χαρίσασθαι· Καίσαρα ἐπικαλοῦμαι.

      For if I have done wrong and have committed anything worthy of death, I don’t refuse to die; but if none of those things is true that they accuse me of, no one can give me up to them. I appeal to Caesar!”

    • 1 Timothy 5:11

      νεωτέρας δὲ χήρας παραιτοῦ· ὅταν γὰρ καταστρηνιάσωσιν τοῦ Χριστοῦ, γαμεῖν θέλουσιν,

      But refuse younger widows, for when they have grown wanton against Christ, they desire to marry,

      Read 1 Timothy 5:11 in the app

    • Titus 3:10

      αἱρετικὸν ἄνθρωπον μετὰ μίαν καὶ δευτέραν νουθεσίαν παραιτοῦ,

      Avoid a factious man after a first and second warning,

      Read Titus 3:10 in the app

  2. to make excuses / decline (3)
    To decline an invitation, to make excuses: “they all alike began to make excuses”, “please have me excused”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 14:18

      καὶ ἤρξαντο ἀπὸ μιᾶς πάντες παραιτεῖσθαι. ὁ πρῶτος εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ἀγρὸν ἠγόρασα καὶ ἔχω ἀνάγκην ἐξελθὼν ἰδεῖν αὐτόν· ἐρωτῶ σε, ἔχε με παρῃτημένον.

      They all as one began to make excuses. “The first said to him, ‘I have bought a field, and I must go and see it. Please have me excused.’

      Read Luke 14:18 in the app

    • Luke 14:19

      καὶ ἕτερος εἶπεν· Ζεύγη βοῶν ἠγόρασα πέντε καὶ πορεύομαι δοκιμάσαι αὐτά· ἐρωτῶ σε, ἔχε με παρῃτημένον.

      “Another said, ‘I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I must go try them out. Please have me excused.’

      Read Luke 14:19 in the app

  3. to request / beg (2)
    To ask, to entreat: “begged that no further word be spoken to them”, “asked as was customary”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 15:6

      Κατὰ δὲ ἑορτὴν ἀπέλυεν αὐτοῖς ἕνα δέσμιον ὃν παρῃτοῦντο.

      Now at the feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whomever they asked of him.

      the sound of a trumpet, and the voice of words; which those who heard it begged that not one more word should be spoken to them,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

12 παρ-αιτέομαι, -οῦμαι, [in LXX for בּקשׁ pi., etc.;] to beg of or from another: Mk 15:6 (Rec. ὅνπερ ᾐτοῦντο, v. Field, Notes, 43). to deprecate; prop., c. neg., to entreat that not (Thuc., al.): He 12:19; to refuse, decline, avoid: o. acc., I Ti 4:7 5:11, II Ti 2:23, Tit 3:10, He 12:25 (EV; but v. Field, Notes, 234). to beg off, ask to be excused, excuse (Polyb., al.) : Lk 14:18, 19 (He 12:25, Field, Notes, 234).†
