Senses (NGSD)

  1. folly, fury (2)
    Folly, senselessness, madness: “folly”, “filled with fury”. Whether Luke 6:11 means “anger” or “folly” is not decided. The sense is not divided.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 6:11

      αὐτοὶ δὲ ἐπλήσθησαν ἀνοίας, καὶ διελάλουν πρὸς ἀλλήλους τί ἂν ποιήσαιεν τῷ Ἰησοῦ.

      But they were filled with rage, and talked with one another about what they might do to Jesus.
