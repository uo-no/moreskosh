ἀποθνῄσκω apothnēskō G599

Gloss

to die; of natural death

Occurrences in the New Testament

111

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to die (103)
    To lose one's life. Includes "dying daily" in 1CO 15:31.
  2. to die (figurative) (8)
    Death as the end of a relationship, such as dying to sin (ROM 6).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

Milligan, NTD, 258 f.; DCG, i, 791b; Cremer, 286; MM, s.v.; on the perfective force of this verb, M, Pr., 112, 114; and on the distinction bet. pres. and aor., M, Pr., 113 f.).
