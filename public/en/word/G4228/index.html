πούς pous G4228

Gloss

a foot; both of men and beasts

Occurrences in the New Testament

93

Senses (IGSD)

  1. foot (80)
    The foot of the body. Includes washing feet, putting at the feet, and the figure of making straight paths (HEB 12:13).
  2. under the feet (13)
    The expression of dominion seen in the quotation "make enemies a footstool."

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

93 πούς, ποδός, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for רֶגֶל;] a foot, both of men and beasts: Mt 4:6(LXX), Mk 9:45, Lk 1:79, Jo 11:44, Ac 7:5, al.; ὑπο τοὺς π., Ro 16:20, I Co 15:25, 27, Eph 1:22, He 2:8; ὑποκάτω τῶν π., Mt 22:44(LXX); πρὸς (παρὰ) τοὺς π., Mk 5:22, Lk 8:41, al.; fig., Mt 15:30, Lk 10:39, Ac 5:2, al.; ἔμπροσθεν τῶν π., Re 3:9 19:10, al.; ἐπὶ τοὺς π., Ac 10:25. By meton., of a person in motion (Ps 118 (119):101): Lk 1:79, Ac 5:9, Ro 3:15 10:15, He 12:13.
