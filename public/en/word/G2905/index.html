Senses (NGSD)

  1. to shout / cry out (9)
    To cry, to shout, to call loudly: “raising their voices”, “crying Hosanna”, “he cried out, ‘Lazarus, come out’”, “cried out for Barabbas”, “cried out, ‘Crucify him’”, “demons crying out”, “he will not cry aloud”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 12:19

      οὐκ ἐρίσει οὐδὲ κραυγάσει, οὐδὲ ἀκούσει τις ἐν ταῖς πλατείαις τὴν φωνὴν αὐτοῦ.

      He will not strive, nor shout, neither will anyone hear his voice in the streets.

      they took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet him, and cried out, “Hosanna! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel!”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 κραυγάζω (< κραυγή), [in LXX: II Es 3:13 (רוּעַ hi.)*;] = κράζω, of animals, to bay, to croak, etc. Of men, to cry out, shout: Mt 12:19, Jo 12:13 19:15, Ac 22:23; seq. λέγων, Jo 18:40 19:6, 12; καὶ λέγων, Lk 4:41 (WH, κράζ -); φωνῇ μεγάλῃ, Jo 11:43 (cf. Abbott, JV, 269 f.).†
