Senses (IGSD)

  1. if (condition) (272)
    Marks a hypothetical condition. Also used in the form "unless (if not)."
  2. whoever (indefinite) (61)
    Combined with a relative, introduces a generalizing conditional clause meaning "whoever, whatever, whenever."
