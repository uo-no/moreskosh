Senses (NGSD)

  1. to shake (5)
    To shake, to quake, to be stirred: “once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heaven”, “the guards … trembled with fear”, “the whole city was stirred”, “the earth quaked”, “figs shaken by a great wind”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 21:10

      καὶ εἰσελθόντος αὐτοῦ εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα ἐσείσθη πᾶσα ἡ πόλις λέγουσα· Τίς ἐστιν οὗτος;

      When he had come into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred up, saying, “Who is this?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 σείω [in LXX chiefly for רעשׁ ;] to shake, move to and fro: τ. γῆν, He 12:26 (LXX); pass., ἡ γῆ, Mt 27:51 (LXX); συκῆ, Re 6:13. Metaph., to agitate, stir up, with fear or some other emotion: Mt 21:10 28:4 (cf. ἀνα-, δια-, κατα-σείω).†
