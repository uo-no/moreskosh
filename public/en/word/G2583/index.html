Senses (NGSD)

  1. sphere, assigned area (3)
    The area assigned to one, one’s sphere: “not beyond limits, but within the area God has assigned”, “according to the sphere”, “within our assigned field”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 10:13

      ἡμεῖς δὲ οὐκ εἰς τὰ ἄμετρα καυχησόμεθα, ἀλλὰ κατὰ τὸ μέτρον τοῦ κανόνος οὗ ἐμέρισεν ἡμῖν ὁ θεὸς μέτρου, ἐφικέσθαι ἄχρι καὶ ὑμῶν

      But we will not boast beyond proper limits, but within the boundaries with which God appointed to us, which reach even to you.

      Read 2 Corinthians 10:13 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 10:15

      οὐκ εἰς τὰ ἄμετρα καυχώμενοι ἐν ἀλλοτρίοις κόποις, ἐλπίδα δὲ ἔχοντες αὐξανομένης τῆς πίστεως ὑμῶν ἐν ὑμῖν μεγαλυνθῆναι κατὰ τὸν κανόνα ἡμῶν εἰς περισσείαν,

      not boasting beyond proper limits in other men’s labors, but having hope that as your faith grows, we will be abundantly enlarged by you in our sphere of influence,

      Read 2 Corinthians 10:15 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 10:16

      εἰς τὰ ὑπερέκεινα ὑμῶν εὐαγγελίσασθαι, οὐκ ἐν ἀλλοτρίῳ κανόνι εἰς τὰ ἕτοιμα καυχήσασθαι.

      so as to preach the Good News even to the parts beyond you, not to boast in what someone else has already done.

      Read 2 Corinthians 10:16 in the app

  2. rule, standard (1)
    A rule, measure or principle: “all who walk by this rule”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Galatians 6:16

      καὶ ὅσοι τῷ κανόνι τούτῳ στοιχήσουσιν, εἰρήνη ἐπ’ αὐτοὺς καὶ ἔλεος, καὶ ἐπὶ τὸν Ἰσραὴλ τοῦ θεοῦ.

      As many as walk by this rule, peace and mercy be on them, and on God’s Israel.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 κανών, -όνος, ὁ (cf. κάννα, and Heb. קָנֶה, a reed), [in LXX: Mi 7:4, Jth 13:6, IV Mac 7:21 (and in Aq., Ps 18 (19):5, Jb 38:5)*;] a rod or bar (Jth 13:6). a measuring rule; hence, metaph, a rule or standard: Ga 6:16. a limit (RV, province): II Co 10:13, 15, 16. (For the history of the word and esp. its later meanings, v. Westc., Canon, App. A; cf. also MM, Exp., xv; Cremer, 744.)†
