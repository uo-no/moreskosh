Senses (NGSD)

  1. to write beforehand (2)
    To write previously, to record in advance: “as I wrote briefly before”, “whatever was written in former times”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 15:4

      ὅσα γὰρ προεγράφη, εἰς τὴν ἡμετέραν διδασκαλίαν ἐγράφη, ἵνα διὰ τῆς ὑπομονῆς καὶ διὰ τῆς παρακλήσεως τῶν γραφῶν τὴν ἐλπίδα ἔχωμεν.

      For whatever things were written before were written for our learning, that through perseverance and through encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.

      Read Romans 15:4 in the app

    • Ephesians 3:3

      κατὰ ἀποκάλυψιν ἐγνωρίσθη μοι τὸ μυστήριον, καθὼς προέγραψα ἐν ὀλίγῳ,

      how that by revelation the mystery was made known to me, as I wrote before in few words,

      Read Ephesians 3:3 in the app

  2. to portray publicly (1)
    To depict openly, to set forth clearly: “whose figure as crucified … was portrayed”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Galatians 3:1

      Ὦ ἀνόητοι Γαλάται, τίς ὑμᾶς ἐβάσκανεν, οἷς κατ’ ὀφθαλμοὺς Ἰησοῦς Χριστὸς προεγράφη ἐσταυρωμένος;

      Foolish Galatians, who has bewitched you not to obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was openly portrayed among you as crucified?

      Read Galatians 3:1 in the app

  3. to be written down beforehand (disputed) (1)
    Those “long ago written down for this judgment”; whether it means recorded beforehand or appointed beforehand is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Jude 1:4

      παρεισέδυσαν γάρ τινες ἄνθρωποι, οἱ πάλαι προγεγραμμένοι εἰς τοῦτο τὸ κρίμα, ἀσεβεῖς, τὴν τοῦ θεοῦ ἡμῶν χάριτα μετατιθέντες εἰς ἀσέλγειαν καὶ τὸν μόνον δεσπότην καὶ κύριον ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν ἀρνούμενοι.

      For there are certain men who crept in secretly, even those who were long ago written about for this condemnation: ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into indecency, and denying our only Master, God, and Lord, Jesus Christ.

      Read Jude 1:4 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 προ-γράφω, [in LXX: Da LXX 3:3 cod., I Mac 10:36*;] to write before: Ro 15:4, Eph 3:3, Ju 4. to write in public, placard, proclaim (Dem., Plut., al.): Ga 3:1 (Lft., in l.). = ζωγραφέω, to pourtray, depict: Ga 3:1 (Syr. Pesh., Chrys.; Field, Notes, 189; CGT, in l.).†
