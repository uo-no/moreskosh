Senses (NGSD)

  1. to quench, to go out (of fire, lamps) (5)
    To put out a fire or lamp; to go out: “he will not quench a smouldering wick”, “quench the flaming arrows of the evil one”, “quenched the power of fire”, “our lamps are going out”, “the fire is not quenched”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 12:20

      κάλαμον συντετριμμένον οὐ κατεάξει καὶ λίνον τυφόμενον οὐ σβέσει, ἕως ἂν ἐκβάλῃ εἰς νῖκος τὴν κρίσιν.

      He won’t break a bruised reed. He won’t quench a smoking flax, until he leads justice to victory.

      Read Matthew 12:20 in the app

    • Matthew 25:8

      αἱ δὲ μωραὶ ταῖς φρονίμοις εἶπαν· Δότε ἡμῖν ἐκ τοῦ ἐλαίου ὑμῶν, ὅτι αἱ λαμπάδες ἡμῶν σβέννυνται.

      The foolish said to the wise, ‘Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.’

      Read Matthew 25:8 in the app

    • Ephesians 6:16

      ἐν πᾶσιν ἀναλαβόντες τὸν θυρεὸν τῆς πίστεως, ἐν ᾧ δυνήσεσθε πάντα τὰ βέλη τοῦ πονηροῦ πεπυρωμένα σβέσαι·

      above all, taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the evil one.

      Read Ephesians 6:16 in the app

  2. to quench (the Spirit) (1)
    To stifle the working of the Holy Spirit: “do not quench the Spirit”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Thessalonians 5:19

      τὸ πνεῦμα μὴ σβέννυτε,

      Don’t quench the Spirit.

      Read 1 Thessalonians 5:19 in the app
