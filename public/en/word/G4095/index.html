πίνω pinō G4095

Gloss

to drink

Occurrences in the New Testament

72

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to drink (63)
    To drink water, wine, etc. Includes the earth drinking in rain (HEB 6:7).
  2. to drink (a cup) (9)
    To drink a "cup" of suffering that must be received. MAT 20:22-23, MRK 10:38-39, JHN 18:11, MAT 26:42.
