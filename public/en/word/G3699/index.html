Senses (NGSD)

  1. where (79)
    Introduces a place or location: "where," "the place where." Includes cases close to a condition (JAS 3:16, HEB 10:18).

    • Matthew 6:19

      Μὴ θησαυρίζετε ὑμῖν θησαυροὺς ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς, ὅπου σὴς καὶ βρῶσις ἀφανίζει, καὶ ὅπου κλέπται διορύσσουσιν καὶ κλέπτουσιν·

      “Don’t lay up treasures for yourselves on the earth, where moth and rust consume, and where thieves break through and steal;

      Read Matthew 6:19 in the app

    • Luke 9:57

      Καὶ πορευομένων αὐτῶν ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ εἶπέν τις πρὸς αὐτόν· Ἀκολουθήσω σοι ὅπου ἐὰν ἀπέρχῃ.

      As they went on the way, a certain man said to him, “I want to follow you wherever you go, Lord.”

      Read Luke 9:57 in the app

    • John 14:4

      καὶ ὅπου ἐγὼ ὑπάγω οἴδατε τὴν ὁδόν.

      You know where I go, and you know the way.”

      Read John 14:4 in the app

  2. whereas / since (contrast / reason) (2)
    Shows contrast or reason. 1CO 3:3, "since there is jealousy"; 2PE 2:11, "though angels …".

    • 1 Corinthians 3:3

      ἔτι γὰρ σαρκικοί ἐστε. ὅπου γὰρ ἐν ὑμῖν ζῆλος καὶ ἔρις, οὐχὶ σαρκικοί ἐστε καὶ κατὰ ἄνθρωπον περιπατεῖτε;

      for you are still fleshly. For insofar as there is jealousy, strife, and factions among you, aren’t you fleshly, and don’t you walk in the ways of men?

      Read 1 Corinthians 3:3 in the app

    • 2 Peter 2:11

      ὅπου ἄγγελοι ἰσχύϊ καὶ δυνάμει μείζονες ὄντες οὐ φέρουσιν κατ’ αὐτῶν βλάσφημον κρίσιν.

      whereas angels, though greater in might and power, don’t bring a slanderous judgment against them before the Lord.

      Read 2 Peter 2:11 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

82 ὅπου, adv. of place, correlat. of ποῦ (q.v.), where. Prop., of place, where; in relative sentences, c. indic: Mt 25:24, 26, Mk 2:4 4:5, 15 5:40 13:14, Jo 3:8 6:62 7:34, 36 11:32 14:3 17:24 20:19, Ro 15:20, Re 2:13; after nouns of place, for relat. prepositional phrase (ἐν ᾧ., etc.), Mt 6:19, 20 13:5 26:57 28:6, Mk 6:55 9:48, Lk 12:33, Jo 1:28 4:20, 46 6:23 7:42 10:40 11:30 12:1 18:1, 20 19:18, 20, 41 20:12, Ac 17:1, Re 2:13 11:8 20:10; seq. ἐκεῖ., Mt 6:21, Lk 12:34 17:37, Jo 12:26; id. pleonast. (= Heb. אֲשֶׁר . . . שָׁם; Aram. דְּ . . . תַּמָּן; cf. Ge 13:3), ὅ. . . . ἐκεῖ, Re 12:6, 14; ὅ. . . . ἐπ' αὐτῶν, Re 17:9; ὅ. ἄν, wherever (M, Pr., 168), c. impf. indic., Mk 6:56; c. pres. subjc., Mt 24:28; ὅ. ἐάν, Mt 26:13, Mk 6:10 9:18 14:9, 14a; in quaest. indir., c. aor. subjc.: Mk 14:14b, Lk 22:11. In late writers (sometimes also in cl.; Bl., § 25, 2), with verbs of motion, = ὅποι, whither: c. indic., Jo 8:21, 22 13:33, 36 14:4 21:18, He 6:20, Ja 3:4; ὅ. ἄν, whithersoever, c. pres. ind., Re 14:4; subjc., Lk 9:57; ὅ. ἐάν, Mt 8:19. Without strict local sense, of time or condition: Col 3:11, He 9:16 10:18, Ja 3:16, II Pe 2:11. Of cause or reason (AV, whereas): I Co 3:3.†
