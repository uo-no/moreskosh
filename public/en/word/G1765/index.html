Senses (NGSD)

  1. to strengthen, to gain strength (2)
    To give strength, to regain strength: “regained strength”, “strengthened him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 22:43

      ὤφθη δὲ αὐτῷ ἄγγελος ἀπ’ οὐρανοῦ ἐνισχύων αὐτόν.

      An angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him.

      Read Luke 22:43 in the app

    • Acts 9:19

      καὶ λαβὼν τροφὴν ἐνίσχυσεν. Ἐγένετο δὲ μετὰ τῶν ἐν Δαμασκῷ μαθητῶν ἡμέρας τινὰς,

      He took food and was strengthened. Saul stayed several days with the disciples who were at Damascus.
