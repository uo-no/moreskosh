Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 παιδάριον, -ου, τό, dimin. of παῖς, [in LXX chiefly for נַעַר;] a little boy, a lad (in late and colloq. Gk. the word seems to be used with greater latitude and even to lose its dimin. force entirely; cf. To 6:2, 3, and v. MM, xviii): Jo 6:9.† SYN.: v.s. παῖς.
