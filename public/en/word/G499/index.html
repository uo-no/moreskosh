Senses (NGSD)

  1. antitype, copy (2)
    That which corresponds, a figure: “the pattern”, “a copy”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Hebrews 9:24

      οὐ γὰρ εἰς χειροποίητα εἰσῆλθεν ἅγια Χριστός, ἀντίτυπα τῶν ἀληθινῶν, ἀλλ’ εἰς αὐτὸν τὸν οὐρανόν, νῦν ἐμφανισθῆναι τῷ προσώπῳ τοῦ θεοῦ ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν·

      For Christ hasn’t entered into holy places made with hands, which are representations of the true, but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us;

      This is a symbol of baptism, which now saves you—not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ** ἀντί-τυπος, -ον (v.s. τύπος), [in LXX: Es 3:13 A*;] act. striking back; metaph., resisting, adverse. Pass. struck back; metaph., corresponding to (MM, VGT, s.v.); as impression of a seal or copy of an archetype (τύπος) (RV, like in pattern), He 9:24; as the reality (of which τύπος is the copy or adumbration) (RV, after a true likeness), I Pe 3:21 (Cremer, 357).†
