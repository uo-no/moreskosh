Senses (NGSD)

  1. under law (1)
    Being under law, following the law: “under the law of Christ”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 9:21

      τοῖς ἀνόμοις ὡς ἄνομος, μὴ ὢν ἄνομος θεοῦ ἀλλ’ ἔννομος Χριστοῦ, ἵνα κερδάνω τοὺς ἀνόμους·

      to those who are without law, as without law (not being without law toward God, but under law toward Christ), that I might win those who are without law.

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:21 in the app

  2. lawful (1)
    According to law, regular: “the lawful assembly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 19:39

      εἰ δέ τι περαιτέρω ἐπιζητεῖτε, ἐν τῇ ἐννόμῳ ἐκκλησίᾳ ἐπιλυθήσεται.

      But if you seek anything about other matters, it will be settled in the regular assembly.
