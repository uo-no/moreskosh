      For most certainly, I tell you, until heaven and earth pass away, not even one smallest letter or one tiny pen stroke shall in any way pass away from the law, until all things are accomplished.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

29 παρ-έρχομαι, [in LXX chiefly for עבר;] to pass, pass by; of persons: absol., Lk 18:37; c. acc. pers., Mk 6:48; c. acc. lot., Ac 16:8; seq. διά, c. gen., Mt 8:28; of things: τ. ποτήριον, Mt 26:39 (ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ), Mt 26:42; of time, Mt 14:15, Mk 14:35, Ac 27:9, I Pe 4:3. Metaph., to pass away, perish: Mt 5:18 24:34, 35, Mk 13:30, 31, Lk 16:17 21:32, 33, II Co 5:17, Ja 1:10, II Pe 3:10; to pass by, neglect, disregard: c. acc. rei, Lk 11:42 15:29. to come to, arrive: Lk 12:37 17:7, Ac 24:7, R, mg. (cf. ἀντι-παρέρχομαι).† SYN.: παραβαίνω.
