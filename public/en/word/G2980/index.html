Bible Green

λαλέω laleō G2980

Gloss

to utter; of inanimate things

Occurrences in the New Testament

297

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to speak / talk (278)
    To utter words aloud. Includes saying content and merely producing speech.
  2. to speak in tongues (19)
    To speak in tongues (unknown languages) as a gift of the Holy Spirit. Limited to the contexts of Acts and 1 Corinthians.
