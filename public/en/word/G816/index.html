Senses (NGSD)

  1. to gaze / look intently (14)
    To fix one's eyes, to stare: “looking intently without turning away”, “gazing into heaven”, “the Israelites could not gaze at the face of Moses”, “why do you stare at us?”
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 4:20

      καὶ πτύξας τὸ βιβλίον ἀποδοὺς τῷ ὑπηρέτῃ ἐκάθισεν· καὶ πάντων οἱ ὀφθαλμοὶ ἐν τῇ συναγωγῇ ἦσαν ἀτενίζοντες αὐτῷ.

      He closed the book, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the synagogue were fastened on him.
