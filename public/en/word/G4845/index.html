Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be filled (1)
    To be filled: “were being filled with water”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 8:23

      πλεόντων δὲ αὐτῶν ἀφύπνωσεν. καὶ κατέβη λαῖλαψ ἀνέμου εἰς τὴν λίμνην, καὶ συνεπληροῦντο καὶ ἐκινδύνευον.

      But as they sailed, he fell asleep. A wind storm came down on the lake, and they were taking on dangerous amounts of water.

      Read Luke 8:23 in the app

  2. (of days) to be fulfilled (2)
    For the days or the time to be completed: “when the day of Pentecost had fully come”, “as the days were drawing to completion”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 9:51

      Ἐγένετο δὲ ἐν τῷ συμπληροῦσθαι τὰς ἡμέρας τῆς ἀναλήμψεως αὐτοῦ καὶ αὐτὸς τὸ πρόσωπον ἐστήρισεν τοῦ πορεύεσθαι εἰς Ἰερουσαλήμ,

      It came to pass, when the days were near that he should be taken up, he intently set his face to go to Jerusalem

      Read Luke 9:51 in the app

    • Acts 2:1

      Καὶ ἐν τῷ συμπληροῦσθαι τὴν ἡμέραν τῆς πεντηκοστῆς ἦσαν πάντες ὁμοῦ ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό,

      Now when the day of Pentecost had come, they were all with one accord in one place.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 συν-πληρόω (Rec. συμπ-), -ῶ, [in LXX: Je 25:12 (מָלֵא) * ;] to fill up completely (Thuc., of manning ships): of a ship filling with water, and by meton., of those on board, Lk 8:23. to complete, fulfil: of time (Je, l.c., and v. MM, xxiv), Lk 9:51, Ac 2:1 (on the "durative inf.," v. M, Pr., 233).†
