Senses (NGSD)

  1. harp (4)
    A harp, a lyre: “flute or harp”, “harps of God”, “holding harps”, “the sound of harps”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 14:7

      ὅμως τὰ ἄψυχα φωνὴν διδόντα, εἴτε αὐλὸς εἴτε κιθάρα, ἐὰν διαστολὴν τοῖς φθόγγοις μὴ δῷ, πῶς γνωσθήσεται τὸ αὐλούμενον ἢ τὸ κιθαριζόμενον;

      Even lifeless things that make a sound, whether pipe or harp, if they didn’t give a distinction in the sounds, how would it be known what is piped or harped?

      Now when he had taken the book, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each one having a harp, and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.

4 κιθάρα, -ας, ἡ, [in LXX chiefly for כִּנּוֹר, freq. in Pss (32 (33):2, al.); and in (Da 3:5, al.) for קִיתָרֹס, itself a transliteration of κίθαρις, the Homeric form of κ. (cf. CB, Dn., lviii);] a lyre, harp: I Co 14:7, Re 5:8 14:2 15:2.†
