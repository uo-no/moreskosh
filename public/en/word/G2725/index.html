Senses (NGSD)

  1. accuser (6)
    An accuser, one who brings charges: “to the accusers also”, “when your accusers arrive”, “to his accusers”, “when the accusers stood up”, “where are those who were accusing you”, “the accuser of our brothers has been thrown down”. The forms include both κατήγορος and κατήγωρ (Rev 12:10).
    Attested: NT only

    • John 8:10

      ἀνακύψας δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς καὶ μηδένα θεασάμενος πλὴν τὴς γυναικός εἶπεν αὐτῇ, ποῦ εἰσιν;; ἐκεῖνοι οἱ κατήγοροί σου οὐδείς σε κατέκρινεν;

      Jesus, standing up, saw her and said, “Woman, where are your accusers? Did no one condemn you?”
