Senses (NGSD)

  1. to eat (6)
    To eat, to munch: “whoever eats my flesh”, “the one who eats me”, “whoever eats this bread”, “eating and drinking”, “he who is eating my bread”. (The examples in John 6 are included in the sense ‘to eat’, and the interpretation of their symbolic meaning is not decided.)
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 24:38

      ὡς γὰρ ἦσαν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ταῖς πρὸ τοῦ κατακλυσμοῦ τρώγοντες καὶ πίνοντες, γαμοῦντες καὶ γαμίζοντες, ἄχρι ἧς ἡμέρας εἰσῆλθεν Νῶε εἰς τὴν κιβωτόν,

      For as in those days which were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ship,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 * τρώγω prop., of animals, to gnaw, munch, crunch (Hom., al.). Of men, to eat raw food, as vegetables, nuts, etc. (Hdt., al.). In late vernacular, simply to eat (= ἐσθίω): Mt 24:38, Jo 6:54, 56, 57, 58 13:18 (LXX ἐσθίων) (v. Kennedy, Sources, 82, 155; MM, xxv).†
