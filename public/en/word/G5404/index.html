    • John 12:13

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 φοῖνιξ (on the accent, v. Bl., § 4, 2; WM, § 6, lc), -ικος, ὁ, [in LXX for תָּמָר,תֹּמֶר,תִּמֹּרָה;] the date-palm, palm: τὰ βαΐα τῶν φ., Jo 12:13; of palm branches, φοίνικες (as Arist., II Mac 10:7, al.), Re 7:9.†
