Senses (NGSD)

  1. to deceive, to lead astray (6)
    To deceive, to mislead (passive: to be deceived). “let no one deceive himself”, “the woman was deceived and fell into transgression”, “as the serpent deceived Eve”, “let no one deceive you”, “by smooth words they deceive”, “sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, deceived me”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 7:11

      ἡ γὰρ ἁμαρτία ἀφορμὴν λαβοῦσα διὰ τῆς ἐντολῆς ἐξηπάτησέν με καὶ δι’ αὐτῆς ἀπέκτεινεν.

      for sin, finding occasion through the commandment, deceived me, and through it killed me.

    • 1 Corinthians 3:18

      Μηδεὶς ἑαυτὸν ἐξαπατάτω· εἴ τις δοκεῖ σοφὸς εἶναι ἐν ὑμῖν ἐν τῷ αἰῶνι τούτῳ, μωρὸς γενέσθω, ἵνα γένηται σοφός,

      Let no one deceive himself. If anyone thinks that he is wise among you in this world, let him become a fool that he may become wise.
