Senses (IGSD)

  1. through (passage / mediation) (135)
    The relation in which something is done by way of, or through, a person (God, Christ, a prophet, a messenger, etc.). Takes the genitive.
  2. by means of (168)
    The relation in which something is done by means of a thing, word, or event: "by." Takes the genitive.
  3. through (space) (36)
    The relation of passing through or extending through a place: "through." Takes the genitive (accusative only in LUK 17:11).
  4. throughout (duration) (27)
    The relation of something continuing across a span of time: "during," "throughout," "by night," "always." Takes the genitive.
  5. after / interval (3)
    The relation marking the point after a period has passed: "after." Takes the genitive. Only three instances.
  6. because of / reason (278)
    The relation in which an event, feeling, or circumstance is the cause: "because of." Takes the accusative.
  7. for the sake of (purpose / benefit) (13)
    The relation in which something is done for someone's benefit or purpose: "for the sake of." Takes the accusative.
  8. in (state / manner) (6)
    The relation in which something is done in a certain state or circumstance: "in a state of," "accompanied by." Takes the genitive.
