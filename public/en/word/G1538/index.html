Bible Green

ἕκαστος hekastos G1538

Gloss

each; every

Occurrences in the New Testament

82

Senses (IGSD)

  1. each one (82)
    A distributive usage: each person in a group, one by one.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

82 * ἕκαστος, -η, ὂν, [in LXX chiefly for אִישׁ;] each, every (Lat. quisque) ; with a noun: Lk 6:44, Jo 19:23, seq. κατά, He 3:13, Re 22:2; εἷς ἕ., Eph 4:16; without a noun: Ac 4:35, Ro 2:6, al.; partit. gen., Ro 14:12, I Co 1:12, al.; in sing, with pl. verb, Lk 2:3, Ac 11:29, al.; in apposition with pl. noun or pron., Lk 2:3, Jo 16:32, Ac 2:8 3:26, al.; εἷς ἕ. (Lat. unusquisque), Ac 2:6 21:26, Col 4:6, al; ἕ. τι ἀδελφῷ (= Heb. אִישׁ לְאָחִיו, Ge 26:31), Mt 18:35 (cf. He 8:11); ἕ. μετὰ τοῦ πλησίον (= אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ, Jg 6:29, al.), Eph 4:25.
