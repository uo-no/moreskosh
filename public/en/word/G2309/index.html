θέλω thelō G2309

Gloss

to will; be willing; wish

Occurrences in the New Testament

208

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to want / wish (164)
    To want, to wish. Includes expressing God's preference ("I desire mercy").
  2. to will / decide (42)
    To choose and decide as an act of will. Expresses God's sovereign will or a person's resolve.
  3. to mean (2)
    In the form "What does this mean?", asks the meaning of something.
