θρησκεία thrēskeia G2356

Gloss

religion in its external aspect; worship

Occurrences in the New Testament

4

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

4 **† θρησκεία (-κία, T), -ας, ἡ (< θρῆσκος), [in LXX: Wi 14:18, 27, Si 22:5 A, IV Mac 5:6, 13*;] religion in its external aspect (MM, Exp., xv), worship: Ac 26:5, Ja 1:26, 27; θ. τῶν ἀγγέλων, Col 2:18.† SYN.: v.s. θρῆσκος.

Forms in the text

  • θρησκεία — nominative · feminine · singular 2
  • θρησκείας — genitive · feminine · singular 1
  • θρησκείᾳ — dative · feminine · singular 1

    having known me from the first, if they are willing to testify, that after the strictest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee.
