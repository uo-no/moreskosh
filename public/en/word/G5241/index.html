Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

1 *† ὑπερ-εν-τυγχάνω to intercede or make petition for (v. Deiss., BS, 121 f.): seq. ὑπέρ, Ro 8:26.†

  • ὑπερεντυγχάνει — indicative · present · active · 3rd person · singular 1

Examples (verses)

    In the same way, the Spirit also helps our weaknesses, for we don’t know how to pray as we ought. But the Spirit himself makes intercession for us with groanings which can’t be uttered.
