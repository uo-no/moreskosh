Senses (NGSD)

  1. one born out of due time (1)
    One born prematurely, a miscarried child: “as to one born before term”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 15:8

      ἔσχατον δὲ πάντων ὡσπερεὶ τῷ ἐκτρώματι ὤφθη κἀμοί.

      and last of all, as to the child born at the wrong time, he appeared to me also.
