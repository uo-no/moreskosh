Senses (NGSD)

  1. to trample (5)
    To trample on, to tread underfoot: “has trampled underfoot the Son of God”, “so that they trampled one another”, “fell along the path and was trampled on”, “lest they trample them underfoot”, “only to be thrown out and trampled by people”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 5:13

      Ὑμεῖς ἐστε τὸ ἅλας τῆς γῆς· ἐὰν δὲ τὸ ἅλας μωρανθῇ, ἐν τίνι ἁλισθήσεται; εἰς οὐδὲν ἰσχύει ἔτι εἰ μὴ βληθὲν ἔξω καταπατεῖσθαι ὑπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων.

      “You are the salt of the earth, but if the salt has lost its flavor, with what will it be salted? It is then good for nothing, but to be cast out and trodden under the feet of men.
