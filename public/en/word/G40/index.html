Senses (IGSD)

  1. holy (159)
    Belonging to God; pure and holy. Used of God, the Holy Spirit, people, and things.
  2. saints (60)
    "The holy ones" as a name for God's people: addressees of letters, those who are persecuted, etc.
  3. sanctuary / holy place (14)
    The holy place of the tabernacle or temple. In the plural, the sanctuary as a whole.
