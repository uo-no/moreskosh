Senses (NGSD)

  1. transgressor (5)
    One who transgresses, who breaks the law: “I make myself out to be a transgressor”, “a transgressor of the law”, “you have become a transgressor of the law”, “a lawbreaker”, “you, a transgressor of the law”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 2:25

      Περιτομὴ μὲν γὰρ ὠφελεῖ ἐὰν νόμον πράσσῃς· ἐὰν δὲ παραβάτης νόμου ᾖς, ἡ περιτομή σου ἀκροβυστία γέγονεν.

      For circumcision indeed profits, if you are a doer of the law, but if you are a transgressor of the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.

      Read Romans 2:25 in the app

    • Romans 2:27

      καὶ κρινεῖ ἡ ἐκ φύσεως ἀκροβυστία τὸν νόμον τελοῦσα σὲ τὸν διὰ γράμματος καὶ περιτομῆς παραβάτην νόμου.

      Won’t those who are physically uncircumcised, but fulfill the law, judge you, who with the letter and circumcision are a transgressor of the law?

      Read Romans 2:27 in the app

    • James 2:9

      εἰ δὲ προσωπολημπτεῖτε, ἁμαρτίαν ἐργάζεσθε, ἐλεγχόμενοι ὑπὸ τοῦ νόμου ὡς παραβάται.

      But if you show partiality, you commit sin, being convicted by the law as transgressors.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** παρα-βάτης, -ου, ὁ (< παραβαίνω), [in Sm.: Ps 16 (17):4 138 (139):19, Je 6:28*;] one who stands beside, the warrior who stands by the charioteer (cf. παραβαίνω, 1). a transgressor (Æsch., παρβάτης): Ga 2:18, Ja 2:9; π. νόμου, Ro 2:25, 27, Ja 2:11.†
