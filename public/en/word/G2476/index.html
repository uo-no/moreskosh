Senses (IGSD)

  1. to stand (105)
    To stand in place on one's own feet.
  2. to set up / place (14)
    To make a person stand, to put in an assigned position.
  3. to stand firm / endure (22)
    To last without falling, to keep standing in the truth.
  4. to stop / halt (4)
    Movement stops (including bleeding stopping).
  5. to establish / fix (8)
    To establish evidence, to keep up an institution, to fix a date.
  6. to weigh out (silver coins) (1)
    Only MAT 26:15: "paid thirty pieces of silver" (weighed out and paid).
