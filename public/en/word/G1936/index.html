Senses (NGSD)

  1. laying on of hands (4)
    The laying on of hands: “through the laying on of hands by the elders”, “through the laying on of my hands”, “when the apostles laid on their hands”, “the laying on of hands”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 8:18

      Now when Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, he offered them money,
