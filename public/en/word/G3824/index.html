παλιγγενεσία palingenesia G3824

Gloss

new birth; renewal; restoration

Occurrences in the New Testament

2

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

2 *† παλινγενεσία (Rec. παλιγγ-), -ας, ἡ (< πάλιν, γένεσις), new birth, renewal, restoration, regeneration; of persons (Plut., Phil., al.), of spiritual regeneration, διὰ λουτροῦ παλινγενεστίας (gen. attr., v. Ellic., in l.), Tit 3:5; of the world, as in Stoics, Jewish Apocal., al. (v. Dalman, Words, 177 ff.), Mt 19:28.†

Forms in the text

  • παλιγγενεσίᾳ — dative · feminine · singular 1
