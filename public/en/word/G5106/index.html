Senses (NGSD)

  1. therefore (3)
    So then, accordingly: “so then”, “therefore”, “this is how I run”.

3 τοί-νυν an inferential particle, [in LXX: Is 3:10 5:13 27:4, Wi 1:11 8:9, a1. * ;] accordingly, therefore: as in cl., after the first word in a sentence, I Co 9:26; acc to later usage, at the beginning, Lk 20:25, He 13:13.†
