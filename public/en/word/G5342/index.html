Senses (IGSD)

  1. to bring / carry (46)
    To carry persons or things. Includes bringing a charge (ACT 25:18, 2PE 2:11), indicating death (HEB 9:16), and a road leading to a city (ACT 12:10).
  2. to bear (fruit) (9)
    Plants bearing or producing fruit. JHN 15, JHN 12:24.
  3. (a voice / prophecy) is brought (3)
    A voice from heaven being brought, prophecy arising. 2PE 1:17-18, 21.
  4. to sustain / uphold (1)
    Only HEB 1:3: sustaining and upholding all things by his word.
  5. to be carried / rushed along (5)
    Being driven along by wind or a ship, carried by the Spirit. Includes moving on toward completion (HEB 6:1).
  6. to endure (2)
    HEB 12:20, ROM 9:22. To bear with patience.
