πονηρός ponēros G4190

Gloss

of persons; oppressed by toils

Occurrences in the New Testament

78

Senses (IGSD)

  1. evil / bad (61)
    A morally bad quality, act, or state. Includes a bad conscience (HEB 10:22).
  2. the evil one (10)
    A nominal usage referring to evil personified, that is, Satan.
  3. envious / evil (eye) (2)
    In the form "evil eye," expresses envy and stinginess. MAT 20:15, MRK 7:22.
  4. bad quality / malignant (5)
    Badness of condition: an unhealthy eye (MAT 6:23, LUK 11:34), bad fruit (MAT 7:17-18), a malignant sore (REV 16:2), etc.
