Senses (NGSD)

  1. lowly / humble / downcast (4)
    Low in station, humble, downhearted: “let the lowly brother boast in his exaltation”, “God, who comforts the downcast”, “lifted up the lowly”, “associate with the lowly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 1:52

      καθεῖλεν δυνάστας ἀπὸ θρόνων καὶ ὕψωσεν ταπεινούς,

      He has put down princes from their thrones, and has exalted the lowly.

      Read Luke 1:52 in the app

    • Romans 12:16

      τὸ αὐτὸ εἰς ἀλλήλους φρονοῦντες, μὴ τὰ ὑψηλὰ φρονοῦντες ἀλλὰ τοῖς ταπεινοῖς συναπαγόμενοι. μὴ γίνεσθε φρόνιμοι παρ’ ἑαυτοῖς.

      Be of the same mind one toward another. Don’t set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Don’t be wise in your own conceits.

      Read Romans 12:16 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 7:6

      ἀλλ’ ὁ παρακαλῶν τοὺς ταπεινοὺς παρεκάλεσεν ἡμᾶς ὁ θεὸς ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ Τίτου·

      Nevertheless, he who comforts the lowly, God, comforted us by the coming of Titus,

      Read 2 Corinthians 7:6 in the app

  2. humble / lowly in heart (4)
    Not proud, humble: “gives grace to the humble”, “humble when face to face”, “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble”, “I am gentle and lowly in heart”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 11:29

      ἄρατε τὸν ζυγόν μου ἐφ’ ὑμᾶς καὶ μάθετε ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ, ὅτι πραΰς εἰμι καὶ ταπεινὸς τῇ καρδίᾳ, καὶ εὑρήσετε ἀνάπαυσιν ταῖς ψυχαῖς ὑμῶν·

      Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

      Read Matthew 11:29 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 10:1

      Αὐτὸς δὲ ἐγὼ Παῦλος παρακαλῶ ὑμᾶς διὰ τῆς πραΰτητος καὶ ἐπιεικείας τοῦ Χριστοῦ, ὃς κατὰ πρόσωπον μὲν ταπεινὸς ἐν ὑμῖν, ἀπὼν δὲ θαρρῶ εἰς ὑμᾶς·

      Now I Paul, myself, entreat you by the humility and gentleness of Christ, I who in your presence am lowly among you, but being absent am bold toward you.

      Read 2 Corinthians 10:1 in the app

    • James 4:6

      But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 ταπεινός, -ή, -όν [in LXX for שָׁפָל, עָנִי, עָנָי, etc. ;] low-lying; metaph., lowly, of low degree, brought low: Ja 1:9, II Co 7:6 (cf. Si 25:23); οἱ τ., opp. to δυνάσται, Lk 1:52; τ. ταπεινοῖς (neut., R, txt.; masc., R, mg.; v. ICC, in l.), Ro 12:16; lowly in spirit, humble, in cl. usually in a slighting sense (v. Tr., Syn., § xlii; but v. also Abbott, Essays, 81), in NT in an honourable sense: II Co 10:1; seq. τ. καρδίᾳ, Mt 11:29 (cf. Ps 33 (34):19); opp. to ὑπερήφανος, Ja 4:6, I Pe 5:5 (LXX).†
