δώδεκα dōdeka G1427

Gloss

twelve

Occurrences in the New Testament

75

Senses (IGSD)

  1. twelve (75)
    A numeral. Counts the twelve apostles, the twelve tribes, etc.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

75 δώδεκα, οἱ, αἱ, τά indecl. numeral, twelve: Mt 9:20 10:1, al.; οἱ δ., the apostles, Mt 10:5, Mk 4:10, al.; in Ac 19:7 24:11, for Rec. δεκαδύο.
