Senses (IGSD)

  1. flesh (physical substance) (29)
    The flesh that forms the body itself. Flesh as material: meat that is eaten, flesh and bones, bodily suffering.
  2. body (including earthly life) (38)
    The human being as a whole body. Includes husband and wife becoming one, and the earthly life lived in the body.
  3. living beings / humanity (14)
    "All flesh" = all living beings, all humankind. Also "flesh and blood" = human being.
  4. kinship / descent (17)
    A bond by birth. Lineage, kin, relations by natural descent.
  5. human standard / sphere (15)
    A human standard or position apart from God. The sphere of judging, boasting, and relying "according to the flesh."
  6. human weakness (3)
    Human weakness: finitude and frailty, weak against one's will.
  7. flesh (principle of sin) (31)
    Human nature as the seat of sinful desire, contrasted by Paul with the Spirit. The source of thoughts and deeds that lead to death.

(τρώγειν) τὴν σ. τοῦ υἱοῦ τ. ἀνθρώπου, Jo 6:52-56; Of the whole substance of the body, = σῶμα: Ac 2:26 (LXX), 31, II Co 12:7, Ga 4:14, Eph 5:29; μία σ., Mk 10:8; εἰς σ. μίαν (Ge 2:24), Mt 19:5, Mk 10:8, I Co 6:16, Eph 5:31; hence, of the material as opp. to the immaterial part of man (cf. As in Heb. idiom, of a living creature: πᾶσα σ. (Heb. כָּל־בָּשָׂר; cf. Bl., § 47, 9), Mt 24:22, Mk 13:20, I Pe 1:24; esp. of man and his mortality (Ps 55 (56):5, Si 28:5, al.), Jo 1:14; πᾶσα σ. Jg 9:2, II Ki 5:1, al.). Of the physical nature as subject to sensation and desire (Plut.), without any ethical disparagement: Ro 7:18 13:14; opp. to πνεῦμα, Mt 26:41, Mk 14:38; τ. θέλημα τῆς σ., Jo 1:13; ἡ ἐπιθυμία τῆς σ., I Jo 2:16; pl., II Pe 2:18; παθεῖν σαρκί, I Pe 4:1; in ethical sense, esp. in Pauline Epp., of the flesh as the seat and vehicle of sinful desires: opp. to νοῦς, Ro 7:25; to πνεῦμα, Ro 8:4-9, 12, 13, Ga 5:16, 17, 19 6:8 (cf.
