Senses (NGSD)

  1. ruler of the synagogue (9)
    The leader or ruler of a synagogue: “Crispus, the ruler of the synagogue”, “Sosthenes, the ruler of the synagogue”, “the rulers of the synagogue sent a message”, “a ruler of the synagogue came to Jesus”, “someone came from the ruler's house”, “arrived at the house of the ruler of the synagogue”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 5:22

      καὶ ἔρχεται εἷς τῶν ἀρχισυναγώγων, ὀνόματι Ἰάϊρος, καὶ ἰδὼν αὐτὸν πίπτει πρὸς τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ

      Behold, one of the rulers of the synagogue, Jairus by name, came; and seeing him, he fell at his feet

      But Jesus, when he heard the message spoken, immediately said to the ruler of the synagogue, “Don’t be afraid, only believe.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 *† ἀρχισυνάγωγος, -ου, ὁ (< συναγωγή), ruler of a synagogue, an administrative officer, supervising the worship (רֹאשׁ הַכְּנֵםֶת): Mk 5:22, 35, 36, 38, Lk 8:49 13:14, Ac 13:15 18:8, 17 (Inscr., v. MM, s.v.; cf. also DB, ext., 101).†
