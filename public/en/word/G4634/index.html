Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

1 σκηνοπηγία, -ας, ἡ (< σκηνή, πήγνυηι), [in LXX for סֻכּוֺת, De 16:16 31:10, Za 14:16, 18, 19; I Es 5:51, I Mac 10:21, II Mac 1:9, 18 * ;] prop., the setting up of tents or dwellings (Arist.); in LXX and NT, ἡ σ., ἡ ἑορτή τῆς σ. (called also ἑορτὴ σκηνῶν, Le 23:34, al.; v. Deiss., LAE, 116 f.), the feast of Tabernacles: Jo 7:2.†
