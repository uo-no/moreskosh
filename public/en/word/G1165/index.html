Senses (NGSD)

  1. to expose to public shame (2)
    To make a public example of, to put to open shame: “made a public spectacle of them”, “not wanting to put her to shame”. Matthew 1:19 has a textual variant, and the reading is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 1:19

      Ἰωσὴφ δὲ ὁ ἀνὴρ αὐτῆς, δίκαιος ὢν καὶ μὴ θέλων αὐτὴν δειγματίσαι, ἐβουλήθη λάθρᾳ ἀπολῦσαι αὐτήν.

      Joseph, her husband, being a righteous man, and not willing to make her a public example, intended to put her away secretly.

      Read Matthew 1:19 in the app

    • Colossians 2:15

      ἀπεκδυσάμενος τὰς ἀρχὰς καὶ τὰς ἐξουσίας ἐδειγμάτισεν ἐν παρρησίᾳ, θριαμβεύσας αὐτοὺς ἐν αὐτῷ.

      Having stripped the principalities and the powers, he made a show of them openly, triumphing over them in it.
