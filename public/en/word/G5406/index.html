Senses (NGSD)

  1. murderer (7)
    One who has killed a person, a murderer: “let none suffer as a murderer”, “no doubt this man is a murderer”, “to release to you a murderer”, “you have become betrayers and murderers”, “burned their city”, “murderers … the lake that burns with fire and sulfur”, “outside are … and the murderers”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 22:7

      ὁ δὲ βασιλεὺς ὠργίσθη, καὶ πέμψας τὰ στρατεύματα αὐτοῦ ἀπώλεσεν τοὺς φονεῖς ἐκείνους καὶ τὴν πόλιν αὐτῶν ἐνέπρησεν.

      When the king heard that, he was angry, and sent his armies, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.
