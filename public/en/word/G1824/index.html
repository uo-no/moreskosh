Senses (NGSD)

  1. at once, immediately (6)
    Immediately, at once: “wished to go at once”, “I sent to you immediately”, “at once”, “sent him there at once”, “at once, on this platter … John’s head”, “I will send him to you at once”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 6:25

      καὶ εἰσελθοῦσα εὐθὺς μετὰ σπουδῆς πρὸς τὸν βασιλέα ᾐτήσατο λέγουσα· Θέλω ἵνα ἐξαυτῆς δῷς μοι ἐπὶ πίνακι τὴν κεφαλὴν Ἰωάννου τοῦ βαπτιστοῦ.

      She came in immediately with haste to the king and requested, “I want you to give me right now the head of John the Baptizer on a platter.”

      Behold, immediately three men stood before the house where I was, having been sent from Caesarea to me.
