Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 * νηφάλιος (-λεος, Rec., in I Ti 3:2, 11), -ον (in cl., -α, -ον), (< νήφω), in cl., of drink, not mixed with wine. In later writers (Plut., al.), of persons, sober, temperate: I Ti 3:2, 11, Tit 2:2.†
