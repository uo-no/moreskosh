Senses (NGSD)

  1. at the same time / together (8)
    At the same time, together: “caught up together with them”, “that we might live with him”, “at the same time he hoped to receive money from Paul”, “at the same time loosening the ropes of the rudders”, “at the same time pray also for us”, “prepare a guest room for me too”, “together they have become worthless”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 24:26

      ἅμα καὶ ἐλπίζων ὅτι χρήματα δοθήσεται αὐτῷ ὑπὸ τοῦ Παύλου· διὸ καὶ πυκνότερον αὐτὸν μεταπεμπόμενος ὡμίλει αὐτῷ.

      Meanwhile, he also hoped that money would be given to him by Paul, that he might release him. Therefore also he sent for him more often and talked with him.

    • Romans 3:12

      πάντες ἐξέκλιναν, ἅμα ἠχρεώθησαν· οὐκ ἔστιν ποιῶν χρηστότητα, οὐκ ἔστιν ἕως ἑνός.

      They have all turned away. They have together become unprofitable. There is no one who does good, no, not so much as one.”

      Read Romans 3:12 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 5:10

      τοῦ ἀποθανόντος περὶ ἡμῶν ἵνα εἴτε γρηγορῶμεν εἴτε καθεύδωμεν ἅμα σὺν αὐτῷ ζήσωμεν.

      who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him.

      Read 1 Thessalonians 5:10 in the app

  2. with (with the dative) (2)
    The prepositional use “with”, “together with”: “in gathering the weeds you root up the wheat with them”, “early in the morning” (ἅμα πρωΐ).
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:29

      ὁ δέ φησιν· Οὔ, μήποτε συλλέγοντες τὰ ζιζάνια ἐκριζώσητε ἅμα αὐτοῖς τὸν σῖτον·

      “But he said, ‘No, lest perhaps while you gather up the darnel weeds, you root up the wheat with them.

    • Matthew 20:1

      Ὁμοία γάρ ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν ἀνθρώπῳ οἰκοδεσπότῃ ὅστις ἐξῆλθεν ἅμα πρωῒ μισθώσασθαι ἐργάτας εἰς τὸν ἀμπελῶνα αὐτοῦ.

      “For the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who was the master of a household, who went out early in the morning to hire laborers for his vineyard.
