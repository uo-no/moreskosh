Senses (NGSD)

  1. to swallow, to devour (4)
    To swallow, devour or engulf (said of water, the earth, beasts and the like): “swallow a camel”, “the earth opened its mouth … and swallowed”, “like a roaring lion … seeking someone to devour”, “the Egyptians were drowned”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 23:24

      ὁδηγοὶ τυφλοί, οἱ διϋλίζοντες τὸν κώνωπα τὴν δὲ κάμηλον καταπίνοντες.

      You blind guides, who strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel!

    • Hebrews 11:29

      Πίστει διέβησαν τὴν Ἐρυθρὰν Θάλασσαν ὡς διὰ ξηρᾶς γῆς, ἧς πεῖραν λαβόντες οἱ Αἰγύπτιοι κατεπόθησαν.

      By faith they passed through the Red Sea as on dry land. When the Egyptians tried to do so, they were swallowed up.

      Read Hebrews 11:29 in the app

    • 1 Peter 5:8

      νήψατε, γρηγορήσατε. ὁ ἀντίδικος ὑμῶν διάβολος ὡς λέων ὠρυόμενος περιπατεῖ ζητῶν τινα καταπιεῖν·

      Be sober and self-controlled. Be watchful. Your adversary, the devil, walks around like a roaring lion, seeking whom he may devour.

      Read 1 Peter 5:8 in the app

  2. to be swallowed up (figurative) (3)
    To be swallowed up, to be overwhelmed: “death has been swallowed up in victory”, “so that he may not be overwhelmed by grief”, “so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 15:54

      ὅταν δὲ τὸ φθαρτὸν τοῦτο ἐνδύσηται ἀφθαρσίαν καὶ τὸ θνητὸν τοῦτο ἐνδύσηται ἀθανασίαν, τότε γενήσεται ὁ λόγος ὁ γεγραμμένος· Κατεπόθη ὁ θάνατος εἰς νῖκος.

      But when this perishable body will have become imperishable, and this mortal will have put on immortality, then what is written will happen: “Death is swallowed up in victory.”

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:54 in the app

      so that, on the contrary, you should rather forgive him and comfort him, lest by any means such a one should be swallowed up with his excessive sorrow.

      For indeed we who are in this tent do groan, being burdened, not that we desire to be unclothed, but that we desire to be clothed, that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.
