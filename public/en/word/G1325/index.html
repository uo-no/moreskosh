Senses (IGSD)

  1. to give (hand over) (261)
    To hand over a thing or person to someone; to give or offer on request.
  2. to grant (88)
    God or one in authority bestowing authority, grace, gifts, opportunity, life, etc.
  3. to pay / entrust (20)
    To pay out as wages, tax, or price. Includes depositing in a bank.
  4. to offer (6)
    To offer one's life or oneself.
  5. to put / place (8)
    To put a thing in a place, or into the heart. Putting on a ring, setting the law in the mind, setting up a door, etc.
  6. to produce / give out (19)
    To produce light, sound, signs, rain, etc. Includes giving up the dead and bearing fruit.
  7. to give as an act (13)
    An expression directing the act itself toward someone: a kiss, a slap, an answer, a defense, glory, etc.
