Senses (NGSD)

  1. to share / contribute to needs (4)
    To share, to take part in material support: “contribute to the needs of the saints”, “if they have shared in spiritual blessings, they ought to serve them in material ones”, “shared with me in giving and receiving”, “let the one who is taught the word share all good things with the teacher”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Romans 12:13

      ταῖς χρείαις τῶν ἁγίων κοινωνοῦντες, τὴν φιλοξενίαν διώκοντες.

      contributing to the needs of the saints, and given to hospitality.

      Read Romans 12:13 in the app

    • Romans 15:27

      εὐδόκησαν γάρ, καὶ ὀφειλέται εἰσὶν αὐτῶν· εἰ γὰρ τοῖς πνευματικοῖς αὐτῶν ἐκοινώνησαν τὰ ἔθνη, ὀφείλουσιν καὶ ἐν τοῖς σαρκικοῖς λειτουργῆσαι αὐτοῖς.

      Yes, it has been their good pleasure, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they owe it to them also to serve them in material things.

      Read Romans 15:27 in the app

    • Galatians 6:6

      Κοινωνείτω δὲ ὁ κατηχούμενος τὸν λόγον τῷ κατηχοῦντι ἐν πᾶσιν ἀγαθοῖς.

      But let him who is taught in the word share all good things with him who teaches.

      Read Galatians 6:6 in the app

  2. to share in / partake (4)
    To be jointly involved, to take part together: “since the children share in flesh and blood, he himself likewise partook of the same”, “as you share Christ's sufferings”, “do not take part in the sins of others”, “takes part in his wicked works”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Timothy 5:22

      χεῖρας ταχέως μηδενὶ ἐπιτίθει, μηδὲ κοινώνει ἁμαρτίαις ἀλλοτρίαις· σεαυτὸν ἁγνὸν τήρει.

      Lay hands hastily on no one. Don’t be a participant in other people’s sins. Keep yourself pure.

      Read 1 Timothy 5:22 in the app

    • Hebrews 2:14

      Ἐπεὶ οὖν τὰ παιδία κεκοινώνηκεν αἵματος καὶ σαρκός, καὶ αὐτὸς παραπλησίως μετέσχεν τῶν αὐτῶν, ἵνα διὰ τοῦ θανάτου καταργήσῃ τὸν τὸ κράτος ἔχοντα τοῦ θανάτου, τοῦτ’ ἔστι τὸν διάβολον,

      Since then the children have shared in flesh and blood, he also himself in the same way partook of the same, that through death he might bring to nothing him who had the power of death, that is, the devil,

      Read Hebrews 2:14 in the app

    • 1 Peter 4:13

      ἀλλὰ καθὸ κοινωνεῖτε τοῖς τοῦ Χριστοῦ παθήμασιν χαίρετε, ἵνα καὶ ἐν τῇ ἀποκαλύψει τῆς δόξης αὐτοῦ χαρῆτε ἀγαλλιώμενοι.

      But because you are partakers of Christ’s sufferings, rejoice, that at the revelation of his glory you also may rejoice with exceeding joy.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 κοινωνέω, -ῶ, [in LXX: II Ch 20:35, Jb 34:8, Ec 9:4 (חבר), Pr 1:11, Wi 6:23, Si 13:1, al.;] to have a share of, go shares in (something) with (some one), take part in: c. gen. rei (as usually in cl., so Pr 1:11), He 2:14; c. dat. rei, Ro 12:13 15:27, I Ti 5:22, I Pe 4:13, II Jn 11; c. dat. pers., seq. εἰς, Phl 4:15; ἐν, Ga 6:6 (cf. συν-κοινωνέω).†
