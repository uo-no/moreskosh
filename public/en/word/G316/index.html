Senses (NGSD)

  1. necessary / indispensable (7)
    Necessary, not to be done without: “the parts of the body that seem weaker are indispensable”, “necessary to urge the brothers to go on ahead”, “it was necessary that the word of God be spoken first”, “provide for necessary things”, “to remain in the flesh is more necessary on your account”, “necessary to send Epaphroditus back”, “for cases of urgent need”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 12:22

      ἀλλὰ πολλῷ μᾶλλον τὰ δοκοῦντα μέλη τοῦ σώματος ἀσθενέστερα ὑπάρχειν ἀναγκαῖά ἐστιν,

      No, much rather, those members of the body which seem to be weaker are necessary.

      Read 1 Corinthians 12:22 in the app

    • Philippians 1:24

      τὸ δὲ ἐπιμένειν ἐν τῇ σαρκὶ ἀναγκαιότερον δι’ ὑμᾶς.

      Yet to remain in the flesh is more needful for your sake.

      Read Philippians 1:24 in the app

    • Titus 3:14

      μανθανέτωσαν δὲ καὶ οἱ ἡμέτεροι καλῶν ἔργων προΐστασθαι εἰς τὰς ἀναγκαίας χρείας, ἵνα μὴ ὦσιν ἄκαρποι.

      Let our people also learn to maintain good works to meet necessary needs, that they may not be unfruitful.

      Read Titus 3:14 in the app

  2. close / intimate (1)
    Close, intimate (persons): “called together his relatives and close friends”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 10:24

      τῇ δὲ ἐπαύριον εἰσῆλθεν εἰς τὴν Καισάρειαν. ὁ δὲ Κορνήλιος ἦν προσδοκῶν αὐτοὺς συγκαλεσάμενος τοὺς συγγενεῖς αὐτοῦ καὶ τοὺς ἀναγκαίους φίλους.

      On the next day they entered into Caesarea. Cornelius was waiting for them, having called together his relatives and his near friends.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 ** ἀναγκαῖος, -αῖα, -αῖον (< ἀνάγκη), [in LXX: Es 8:13, Wi 16:3, Sir prol. 22, II Mac 4:23 Mac 9:21, IV Mac 1:2*;] necessary: Ac 13:46, I Co 12:22, II Co 9:5, Phl 2:25, Tit 3:14, He 8:3; comp. -αιότερον, Phl 1:24. Of persons connected by bonds of nature or friendship, near, intimate (Field, Notes, 118; ΜΜ, VGT, s.v.): ἀ. φίλοι, Ac 10:24.†
