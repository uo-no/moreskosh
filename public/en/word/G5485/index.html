χάρις charis G5485

Gloss

that which causes favourable regard; gracefulness; grace

Occurrences in the New Testament

155

Senses (IGSD)

  1. grace (God's saving and transforming grace) (108)
    Saving grace given by God unilaterally, not on the basis of human merit. Refers to God's transforming work flowing out through Christ's redemptive work, and holds the center of New Testament theology as the ground and power of salvation.
  2. favor (33)
    Goodwill and protective care directed toward a particular person or group. Used both of patronage in human relations (political consideration, social kindness) and of the special care God shows a particular person, carrying on the Old Testament usage "to find favor in the eyes" (חֵן).
  3. thanks / gratitude (12)
    Gratitude for grace or favor received, or its expression. Appears mostly in the fixed expression χάριν ἔχω (to give thanks).
  4. grace / charm (of speech) (2)
    The graceful and attractive quality of speech or manner of speaking. Refers to the dignity of words that draws the hearer.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

155 χάρις, -ιτος, ἡ Abbott-Smith does not include the article here., acc., χάριν (χάριτα in Ac 24:27, Ju 4; v. WH, App., 157), [in LXX chiefly for חֵן ;] objectively, that which causes favourable regard, gracefulness, grace, loveliness of form, graciousness of speech (cl.; Ec 10:12, Si 21:16, al.): Col 4:6; λόγοι τ. χάριτος (gen. qual.), Lk 4:22. Subjectively, on the part of the giver, grace, graciousness, kindness, goodwill, favour: Lk 2:52, Ac 7:10, al.; esp. in NT of the divine favour, grace, with emphasis on its freeness and universality: Lk 1:30, Ac 14:26, Ro 1:7, I Co 1:3, al.; opp. to ὀφείλημα, Ro 4:4, 16; to ἔργα, Ro 11:6; on the part of the receiver, a sense of favour received, thanks, gratitude: Ro 6:17 7:25, al.; χ. ἔχειν, to be thankful, Lk 17:9, I Ti 1:12, al. Objectively, of the effect of grace, a state of grace: Ro 5:2, II Ti 2:1, I Pe 5:12, II Pe 3:18; a proof or gift of grace (cl., a favour): Jo 1:16, Ac 6:8, Ro 1:5, I Co 3:10, II Co 9:8, Ga 2:9, Eph 3:2, I Pe 5:5, 10 al. (For fuller treatment of the NT usage, v. AR, Eph., 221 ff.; DB, ii, 254 ff.; DCG, i, 686 ff.; Cremer, s.v.).
