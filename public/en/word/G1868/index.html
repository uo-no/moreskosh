Senses (NGSD)

  1. praise / commendation (11)
    Praise, honour, approval: “their praise is not from people but from God”, “praise and glory and honour”, “to the praise of God's glory”, “if there is anything worthy of praise”, “to punish those who do evil and praise those who do good”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 2:29

      ἀλλ’ ὁ ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ Ἰουδαῖος, καὶ περιτομὴ καρδίας ἐν πνεύματι οὐ γράμματι, οὗ ὁ ἔπαινος οὐκ ἐξ ἀνθρώπων ἀλλ’ ἐκ τοῦ θεοῦ.

      but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter; whose praise is not from men, but from God.

      Read Romans 2:29 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 8:18

      συνεπέμψαμεν δὲ μετ’ αὐτοῦ τὸν ἀδελφὸν οὗ ὁ ἔπαινος ἐν τῷ εὐαγγελίῳ διὰ πασῶν τῶν ἐκκλησιῶν

      We have sent together with him the brother whose praise in the Good News is known throughout all the assemblies.

      Read 2 Corinthians 8:18 in the app

    • Philippians 1:11

      πεπληρωμένοι καρπὸν δικαιοσύνης τὸν διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ εἰς δόξαν καὶ ἔπαινον θεοῦ.

      being filled with the fruits of righteousness which are through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.
