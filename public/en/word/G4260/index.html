Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be advanced in years (3)
    To be getting on in years, to grow old: “was advanced in years”, “my wife too is advanced in years”, “being of great age”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 1:7

      καὶ οὐκ ἦν αὐτοῖς τέκνον, καθότι ἦν ἡ Ἐλισάβετ στεῖρα, καὶ ἀμφότεροι προβεβηκότες ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις αὐτῶν ἦσαν.

      But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they both were well advanced in years.

      Zacharias said to the angel, “How can I be sure of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is well advanced in years.”

      Read Luke 1:18 in the app

    • Luke 2:36

  2. to go farther (2)
    To go a little farther, to advance: “going a little farther”, “when they had gone on a little”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 4:21

      Καὶ προβὰς ἐκεῖθεν εἶδεν ἄλλους δύο ἀδελφούς, Ἰάκωβον τὸν τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου καὶ Ἰωάννην τὸν ἀδελφὸν αὐτοῦ, ἐν τῷ πλοίῳ μετὰ Ζεβεδαίου τοῦ πατρὸς αὐτῶν καταρτίζοντας τὰ δίκτυα αὐτῶν, καὶ ἐκάλεσεν αὐτούς.

      Going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. He called them.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 προ-βαίνω, [in LXX chiefly for בּוֹא;] to go forwards, go on, advance: Mt 4:21, Mk 1:19. Metaph., of age (Ge 18:11, al., Lys., Diod., al.), ἐν τ. ἡμέραις, Lk 1:7, 18 2:36.†
