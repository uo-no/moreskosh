Senses (NGSD)

  1. to call together (8)
    To call people together (middle: to call to oneself): “called together relatives and close friends”, “called together the leaders of the Jews”, “summoned the whole council”, “calls together friends and neighbours”, “called the twelve together”, “called together the chief priests”, “called together the whole battalion”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 15:16

      Οἱ δὲ στρατιῶται ἀπήγαγον αὐτὸν ἔσω τῆς αὐλῆς, ὅ ἐστιν πραιτώριον, καὶ συγκαλοῦσιν ὅλην τὴν σπεῖραν.

      The soldiers led him away within the court, which is the Praetorium; and they called together the whole cohort.
