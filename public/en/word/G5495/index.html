Gloss

the hand

Occurrences in the New Testament

176

Senses (IGSD)

  1. hand (128)
    The hand of the body.
  2. hand (power / control / protection) (18)
    The reach of the power of God or people; being under their protection.
  3. to hand over / seize (into an enemy's hand) (19)
    The hand in contexts of seizing and bringing under control, or betraying and handing over (into an enemy's hand).
  4. hand (agent / means) (11)
    "By the hand of …", showing the agent of an act.
