Senses (NGSD)

  1. speed (ἐν τάχει: quickly) (8)
    Speed. Mainly in the form ἐν τάχει, ‘quickly’, ‘soon’: “I intend to come soon, but”, “happened swiftly”, “get out of Jerusalem quickly”, “will have it done swiftly”, “what must happen soon”, “the God of peace will swiftly …”, “wishing to go to you soon”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 18:8

      λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι ποιήσει τὴν ἐκδίκησιν αὐτῶν ἐν τάχει. πλὴν ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ἐλθὼν ἆρα εὑρήσει τὴν πίστιν ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς;

      I tell you that he will avenge them quickly. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 τάχος, -ους, τό [in LXX chiefly for parts and derivatives of מָהַר ;] swiftness, speed. Adverbially, ἐν τ. (= ταχέως), quickly, speedily, soon: Lk 18:8, Ac 12:7 22:18 25:4, Ro 16:20, I Ti 3:14, Re 1:1 22:6.†
