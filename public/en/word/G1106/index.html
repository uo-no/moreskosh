Senses (NGSD)

  1. opinion / judgment / advice (4)
    Opinion, judgment, advice, view: “in the same mind and the same judgment”, “I give my judgment”, “in my judgment”, “I give you my opinion”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 1:10

      Παρακαλῶ δὲ ὑμᾶς, ἀδελφοί, διὰ τοῦ ὀνόματος τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ ἵνα τὸ αὐτὸ λέγητε πάντες, καὶ μὴ ᾖ ἐν ὑμῖν σχίσματα, ἦτε δὲ κατηρτισμένοι ἐν τῷ αὐτῷ νοῒ καὶ ἐν τῇ αὐτῇ γνώμῃ.

      Now I beg you, brothers, through the name of our Lord, Jesus Christ, that you all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be perfected together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

      Περὶ δὲ τῶν παρθένων ἐπιταγὴν κυρίου οὐκ ἔχω, γνώμην δὲ δίδωμι ὡς ἠλεημένος ὑπὸ κυρίου πιστὸς εἶναι.

      Now concerning virgins, I have no commandment from the Lord, but I give my judgment as one who has obtained mercy from the Lord to be trustworthy.

      When he had spent three months there, and a plot was made against him by Jews as he was about to set sail for Syria, he determined to return through Macedonia.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 γνώμη, -ης, ἡ (< γιγνώσκω), [in LXX: Da TH 2:15 (דָּת), Ps 82(83):3 (סוֹד), elsewhere, chiefly II Es, for טְעֵם ;] a means of knowing, a token (Theogn.). In Attic writers, the mind, its operations and results (v. Edwards, Lex., App., A); mind, understanding; purpose, intention: Ac 20:3; judgment, opinion: I Co 1:10, Re 17:13; counsel, advice: I Co 7:25, 40 II Co 8:10, Phm 14; royal purpose, decree (as in Da, I and II Es): Re 17:17 (Cremer, 671). †
