Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be storm-tossed (1)
    To be battered by a tempest: “violently storm-tossed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 27:18

      σφοδρῶς δὲ χειμαζομένων ἡμῶν τῇ ἑξῆς ἐκβολὴν ἐποιοῦντο,

      As we labored exceedingly with the storm, the next day they began to throw things overboard.

1 χειμάζω (< χεῖμα, winter cold) [in LXX: Pr 26:10 (†) * ;] to expose to winter cold, go into winter quarters. to drive with storm; pass., to be driven with storm, tempest-tossed: Ac 27:18.†
