Senses (NGSD)

  1. end, completion (6)
    End, completion, consummation; mostly ‘the end of the world’ or ‘of the age’: “at the end of the age”, “so it will be at the end of the age”, “to the end of the age”, “at the end of the age, when you are to come”, “he has appeared once for all at the end of the ages”, “at the end of the age”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:39

      The enemy who sowed them is the devil. The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 συντέλεια, -ας, ἡ (< συντελέω), [in LXX chiefly for כָּלָה and (in Da) for קֵץ ;] in cl., a joint payment or contribution for public service; hence, generally, joint action (Plat.). In late writers (Polyb., al.), consummation, completion: σ. τ. αἰῶνος, -ων (cf. Da LXX TH 12:13, al.), Mt 13:39, 40, 49 24:3 28:20, He 9:26.†
