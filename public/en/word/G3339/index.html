μεταμορφόομαι metamorphoomai G3339

Gloss

transfigure; of Christ's transfiguration

Occurrences in the New Testament

4

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

4 **† μετα-μορφόω, -ῶ [in Sm.: Ps 33 (34):1*;] to transform, transfigure: pass., of Christ's transfiguration, Mt 17:2, Mk 9:2 (cf. Lk 9:29); of Christians, Ro 12:2, II Co 3:18.† SYN.: μετασχηματίζω, to change in fashion or appearance, v.s. μορφή, and cf. Lft., Phl., 125 ff.

Forms in the text

  • μετεμορφώθη — indicative · aorist · passive · 3rd person · singular 2
  • μεταμορφούμεθα — indicative · present · passive · 1st person · plural 1
  • μεταμορφοῦσθε — imperative · present · passive · 2nd person · plural 1
