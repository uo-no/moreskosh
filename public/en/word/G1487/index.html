Senses (IGSD)

  1. if (373)
    Introduces a condition: "if." In argument, it can carry the sense "since it is the case that."
  2. except (exception) (77)
    Expresses an exception in the form εἰ μή: "unless," "except."
  3. whether (35)
    Introduces an indirect question: "whether."
  4. even if (16)
    Expresses concession in the form εἰ καί: "even if."
  5. surely (oath) (1)
    A Hebraic oath formula (εἰ μήν). Only HEB 6:14: "surely I will …".
