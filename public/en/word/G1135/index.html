γυνή gynē G1135

Gloss

a woman; married or unmarried

Occurrences in the New Testament

216

Senses (IGSD)

  1. woman (121)
    An adult female.
  2. wife (95)
    A wife, in relation to her husband.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

215 γυνή, -αικός, ἡ, [in LXX for אִשָּׁה ;] a woman, married or unmarried: Mt 11:11 14:21, al.; ὕπανδρος γ., Ro 7:2; γ. χήρα, Lk 4:26; in vocat., γύναι implies neither reproof nor severity, but is used freq. as a term of respect and endearment, Mt 15:28, Jo 2:4, 4:21 19:26. a wife: Mt 1:20, I Co 7:3, 4 al.; γ. ἀπολύειν, Mk 10:2, al.; γ. ἔχειν Mk 6:18; γ. λαβεῖν, Mk 12:19; γ. γαμεῖν, Lk 14:20. a deaconess, I Ti 3:11 (CGT, in l.).
