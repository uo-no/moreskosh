Senses (NGSD)

  1. to let down, to lower (7)
    To lower by ropes, to let down: “lowered in a basket through the wall”, “lowered the gear”, “were about to let down anchors from the bow”, “let down your nets”, “at your word I will let down the nets”, “let down the bed”, “was lowered in a basket”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 2:4

      καὶ μὴ δυνάμενοι προσενέγκαι αὐτῷ διὰ τὸν ὄχλον ἀπεστέγασαν τὴν στέγην ὅπου ἦν, καὶ ἐξορύξαντες χαλῶσι τὸν κράβαττον ὅπου ὁ παραλυτικὸς κατέκειτο.

      When they could not come near to him for the crowd, they removed the roof where he was. When they had broken it up, they let down the mat that the paralytic was lying on.
