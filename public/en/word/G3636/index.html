Senses (NGSD)

  1. lazy, sluggish (2)
    Lazy, hesitating to act: “you wicked and lazy servant”, “diligent, not slothful”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 25:26

      ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ κύριος αὐτοῦ εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Πονηρὲ δοῦλε καὶ ὀκνηρέ, ᾔδεις ὅτι θερίζω ὅπου οὐκ ἔσπειρα καὶ συνάγω ὅθεν οὐ διεσκόρπισα;

      “But his lord answered him, ‘You wicked and slothful servant. You knew that I reap where I didn’t sow, and gather where I didn’t scatter.

      Read Matthew 25:26 in the app

    • Romans 12:11

      τῇ σπουδῇ μὴ ὀκνηροί, τῷ πνεύματι ζέοντες, τῷ κυρίῳ δουλεύοντες,

      not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord,

      Read Romans 12:11 in the app

  2. irksome (1)
    Tiresome, bothersome: “to write the same things is no trouble to me”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Philippians 3:1

      Τὸ λοιπόν, ἀδελφοί μου, χαίρετε ἐν κυρίῳ. τὰ αὐτὰ γράφειν ὑμῖν ἐμοὶ μὲν οὐκ ὀκνηρόν, ὑμῖν δὲ ἀσφαλές.

      Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord! To write the same things to you, to me indeed is not tiresome, but for you it is safe.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 ὀκνηρός, -ά, -όν (< ὀκνέω), [in LXX for עָצֵל Pr 6:6, 9 al.;] shrinking, hesitating, timid: c. dat. (Bl., § 38, 2), Ro 12:11; hence, slothful: Mt 25:26. Of things, that which causes shrinking, irksome: Phl 3:1.†
