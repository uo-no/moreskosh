Senses (NGSD)

  1. torment (6)
    Torture, torment, suffering inflicted: “their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it stings someone”, “the smoke of their torment”, “give her such torment and grief”, “in fear of her torment”, “in fear of her torment”, “torment”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      They were given power, not to kill them, but to torment them for five months. Their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it strikes a person.
