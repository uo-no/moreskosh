Senses (NGSD)

  1. to keep silent, to be quiet (3)
    To fall silent, to grow quiet, to stop: “on hearing this they fell silent”, “they could say nothing”, “we fell silent, saying, Let the Lord’s will be done”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Luke 14:4

      οἱ δὲ ἡσύχασαν. καὶ ἐπιλαβόμενος ἰάσατο αὐτὸν καὶ ἀπέλυσεν.

      But they were silent. He took him, and healed him, and let him go.

      Read Luke 14:4 in the app

    • Acts 11:18

      ἀκούσαντες δὲ ταῦτα ἡσύχασαν καὶ ἐδόξασαν τὸν θεὸν λέγοντες· Ἄρα καὶ τοῖς ἔθνεσιν ὁ θεὸς τὴν μετάνοιαν εἰς ζωὴν ἔδωκεν.

      When they heard these things, they held their peace and glorified God, saying, “Then God has also granted to the Gentiles repentance to life!”

      Read Acts 11:18 in the app

    • Acts 21:14

      μὴ πειθομένου δὲ αὐτοῦ ἡσυχάσαμεν εἰπόντες· Τοῦ κυρίου τὸ θέλημα γινέσθω.

      When he would not be persuaded, we ceased, saying, “The Lord’s will be done.”

      Read Acts 21:14 in the app

  2. to rest, to live quietly (2)
    To rest, to keep the sabbath rest, to live a quiet life: “they rested because it was the sabbath”, “make it your ambition to live quietly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 23:56

      ὑποστρέψασαι δὲ ἡτοίμασαν ἀρώματα καὶ μύρα. Καὶ τὸ μὲν σάββατον ἡσύχασαν κατὰ τὴν ἐντολήν,

      They returned and prepared spices and ointments. On the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.

      Read Luke 23:56 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 4:11

      καὶ φιλοτιμεῖσθαι ἡσυχάζειν καὶ πράσσειν τὰ ἴδια καὶ ἐργάζεσθαι ταῖς χερσὶν ὑμῶν, καθὼς ὑμῖν παρηγγείλαμεν,

      and that you make it your ambition to lead a quiet life, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, even as we instructed you,

      Read 1 Thessalonians 4:11 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ἡσυχάζω [in LXX chiefly for שׁקט;] to be still; to rest from labour: Lk 23:56; to live quietly: I Th 4:11; to be silent: Lk 14:3, Ac 11:18 21:14 (cf. Jb 32:7, Ne 5:8).† SYN.: σιγάω (q.v.), σιωπάω.
