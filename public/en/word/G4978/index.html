σχίσμα schisma G4978

Gloss

a rent

Occurrences in the New Testament

8

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

8 * σχίσμα, -τος, τό (< σχίζω), a rent (Arist., al.): Mt 9:16, Mk 2:21. Metaph., a dissension, division: Jo 7:43 9:16 10:19, I Co 1:10 11:18 12:25.† SYN.: v.s. αἵρεσις
