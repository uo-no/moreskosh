σημεῖον sēmeion G4592

Gloss

a sign; mark; token

Occurrences in the New Testament

77

Senses (IGSD)

  1. sign (77)
    A visible proof or marker. Includes a sign as a miracle, a demanded sign, a sign of the end, and a sign of authenticity (2TH 3:17).
