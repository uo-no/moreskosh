παράδοσις paradosis G3862

Gloss

a handing down or over; transfer; transmission

Occurrences in the New Testament

13

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

13 παρά-δοσις, -εως, ἡ (< παραδίδωμι), [in LXX: Es 7:26 (אֱסוּר), Je 39 (32):4 (נתן ni.), Je 41 (34):2*;] a handing down or over, transfer, transmission (Arist., Polyb., al., LXX). tradition of doctrine (Plat., Epict., al.); by meton., of the doctrine itself : Mt 15:2, 3, 6, Mk 7:3 ff., I Co 11:2, Ga 1:14, Col 2:8, II Th 2:15 3:9.†
