ἐσθίω esthiō G5315

Gloss

ate

Occurrences in the New Testament

158

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to eat (155)
    To eat food. Includes sharing a meal.
  2. to devour / consume (2)
    Fire or rust burning up and corroding (HEB 10:27, JAS 5:3).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

90 (KJV) φάγομαι Hellenistic for cl. ἔδομαι, v.s. ἐσθίω.
