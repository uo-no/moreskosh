Senses (NGSD)

  1. greeting (spoken) (7)
    A greeting by word of mouth: “wondered what sort of greeting this might be”, “when the sound of your greeting came to my ears”, “as soon as your greeting reached my ears”, “greetings in the marketplaces”, “to be greeted in the squares”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 23:7

      καὶ τοὺς ἀσπασμοὺς ἐν ταῖς ἀγοραῖς καὶ καλεῖσθαι ὑπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων· Ῥαββί.

      the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called ‘Rabbi, Rabbi’ by men.

      Read Matthew 23:7 in the app

    • Luke 1:29

      ἡ δὲ ἐπὶ τῷ λόγῳ διεταράχθη καὶ διελογίζετο ποταπὸς εἴη ὁ ἀσπασμὸς οὗτος.

      But when she saw him, she was greatly troubled at the saying, and considered what kind of salutation this might be.

      Read Luke 1:29 in the app

    • Luke 1:44

      ἰδοὺ γὰρ ὡς ἐγένετο ἡ φωνὴ τοῦ ἀσπασμοῦ σου εἰς τὰ ὦτά μου, ἐσκίρτησεν ἐν ἀγαλλιάσει τὸ βρέφος ἐν τῇ κοιλίᾳ μου.

      For behold, when the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy!

      Read Luke 1:44 in the app

  2. greeting (written) (3)
    A greeting added at the end of a letter in one's own hand: “I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.”
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 16:21

      Ὁ ἀσπασμὸς τῇ ἐμῇ χειρὶ Παύλου.

      This greeting is by me, Paul, with my own hand.

      Read 1 Corinthians 16:21 in the app

    • Colossians 4:18

      Ὁ ἀσπασμὸς τῇ ἐμῇ χειρὶ Παύλου. μνημονεύετέ μου τῶν δεσμῶν. ἡ χάρις μεθ’ ὑμῶν.

      I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you. Amen.

      Read Colossians 4:18 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 3:17

      Ὁ ἀσπασμὸς τῇ ἐμῇ χειρὶ Παύλου, ὅ ἐστιν σημεῖον ἐν πάσῃ ἐπιστολῇ· οὕτως γράφω.

      I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand, which is the sign in every letter. This is how I write.

      Read 2 Thessalonians 3:17 in the app
