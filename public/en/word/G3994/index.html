Senses (NGSD)

  1. mother-in-law (6)
    A mother-in-law, the mother of a wife or husband: “daughter-in-law and mother-in-law”, “saw his mother-in-law lying sick with a fever”, “Simon’s mother-in-law lay ill with a fever”, “set against her mother-in-law”, “opposing her mother-in-law”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:14

      Καὶ ἐλθὼν ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν Πέτρου εἶδεν τὴν πενθερὰν αὐτοῦ βεβλημένην καὶ πυρέσσουσαν·

      When Jesus came into Peter’s house, he saw his wife’s mother lying sick with a fever.

      For I came to set a man at odds against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.
