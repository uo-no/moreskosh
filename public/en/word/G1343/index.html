Senses (IGSD)

  1. righteousness (given by God / justification) (30)
    The status or quality of righteousness given by God to people through faith, not through works of the law. Appears as "the righteousness of God" and "righteousness by faith," and carries the core of soteriology.
  2. righteousness (ethical rightness / obedience to God) (57)
    Rightness of aim, thought, and conduct as a whole, in line with God's will. Refers to the ethical quality required of individual life and community, in contexts such as "hunger and thirst for righteousness," "to do righteousness," and "persecuted for righteousness' sake."
  3. justice (fairness in judgment) (4)
    Justice as the quality of fair judgment. Appears in legal and ethical contexts as "to judge justly" and "king of righteousness," and refers more to the fairness of adjudication and rule than the moral righteousness of sense 2.
