Senses (NGSD)

  1. two hundred (numeral) (8)
    The number two hundred: “two hundred soldiers”, “two hundred and seventy-six persons in the ship”, “two hundred cubits”, “two hundred denarii”, “two hundred and sixty days”, “two hundred denarii worth of bread”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 6:37

      ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Δότε αὐτοῖς ὑμεῖς φαγεῖν. καὶ λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Ἀπελθόντες ἀγοράσωμεν δηναρίων διακοσίων ἄρτους καὶ δώσομεν αὐτοῖς φαγεῖν;

      But he answered them, “You give them something to eat.” They asked him, “Shall we go and buy two hundred denarii worth of bread and give them something to eat?”
