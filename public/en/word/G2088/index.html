Senses (NGSD)

  1. differently (1)
    Otherwise, in another way: “if you think differently”. The representative examples in the LXX and the Fathers are of the adjective ἕτερος, and the adverb has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 3:15

      ὅσοι οὖν τέλειοι, τοῦτο φρονῶμεν· καὶ εἴ τι ἑτέρως φρονεῖτε, καὶ τοῦτο ὁ θεὸς ὑμῖν ἀποκαλύψει·

      Let us therefore, as many as are perfect, think this way. If in anything you think otherwise, God will also reveal that to you.
