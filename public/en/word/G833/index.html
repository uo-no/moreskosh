Senses (NGSD)

  1. courtyard (6)
    The courtyard of a house or residence: “the courtyard of the high priest”, “Peter was sitting in the courtyard”, “they kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard”, “as he sat in the courtyard”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:58

      ὁ δὲ Πέτρος ἠκολούθει αὐτῷ ἀπὸ μακρόθεν ἕως τῆς αὐλῆς τοῦ ἀρχιερέως, καὶ εἰσελθὼν ἔσω ἐκάθητο μετὰ τῶν ὑπηρετῶν ἰδεῖν τὸ τέλος.

      But Peter followed him from a distance to the court of the high priest, and entered in and sat with the officers, to see the end.

      Read Matthew 26:58 in the app

    • Mark 14:54

      καὶ ὁ Πέτρος ἀπὸ μακρόθεν ἠκολούθησεν αὐτῷ ἕως ἔσω εἰς τὴν αὐλὴν τοῦ ἀρχιερέως καὶ ἦν συγκαθήμενος μετὰ τῶν ὑπηρετῶν καὶ θερμαινόμενος πρὸς τὸ φῶς.

      Peter had followed him from a distance, until he came into the court of the high priest. He was sitting with the officers, and warming himself in the light of the fire.

      Read Mark 14:54 in the app

    • Luke 22:55

      περιαψάντων δὲ πῦρ ἐν μέσῳ τῆς αὐλῆς καὶ συγκαθισάντων ἐκάθητο ὁ Πέτρος μέσος αὐτῶν.

      When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and had sat down together, Peter sat among them.

      Read Luke 22:55 in the app

      Then the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders of the people were gathered together in the court of the high priest, who was called Caiaphas.

    • Mark 15:16

      Οἱ δὲ στρατιῶται ἀπήγαγον αὐτὸν ἔσω τῆς αὐλῆς, ὅ ἐστιν πραιτώριον, καὶ συγκαλοῦσιν ὅλην τὴν σπεῖραν.

      The soldiers led him away within the court, which is the Praetorium; and they called together the whole cohort.

      “When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe.

  3. sheepfold (2)
    A fold for sheep: “does not enter the sheepfold by the door”, “other sheep that are not of this fold”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • John 10:1

      Ἀμὴν ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ὁ μὴ εἰσερχόμενος διὰ τῆς θύρας εἰς τὴν αὐλὴν τῶν προβάτων ἀλλὰ ἀναβαίνων ἀλλαχόθεν ἐκεῖνος κλέπτης ἐστὶν καὶ λῃστής·

      “Most certainly, I tell you, one who doesn’t enter by the door into the sheep fold, but climbs up some other way, is a thief and a robber.

    • John 10:16

      I have other sheep which are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd.

      Read John 10:16 in the app

  4. court (of the temple) (1)
    The court outside the sanctuary: “leave out the court outside the temple; do not measure it”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Revelation 11:2

      καὶ τὴν αὐλὴν τὴν ἔξωθεν τοῦ ναοῦ ἔκβαλε ἔξωθεν, καὶ μὴ αὐτὴν μετρήσῃς, ὅτι ἐδόθη τοῖς ἔθνεσιν, καὶ τὴν πόλιν τὴν ἁγίαν πατήσουσιν μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

12 αὐλή, -ῆς, ἡ, [in LXX chiefly for חָצֵר;] in Hom., an open courtyard before a house, hence, an enclosure in the open, a sheepfold: Jo 10:1, 16. the court, courtyard, round which a house is built: Mt 26:3, 58, 69, Mk 14:54, 66 15:16, Lk 11:21 22:55, Jo 18:15; τ. ναοῦ, Re 11:2. a dwelling, a palace (so, acc. to Grimm-Th., s.v.): Mt 26:3, 58, Mk 14:54 15:16, Lk 11:21, Jo 18:15 (but v. MM, s.v.; cf. also DB, ii, 25, 287).†
