Senses (NGSD)

  1. physician (7)
    A physician: “Luke, the beloved physician”, “it is not the healthy who need a physician, but the sick”, “Physician, heal yourself”, “had suffered much under many physicians”, “the sick need a physician”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 9:12

      ὁ δὲ ἀκούσας εἶπεν· Οὐ χρείαν ἔχουσιν οἱ ἰσχύοντες ἰατροῦ ἀλλὰ οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες.

      When Jesus heard it, he said to them, “Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do.

      and had suffered many things by many physicians, and had spent all that she had, and was no better, but rather grew worse,

      καὶ ἀποκριθεὶς ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν πρὸς αὐτούς· Οὐ χρείαν ἔχουσιν οἱ ὑγιαίνοντες ἰατροῦ ἀλλὰ οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες·

      Jesus answered them, “Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do.
