Bible Green

ἀπολύω apolyō G630

Gloss

to set free; release

Occurrences in the New Testament

65

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to release (29)
    To set a prisoner or accused person free.
  2. to dismiss / send home (20)
    To send people away from a place. Includes letting the Lord's servant depart in peace (LUK 2:29).
  3. to divorce (13)
    Divorce of a married couple.
  4. to forgive (debt / sin) (3)
    To cancel or forgive a debt. MAT 18:27, LUK 6:37.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

64 ἀπο-λύω, [in LXX for שׁוּב, etc., freq. in I-IV Mac;] to set free, release: Lk 13:12, Jo 19:10, al.; a debtor, Mt 18:27; metaph., of forgiveness, Lk 6:37. to let go, dismiss (Field, Notes, 9 f.): Mt 15:23, Lk 2:29 9:12, Ac 19:41, al.; of divorce, τ. γυναῖκα: Mt 1:19 5:31, 32 19:3, 8, 9, Mk 10:2, 4, 11, Lk 16:18; with ref. to Gk. and Rom. (net Jewish) custom, τ. ἄνδρα: Mk 10:12. Mid., to depart: Ac 28:25 (MM, s.v.).
