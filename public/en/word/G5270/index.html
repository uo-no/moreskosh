Senses (NGSD)

  1. under (11)
    Under (place): “under your feet”, “under the fig tree”, “under a basket”, “the dogs under the table”, “shake off the dust from under your feet”, “put under his feet”, “the moon under the feet of the woman clothed with the sun”, “in heaven, on earth, and under the earth”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 22:44

      Εἶπεν κύριος τῷ κυρίῳ μου· Κάθου ἐκ δεξιῶν μου ἕως ἂν θῶ τοὺς ἐχθρούς σου ὑποκάτω τῶν ποδῶν σου;

      ‘The Lord said to my Lord, sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet’?
