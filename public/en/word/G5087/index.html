τίθημι tithēmi G5087

Gloss

causative of; to place; lay

Occurrences in the New Testament

100

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to put / place (63)
    To put things in a place. Includes laying money at the feet (Acts 4–5) and the quotation of putting enemies as a footstool.
  2. to lay down (one's life) (10)
    To lay down one's life for the sheep in JHN 10.
  3. to appoint (11)
    To appoint to a role (JHN 15:16, ACT 20:28).
  4. to kneel (6)
    The fixed formula "to place the knees."
  5. to set / resolve (in the heart) (6)
    To resolve in the heart (ACT 19:21), to lay up in the heart (LUK 1:66).
  6. to put forward (2)
    To set forth in a parable (MRK 4:30), to offer without charge (1CO 9:18).
  7. to deposit / entrust (2)
    The money deposited in the bank in LUK 19.
