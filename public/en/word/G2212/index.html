ζητέω zēteō G2212

Gloss

to seek; seek for

Occurrences in the New Testament

117

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to seek (38)
    To search for a person or thing.
  2. to try to obtain / try to meet (25)
    To aim at an opportunity, a contact, or obtaining something.
  3. to look for an opportunity (to seize / kill) (17)
    To lie in wait with hostility, looking for an opportunity (to seize or kill).
  4. to seek (value) (30)
    To seek what is to be pursued, such as the kingdom of God or glory.
  5. to demand (7)
    To be required as a responsibility (LUK 12:48).
