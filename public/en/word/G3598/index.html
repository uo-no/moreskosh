ὁδός hodos G3598

Gloss

a way; path; road

Occurrences in the New Testament

101

Senses (IGSD)

  1. road (physical) (63)
    A road for walking, and a journey. Includes the Sabbath day's journey (ACT 1:12).
  2. way (of life) (25)
    A way of conduct, a way of life that follows God. Includes the new and living way of HEB 10:20.
  3. way (the way of the Lord / of Christ) (13)
    The quotation about preparing the way of the Lord, and "this Way" in Acts (Christianity itself).
