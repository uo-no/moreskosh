Senses (IGSD)

  1. to hand over (92)
    To hand over into the hands of authorities or enemies. In Judas' act it takes on the sense of betrayal.
  2. to entrust (15)
    To entrust property or all things (MAT 25, 11:27).
  3. to hand down / deliver (9)
    To hand down tradition or teaching (LUK 1:2, etc.).
  4. (fruit) is ripe / allows (1)
    Only MRK 4:29: the state in which fruit is ripe and permits harvest.
  5. to give up (spirit / life) (2)
    Giving up the spirit in JHN 19:30; risking one's life in ACT 15:26.
