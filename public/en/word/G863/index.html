ἀφίημι aphiēmi G863

Gloss

to send forth; send away; let go

Occurrences in the New Testament

143

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to forgive (47)
    To cancel sin or debt.
  2. to allow / let be (32)
    To let someone do as they wish, not to stop them.
  3. to leave / depart (31)
    A person or illness leaving.
  4. to leave behind / abandon (18)
    To leave possessions or people behind and follow; to abandon.
  5. to leave / let remain (9)
    To leave in place; to dismiss.
  6. to neglect (3)
    To leave the important things undone.
  7. to divorce (3)
    To dissolve a marriage (1CO 7).
