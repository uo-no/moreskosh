Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be sober, to be watchful (6)
    To be level-headed, self-controlled, not drunk (figuratively, to be awake): “be sober and watchful”, “be sober-minded for prayer”, “gird up your minds and be sober”, “let us be sober”, “putting on the breastplate of faith and love”, “be sober in all things”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Thessalonians 5:6

      ἄρα οὖν μὴ καθεύδωμεν ὡς οἱ λοιποί, ἀλλὰ γρηγορῶμεν καὶ νήφωμεν.

      so then let’s not sleep, as the rest do, but let’s watch and be sober.

    • 2 Timothy 4:5

      σὺ δὲ νῆφε ἐν πᾶσιν, κακοπάθησον, ἔργον ποίησον εὐαγγελιστοῦ, τὴν διακονίαν σου πληροφόρησον.

      But you be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, and fulfill your ministry.

      Read 2 Timothy 4:5 in the app

    • 1 Peter 4:7

      Πάντων δὲ τὸ τέλος ἤγγικεν. σωφρονήσατε οὖν καὶ νήψατε εἰς προσευχάς·

      But the end of all things is near. Therefore be of sound mind, self-controlled, and sober in prayer.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 * νήφω, to be sober, abstain from wine; metaph., of moral alertness, to be sober, calm, circumspect: I Th 5:6 8, II Ti 4:5 (v. Ellic., in l.), I Pe 1:13 4:7 5:8 (cf. ἀνα-, ἐκ-νήφω, and v. MM, xvii).† SYN.: ἀγρυπνέω, γρηγορέω.
