Senses (NGSD)

  1. bosom, breast (4)
    The bosom or chest: “leaning on Jesus’ bosom”, “the only God who is in the bosom of the Father”, “to Abraham’s bosom”, “is in Abraham’s bosom”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 16:22

      ἐγένετο δὲ ἀποθανεῖν τὸν πτωχὸν καὶ ἀπενεχθῆναι αὐτὸν ὑπὸ τῶν ἀγγέλων εἰς τὸν κόλπον Ἀβραάμ· ἀπέθανεν δὲ καὶ ὁ πλούσιος καὶ ἐτάφη.

      The beggar died, and he was carried away by the angels to Abraham’s bosom. The rich man also died and was buried.

      θεὸν οὐδεὶς ἑώρακεν πώποτε· μονογενὴς θεὸς ὁ ὢν εἰς τὸν κόλπον τοῦ πατρὸς ἐκεῖνος ἐξηγήσατο.

      No one has seen God at any time. The only born Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared him.

  2. fold of a garment (1)
    The fold of a robe, the lap: “will be put into your lap”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 6:38

      “Give, and it will be given to you: good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over, will be given to you. For with the same measure you measure it will be measured back to you.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 κόλπος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for חֵיק, חוֹק, חֵק;] prop., the bosom: Jo 13:23; fig., of close association, ἐν (εἰς τὸν) τοῖς κ. Ἀβραάμ, Lk 16:22, 23 (cf. IV Mac 13:16); ὁ ὢν εἰς τὸν κ. τ. πατρός, Jo 1:18. The bosom or fold of a loose garment falling over a girdle, used as a pocket, hence fig., εἰς τὸν κ. (Is 65:6, Je 39 (32):18): Lk 6:38. A bosom-like hollow, as a bay or gulf: Ac 27:39.†
