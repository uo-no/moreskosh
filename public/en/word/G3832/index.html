Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 πανοικεί (Rec., LTr., -κί), adv. (< πᾶς, οἶκος), a word rejected by strict Atticists, though found once in Plat. (Eryx., 392 c); [in LXX: Ex 1:1 (בַּיִת), III Mac 3:27 (where A in each case reads -κίᾳ, the Attic form, cf. Ge 50:8, al.)*;] with all the household: Ac 16:34.†
