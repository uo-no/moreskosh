Senses (NGSD)

  1. impossible (1)
    Not possible, not such as will fail to happen: “it is impossible that causes of stumbling should not come”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 17:1

      Εἶπεν δὲ πρὸς τοὺς μαθητὰς αὐτοῦ· Ἀνένδεκτόν ἐστιν τοῦ τὰ σκάνδαλα μὴ ἐλθεῖν, πλὴν οὐαὶ δι’ οὗ ἔρχεται·

      He said to the disciples, “It is impossible that no occasions of stumbling should come, but woe to him through whom they come!
