κύριος kyrios G2962

Gloss

having power or authority

Occurrences in the New Testament

714

Senses (IGSD)

  1. Lord / the Lord God (title for God) (620)
    The title for God taking up the Old Testament יהוה, or a divine title for Christ (especially after the resurrection). Depending on context, it refers to either God the Father or the Lord Jesus.
  2. master / owner (human relations) (65)
    A human master or owner who has authority over slaves, servants, and property.
  3. sir / master (respectful address) (23)
    A respectful form of address used to a teacher, judge, official, or stranger. A social courtesy that does not necessarily imply theological sovereignty.
  4. ruler / sovereign (social status) (6)
    A title of one holding authority in institutional and social contexts. Includes the emperor, and polytheistic contexts of many "lords."
