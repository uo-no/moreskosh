Senses (NGSD)

  1. appeal; question (1)
    A question, a petition: “the appeal of a good conscience toward God”. Whether 1 Peter 3:21 means “request” or “pledge” is not decided.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Peter 3:21

      ὃ καὶ ὑμᾶς ἀντίτυπον νῦν σῴζει βάπτισμα, οὐ σαρκὸς ἀπόθεσις ῥύπου ἀλλὰ συνειδήσεως ἀγαθῆς ἐπερώτημα εἰς θεόν, δι’ ἀναστάσεως Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ,

      This is a symbol of baptism, which now saves you—not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,
