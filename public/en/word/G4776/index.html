Senses (NGSD)

  1. to sit down together, to seat together (2)
    To sit together, or to make sit together: “made us sit with him”, “were sitting together”. The sense is not divided between transitive and intransitive.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 22:55

      περιαψάντων δὲ πῦρ ἐν μέσῳ τῆς αὐλῆς καὶ συγκαθισάντων ἐκάθητο ὁ Πέτρος μέσος αὐτῶν.

      When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and had sat down together, Peter sat among them.

    • Ephesians 2:6

      καὶ συνήγειρεν καὶ συνεκάθισεν ἐν τοῖς ἐπουρανίοις ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ,

      and raised us up with him, and made us to sit with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,
