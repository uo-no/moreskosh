Senses (NGSD)

  1. to long for / yearn for (9)
    To long earnestly, to yearn, to desire ardently: “long for the pure milk”, “longing to see you”, “longing to put on our heavenly dwelling”, “long for you”, “longed to see you”, “the Spirit that he made to dwell in us yearns jealously”, “I long to see you”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 1:11

      ἐπιποθῶ γὰρ ἰδεῖν ὑμᾶς, ἵνα τι μεταδῶ χάρισμα ὑμῖν πνευματικὸν εἰς τὸ στηριχθῆναι ὑμᾶς,

      For I long to see you, that I may impart to you some spiritual gift, to the end that you may be established;

    • Philippians 1:8

      μάρτυς γάρ μου ὁ θεός, ὡς ἐπιποθῶ πάντας ὑμᾶς ἐν σπλάγχνοις Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ.

      For God is my witness, how I long after all of you in the tender mercies of Christ Jesus.

      Read Philippians 1:8 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 1:4

      ἐπιποθῶν σε ἰδεῖν, μεμνημένος σου τῶν δακρύων, ἵνα χαρᾶς πληρωθῶ

      longing to see you, remembering your tears, that I may be filled with joy;
