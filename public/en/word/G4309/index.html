προορίζω proorizō G4309

Gloss

to predetermine; foreordain

Occurrences in the New Testament

5

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

5 *† προ-ορίζω, to predetermine, foreordain: c. acc., Ro 8:30; id. et inf., Ac 4:28; id. seq. εἰς, I Co 2:7, Eph 1:5; c. dupl. acc., Ro 8:26; pass., Eph 1:11.†

Forms in the text

  • προορισθέντες — participle · aorist · passive · nominative · masculine · plural 1
