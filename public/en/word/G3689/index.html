Senses (NGSD)

  1. truly / really (10)
    Truly, actually, really: “God is really among you”, “one who is truly a widow”, “the life that is truly life”, “you will be free indeed”, “the Lord has risen indeed”, “this man really was righteous”, “held that John was a prophet”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 11:32

      ἀλλὰ εἴπωμεν· Ἐξ ἀνθρώπων ἐφοβοῦντο τὸν ὄχλον, ἅπαντες γὰρ εἶχον τὸν Ἰωάννην ὄντως ὅτι προφήτης ἦν.

      If we should say, ‘From men’”—they feared the people, for all held John to really be a prophet.

      ἐὰν οὖν ὁ υἱὸς ὑμᾶς ἐλευθερώσῃ, ὄντως ἐλεύθεροι ἔσεσθε.

      If therefore the Son makes you free, you will be free indeed.

    • 1 Timothy 5:3

      Χήρας τίμα τὰς ὄντως χήρας.

      Honor widows who are widows indeed.
