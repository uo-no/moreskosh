Senses (NGSD)

  1. wilderness, deserted place (4)
    A wilderness, an uninhabited place: “dangers in the wilderness”, “in deserts and mountains”, “in such a desolate place”, “in this wilderness”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 15:33

      καὶ λέγουσιν αὐτῷ οἱ μαθηταί· Πόθεν ἡμῖν ἐν ἐρημίᾳ ἄρτοι τοσοῦτοι ὥστε χορτάσαι ὄχλον τοσοῦτον;

      The disciples said to him, “Where could we get so many loaves in a deserted place as to satisfy so great a multitude?”

      His disciples answered him, “From where could one satisfy these people with bread here in a deserted place?”
