Senses (NGSD)

  1. to exercise self-control (2)
    To control oneself, to be temperate: “exercises self-control”, “if they cannot control themselves”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 7:9

      εἰ δὲ οὐκ ἐγκρατεύονται, γαμησάτωσαν, κρεῖττον γάρ ἐστιν γαμῆσαι ἢ πυροῦσθαι.

      But if they don’t have self-control, let them marry. For it’s better to marry than to burn with passion.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:9 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 9:25

      πᾶς δὲ ὁ ἀγωνιζόμενος πάντα ἐγκρατεύεται, ἐκεῖνοι μὲν οὖν ἵνα φθαρτὸν στέφανον λάβωσιν, ἡμεῖς δὲ ἄφθαρτον.

      Every man who strives in the games exercises self-control in all things. Now they do it to receive a corruptible crown, but we an incorruptible.
