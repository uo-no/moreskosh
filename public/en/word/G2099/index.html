εὐαγγελιστής euangelistēs G2099

Gloss

evangelist

Occurrences in the New Testament

3

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

3 *† εὐαγγελιστής, -οῦ, ὁ an evangelist; in NT, a preacher of the gospel: Ac 21:8, Eph 4:11, II Ti 4:5; later, a writer of a gospel (eccl.).†

Forms in the text

  • εὐαγγελιστοῦ — genitive · masculine · singular 2
  • εὐαγγελιστάς — accusative · masculine · plural 1
