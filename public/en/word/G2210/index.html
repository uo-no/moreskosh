Senses (NGSD)

  1. to suffer loss, to forfeit (6)
    To suffer loss, to lose (passive: to be harmed). “that person will suffer loss”, “so that you suffered no loss through us”, “and lose or forfeit himself”, “what if he forfeits his life”, “forfeits his life”, “I have suffered the loss of all things”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 16:26

      τί γὰρ ὠφεληθήσεται ἄνθρωπος ἐὰν τὸν κόσμον ὅλον κερδήσῃ τὴν δὲ ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ ζημιωθῇ; ἢ τί δώσει ἄνθρωπος ἀντάλλαγμα τῆς ψυχῆς αὐτοῦ;

      For what will it profit a man if he gains the whole world and forfeits his life? Or what will a man give in exchange for his life?

      For what does it profit a man if he gains the whole world, and loses or forfeits his own self?
