Senses (NGSD)

  1. Hades / realm of the dead (10)
    The world of the dead, Hades: “you will not abandon my soul to Hades”, “brought down to Hades”, “in Hades, being in torment”, “the gates of Hades”, “the keys of Death and Hades”, “Hades followed with it”, “Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 11:23

      καὶ σύ, Καφαρναούμ, μὴ ἕως οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθήσῃ; ἕως ᾅδου καταβήσῃ· ὅτι εἰ ἐν Σοδόμοις ἐγενήθησαν αἱ δυνάμεις αἱ γενόμεναι ἐν σοί, ἔμεινεν ἂν μέχρι τῆς σήμερον.

      You, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, you will go down to Hades. For if the mighty works had been done in Sodom which were done in you, it would have remained until today.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 ᾅδης, -ου, ὁ [in LXX ehiefly for שְׁאוֹל, also for דּוּמָה, מָוֶת, etc.;] in Hom., Hades (Pluto), the god of the underworld. the abode of Hades, the underivorld; in NT, the abode of departed spirits, Hades: ἐν τ. ᾅ., Lk 16:23; εἰς ᾅ., Ac 2:27, 31; πύλαι ᾅδου, Mt 16:18; κλεῖς τοῦ ᾅ., Re 1:18; metaph., ἕως ᾅ., Mt 11:23, Lk 10:15; personified, Re 6:8 20:13, 14 (Cremer, 67, 610; MM, VGT, s.v.).†
