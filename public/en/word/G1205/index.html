Senses (NGSD)

  1. come (summons) (8)
    “Come here”: “come and have breakfast”, “come and see”, “come to me”, “come, see the place where he lay”, “come to the wedding feast”, “come, you who are blessed”, “gather for the great supper of God”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 11:28

      Δεῦτε πρός με πάντες οἱ κοπιῶντες καὶ πεφορτισμένοι, κἀγὼ ἀναπαύσω ὑμᾶς.

      “Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.

      Then the King will tell those on his right hand, ‘Come, blessed of my Father, inherit the Kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world;

      Read Matthew 25:34 in the app

    • John 4:29

      Δεῦτε ἴδετε ἄνθρωπον ὃς εἶπέ μοι πάντα ὅσα ἐποίησα· μήτι οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ χριστός;

      “Come, see a man who told me everything that I have done. Can this be the Christ?”

      Read John 4:29 in the app

  2. come, let us … (exhortation) (2)
    “Come, let us …”: “come, let us kill him.”

      But the farmers, when they saw the son, said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him and seize his inheritance.’

      But those farmers said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’

    “Follow me.”
    Attested: NT only
