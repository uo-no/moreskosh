Senses (NGSD)

  1. little dog (4)
    A small dog (a house dog): “to throw it to the little dogs”, “even the little dogs eat”, “it is not good to give it to the little dogs”, “even the little dogs under the table”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 15:26

      ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν· Οὐκ ἔστιν καλὸν λαβεῖν τὸν ἄρτον τῶν τέκνων καὶ βαλεῖν τοῖς κυναρίοις.

      But he answered, “It is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”
