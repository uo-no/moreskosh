Senses (NGSD)

  1. to repay (for kindness, in thanks) (4)
    To repay good that has been done, to return thanks: “they cannot repay you, so you will be rewarded”, “you will be repaid at the resurrection”, “what thanks can we return to God”, “who has first given to God and been repaid”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 14:14

      καὶ μακάριος ἔσῃ, ὅτι οὐκ ἔχουσιν ἀνταποδοῦναί σοι, ἀνταποδοθήσεται γάρ σοι ἐν τῇ ἀναστάσει τῶν δικαίων.

      and you will be blessed, because they don’t have the resources to repay you. For you will be repaid in the resurrection of the righteous.”

      Read Luke 14:14 in the app

    • Romans 11:35

      ἢ τίς προέδωκεν αὐτῷ, καὶ ἀνταποδοθήσεται αὐτῷ;

      “Or who has first given to him, and it will be repaid to him again?”

      Read Romans 11:35 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 3:9

      τίνα γὰρ εὐχαριστίαν δυνάμεθα τῷ θεῷ ἀνταποδοῦναι περὶ ὑμῶν ἐπὶ πάσῃ τῇ χαρᾷ ᾗ χαίρομεν δι’ ὑμᾶς ἔμπροσθεν τοῦ θεοῦ ἡμῶν,

      For what thanksgiving can we give again to God for you, for all the joy with which we rejoice for your sakes before our God,

      Read 1 Thessalonians 3:9 in the app

  2. to retaliate, to recompense (judgment) (3)
    To repay evil, to take vengeance: “repay with affliction those who afflict you”, “I will repay”, “I will repay”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 12:19

      μὴ ἑαυτοὺς ἐκδικοῦντες, ἀγαπητοί, ἀλλὰ δότε τόπον τῇ ὀργῇ, γέγραπται γάρ· Ἐμοὶ ἐκδίκησις, ἐγὼ ἀνταποδώσω, λέγει κύριος.

      Don’t seek revenge yourselves, beloved, but give place to God’s wrath. For it is written, “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay, says the Lord.”

      Read Romans 12:19 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 1:6

      εἴπερ δίκαιον παρὰ θεῷ ἀνταποδοῦναι τοῖς θλίβουσιν ὑμᾶς θλῖψιν

      For it is a righteous thing with God to repay affliction to those who afflict you,

      Read 2 Thessalonians 1:6 in the app

    • Hebrews 10:30

      οἴδαμεν γὰρ τὸν εἰπόντα· Ἐμοὶ ἐκδίκησις, ἐγὼ ἀνταποδώσω· καὶ πάλιν· Κρινεῖ κύριος τὸν λαὸν αὐτοῦ.

      For we know him who said, “Vengeance belongs to me. I will repay,” says the Lord. Again, “The Lord will judge his people.”

      Read Hebrews 10:30 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 ἀντ-απο-δίδωμι (ἀντί, ἀποδίδωμι), [in LXX for שׁלם pi., גּמל, שׁוּב hi., etc.;] to give back as an equivalent, recompense, requital (the ἀντί expressing the idea of full, complete return; v. Lft., Notes, 46); in favourable sense: Lk 14:14, Ro 11:35, I Th 3:9; in unfavourable sense: Ro 12:19, II Th 1:6, He 10:30.†
