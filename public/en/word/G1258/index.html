Senses (NGSD)

  1. language, dialect (6)
    A language or dialect: “called Akeldama in their own language”, “in our own language”, “in the language of our native land”, “addressed them in the Hebrew language”, “said in Hebrew”, “I heard a voice saying to me in Hebrew”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 1:19

      καὶ γνωστὸν ἐγένετο πᾶσι τοῖς κατοικοῦσιν Ἰερουσαλήμ, ὥστε κληθῆναι τὸ χωρίον ἐκεῖνο τῇ ἰδίᾳ διαλέκτῳ αὐτῶν Ἁκελδαμάχ, τοῦτ’ ἔστιν Χωρίον Αἵματος.

      It became known to everyone who lived in Jerusalem that in their language that field was called ‘Akeldama,’ that is, ‘The field of blood.’

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 διά-λεκτος, -ου, ἡ (< διαλέγομαι), [in LXX: Da LXX 1:4 (לָשׁוֹן), Es 9:26 * ;] conversation, discourse, speech (Plat., Dem., al.). As in Polyb. and later writers, the language or dialect of a particular country or district: Ac 1:19 2:6, 8 21:40 22:2 26:14.†
