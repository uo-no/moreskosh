Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be spent (1)
    To be used up completely: “I will gladly be utterly spent”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 12:15

      ἐγὼ δὲ ἥδιστα δαπανήσω καὶ ἐκδαπανηθήσομαι ὑπὲρ τῶν ψυχῶν ὑμῶν. εἰ περισσοτέρως ὑμᾶς ἀγαπῶν, ἧσσον ἀγαπῶμαι;

      I will most gladly spend and be spent for your souls. If I love you more abundantly, am I loved the less?

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 *† ἐκ-δαπανάω, -ῶ strengthened form of δαπανάω, to spend wholly; pass., with reflexive force, to spend oneself wholly: seq. ὑπέρ, II Co 12:15.†
