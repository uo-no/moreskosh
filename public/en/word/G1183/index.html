Senses (NGSD)

  1. to take or pay a tithe (2)
    To take a tenth (active); to pay it (passive): “took tithes”, “paid tithes”. The sense is not divided by the difference between active and passive.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Hebrews 7:6

      ὁ δὲ μὴ γενεαλογούμενος ἐξ αὐτῶν δεδεκάτωκεν Ἀβραάμ, καὶ τὸν ἔχοντα τὰς ἐπαγγελίας εὐλόγηκεν.

      but he whose genealogy is not counted from them has accepted tithes from Abraham, and has blessed him who has the promises.

    • Hebrews 7:9

      καὶ ὡς ἔπος εἰπεῖν, δι’ Ἀβραὰμ καὶ Λευὶ ὁ δεκάτας λαμβάνων δεδεκάτωται,

      We can say that through Abraham even Levi, who receives tithes, has paid tithes,
