Senses (NGSD)

  1. region, district (3)
    A region, a district: “in the regions of Achaia”, “the regions of Syria and Cilicia”, “there is no more room in these regions”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Romans 15:23

      νυνὶ δὲ μηκέτι τόπον ἔχων ἐν τοῖς κλίμασι τούτοις, ἐπιποθίαν δὲ ἔχων τοῦ ἐλθεῖν πρὸς ὑμᾶς ἀπὸ ἱκανῶν ἐτῶν,

      but now, no longer having any place in these regions, and having these many years a longing to come to you,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 κλίμα, -τος, τό (< κλίνω), [in LXX: Jg 20:2 A (פִּנָּה)*;] an inclination, slope, esp. the slope from the equator to the pole (Arist). a region (Polyb., al.): pl., Ro 15:23, II Co 11:10, Ga 1:21.†
