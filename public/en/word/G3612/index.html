Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 οἴκημα, -τος, τό (< οἰκέω), [in LXX: Ez 16:24 (גַּב), To 2:4, Wi 13:15*;] a dwelling. As a euphemism for other definite terms (e.g. brothel. Hdt., ii, 121; cf. Ez 16:24), a prison (Thuc., iv, 47 f.): Ac 12:7.†
