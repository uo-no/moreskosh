δικαίωσις dikaiōsis G1347

Gloss

the act of pronouncing righteous; justification; acquittal

Occurrences in the New Testament

2

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

2 δικαίωσις, -εως, ἡ (< δικαιόω), [in LXX for מִשְׁפָּט, Le 24:22 * ;] the act of pronouncing righteous, justification, acquittal: Ro 4:25 5:18 (Cremer, 199).†

Forms in the text

  • δικαίωσιν — accusative · feminine · singular 2

Examples (verses)

  • Romans 4:25

    ὃς παρεδόθη διὰ τὰ παραπτώματα ἡμῶν καὶ ἠγέρθη διὰ τὴν δικαίωσιν ἡμῶν.

    who was delivered up for our trespasses, and was raised for our justification.
