Senses (NGSD)

  1. how large (2)
    How great, how large: “with what large letters”, “how great this man was”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Galatians 6:11

      Ἴδετε πηλίκοις ὑμῖν γράμμασιν ἔγραψα τῇ ἐμῇ χειρί.

      See with what large letters I write to you with my own hand.

      Read Galatians 6:11 in the app

    • Hebrews 7:4

      Θεωρεῖτε δὲ πηλίκος οὗτος ᾧ δεκάτην Ἀβραὰμ ἔδωκεν ἐκ τῶν ἀκροθινίων ὁ πατριάρχης.

      Now consider how great this man was, to whom even Abraham the patriarch gave a tenth out of the best plunder.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 πηλίκος, -η, -ον, [in LXX: Za 2:2 (6) (כַּמָּה), IV Mac 15:22*;] interrog., how large, how great? (prop., of magnitude, as πόσος of quantity): in exclamations, = ἡλίκος (v. Bl., § 51, 4), Ga 6:11; of personal greatness, He 7:4.†
