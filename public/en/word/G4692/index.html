Senses (NGSD)

  1. to hasten (6)
    To hurry, to cause to hasten: “hasten the coming of the day of God”, “hurry … get out”, “was hurrying to reach … if possible”, “hurry and come down”, “hurried down”, “make haste and go”. 2 Peter 3:12 includes the transitive use meaning ‘to hasten (something)’, but it is not made into a separate sense.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 2:16

      καὶ ἦλθαν σπεύσαντες καὶ ἀνεῦραν τήν τε Μαριὰμ καὶ τὸν Ἰωσὴφ καὶ τὸ βρέφος κείμενον ἐν τῇ φάτνῃ·

      They came with haste and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby was lying in the feeding trough.
