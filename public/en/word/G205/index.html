Senses (NGSD)

  1. choicest spoils (1)
    The best part of the booty: “a tenth of the spoils”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 7:4

      Θεωρεῖτε δὲ πηλίκος οὗτος ᾧ δεκάτην Ἀβραὰμ ἔδωκεν ἐκ τῶν ἀκροθινίων ὁ πατριάρχης.

      Now consider how great this man was, to whom even Abraham the patriarch gave a tenth out of the best plunder.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * ἀκροθίνιον, -ου, τό (< ἄκρος, θίς, a heap), prop., the top of a heap, hence, in pl., first-fruits (Xen.; MM, VGT, s.v.). In war, the choicest spoils (cf. Hdt., viii, 121 f.): He 7:4.†
