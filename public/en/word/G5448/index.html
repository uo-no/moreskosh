Senses (NGSD)

  1. to puff up, to be conceited (7)
    To make proud or conceited (passive: to be puffed up). “you are puffed up”, “love is not arrogant”, “so that none of you may be puffed up against another”, “some have become arrogant”, “those who are arrogant”, “knowledge puffs up”, “puffed up without reason by his fleshly mind”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

      Ταῦτα δέ, ἀδελφοί, μετεσχημάτισα εἰς ἐμαυτὸν καὶ Ἀπολλῶν δι’ ὑμᾶς, ἵνα ἐν ἡμῖν μάθητε τό· Μὴ ὑπὲρ ἃ γέγραπται, ἵνα μὴ εἷς ὑπὲρ τοῦ ἑνὸς φυσιοῦσθε κατὰ τοῦ ἑτέρου.

      Now these things, brothers, I have in a figure transferred to myself and Apollos for your sakes, that in us you might learn not to think beyond the things which are written, that none of you be puffed up against one another.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 *† φυσιόω, -ῶ (< φῦσα, bellows), = cl. φυσάω, to puff or blow up, inflate. Metaph., to puff up, make proud: I Co 8:1. Pass., to be puffed up with pride: I Co 4:18, 19 5:2 13:4; seq. ὑπό, Col 2:18; seq. ὑπὲρ . . . κατά, I Co 4:6 (on the form of the subjc., v. M, Pr., 54; Bl., § 22, 3).†
