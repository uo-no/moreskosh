Senses (NGSD)

  1. inner room, storeroom (4)
    An inner room, a closet, a storehouse: “in the inner room”, “neither storehouse nor barn”, “out in the wilderness”, “is in the inner rooms”, “go into your own room”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 6:6

      σὺ δὲ ὅταν προσεύχῃ, εἴσελθε εἰς τὸ ταμεῖόν σου καὶ κλείσας τὴν θύραν σου πρόσευξαι τῷ πατρί σου τῷ ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ· καὶ ὁ πατήρ σου ὁ βλέπων ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ ἀποδώσει σοι.

      But you, when you pray, enter into your inner room, and having shut your door, pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly.

      “If therefore they tell you, ‘Behold, he is in the wilderness,’ don’t go out; or ‘Behold, he is in the inner rooms,’ don’t believe it.

4 ταμεῖον (late syncopated form of cl. ταμιεῖον; v.
