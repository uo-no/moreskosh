δύναμαι dynamai G1410

Gloss

to be able; have power; whether by personal ability

Occurrences in the New Testament

209

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to be able (209)
    To be possible by power or position. In the negative, "cannot."

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

210 δύναμαι, depon., [in LXX chiefly for יָכֹל ;] to be able, have power, whether by personal ability, permission, or opportunity: c. inf. (M, Pr., 205; WM, § 44, 3) pres., Mt 6:24, Mk 2:7, Jo 3:2, I Co 10:21, al.; c. inf. aor., Mt 3:9, Mk 1:45, Jo 3:3, 4 Ro 8:39, al.; c. acc., to be able to do something: Mk 9:22, Lk 12:26; II Co 13:8; absol., to be able, capable, powerful: I Co 3:2 10:13.
