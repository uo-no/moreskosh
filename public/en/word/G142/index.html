Bible Green

αἴρω airō G142

Gloss

to raise; take up; lift or draw up

Occurrences in the New Testament

101

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to take up / lift (78)
    To pick up, shoulder, take up the cross.
  2. to take away (14)
    To carry off, to remove. Includes judgment being taken away (ACT 8:33).
  3. take away! (4)
    The cry of the crowd (LUK 23:18, JHN 19:15).
  4. to raise (a voice) (2)
    To raise one's voice.
  5. to reap (what one did not sow) (2)
    Only LUK 19:21, 22.
  6. to keep in suspense (1)
    Only JHN 10:24, "How long will you keep us in suspense?"

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

101 αἴρω, [in LXX chiefly for נשׂא, also for לקח, etc.;] to raise, take up, lift or draw up: Jo 8:59 11:41, Ac 27:17, al. to bear, carry: Mt 4:6 16:24, al. to bear or take away, carry off, remove: Mt 21:21, Jo 19:31, I Co 5:12 6:15 (v. Lft., Notes, 216), al.; of the taking away sin by Christ, Jo 1:29, I Jo 3:5. (Cf. ἀπ’, ἐξ-, ἐπ-, μετ-, συν-, ὑπερ-αίρω). For exx. from π., v. MM, VGT, s.v.)
