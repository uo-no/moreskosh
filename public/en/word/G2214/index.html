Senses (NGSD)

  1. debate / controversy (6)
    Discussion, controversy, dispute: “a sharp debate arose”, “after much debate”, “avoid foolish, ignorant controversies”, “avoid controversies”, “a discussion arose about purification”, “quarrels over words”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 3:25

      Ἐγένετο οὖν ζήτησις ἐκ τῶν μαθητῶν Ἰωάννου μετὰ Ἰουδαίου περὶ καθαρισμοῦ.

      Therefore a dispute arose on the part of John’s disciples with some Jews about purification.

      Read John 3:25 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 6:4

      τετύφωται, μηδὲν ἐπιστάμενος, ἀλλὰ νοσῶν περὶ ζητήσεις καὶ λογομαχίας, ἐξ ὧν γίνεται φθόνος, ἔρις, βλασφημίαι, ὑπόνοιαι πονηραί,

      he is conceited, knowing nothing, but obsessed with arguments, disputes, and word battles, from which come envy, strife, insulting, evil suspicions,

      Read 1 Timothy 6:4 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 2:23

      τὰς δὲ μωρὰς καὶ ἀπαιδεύτους ζητήσεις παραιτοῦ, εἰδὼς ὅτι γεννῶσι μάχας·

      But refuse foolish and ignorant questionings, knowing that they generate strife.

      Read 2 Timothy 2:23 in the app

  2. inquiry / investigation (1)
    Inquiry into a matter, investigation: “being at a loss how to investigate these questions”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 25:20

      ἀπορούμενος δὲ ἐγὼ τὴν περὶ τούτων ζήτησιν ἔλεγον εἰ βούλοιτο πορεύεσθαι εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα κἀκεῖ κρίνεσθαι περὶ τούτων.

      Being perplexed how to inquire concerning these things, I asked whether he was willing to go to Jerusalem and there be judged concerning these matters.
