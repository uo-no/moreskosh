ἔργον ergon G2041

Gloss

work; task; employment

Occurrences in the New Testament

169

Senses (IGSD)

  1. work / deed (144)
    What a person does; what God does.
  2. task / labor (15)
    Work as an assigned mission (Jesus' task, a servant's allotted work).
  3. thing made / result (10)
    A thing that results from acting (something made by hand, a built portion).
