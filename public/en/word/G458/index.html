ἀνομία anomia G458

Gloss

lawlessness; iniquity

Occurrences in the New Testament

15

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

15 ἀνομία, -ας, ἡ (< ἄνομος), [in LXX for עָוֺן, פֶּשַׁע, תּוֹעֵבָה, רֶשַׁע, etc.;] lawlessness, iniquity: Mt 7:23 13:41 23:28 24:12, Ro 6:19, II Co 6:14, II Th 2:3, 7, Tit 2:14, He 1:9, I Jn 3:4; in pl. (as LXX, Ps 31:1, al.; v. Bl., § 32, 6; Swete, Mk., 153), of acts or manifestations of lawlessness: Ro 4:7(LXX), He 10:17.† SYN.: v.s. ἁμάρτημα, ἄνομος.
