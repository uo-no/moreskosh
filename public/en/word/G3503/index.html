Senses (NGSD)

  1. youth (4)
    One’s youth, the time of being young: “because you are young”, “from your youth”, “since childhood”, “from my youth”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 10:20

      ὁ δὲ ἔφη αὐτῷ· Διδάσκαλε, ταῦτα πάντα ἐφυλαξάμην ἐκ νεότητός μου.

      He said to him, “Teacher, I have observed all these things from my youth.”
