Senses (IGSD)

  1. day (calendar day / number of days) (148)
    A calendar day and its number. A unit of 24 hours including the night.
  2. time / period (111)
    Days expressing "the time of …" and "the period of that time." Also used of the reign or lifespan of a person.
  3. the last day (52)
    "The day" on which God's judgment and salvation appear. The day of the Lord, the day of judgment, the day of Christ. Includes the case where a human judgment seat is meant.
  4. appointed day (24)
    A specific day fixed by calendar or custom, such as a festival, the Sabbath, the Lord's day, the eighth day.
  5. daily / every day (38)
    Every day, day by day: repetition and continuity.
  6. daytime (13)
    The hours of daylight, as opposed to night. While the sun is up.
  7. age (years of life) (3)
    The years of life, where being old is expressed as "advanced in days."
