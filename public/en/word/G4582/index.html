Senses (NGSD)

  1. moon (9)
    The moon (heavenly body): “the moon turned to blood”, “the moon will not give its light”, “signs in sun and moon and stars”, “the glory of the moon”, “the moon under her feet”, “the city has no need of the moon to shine”, “a third of the moon”, “the moon like blood”.
