Senses (NGSD)

  1. perishability / decay (7)
    Perishing, corruption, perishableness: “sown perishable”, “the perishable cannot inherit the imperishable”, “set free from its bondage to corruption”, “having escaped from corruption”, “the one who sows to the flesh will from the flesh reap corruption”, “things that perish with use”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 8:21

      ὅτι καὶ αὐτὴ ἡ κτίσις ἐλευθερωθήσεται ἀπὸ τῆς δουλείας τῆς φθορᾶς εἰς τὴν ἐλευθερίαν τῆς δόξης τῶν τέκνων τοῦ θεοῦ.

      that the creation itself also will be delivered from the bondage of decay into the liberty of the glory of the children of God.

      Read Romans 8:21 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 15:50

      Τοῦτο δέ φημι, ἀδελφοί, ὅτι σὰρξ καὶ αἷμα βασιλείαν θεοῦ κληρονομῆσαι οὐ δύναται, οὐδὲ ἡ φθορὰ τὴν ἀφθαρσίαν κληρονομεῖ.

      Now I say this, brothers, that flesh and blood can’t inherit God’s Kingdom; neither does the perishable inherit imperishable.

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:50 in the app

    • Colossians 2:22

      ἅ ἐστιν πάντα εἰς φθορὰν τῇ ἀποχρήσει, κατὰ τὰ ἐντάλματα καὶ διδασκαλίας τῶν ἀνθρώπων;

      (all of which perish with use), according to the precepts and doctrines of men?

      Read Colossians 2:22 in the app

  2. destruction / ruin (2)
    Destruction, ruin: “born to be destroyed”, “will also be destroyed in their destruction”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Peter 2:12

      οὗτοι δέ, ὡς ἄλογα ζῷα γεγεννημένα φυσικὰ εἰς ἅλωσιν καὶ φθοράν, ἐν οἷς ἀγνοοῦσιν βλασφημοῦντες, ἐν τῇ φθορᾷ αὐτῶν καὶ φθαρήσονται,

      But these, as unreasoning creatures, born natural animals to be taken and destroyed, speaking evil in matters about which they are ignorant, will in their destroying surely be destroyed,

      Read 2 Peter 2:12 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 φθορά, -ᾶς, ἡ (< φθείρω) [in LXX for שַׁחַת, חֶבֶל, etc. ;] destruction, corruption, decay (v. Mayor on II Pe, App.,175 ff.): Ro 8:21, I Co 15:42, Col 2:22, II Pe 2:12; opp. to ζωὴ αἰώνιος, Ga 6:8; by meton., of that which is subject to corruption, I Co 15:50; of moral decay, II Pe 1:4 2:12, 19 (cf. Wi 14:12).†
