Senses (NGSD)

  1. present, now existing (perfect participle) (5)
    Present, now existing (perfect participle): “neither things present nor things to come”, “on account of the present distress”, “the present evil age”, “a symbol pointing to the present time”, “things present or things to come later”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 8:38

      πέπεισμαι γὰρ ὅτι οὔτε θάνατος οὔτε ζωὴ οὔτε ἄγγελοι οὔτε ἀρχαὶ οὔτε ἐνεστῶτα οὔτε μέλλοντα οὔτε δυνάμεις

      For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers,

  2. to come, to be at hand (2)
    To arrive, to be impending (perfect and future): “as though the day of the Lord had already come”, “difficult times will come”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Thessalonians 2:2

      εἰς τὸ μὴ ταχέως σαλευθῆναι ὑμᾶς ἀπὸ τοῦ νοὸς μηδὲ θροεῖσθαι μήτε διὰ πνεύματος μήτε διὰ λόγου μήτε δι’ ἐπιστολῆς ὡς δι’ ἡμῶν, ὡς ὅτι ἐνέστηκεν ἡ ἡμέρα τοῦ κυρίου.

      not to be quickly shaken in your mind or troubled, either by spirit or by word or by letter as if from us, saying that the day of Christ has already come.
