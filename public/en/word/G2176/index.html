Senses (NGSD)

  1. left (9)
    Left, the left side: “one at your right and one at your left”, “the seat at the left”, “the sheep on his right, the goats on the left”, “to those on his left”, “one on the right and one on the left”, “in the right hand … in the left”, “leaving Cyprus on the left”.
