Senses (NGSD)

  1. enmity, hatred (6)
    Enmity, hatred, hostility: “the dividing wall of hostility”, “putting to death the enmity through the cross”, “enmity, strife, jealousy”, “do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God”, “they had been at enmity with each other”, “the mind of the flesh is hostile to God”.
