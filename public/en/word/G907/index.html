Bible Green

βαπτίζω baptizō G907

Gloss

to dip; immerse; sink

Occurrences in the New Testament

77

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to baptize (with water) (63)
    To baptize with water, or to receive it, as a religious rite. Used widely from John's baptism through the baptismal practice of Jesus' disciples to the initiation rite of the early church.
  2. to baptize with the Holy Spirit / fire (7)
    An expression for baptism in which the Holy Spirit or fire, not water, is the "element." Appears as a fixed expression where John the Baptist contrasts his baptism with Jesus', and refers to God's transforming work.
  3. to be overwhelmed (by suffering; figurative) (5)
    A figurative use: being "immersed" in overwhelming suffering or death. Used where Jesus speaks of his own death as a "baptism"; the sense of the baptismal rite is faint.
  4. to wash (ceremonially) (2)
    Washing the body or vessels with water for religious cleansing. Refers not to the baptismal rite but to everyday purification under Jewish purity rules.
