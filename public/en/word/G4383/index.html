πρόσωπον prosōpon G4383

Gloss

of persons

Occurrences in the New Testament

76

Senses (IGSD)

  1. face (34)
    The face of the body. To lower the face, to turn the face.
  2. presence / face to face (30)
    Meeting face to face, being in the presence. Includes "to appear in the presence of God" (HEB 9:24) and "face to face" (1CO 13:12).
  3. outward appearance (10)
    How one looks from outside, and the evaluation based on it. Includes "you do not regard people's faces" (MAT 22:16) and admiring people for their appearance (JUD 1:16).
  4. face (of the earth) (2)
    The surface of the earth. ACT 17:26, LUK 21:35.
