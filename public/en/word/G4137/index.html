Gloss

to fill; make full; fill to the full

Occurrences in the New Testament

86

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to fill (24)
    To fill a container, place, or person. Includes the heart being filled with sorrow or deceit (JHN 16:6, ACT 5:3) and supplying a lack of need (PHP 4:18-19).
  2. to complete / be filled (14)
    Joy, obedience, work, etc. reaching a complete state. Includes being filled and complete in Christ (COL 2:10).
  3. (time / period) is fulfilled (5)
    A set period reaching its end. Usages concerning years, days, and "the time."
  4. to accomplish / carry out (9)
    To carry a task, mission, preaching, or word through to the end. Includes proclaiming the gospel everywhere (ROM 15:19, COL 1:25).
  5. to fulfill (prophecy / law) (34)
    Prophecy, scriptural word, or law being realized in events. Includes the fulfillment formula "it happened that … might be fulfilled."
