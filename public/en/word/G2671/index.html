κατάρα katara G2671

Gloss

a curse

Occurrences in the New Testament

6

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

6 κατ-άρα, -ας, -ἡ, [in LXX chiefly for קְלָלָה;] a curse: Ga 3:10, 13, He 6:8, II Pe 2:14; opp. to εὐλογία, Ja 3:10; concrete, of Christ, Ga 3:13 (v. Lft. in l.).†

Forms in the text

  • κατάρας — genitive · feminine · singular 3
  • κατάρα — nominative · feminine · singular 2
  • κατάραν — accusative · feminine · singular 1
