φωνή phōnē G5456

Gloss

a voice

Occurrences in the New Testament

139

Senses (IGSD)

  1. voice (115)
    A voice uttered by a person or God.
  2. sound (21)
    A sound, such as of wind or a trumpet.
  3. language (3)
    A language: in the context of the "kinds of languages" in 1CO 14. Includes the donkey speaking with a human voice (2PE 2:16).
