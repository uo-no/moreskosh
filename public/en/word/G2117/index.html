Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

59 εὐθύς, -εῖα, -ύ [in LXX chiefly for יָשַׁר ;] straight, direct: τρίβοι, Mt 3:3, Mk 1:3, Lk 3:4 (LXX); εἰς εὐθειας, (sc. ὁδούς), Lk 3:5; εὐ. ὐδός, fig., Ac 13:10, II Pe 2:15; as pr. name of a street, Ac 9:11. In moral sense, straightforward, right: καρδία, Ac 8:21 (cf. Ps 7:11 31 (32):11, al.).†
