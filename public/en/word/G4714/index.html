Senses (NGSD)

  1. strife / riot / rebellion (8)
    Dispute, riot, rebellion, revolt: “a sharp dissension and debate arose”, “in danger of being charged with rioting”, “one who stirs up riots”, “a dissension arose between Pharisees and Sadducees”, “the dissension became violent”, “thrown into prison for insurrection and murder”, “the rebels who had committed insurrection”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 15:7

      ἦν δὲ ὁ λεγόμενος Βαραββᾶς μετὰ τῶν στασιαστῶν δεδεμένος οἵτινες ἐν τῇ στάσει φόνον πεποιήκεισαν.

      There was one called Barabbas, bound with his fellow insurgents, men who in the insurrection had committed murder.

      Read Mark 15:7 in the app

    • Luke 23:25

      ἀπέλυσεν δὲ τὸν διὰ στάσιν καὶ φόνον βεβλημένον εἰς φυλακὴν ὃν ᾐτοῦντο, τὸν δὲ Ἰησοῦν παρέδωκεν τῷ θελήματι αὐτῶν.

      He released him who had been thrown into prison for insurrection and murder, for whom they asked, but he delivered Jesus up to their will.

      Read Luke 23:25 in the app

    • Acts 23:7

      τοῦτο δὲ αὐτοῦ λαλοῦντος ἐγένετο στάσις τῶν Φαρισαίων καὶ Σαδδουκαίων, καὶ ἐσχίσθη τὸ πλῆθος.

      When he had said this, an argument arose between the Pharisees and Sadducees, and the crowd was divided.

      Read Acts 23:7 in the app

  2. standing / position (1)
    Standing, continued existence: “as long as the first tent is still standing” (Heb 9:8).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Hebrews 9:8

      τοῦτο δηλοῦντος τοῦ πνεύματος τοῦ ἁγίου, μήπω πεφανερῶσθαι τὴν τῶν ἁγίων ὁδὸν ἔτι τῆς πρώτης σκηνῆς ἐχούσης στάσιν,

      The Holy Spirit is indicating this, that the way into the Holy Place wasn’t yet revealed while the first tabernacle was still standing.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 στάσις, -εως, ἡ (< ἵστημι), [in LXX chiefly for עמד, its parts and derivatives, also for רִיב (Pr 17:14), etc. (v. Deiss., BS, 158 f.) ;] a standing, place, status: σ. ἔχειν (Polyb., v, 5, 3; and cf. Lat. locum habere), He 9:8. insurrection, sedition: Mk 15:7, Lk 23:19, 25, Ac 19:40 24:5. In poets and late prose, strife, dissension (cf. MM, xxiii) Ac 15:2 23:7, 10.†
