Senses (NGSD)

  1. to wait, to await (6)
    To wait, to look forward to: “I am waiting for him with the brothers”, “wait for one another”, “was waiting for him”, “waiting until his enemies are made a footstool”, “he was looking forward to the city that has foundations”, “the farmer … waits”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 17:16

      Ἐν δὲ ταῖς Ἀθήναις ἐκδεχομένου αὐτοὺς τοῦ Παύλου, παρωξύνετο τὸ πνεῦμα αὐτοῦ ἐν αὐτῷ θεωροῦντος κατείδωλον οὖσαν τὴν πόλιν.

      Now while Paul waited for them at Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he saw the city full of idols.

      Therefore let no one despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come to me; for I expect him with the brothers.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ἐκ-δέχομαι [in LXX for עֶרֶב, קָבַץ, etc. ;] to take or receive from (Hom., Hdt., a1.). (Rare in cl.), to expect, await: c. acc rei, Jo 5:3, He 11:10, Ja 5:7; c. acc pers., Ac 17:16, I Co 11:33 16:11; seq. ἕως, He 10:13 (Cremer, 687).†
