Senses (NGSD)

  1. festival / feast (25)
    A religious holy day or festival (or its period) that the people gather to celebrate at an appointed time. Stated with the names of particular festivals such as Passover or Booths, it marks the time and setting of actions such as people “going up to the feast” or doing things “during the feast”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:5

      ἔλεγον δέ· Μὴ ἐν τῇ ἑορτῇ, ἵνα μὴ θόρυβος γένηται ἐν τῷ λαῷ.

      But they said, “Not during the feast, lest a riot occur among the people.”
