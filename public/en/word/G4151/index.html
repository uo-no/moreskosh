Senses (IGSD)

  1. Holy Spirit (245)
    The Spirit of God, the Holy Spirit. Refers to his person and work (filling, guiding, testifying, speaking to the church). The "seven spirits" of Revelation are also placed here as the full working of the Spirit of God.
  2. human spirit (heart / life) (56)
    The human spirit as the inner person (heart). Contrasted with flesh and body; the spirit of life committed to God at death.
  3. spiritual being (15)
    A being as a spirit itself, such as angels or the spirits of the dead. Includes the mode of being in "God is spirit," and the spirit behind prophecy.
  4. demon (43)
    An unclean or evil spirit. A being that dwells in people, harms them, and is driven out.
  5. disposition / influence (spirit of …) (15)
    The usage calling a quality or influence a "spirit of …". Includes qualities given by God (spirit of wisdom, spirit of power).
  6. wind / breath (5)
    Wind as moving air, and breath that gives life.
