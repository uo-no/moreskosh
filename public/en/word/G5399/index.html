φοβέομαι phobeomai G5399

Gloss

to put to flight

Occurrences in the New Testament

95

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to fear (77)
    The feeling of fear toward danger or people.
  2. to revere (18)
    Reverence toward God, respect toward people (MRK 6:20, LUK 18:2, etc.).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry
