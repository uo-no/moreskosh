Senses (IGSD)

  1. to happen / occur (events) (320)
    Events, phenomena, or circumstances occurring or coming to pass. Used widely of historical events, miracles, sufferings, the fulfillment of prophecy, etc.; the most frequent usage, meaning "it happened."
  2. to become (change) (215)
    A person, thing, or situation passing into a new quality, state, or role. Takes a predicate complement, εἰς phrase, or ὡς phrase, and expresses a change of state: "to become."
  3. to be born / be made / appear (birth / creation / appearance) (102)
    A person, thing, or voice coming into existence or appearing for the first time. Includes creation by God, birth, public appearance, and a voice arising.
  4. to be / come (presence / arrival) (26)
    A person, group, or event being located in a place or situation, or arriving there. Often with ἐν + place or εἰς + place.
  5. to belong to (possession / affiliation) (4)
    A person coming under someone's authority by marriage or legal relation, or a thing coming to be someone's possession. Appears in the form γίνομαι + dative.
