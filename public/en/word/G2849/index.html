Senses (NGSD)

  1. to punish (2)
    To punish, to chastise: “those under punishment”, “finding no way to punish”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 4:21

      οἱ δὲ προσαπειλησάμενοι ἀπέλυσαν αὐτούς, μηδὲν εὑρίσκοντες τὸ πῶς κολάσωνται αὐτούς, διὰ τὸν λαόν, ὅτι πάντες ἐδόξαζον τὸν θεὸν ἐπὶ τῷ γεγονότι·

      When they had further threatened them, they let them go, finding no way to punish them, because of the people; for everyone glorified God for that which was done.

    • 2 Peter 2:9

      οἶδεν κύριος εὐσεβεῖς ἐκ πειρασμοῦ ῥύεσθαι, ἀδίκους δὲ εἰς ἡμέραν κρίσεως κολαζομένους τηρεῖν,

      then the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptation and to keep the unrighteous under punishment for the day of judgment,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 κολάζω (< κόλος, docked), [in LXX: Da 6:12 (13) (no Heb.), I Es 8:24, Wi 3:4 (and freq.), I Mac 7:7, al.;] to curtail, dock, prune. to check, restrain. to chastise, correct, punish: pass., II Pe 2:9; mid., cause to be punished (III Mac 7:3): Ac 4:21.†
