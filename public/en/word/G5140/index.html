τρεῖς treis G5140

Gloss

three

Occurrences in the New Testament

68

Senses (IGSD)

  1. three (68)
    The numeral. Includes the use as part of the place name "Three Taverns" (ACT 28:15).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

68 τρεῖς, οἱ, αἱ, τρία, τά three: Mt 12:40, al.; μετὰ τ. ἡμέρας = τῇ τρίτῃ ἡ., Mk 10:34, al. (cf. Field, Notes, 11 ff.)
