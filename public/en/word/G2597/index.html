Bible Green

καταβαίνω katabainō G2597

Gloss

to go or come down; descend

Occurrences in the New Testament

81

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to go down / descend (81)
    To move from a high place to a low one. Includes descending from heaven and coming upon a person. Being "brought down to Hades" in MAT 11:23 and LUK 10:15 is included here as a passive expression of judgment.
