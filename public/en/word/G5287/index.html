ὑπόστασις hypostasis G5287

Gloss

a support; base or foundation

Occurrences in the New Testament

5

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

5 (KJV) ὑπό-στασις, -εως, ἡ (< ὑφίστημι, to set under, stand under, support) [in LXX for מַצָּב (I Ki 13:23 14:4 B), תִּקְוָה (Ru 1:12, Ez 19:5), etc., also in Wi 16:21 ;] a support, base or foundation (in various senses). substance (Arist., al.; opp. to φαντασία, ἔμφασις): He 1:3 steadiness, firmness (Polyb., al.), hence, assurance, confidence: II Co 9:4 11:17, He 3:14 11:1 (here perhaps title-deed, as that which gives reality or guarantee; v. MM, xxv).†
