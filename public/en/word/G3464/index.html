Senses (NGSD)

  1. ointment / perfume (14)
    Perfumed oil, spices, ointment: “very expensive ointment”, “an alabaster flask of ointment”, “prepared spices and ointments”, “anointed his feet with ointment”, “myrrh”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:7

      προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ γυνὴ ἔχουσα ἀλάβαστρον μύρου βαρυτίμου καὶ κατέχεεν ἐπὶ τῆς κεφαλῆς αὐτοῦ ἀνακειμένου.

      a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very expensive ointment, and she poured it on his head as he sat at the table.
