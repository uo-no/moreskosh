Occurrences in the New Testament

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to see (91)
    To see with the eyes. Includes sight being restored.
  2. to take care / beware (25)
    To pay attention as a warning: "Watch out, take care." Includes seeing to it that something is carried out.
  3. to see / perceive (with the mind) (14)
    To see and understand; to consider.
  4. to regard (a person) (2)
    To be concerned with a person's status and the like (MAT 22:16).
  5. to face (a direction) (1)
    Only the orientation of the harbor in ACT 27:12.
