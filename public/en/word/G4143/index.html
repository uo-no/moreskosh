Senses (NGSD)

  1. boat / ship (67)
    A vehicle on water, from a fishing boat to a large merchant ship.

      Going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. He called them.

67 πλοῖον, -ου, τό (< πλέω), [in LXX chiefly for אֳנִיָּה;] a boat, also (= obsol. ναῦς) a ship: Mt 4:21, 22, and freq. in Gosp. and Ac, Ja 3:4, Re 8:9 18:19.
