Senses (NGSD)

  1. seal (of a scroll) (11)
    The seal of a scroll or book: “a scroll sealed with seven seals”, “open the seals”, “when the Lamb opened the first seal”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Revelation 5:1

      Καὶ εἶδον ἐπὶ τὴν δεξιὰν τοῦ καθημένου ἐπὶ τοῦ θρόνου βιβλίον γεγραμμένον ἔσωθεν καὶ ὄπισθεν, κατεσφραγισμένον σφραγῖσιν ἑπτά.

      I saw, in the right hand of him who sat on the throne, a book written inside and outside, sealed shut with seven seals.

  2. seal of God (3)
    The seal of God marked on people, the inscription on God's firm foundation: “the seal of the living God”, “those without the seal of God”, the seal “The Lord knows those who are his”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Timothy 2:19

      However, God’s firm foundation stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,” and, “Let every one who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.”

  3. seal (confirmation, sign) (2)
    A seal as proof and confirmation: “you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord”, “a seal of the righteousness of faith”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 4:11

      καὶ σημεῖον ἔλαβεν περιτομῆς, σφραγῖδα τῆς δικαιοσύνης τῆς πίστεως τῆς ἐν τῇ ἀκροβυστίᾳ, εἰς τὸ εἶναι αὐτὸν πατέρα πάντων τῶν πιστευόντων δι’ ἀκροβυστίας, εἰς τὸ λογισθῆναι αὐτοῖς τὴν δικαιοσύνην,

      He received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith which he had while he was in uncircumcision, that he might be the father of all those who believe, though they might be in uncircumcision, that righteousness might also be accounted to them.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

16 σφραγίς, -ῖδος, ἡ [in LXX: Ex 28:11, al. (חֹתָם) 35:22 (חָח), Si 17:22, al. ;] a seal, signet: Re 7:2. The impression of a seal or signet, a seal (on its various purposes, v.s. σφραγίζω): on a book or roll, Re 5:1, 2, 5, 9 6:1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12 8:1; metaph., Ro 4:11, I Co 9:2, II Ti 2:19, Re 9:4.†
