Senses (NGSD)

  1. pasture (1)
    Pasture, grazing land: “will find pasture”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 10:9

      ἐγώ εἰμι ἡ θύρα· δι’ ἐμοῦ ἐάν τις εἰσέλθῃ σωθήσεται καὶ εἰσελεύσεται καὶ ἐξελεύσεται καὶ νομὴν εὑρήσει.

      I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out and will find pasture.

      Read John 10:9 in the app

  2. spreading (1)
    A spreading, extending: “their talk will spread like gangrene”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Timothy 2:17

      καὶ ὁ λόγος αὐτῶν ὡς γάγγραινα νομὴν ἕξει· ὧν ἐστιν Ὑμέναιος καὶ Φίλητος,

      and those words will consume like gangrene, of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus:

2 νομή, -ῆς, ἡ (< νέμω, to pasture), [in LXX chiefly for מִרְעֶה, also for נָוֶה, etc.;] a pasture, pasturage: fig., Jo 10:9. a grazing, feeding; metaph., of a spreading sore, II Ti 2:17 (Polyb.).†
