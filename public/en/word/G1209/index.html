δέχομαι dechomai G1209

Gloss

depon

Occurrences in the New Testament

56

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to receive / welcome (44)
    To receive or welcome a person or word.
  2. to accept (8)
    To receive (formally) a letter or an object.
  3. to take in hand (4)
    To take in hand.
