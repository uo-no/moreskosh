Senses (NGSD)

  1. ten thousand / myriad (8)
    Ten thousand, also a countless host: “fifty thousand pieces of silver”, “how many thousands of Jews have believed”, “innumerable angels in festal gathering”, “the Lord came with ten thousands of his holy ones”, “a crowd of many thousands”, “myriads of myriads”, “the number of mounted troops was twice ten thousand times ten thousand”.
