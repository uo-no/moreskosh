ἔρχομαι erchomai G2064

Gloss

to come

Occurrences in the New Testament

630

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to come / go (558)
    A person coming here, and visiting or arriving. "The Coming One" (the Messiah) and the second coming are also here.
  2. to arrive (60)
    A time, day, event, kingdom, etc. coming.
  3. to reach / appear (12)
    To reach a state, to come to light.
