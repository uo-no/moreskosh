Senses (NGSD)

  1. to strive earnestly / make every effort (11)
    To strive eagerly, to do one's utmost, to hasten: “be diligent to make your calling and election sure”, “eager to maintain the unity”, “endeavoured eagerly to see you face to face”, “do your best to present yourself to God as one approved”, “do your best to come to me soon”, “let us strive to enter that rest”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Galatians 2:10

      μόνον τῶν πτωχῶν ἵνα μνημονεύωμεν, ὃ καὶ ἐσπούδασα αὐτὸ τοῦτο ποιῆσαι.

      They only asked us to remember the poor—which very thing I was also zealous to do.

    • 2 Timothy 2:15

      σπούδασον σεαυτὸν δόκιμον παραστῆσαι τῷ θεῷ, ἐργάτην ἀνεπαίσχυντον, ὀρθοτομοῦντα τὸν λόγον τῆς ἀληθείας.

      Give diligence to present yourself approved by God, a workman who doesn’t need to be ashamed, properly handling the Word of Truth.

      Read 2 Timothy 2:15 in the app

    • Hebrews 4:11

      σπουδάσωμεν οὖν εἰσελθεῖν εἰς ἐκείνην τὴν κατάπαυσιν, ἵνα μὴ ἐν τῷ αὐτῷ τις ὑποδείγματι πέσῃ τῆς ἀπειθείας.

      Let’s therefore give diligence to enter into that rest, lest anyone fall after the same example of disobedience.
