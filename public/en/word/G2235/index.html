Gloss

always of time

Occurrences in the New Testament

60

Senses (IGSD)

  1. already / now (60)
    Already, now (by this time).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

61 ἤδη adv., in NT, always of time; now, already: Mt 3:10, Mk 4:37, Lk 7:6, Jo 4:36, al.; νῦν . . . ἤ., now already, I Jn 4:3; ἤ. ποτέ, now at length, c. fut., Ro 1:10 (cf. ἄρτι).
