Senses (NGSD)

  1. to carry away, to take away (6)
    To carry off, to lead away: “to carry your gift to Jerusalem”, “when handkerchiefs or aprons that had touched Paul were carried to the sick”, “was carried by the angels”, “bound Jesus and led him away”, “was led by the Spirit into the wilderness”, “was carried away … in the Spirit”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 15:1

      Καὶ εὐθὺς πρωῒ συμβούλιον ποιήσαντες οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς μετὰ τῶν πρεσβυτέρων καὶ γραμματέων καὶ ὅλον τὸ συνέδριον δήσαντες τὸν Ἰησοῦν ἀπήνεγκαν καὶ παρέδωκαν Πιλάτῳ.

      Immediately in the morning the chief priests, with the elders, scribes, and the whole council, held a consultation, bound Jesus, carried him away, and delivered him up to Pilate.
