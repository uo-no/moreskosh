Senses (IGSD)

  1. name (135)
    A name given to a person or place. Includes naming and giving one's name.
  2. name (a person's authority / presence) (88)
    Of God or Christ, the usage in which the name represents the person himself, authority, and power. Baptism, prayer, proclamation, and suffering are linked "in the name" and "for the name."
  3. person (in a count) (3)
    Where "name" directly serves as a way of counting persons.
  4. reputation / fame (3)
    A name as how one is regarded by others, reputation.
