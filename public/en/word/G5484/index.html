Senses (NGSD)

  1. for the sake of / on account of (8)
    “For the sake of”, “because of”, indicating purpose or reason: “why did he murder him?”, “give no occasion for reviling”, “a prisoner on your behalf”, “for this reason I bow my knees”, “because of transgressions”, “for gain”, “teaching for shameful gain”, “therefore (her sins are forgiven)”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Galatians 3:19

      Τί οὖν ὁ νόμος; τῶν παραβάσεων χάριν προσετέθη, ἄχρις οὗ ἔλθῃ τὸ σπέρμα ᾧ ἐπήγγελται, διαταγεὶς δι’ ἀγγέλων ἐν χειρὶ μεσίτου·

      Then why is there the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring should come to whom the promise has been made. It was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.

      Read Galatians 3:19 in the app

    • Ephesians 3:14

      Τούτου χάριν κάμπτω τὰ γόνατά μου πρὸς τὸν πατέρα,

      For this cause, I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,

      Read Ephesians 3:14 in the app

    • Titus 1:11

      οὓς δεῖ ἐπιστομίζειν, οἵτινες ὅλους οἴκους ἀνατρέπουσιν διδάσκοντες ἃ μὴ δεῖ αἰσχροῦ κέρδους χάριν.

      whose mouths must be stopped: men who overthrow whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for dishonest gain’s sake.

      Read Titus 1:11 in the app

  2. therefore (οὗ χάριν) (1)
    “Therefore”: “therefore I tell you” (Luke 7:47).
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 7:47

      οὗ χάριν, λέγω σοι, ἀφέωνται αἱ ἁμαρτίαι αὐτῆς αἱ πολλαί, ὅτι ἠγάπησεν πολύ· ᾧ δὲ ὀλίγον ἀφίεται, ὀλίγον ἀγαπᾷ.

      Therefore I tell you, her sins, which are many, are forgiven, for she loved much. But one to whom little is forgiven, loves little.”
