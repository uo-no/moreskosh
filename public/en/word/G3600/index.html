Senses (NGSD)

  1. to suffer, to be distressed (4)
    To be in pain, to grieve, to be anxious: “grieving”, “I am in agony in this flame”, “you are in torment”, “were anxiously searching for you”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 2:48

      καὶ ἰδόντες αὐτὸν ἐξεπλάγησαν, καὶ εἶπεν πρὸς αὐτὸν ἡ μήτηρ αὐτοῦ· Τέκνον, τί ἐποίησας ἡμῖν οὕτως; ἰδοὺ ὁ πατήρ σου καὶ ἐγὼ ὀδυνώμενοι ἐζητοῦμέν σε.

      When they saw him, they were astonished; and his mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us this way? Behold, your father and I were anxiously looking for you.”
