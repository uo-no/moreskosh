γῆ gē G1093

Gloss

the earth; world

Occurrences in the New Testament

250

Senses (IGSD)

  1. earth / world (165)
    The whole world where humans live. As in "kings of the earth" and "peace on earth," and also the whole universe in the pair "heaven and earth."
  2. land / region (28)
    A particular region or country. Often accompanied by a name, as in "the land of Israel" and "the land of Sodom."
  3. soil / ground (38)
    Soil where seed falls, or the ground under the feet.
  4. land / shore (19)
    Dry land, paired with the sea. Used in scenes of coming ashore from a boat.
