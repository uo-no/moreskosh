Senses (NGSD)

  1. prison (4)
    A prison, a jail: “the doors of the prison”, “was in prison”, “John in the prison”, “the foundations of the prison were shaken”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 11:2

      Ὁ δὲ Ἰωάννης ἀκούσας ἐν τῷ δεσμωτηρίῳ τὰ ἔργα τοῦ χριστοῦ πέμψας διὰ τῶν μαθητῶν αὐτοῦ

      Now when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples

      “We found the prison shut and locked, and the guards standing before the doors, but when we opened them, we found no one inside!”
