Senses (NGSD)

  1. crafty (1)
    Cunning, sly: “crafty”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 12:16

      ἔστω δέ, ἐγὼ οὐ κατεβάρησα ὑμᾶς· ἀλλὰ ὑπάρχων πανοῦργος δόλῳ ὑμᾶς ἔλαβον.

      Even so, I myself didn’t burden you. But you might say that being crafty, I caught you with deception.
