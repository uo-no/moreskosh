Senses (NGSD)

  1. worship, service (5)
    Worship of God, service: “regulations for worship”, “performed the worship”, “will think he is offering service to God”, “spiritual worship”, “the worship”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 16:2

      ἀποσυναγώγους ποιήσουσιν ὑμᾶς· ἀλλ’ ἔρχεται ὥρα ἵνα πᾶς ὁ ἀποκτείνας ὑμᾶς δόξῃ λατρείαν προσφέρειν τῷ θεῷ.

      They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time is coming that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 λατρεία, -ας, ἡ (< λατρεύω, q.v.), [in LXX (always of divine service): Ex 12:25, 26 13:5, Jos 22:27, I Ch 28:13 (עֲבֹדָה), I Mac 1:43 2:19, 22, III Mac 4:14*;] hired service, service. (in cl. also) divine service, worship: Jo 16:2, Ro 9:4 12:1, He 9:1, 6.†
