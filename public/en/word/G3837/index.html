Senses (NGSD)

  1. everywhere (8)
    In every place, everywhere: “in every church everywhere”, “all people everywhere”, “in every way and everywhere”, “spoken against everywhere”, “preaching everywhere”, “the Lord worked with them”, “his fame everywhere”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 1:28

      καὶ ἐξῆλθεν ἡ ἀκοὴ αὐτοῦ εὐθὺς πανταχοῦ εἰς ὅλην τὴν περίχωρον τῆς Γαλιλαίας.

      The report of him went out immediately into all the region of Galilee and its surrounding area.

      Read Mark 1:28 in the app

    • Luke 9:6

      ἐξερχόμενοι δὲ διήρχοντο κατὰ τὰς κώμας εὐαγγελιζόμενοι καὶ θεραπεύοντες πανταχοῦ.

      They departed and went throughout the villages, preaching the Good News and healing everywhere.

      Read Luke 9:6 in the app

    • Acts 24:3

      πάντῃ τε καὶ πανταχοῦ ἀποδεχόμεθα, κράτιστε Φῆλιξ, μετὰ πάσης εὐχαριστίας.

      we accept it in all ways and in all places, most excellent Felix, with all thankfulness.
