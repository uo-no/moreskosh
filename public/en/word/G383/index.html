Senses (NGSD)

  1. to stir up (2)
    To agitate, to incite an uproar: “he stirs up the people” (twice).
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 15:11

      οἱ δὲ ἀρχιερεῖς ἀνέσεισαν τὸν ὄχλον ἵνα μᾶλλον τὸν Βαραββᾶν ἀπολύσῃ αὐτοῖς.

      But the chief priests stirred up the multitude, that he should release Barabbas to them instead.

      Read Mark 15:11 in the app

    • Luke 23:5

      οἱ δὲ ἐπίσχυον λέγοντες ὅτι Ἀνασείει τὸν λαὸν διδάσκων καθ’ ὅλης τῆς Ἰουδαίας, καὶ ἀρξάμενος ἀπὸ τῆς Γαλιλαίας ἕως ὧδε.

      But they insisted, saying, “He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee even to this place.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ** ἀνα-σείω, [in Aq.: I Ki 26:19, Jb 2:3; Aq., Sm.: Is 36:18*;] to shake out, shake back, move to and fro (Thuc., al.). In late writers (Diod., al.; v. MM, VGT, s.v.), to stir up; metaph., to excite: τ. ὄχλον, Mk 15:11; τ. λαόν, Lk 23:5.†
