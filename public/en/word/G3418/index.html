Senses (NGSD)

  1. tomb (8)
    A tomb, a burial place: “his tomb is with us to this day”, “laid in the tomb at Shechem”, “came to the tomb”, “laid in a tomb”, “lived among the tombs”, “among the tombs and on the mountains crying out”, “refuse to let them be placed in a tomb”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 5:3

      ὃς τὴν κατοίκησιν εἶχεν ἐν τοῖς μνήμασιν, καὶ οὐδὲ ἁλύσει οὐκέτι οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο αὐτὸν δῆσαι

      He lived in the tombs. Nobody could bind him any more, not even with chains,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 μνῆμα, -τος, τό (< μνάομαι), [in LXX for קֶבֶר, קְבוּרָה;] a memorial. a sepulchral monument, a sepulchre, tomb: Mk 5:3, 5 15:46 16:2 (WH, μνημεῖον), Lk 8:27 23:53 24:1, Ac 2:29 7:16, Re 11:9.† SYN.: μνημεῖον.
