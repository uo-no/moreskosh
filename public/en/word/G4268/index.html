Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

2 **† πρό-γνωσις, -εως, ἡ (< προγινώσκω), [in LXX: Jth 9:6 11:19*;] foreknowledge: Ac 2:23, I Pe 1:2.†

  • πρόγνωσιν — accusative · feminine · singular 1

Examples (verses)
