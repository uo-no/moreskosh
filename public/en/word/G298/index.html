Senses (NGSD)

  1. blameless (1)
    With nothing open to blame: “one without spot or blemish”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Peter 3:14

      Διό, ἀγαπητοί, ταῦτα προσδοκῶντες σπουδάσατε ἄσπιλοι καὶ ἀμώμητοι αὐτῷ εὑρεθῆναι ἐν εἰρήνῃ,

      Therefore, beloved, seeing that you look for these things, be diligent to be found in peace, without defect and blameless in his sight.
