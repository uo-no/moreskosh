Senses (NGSD)

  1. to restore, to straighten (3)
    To set up again, to make straight, to strengthen: “I will rebuild the fallen tent”, “strengthen the drooping hands”, “her back was straightened”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 13:13

      καὶ ἐπέθηκεν αὐτῇ τὰς χεῖρας· καὶ παραχρῆμα ἀνωρθώθη, καὶ ἐδόξαζεν τὸν θεόν.

      He laid his hands on her, and immediately she stood up straight and glorified God.

      ‘After these things I will return. I will again build the tabernacle of David, which has fallen. I will again build its ruins. I will set it up
