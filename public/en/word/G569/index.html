Senses (NGSD)

  1. to disbelieve (6)
    Not to believe, not to trust: “to those who do not believe”, “some were convinced, others disbelieved”, “they would not believe it”, “still disbelieved for joy”, “they did not believe”, “whoever does not believe will be condemned”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 16:11

      κἀκεῖνοι ἀκούσαντες ὅτι ζῇ καὶ ἐθεάθη ὑπ’ αὐτῆς ἠπίστησαν.

      When they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they disbelieved.

      Read Mark 16:11 in the app

    • Luke 24:11

      καὶ ἐφάνησαν ἐνώπιον αὐτῶν ὡσεὶ λῆρος τὰ ῥήματα ταῦτα, καὶ ἠπίστουν αὐταῖς.

      These words seemed to them to be nonsense, and they didn’t believe them.

      Read Luke 24:11 in the app

    • Acts 28:24

      καὶ οἱ μὲν ἐπείθοντο τοῖς λεγομένοις οἱ δὲ ἠπίστουν,

      Some believed the things which were spoken, and some disbelieved.

      Read Acts 28:24 in the app

  2. to be unfaithful / not believe (disputed) (2)
    “What if some of them were unfaithful?”, “if we are faithless”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 3:3

      τί γάρ; εἰ ἠπίστησάν τινες, μὴ ἡ ἀπιστία αὐτῶν τὴν πίστιν τοῦ θεοῦ καταργήσει;

      For what if some were without faith? Will their lack of faith nullify the faithfulness of God?

      Read Romans 3:3 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 2:13

      εἰ ἀπιστοῦμεν, ἐκεῖνος πιστὸς μένει· ἀρνήσασθαι ἑαυτὸν οὐ δύναται.

      If we are faithless, he remains faithful; for he can’t deny himself.”

      Read 2 Timothy 2:13 in the app
