Senses (NGSD)

  1. to fix one’s eyes on, to watch (6)
    To look carefully, to fix one’s attention on, to take heed: “we fix our eyes not on what is seen but on what is unseen”, “keep watch on yourself”, “see to it that the light within you is not darkness”, “keep your eyes on those who follow my example”, “look not only to your own interests but also to those of others”, “watch carefully”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 11:35

      σκόπει οὖν μὴ τὸ φῶς τὸ ἐν σοὶ σκότος ἐστίν.

      Therefore see whether the light that is in you isn’t darkness.

      Read Luke 11:35 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 4:18

      μὴ σκοπούντων ἡμῶν τὰ βλεπόμενα ἀλλὰ τὰ μὴ βλεπόμενα, τὰ γὰρ βλεπόμενα πρόσκαιρα, τὰ δὲ μὴ βλεπόμενα αἰώνια.

      while we don’t look at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen. For the things which are seen are temporal, but the things which are not seen are eternal.

      Read 2 Corinthians 4:18 in the app

    • Philippians 2:4

      μὴ τὰ ἑαυτῶν ἕκαστοι σκοποῦντες, ἀλλὰ καὶ τὰ ἑτέρων ἕκαστοι.

      each of you not just looking to his own things, but each of you also to the things of others.
