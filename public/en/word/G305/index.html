ἀναβαίνω anabainō G305

Gloss

to go up; ascend; of persons

Occurrences in the New Testament

81

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to go up / ascend (74)
    To move to a high place. Includes boarding a boat or carriage (ACT 8:31, JHN 21:11).
  2. (a thought) arises (3)
    A thought rises in the heart. 1CO 2:9, ACT 7:23, LUK 24:38.
  3. (a plant) grows up (4)
    Plants spring up and grow. Only the four instances in the parable of the seed.
