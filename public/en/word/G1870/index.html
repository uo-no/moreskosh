Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be ashamed (of) (11)
    To regard as a shame, to be ashamed: “do not be ashamed of the testimony about the Lord”, “I am not ashamed”, “was not ashamed of my chain”, “not ashamed to be called their God”, “I am not ashamed of the gospel”, “things of which you are now ashamed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 9:26

      ὃς γὰρ ἂν ἐπαισχυνθῇ με καὶ τοὺς ἐμοὺς λόγους, τοῦτον ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ἐπαισχυνθήσεται, ὅταν ἔλθῃ ἐν τῇ δόξῃ αὐτοῦ καὶ τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ τῶν ἁγίων ἀγγέλων.

      For whoever will be ashamed of me and of my words, of him will the Son of Man be ashamed when he comes in his glory, and the glory of the Father, and of the holy angels.

      τίνα οὖν καρπὸν εἴχετε τότε ἐφ’ οἷς νῦν ἐπαισχύνεσθε; τὸ γὰρ τέλος ἐκείνων θάνατος·

      What fruit then did you have at that time in the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those things is death.
