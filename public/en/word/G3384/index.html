μήτηρ mētēr G3384

Gloss

mother

Occurrences in the New Testament

83

Senses (IGSD)

  1. mother (81)
    A birth mother, and the address to an older woman (1TI 5:2).
  2. mother (figurative) (2)
    A figure for a source that begets and nurtures. The Jerusalem above in GAL 4:26, the "mother of harlots" in REV 17:5.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

83 μήτηρ, gen., μητρός, ἡ, [in LXX chiefly for אֵם;] mother: Mt 1:18 2:11, al.; fig., of one who takes the place of a mother, ἰδοὺ, ἡ μ. μου, Mt 12:49 (cf. Mt 12:50, Mk 3:35, Jo 19:27, Ro 16:13, I Ti 5:2); of a city, ἥτις ἐσὶν μ. ἡμῶν, Ga 4:26; symbolically of Babylon, ἡ μ. τ. πορνῶν, Re 17:5.
