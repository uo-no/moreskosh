Gloss

mastery; control

Occurrences in the New Testament

4

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

4 ** ἐγκράτεια, -ας, ἡ (< ἐγκρατής), [in LXX: Si 18:15, 30 IV Mac 5:34 * ;] prop., mastery, control. (sc. ἑαθτοῦ) self-control: Ac 24:25, Ga 5:23, II Pe 1:6 (v. DB, iv, 558b 695a; Page on Ac, l.c.).†
