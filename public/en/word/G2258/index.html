Senses (IGSD)

  1. existence / presence (516)
    Expresses a person, thing, or event being or existing. Includes being at a place, and a time or event coming around.
  2. copula (is) (1,763)
    Links subject and predicate, asserting "is." Combined with a participle, it can express progressive or continuous action ("keeps doing").
  3. belonging / origin (117)
    Combined with the genitive, or ἐκ or παρά, shows "belonging to, being of, coming from."
  4. meaning / interpretation (40)
    In the form ὅ ἐστιν, interposes a translation or explanation; also, within an interpretation, identifies "X refers to Y."
  5. possession (17)
    Combined with a dative person, expresses "there is (to that person) …" = "has."
  6. possibility / ability (2)
    An impersonal use of ἔστιν followed by an infinitive, expressing "it is possible to" and "it is (not) within one's authority to." Only two instances.
  7. to become (3)
    A future use combined with εἰς expressing the change "to become." Only three instances.
