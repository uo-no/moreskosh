ἀνοίγω anoigō G455

Gloss

to open

Occurrences in the New Testament

77

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to open (50)
    To open a door, gate, heaven, tomb, scroll, etc.
  2. to open (eyes / ears) (13)
    To enable seeing or hearing. Includes the spiritual opening of eyes (ACT 26:18).
  3. to open (the mouth) (9)
    To begin to speak. Includes opening one's heart and speaking frankly (2CO 6:11).
  4. to open (a door / opportunity) (5)
    An opportunity is given, such as a door opening for the mission.
