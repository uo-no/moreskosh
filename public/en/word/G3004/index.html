Senses (IGSD)

  1. to say (2,261)
    Voicing and conveying words. Includes conveying assertions and commands, and Scripture or the Lord "saying."
  2. to call (46)
    To call a person or thing by name, to designate as. In the passive, "to be called."
  3. to say in one's heart (24)
    With ἐν ἑαυτῷ (in oneself) etc., expresses an unspoken thought or self-question.
  4. to mean / refer to (8)
    Concerns the true meaning of a statement, or what a word points to: "it means," "he is speaking of."
  5. translated, … / which means … (3)
    A fixed formula presenting the translation of a foreign word: "translated, it means …". Only three instances.
