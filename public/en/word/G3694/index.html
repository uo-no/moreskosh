Senses (NGSD)

  1. to follow after (accompanying) (18)
    To follow after a person or thing, as in "follow after me" and "go after…"; also to go after someone.

    • Matthew 4:19

      καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς· Δεῦτε ὀπίσω μου, καὶ ποιήσω ὑμᾶς ἁλιεῖς ἀνθρώπων.

      He said to them, “Come after me, and I will make you fishers for men.”

    • Luke 9:23

      Ἔλεγεν δὲ πρὸς πάντας· Εἴ τις θέλει ὀπίσω μου ἔρχεσθαι, ἀρνησάσθω ἑαυτὸν καὶ ἀράτω τὸν σταυρὸν αὐτοῦ καθ’ ἡμέραν, καὶ ἀκολουθείτω μοι.

      He said to all, “If anyone desires to come after me, let him deny himself, take up his cross, and follow me.

      Read Luke 9:23 in the app

    • Acts 20:30

      καὶ ἐξ ὑμῶν αὐτῶν ἀναστήσονται ἄνδρες λαλοῦντες διεστραμμένα τοῦ ἀποσπᾶν τοὺς μαθητὰς ὀπίσω ἑαυτῶν·

      Men will arise from among your own selves, speaking perverse things, to draw away the disciples after them.

      Read Acts 20:30 in the app

  2. behind / backward (12)
    As position or direction: behind, backward, at the back. Includes the form εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω.

    • Matthew 16:23

      ὁ δὲ στραφεὶς εἶπεν τῷ Πέτρῳ· Ὕπαγε ὀπίσω μου, Σατανᾶ· σκάνδαλον εἶ ἐμοῦ, ὅτι οὐ φρονεῖς τὰ τοῦ θεοῦ ἀλλὰ τὰ τῶν ἀνθρώπων.

      But he turned and said to Peter, “Get behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to me, for you are not setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of men.”

      Read Matthew 16:23 in the app

    • Luke 7:38

      Standing behind at his feet weeping, she began to wet his feet with her tears, and she wiped them with the hair of her head, kissed his feet, and anointed them with the ointment.

  3. one who comes after (5)
    In the form ὁ ὀπίσω μου ἐρχόμενος, one who comes after me, one who comes later in time.

    • Matthew 3:11

      Ἐγὼ μὲν ὑμᾶς βαπτίζω ἐν ὕδατι εἰς μετάνοιαν· ὁ δὲ ὀπίσω μου ἐρχόμενος ἰσχυρότερός μού ἐστιν, οὗ οὐκ εἰμὶ ἱκανὸς τὰ ὑποδήματα βαστάσαι· αὐτὸς ὑμᾶς βαπτίσει ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ καὶ πυρί·

      “I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, but he who comes after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

35 ὀπίσω, adv. of place and time, [in LXX chiefly for אַחֲרֵי, אַחַר and cogn. forms;] prop., as in cl., adv., of time (not in NT); of place, back, behind, after: Mt 24:18, Lk 7:38; τὰ ὀ., Phl 3:13; εἰς τὰ ὀ., Mk 13:16, Lk 9:62 17:31, Jo 6:66 18:6 20:14. By a usage not found in cl., and in LXX representing the Heb. prep. אַחֲרֵי (Bl., § 40, 8; Thackeray, Gr., 46 f.), but also prob. general in vernacular (M, Pr., 99), as prep. c. gen.; of time, after: Mt 3:11, Mk 1:7, Jo 1:15, 27, 30; of place, behind, after: Mt 4:19 10:38 16:23, 24, Mk 1:17, 20 8:33, 34, Lk 9:23 14:27 19:14 21:8, II Pe 2:10, Ju 7, Re 1:10 12:15; in constr. praegn. (v. Swete on Re 13:3), Jo 12:19, Ac 5:17 20:30, I Ti 5:15, Re 13:3.†
