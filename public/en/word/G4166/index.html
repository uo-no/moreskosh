ποιμήν poimēn G4166

Gloss

a shepherd

Occurrences in the New Testament

18

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

18 ποιμήν, -ένος, ὁ, [in LXX for רֹעֶה;] a shepherd: Mt 9:36 25:32 26:31(LXX), Mk 6:34 14:27, Lk 2:8, 15, 18, 20, Jo 10:2, 12. Metaph., of Christ; Jo 10:11, 14, 16, He 13:20, I Pe 2:25; of Christian pastors, Eph 4:11 (cf. Hom., Il., i, 263, ποιμένα λαῶν).†
