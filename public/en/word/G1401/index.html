δοῦλος doulos G1401

Gloss

in bondage to; subject to

Occurrences in the New Testament

124

Senses (IGSD)

  1. servant / slave (124)
    One in the status of serving a master. Includes the self-designation as servant of God or Christ, and the servants in parables.

SYN.: διάκονος (q.v.), θεράπων, ὐπηρέτης (v. DB, iii, 377; iv, 461, 469; DCG, i, 221; ii, 613; Cremer, 215, 702).
