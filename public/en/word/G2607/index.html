Senses (NGSD)

  1. to blame, to condemn (3)
    To blame, to censure: “our heart condemns us”, “there was reason to blame him”. The reading of the perfect passive participle in Galatians 2:11 (stood condemned or was to be blamed) is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Galatians 2:11

      Ὅτε δὲ ἦλθεν Κηφᾶς εἰς Ἀντιόχειαν, κατὰ πρόσωπον αὐτῷ ἀντέστην, ὅτι κατεγνωσμένος ἦν·

      But when Peter came to Antioch, I resisted him to his face, because he stood condemned.

      because if our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart, and knows all things.
