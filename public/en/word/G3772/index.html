Senses (IGSD)

  1. sky / heaven (firmament) (65)
    The visible sky. Used in scenes involving weather, birds, and stars.
  2. heaven (where God dwells) (150)
    The heavenly realm where God and the angels dwell. The place of glory and reward, and the direction of prayer.
  3. heaven (heaven and earth / the whole world) (13)
    The whole world, in the pair "heaven and earth" and the form "under heaven."
  4. "heaven" in the kingdom of heaven (40)
    "Heaven" in the expression "kingdom of heaven" peculiar to Matthew. An expression including the reverent paraphrase that avoids naming God directly.
  5. God (by metonymy) (5)
    The usage in which "heaven" stands as a paraphrase for God himself.
