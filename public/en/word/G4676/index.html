Senses (NGSD)

  1. cloth, handkerchief (4)
    A cloth, a handkerchief: “handkerchiefs and aprons”, “the cloth wrapped around his face”, “the cloth that had covered his head”, “wrapped in a handkerchief”.
    Attested: NT only
