Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be puffed up (3)
    To be conceited, to become arrogant: “becoming conceited”, “puffed up”, “the conceited”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Timothy 3:6

      μὴ νεόφυτον, ἵνα μὴ τυφωθεὶς εἰς κρίμα ἐμπέσῃ τοῦ διαβόλου.

      not a new convert, lest being puffed up he fall into the same condemnation as the devil.

      Read 1 Timothy 3:6 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 6:4

      τετύφωται, μηδὲν ἐπιστάμενος, ἀλλὰ νοσῶν περὶ ζητήσεις καὶ λογομαχίας, ἐξ ὧν γίνεται φθόνος, ἔρις, βλασφημίαι, ὑπόνοιαι πονηραί,

      he is conceited, knowing nothing, but obsessed with arguments, disputes, and word battles, from which come envy, strife, insulting, evil suspicions,

      Read 1 Timothy 6:4 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 3:4

      προδόται, προπετεῖς, τετυφωμένοι, φιλήδονοι μᾶλλον ἢ φιλόθεοι,

      traitors, headstrong, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 * τυφόω, -ῶ (< τῦφος, smoke; metaph., conceit), prop., to wrap in smoke; used only metaph., to puff up, becloud with pride: pass., I Ti 3:6 6:4, II Ti 3:4 (Dem., Arist., al.).†
