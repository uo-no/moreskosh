Senses (NGSD)

  1. to slap (2)
    To strike with the open hand: “strikes you on the right cheek”, “slapped him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 5:39

      ἐγὼ δὲ λέγω ὑμῖν μὴ ἀντιστῆναι τῷ πονηρῷ· ἀλλ’ ὅστις σε ῥαπίζει εἰς τὴν δεξιὰν σιαγόνα, στρέψον αὐτῷ καὶ τὴν ἄλλην·

      But I tell you, don’t resist him who is evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ῥαπίζω (< ῥαπίς, a rod), [in LXX: Jg 16:25, Ho 11:4, I Es 4:31 * ;] prop., to strike with a rod. In late writers, to strike (the face) with the palm of the hand: Mt 26:67; c. acc. pers., seq. εἰς τ. σιαγόνα, Mt 5:39 (cf. Field, Notes, 40, 105).†
