ἀποστασία apostasia G646

Gloss

defection; apostasy; revolt

Occurrences in the New Testament

2

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

2 † ἀποστασία, -ας, ἡ (< ἀφίστημι), [in LXX for מַעַל, etc.;] defection, apostasy, revolt; in late Gk. (MM, Exp., viii; Lft., Notes, 111; Cremer, 308) for cl. ἀπόστασις, freq. in sense of political revolt, in LXX (e.g. Jos 22:22, II Ch 29:19, Je 2:19) and NT always of religious apostasy: Ac 21:21, II Th 2:3.†
