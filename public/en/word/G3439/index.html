μονογενής monogenēs G3439

Gloss

only begotten; of sons and daughters

Occurrences in the New Testament

9

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

9 μονογενής, -ές (< μόνος, γένος), [in LXX: Jg 11:34, Ps 21 (22):20 24 (25):16 34 (35):17 (יָחִיד), To 3:15 6:10, 14 8:17, Wi 7:22, Ba 4:16*;] only, only begotten (DCG, ii, 281), of sons and daughters: Lk 7:12 8:42 9:38, He 11:17; of Christ, Jo 3:16, 18, I Jn 4:9; μ. παρὰ πατρός, Jo 1:14; μ. θεός, Jo 1:18.†
