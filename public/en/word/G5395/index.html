Senses (NGSD)

  1. flame (7)
    A flame: “in flaming fire”, “in the flame of a burning bush”, “eyes like a flame”, “I am suffering in this flame”, “his eyes were like a flame of fire”, “a flame of blazing fire”, “who makes his angels winds and his ministers a flame of fire”.
