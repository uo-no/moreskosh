ὥρα hōra G5610

Gloss

any time or period fixed by nature; esp

Occurrences in the New Testament

106

Senses (IGSD)

  1. hour (appointed time) (47)
    The hour of an event, that time. Includes Christ's "hour."
  2. hour (54)
    The length of one hour, a way of counting time.
  3. for a while (5)
    A short period (JHN 5:35, 2CO 7:8, GAL 2:5).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

100 ὥρα, -ας, ἡ, [in LXX chiefly for עֵת and in Da for שָׁעָה ;] any time or period fixed by nature, esp. a season (Hom., Hdt., Plat., al.). A part of the day, and esp. a twelfth part of day or night, an hour: Mt 24:36, Mk 13:32, Ac 10:3, al.; accus. in ans. to "when"? (M, Pr., 63, 215; Bl., § 34, 8), Jo 4:52, Ac 10:3, 30 I Co 15:30, Re 3:3; acc. of duration, Mt 20:12 26:40, Mk 14:37; inexactly, πρὸς ὥραν, for a season, for a time, Jo 5:35, II Co 7:8, Ga 2:5; πρὸς καιρὸν ὥρας, for a short season (ICC, in l.), I Th 2:17. A definite point of time, time, hour: Mt 26:45; c. gen. rei, Lk 1:10 14:17, Re 3:10, al.; c. gen. pers., Lk 22:53, Jo 2:4 7:30, al.; ἡ ἄρτι ὥρα, I Co 4:11; ἐσχάτη ὥ., I Jo 2:18; seq. ὅτε, Jo 4:21, 23 5:25 16:25; ἵνα, Jo 12:23, al.; c. acc. et inf., Ro 13:11 (cf. DB, ext., 475b, 476b).
