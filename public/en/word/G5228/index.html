Senses (IGSD)

  1. for / on behalf of (benefit / representation) (104)
    Works as a prepositional phrase showing that an act benefits a person or matter, or stands in its place. Used widely in contexts where the subject acts for the object person: prayer, service, sacrifice, substitutionary atonement.
  2. about / concerning (topic / reference) (19)
    Works as a prepositional phrase introducing the object or topic toward which speech, thought, concern, or prayer is directed. Expresses a reference relation equivalent to "speak, write, appeal, hope about …".
  3. because of / for the sake of (cause / motive) (7)
    Works as a prepositional phrase introducing the matter that is the cause or motive of an act or experience. Appears in contexts such as "suffer, boast, rejoice because of …", and differs from sense 1 (benefit) in focusing on the subject's motivation.
  4. more than / beyond (comparison / degree) (13)
    Works as a prepositional phrase showing an excess in degree beyond a standard or person. Expresses comparative "more than" or "beyond" in quantity or intensity.
  5. above / over (position / rank) (6)
    Works as a prepositional phrase showing a higher position in rank, authority, or order. More often used for height of authority or grade than for physical height.
  6. even more / all the more (adverb) (1)
    Works as an adverb showing an excess of degree without naming what is compared. Corresponds to the prepositional use of sense 4 (comparison / degree) in a form that takes no noun phrase.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

150 ὑπέρ (when following subst.—poët.—ὕπερ; so as adv., II Co 11:23), prep. c. gen., acc. C. gen., primarily of place (rest or motion), over, above, across, beyond, hence, metaph., for, on behalf of: of prayer, Mt 5:44, Ac 8:24, Ro 10:1, Ja 5:16, al.; of laying down life, Jo 10:11, Ro 9:3, al.; esp. of Christ giving his life for man's redemption, Mk 14:24, Jo 10:15, Ac 21:13, Ro 5:6-8, al.; opp. to κατά, Mk 9:40, Lk 9:50, Ro 8:31. Causal, for, because of, for the sake of: c. gen. pers., Ac 5:41, Ro 1:5, Phl 1:29, II Co 12:10, al.; c. gen. rei, Jo 11:4, Ro 15:8, II Co 1:6, al. = ἀντί (v. M, Pr., 105), for, instead of, in the name of : I Co 15:29, II Co 5:15, 21 Ga 3:13, Col 1:7, Phm 13 (cf. Field, Notes, 225). In more colourless sense, = περί (M, Pr., l.c.), for, concerning, with regard to: Ro 9:27, II Co 1:6 8:23 12:8, Phl 1:7, II Th 2:1, al. C. acc., primarily of place, over, beyond, across, hence, metaph., of measure or degree in excess, above, beyond, over, more than: Mt 10:24, 37 Lk 6:40, Ac 26:13, I Co 10:13, II Co 1:8, Eph 1:22 3:20, Phm 16, al.; after comparatives = than (Jg 11:25, al.), Lk 16:8, He 4:12. As adv. (v. supr. ad init.), more: ὕπερ ἐγώ, I more, II Co 11:23; in compounds, v.s. ὑπεράνω, ὑπερλίαν, ὑπερπερισσῶς. In composition: over (ὑπεραίρω), beyond (ὑπερβάλλω), more (ὐπερνικάω), on behalf of (ὐπερεντυγχάνω).
