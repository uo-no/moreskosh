Senses (NGSD)

  1. why, for what purpose (6)
    An interrogative adverb asking for reason or purpose: ‘why’, ‘to what end’. “why is my freedom judged by another’s conscience”, “why did the Gentiles rage”, “why wrong one another”, “why should it use up the ground”, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me”, “why do you think evil in your hearts”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said, “Why do you think evil in your hearts?
