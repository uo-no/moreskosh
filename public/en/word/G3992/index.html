πέμπω pempō G3992

Gloss

to send

Occurrences in the New Testament

79

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to send (75)
    To send a person or thing. Includes putting in the sickle (REV 14:15, 18) and sending a delusion (2TH 2:11).
  2. to send for (a person) (4)
    To send for a person, to have them brought. ACT 10:32-33, 19:31, 20:17.
