πολύς polys G4183

Gloss

much; many; great

Occurrences in the New Testament

416

Senses (IGSD)

  1. many (number) (206)
    Many of countable things. A great number of people or things.
  2. much (quantity) (67)
    A large quantity of uncountable things such as time, commotion, toil.
  3. great / severe (62)
    Large in scale or degree. Includes the adverbial "greatly, exceedingly."
  4. greater (75)
    In comparison, greater in quantity or value than another. Includes the superlative "most."
  5. long / often (time / frequency) (5)
    Length of time or frequency. For long, often, late (in the hour).
