Senses (NGSD)

  1. to dishonor, to treat with contempt (7)
    To insult, dishonor or slight (passive: to be dishonored). “rejoiced that they were counted worthy to suffer dishonour for the Name”, “you have dishonoured the poor man”, “you dishonour me”, “treating him shamefully”, “they dishonoured him”, “so that their bodies were dishonoured among themselves”, “by breaking the law you dishonour God”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 12:4

      καὶ πάλιν ἀπέστειλεν πρὸς αὐτοὺς ἄλλον δοῦλον· κἀκεῖνον ἐκεφαλίωσαν καὶ ἠτίμασαν.

      Again, he sent another servant to them; and they threw stones at him, wounded him in the head, and sent him away shamefully treated.
