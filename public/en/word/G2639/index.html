Senses (NGSD)

  1. to enroll (1)
    To place on a list: “put on the list of widows”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Timothy 5:9

      Χήρα καταλεγέσθω μὴ ἔλαττον ἐτῶν ἑξήκοντα γεγονυῖα, ἑνὸς ἀνδρὸς γυνή,

      Let no one be enrolled as a widow under sixty years old, having been the wife of one man,

1 κατα-λέγω, [in LXX: De 19:16 (ענה), II Mac 7:30 R*;] to lay down; mid. (in Hom.), to lie down. to narrate (LXX, De 19:16, II Mac 7:30). to choose out, hence, to enrol (as of soldiers, Hdt., Thuc., a1., v. LS, s.v.): of widows, pass., I Ti 5:9 (cf. Ellic.; CGT, in l.).†
