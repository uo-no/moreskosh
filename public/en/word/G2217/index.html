Senses (NGSD)

  1. darkness, gloom (5)
    Darkness, gloom: “cast into utter darkness”, “the gloom of darkness”, “to darkness and tempest”, “kept under darkness in chains”, “the blackest darkness”.
    Attested: NT only

      For you have not come to a mountain that might be touched and that burned with fire, and to blackness, darkness, storm,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** ζόφος, -ου, ὁ (akin to γνόφος, q.v.), [in Sm.: Ex 10:22, Jb 28:3, Ps 10(11):2 90(91):6, Is 59:9*;] in Hom. the gloom of the under-world; hence, darkness, deep gloom (poët. and late prose writers): He 12:18, II Pe 2:4, 17, Ju 6, 13.†
