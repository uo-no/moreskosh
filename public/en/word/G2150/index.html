Senses (NGSD)

  1. godliness / reverence toward God (15)
    Reverence toward God, piety, a godly life: “in all godliness and dignity”, “the mystery of godliness”, “train yourself for godliness”, “having the appearance of godliness”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 3:12

      ἰδὼν δὲ ὁ Πέτρος ἀπεκρίνατο πρὸς τὸν λαόν· Ἄνδρες Ἰσραηλῖται, τί θαυμάζετε ἐπὶ τούτῳ, ἢ ἡμῖν τί ἀτενίζετε ὡς ἰδίᾳ δυνάμει ἢ εὐσεβείᾳ πεποιηκόσιν τοῦ περιπατεῖν αὐτόν;

      When Peter saw it, he responded to the people, “You men of Israel, why do you marvel at this man? Why do you fasten your eyes on us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?
