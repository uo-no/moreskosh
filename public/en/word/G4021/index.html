Senses (NGSD)

  1. meddlesome (1)
    Prying into what is not one’s concern: “gossips and busybodies”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Timothy 5:13

      ἅμα δὲ καὶ ἀργαὶ μανθάνουσιν, περιερχόμεναι τὰς οἰκίας, οὐ μόνον δὲ ἀργαὶ ἀλλὰ καὶ φλύαροι καὶ περίεργοι, λαλοῦσαι τὰ μὴ δέοντα.

      Besides, they also learn to be idle, going about from house to house. Not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, saying things which they ought not.

      Read 1 Timothy 5:13 in the app

  2. magic arts (1)
    Magic, curious arts: “those who had practised magic”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 19:19

      ἱκανοὶ δὲ τῶν τὰ περίεργα πραξάντων συνενέγκαντες τὰς βίβλους κατέκαιον ἐνώπιον πάντων· καὶ συνεψήφισαν τὰς τιμὰς αὐτῶν καὶ εὗρον ἀργυρίου μυριάδας πέντε.

      Many of those who practiced magical arts brought their books together and burned them in the sight of all. They counted their price, and found it to be fifty thousand pieces of silver.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 * περίεργος, -ον, of persons; over careful. curious, meddling, a busybody: I Ti 5:13. Of things; over-wrought. superfluous. curious, uncanny; τὰ π., curious arts, magic: Ac 19:19 (v. Deiss., BS, 3235.).†
