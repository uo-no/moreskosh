Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

4 πικρία, -ας, ἡ (< πικρός), [in LXX chiefly for מַר, מָרָה and cognate forms;] bitterness; of taste (Arist., Je 15:17, al.); metaph., of temper, character, etc.: Ro 3:14(LXX), Eph 4:31; ῥίζα πικρίας, He 12:15; χολὴ π., a malignant disposition, Ac 8:23.†
