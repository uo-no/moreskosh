Senses (NGSD)

  1. more, beyond (3)
    What is over and above; more than that: “anything more than this comes from evil”, “what more are you doing than others”, “it is superfluous”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 5:37

      ἔστω δὲ ὁ λόγος ὑμῶν ναὶ ναί, οὒ οὔ· τὸ δὲ περισσὸν τούτων ἐκ τοῦ πονηροῦ ἐστιν.

      But let your ‘Yes’ be ‘Yes’ and your ‘No’ be ‘No.’ Whatever is more than these is of the evil one.

      Read Matthew 5:37 in the app

    • Matthew 5:47

      καὶ ἐὰν ἀσπάσησθε τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς ὑμῶν μόνον, τί περισσὸν ποιεῖτε; οὐχὶ καὶ οἱ ἐθνικοὶ τὸ αὐτὸ ποιοῦσιν;

      If you only greet your friends, what more do you do than others? Don’t even the tax collectors do the same?

      Read Matthew 5:47 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 9:1

      Περὶ μὲν γὰρ τῆς διακονίας τῆς εἰς τοὺς ἁγίους περισσόν μοί ἐστιν τὸ γράφειν ὑμῖν,

      It is indeed unnecessary for me to write to you concerning the service to the saints,

      Read 2 Corinthians 9:1 in the app

  2. abundant, advantage (2)
    Being abundant or superior; advantage: “that they may have it abundantly”, “what advantage is there”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 10:10

      ὁ κλέπτης οὐκ ἔρχεται εἰ μὴ ἵνα κλέψῃ καὶ θύσῃ καὶ ἀπολέσῃ· ἐγὼ ἦλθον ἵνα ζωὴν ἔχωσιν καὶ περισσὸν ἔχωσιν.

      The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.

      Read John 10:10 in the app

    • Romans 3:1

      Τί οὖν τὸ περισσὸν τοῦ Ἰουδαίου, ἢ τίς ἡ ὠφέλεια τῆς περιτομῆς;

      Then what advantage does the Jew have? Or what is the profit of circumcision?

      Read Romans 3:1 in the app

  3. exceedingly (ἐκ περισσοῦ) (1)
    Extremely, very much: “they were utterly astounded in their hearts”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 6:51

      καὶ ἀνέβη πρὸς αὐτοὺς εἰς τὸ πλοῖον, καὶ ἐκόπασεν ὁ ἄνεμος. καὶ λίαν ἐκ περισσοῦ ἐν ἑαυτοῖς ἐξίσταντο,

      He got into the boat with them; and the wind ceased, and they were very amazed among themselves, and marveled;

      Read Mark 6:51 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 περισσός, -ή, -όν, [in LXX for יֶתֶר and cogn. forms;] more than sufficient, over and above, abundant: Jo 10:10, II Co 9:1; c. gen. (a popular substitute for πλειών, Bl., § 11, 34), Mt 5:37; id. c. ellips. gen., Mt 5:47 (EV, more than others; but v. infr.); ἐκ περισσοῦ, Mk 6:51, Rec., T. (on ὑπὲρ ὲκ π., v.s. ὑπερεκπερισσοῦ). Compar. neut., -ότερον: Lk 12:4, 48; c. gen., Mk 12:33; adverbially (cf. περισσῶς), more abundantly, II Co 10:8, He 6:17 7:15; c. gen., π. πάντων, I Co 15:10; pleonast., μᾶλλον π., Mk 7:36. out of the common, pre-eminent, superior: Mt 5:47 (Thayer, s.v., but v. supr.); τὸ π., as subst., Ro 3:1; compar., -ότερος, c. gen., Mt 11:9, Lk 7:26; c. subst., Mk 12:40, Lk 20:47, I Co 12:23, 24, II Co 2:7.†
