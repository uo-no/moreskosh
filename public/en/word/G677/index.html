Senses (NGSD)

  1. without offense (3)
    Giving no cause of stumbling, blameless: “one who gives no offence”, “a conscience without offence”, “the blameless”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 24:16

      ἐν τούτῳ καὶ αὐτὸς ἀσκῶ ἀπρόσκοπον συνείδησιν ἔχειν πρὸς τὸν θεὸν καὶ τοὺς ἀνθρώπους διὰ παντός.

      In this I also practice always having a conscience void of offense toward God and men.

      Read Acts 24:16 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 10:32

      ἀπρόσκοποι καὶ Ἰουδαίοις γίνεσθε καὶ Ἕλλησιν καὶ τῇ ἐκκλησίᾳ τοῦ θεοῦ,

      Give no occasion for stumbling, whether to Jews, to Greeks, or to the assembly of God;

      Read 1 Corinthians 10:32 in the app

    • Philippians 1:10

      εἰς τὸ δοκιμάζειν ὑμᾶς τὰ διαφέροντα, ἵνα ἦτε εἰλικρινεῖς καὶ ἀπρόσκοποι εἰς ἡμέραν Χριστοῦ,

      so that you may approve the things that are excellent, that you may be sincere and without offense to the day of Christ,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 **† ἀπρόσκοπος, -ον (< προσκόπτω), [in LXX: Si 35 (32):21, III Mac 3:8*;] act., not causing to stumble: metaph., of not leading others into sin, I Co 10:32. Pass., not stumbling, without offence, blameless: Ac 24:16, Phl 1:10 (for exx., v. MM, s.v.).†
