Senses (NGSD)

  1. to talk with (6)
    To speak together, to converse, to confer: “having conferred with his council”, “the people talked with one another”, “were talking with Jesus”, “spoke … with the chief priests”, “Elijah appeared with Moses, and they were talking”, “Moses and Elijah talking with Jesus”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 17:3

      καὶ ἰδοὺ ὤφθη αὐτοῖς Μωϋσῆς καὶ Ἠλίας συλλαλοῦντες μετ’ αὐτοῦ.

      Behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them talking with him.

      Amazement came on all and they spoke together, one with another, saying, “What is this word? For with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!”
