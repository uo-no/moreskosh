μαθητής mathētēs G3101

Gloss

a disciple; opp

Occurrences in the New Testament

262

Senses (IGSD)

  1. disciple (262)
    One who learns from and follows a teacher. Mostly refers to Jesus' disciples.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

261 μαθητής, -οῦ, ὁ (μανθάνω), [in LXX only as v.l. (A) in Je 13:21 20:11 26 (46):9*;] a disciple: opp. to διδάσκαλος, Mt 10:24, Lk 6:40; Ἰωάννου, Mt 9:14, Lk 7:18, Jo 3:25; τ. Φαρισαίων, Mt 22:16, Mk 2:18, Lk 5:33; Μωυσέως, Jo 9:28; Ἰησοῦ, Lk 6:17 7:11 19:37, Jo 6:66 7:3 19:38; esp. the twelve, Mt 10:1 11:1, Mk 7:17, Lk 8:9, Jo 2:2, al.; later, of Christians generally, Ac 6:1, 2, 7 9:19, al.; τ. κυρίου, Ac 9:1.
