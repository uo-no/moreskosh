Senses (NGSD)

  1. error / deception / delusion (10)
    Straying, deception, error (moral and religious): “spirits of deceit”, “our appeal does not spring from error or impure motives”, “a working of delusion”, “fall into error”, “the last fraud will be worse than the first”, “bring back a sinner from the error of his way”, “receiving the penalty for their error”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 πλάνη, -ης, ἡ, [in LXX: Pr 14:8 (מִרְמָה), Wi 1:12, al;] a wandering. Metaph., a going astray, an error (in NT always with respect to morals or religion): Mt 27:64, Ro 1:27, Eph 4:14, I Th 2:3, II Th 2:11, Ja 5:20, II Pe 2:18 3:17, I Jn 4:6, Ju 11.†
