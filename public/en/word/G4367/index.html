Senses (NGSD)

  1. to command (6)
    To command, to direct: “all that the Lord has commanded you”, “commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ”, “offer the gift that Moses commanded”, “as the angel of the Lord had commanded”, “what was commanded”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      ἐγερθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰωσὴφ ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕπνου ἐποίησεν ὡς προσέταξεν αὐτῷ ὁ ἄγγελος κυρίου καὶ παρέλαβεν τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ·

      Joseph arose from his sleep, and did as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and took his wife to himself;
