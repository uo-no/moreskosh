Senses (NGSD)

  1. to command / instruct (32)
    To command with authority, give instructions, make a declaration, solemnly charge. Includes prohibition, ordering silence, and exhortation.

    • Matthew 10:5

      Τούτους τοὺς δώδεκα ἀπέστειλεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς παραγγείλας αὐτοῖς λέγων· Εἰς ὁδὸν ἐθνῶν μὴ ἀπέλθητε καὶ εἰς πόλιν Σαμαριτῶν μὴ εἰσέλθητε·

      Jesus sent these twelve out and commanded them, saying, “Don’t go among the Gentiles, and don’t enter into any city of the Samaritans.

      They agreed with him. Summoning the apostles, they beat them and commanded them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.
