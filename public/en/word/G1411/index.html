δύναμις dynamis G1411

Gloss

power; might; strength

Occurrences in the New Testament

119

Senses (IGSD)

  1. power (79)
    The power held by God or people.
  2. mighty work / miracle (23)
    In the plural, the miracles themselves.
  3. powers (mighty ones) (10)
    Heavenly powers; the ranks of rule and authority.
  4. ability (6)
    As in MAT 25:15, "according to each one's ability."
  5. meaning (1)
    Only the "meaning of the language" in 1CO 14:11.
