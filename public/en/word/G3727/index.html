Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

11 ὅρκος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for שֶׁבַע, שִׁבְעָה and cogn. forms;] an oath: Mt 14:7 26:72, Lk 1:73, Ac 2:30, He 6:16, 17, Ja 5:12; p1., Mt 5:33(LXX) 14:9, Mk 6:26.†
