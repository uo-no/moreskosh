Senses (NGSD)

  1. paradise, garden (3)
    A garden, paradise: “today you will be with me in paradise”, “was caught up into paradise”, “the tree of life in the paradise of God”. The interpretation of the referent (the place) is not decided.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 23:43

      καὶ εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ἀμήν σοι λέγω σήμερον μετ’ ἐμοῦ ἔσῃ ἐν τῷ παραδείσῳ.

      Jesus said to him, “Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.”

    • 2 Corinthians 12:4

      ὅτι ἡρπάγη εἰς τὸν παράδεισον καὶ ἤκουσεν ἄρρητα ῥήματα ἃ οὐκ ἐξὸν ἀνθρώπῳ λαλῆσαι.

      how he was caught up into Paradise and heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter.

    • Revelation 2:7

      ὁ ἔχων οὖς ἀκουσάτω τί τὸ πνεῦμα λέγει ταῖς ἐκκλησίαις. τῷ νικῶντι δώσω αὐτῷ φαγεῖν ἐκ τοῦ ξύλου τῆς ζωῆς, ὅ ἐστιν ἐν τῷ παραδείσῳ τοῦ θεοῦ.

      He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the assemblies. To him who overcomes I will give to eat from the tree of life, which is in the Paradise of my God.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 παράδεισος, -ου, ὁ (an Oriental word, first used by Xen. of the parks of Persian kings and nobles), [in LXX chiefly for גַּן, Ge 1:8 ff., al.; also for עֵדֶן, Is 51:3; פַּרְדֵּס, Ne 2:8, Ec 2:5;] a park, pleasure-ground, garden (LXX), an orchard (in π., v. MM, ii, xviii; Deiss., BS, 148). Paradise, the abode of the blessed dead: Lk 23:43, II Co 12:4, Re 2:7.†
