ἀρχιερεύς archiereus G749

Gloss

high-priest

Occurrences in the New Testament

122

Senses (IGSD)

  1. high priest (63)
    The high priest as an individual office.
  2. chief priests (59)
    The leading body of the priestly class (members of the council, etc.).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

122 ἀρχ-ιερεύς, -έως, ὁ, [in LXX for כֹּהֵן, הַגָּדוֹל כּ״, הָרֹאשׁ כּ״;] high-priest: Mk 2:26 14:47, al.; of Christ: He 2:17 3:1, al. In pl., chief priests, including ex-high-priests and members of high-priestly families: Mt 2:4, Mk 8:31, al. (Cremer, 294; DCG, i, 297 f.; MM, s.v.).
