Senses (NGSD)

  1. fifty (7)
    The numeral fifty: “for about four hundred and fifty years”, “a hundred and fifty-three fish”, “you are not yet fifty years old”, “write fifty”, “fifty denarii”, “in groups of fifty”, “in companies of fifty”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 6:40

      καὶ ἀνέπεσαν πρασιαὶ πρασιαὶ κατὰ ἑκατὸν καὶ κατὰ πεντήκοντα.

      They sat down in ranks, by hundreds and by fifties.

      For they were about five thousand men. He said to his disciples, “Make them sit down in groups of about fifty each.”
