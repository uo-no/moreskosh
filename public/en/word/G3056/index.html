Senses (IGSD)

  1. word / saying (190)
    A concrete linguistic expression of inner thought. Refers to a statement, word, or phrase with a coherent meaning.
  2. the word / word of the gospel (91)
    The whole of the teaching, commands, and promises given by God, the Lord, or Christ; especially the gospel message conveyed in preaching and discipleship.
  3. speech / discourse (talk / address) (22)
    The act of speaking itself, or speech as a manner or faculty of discourse. Stands out especially when contrasted with writing, deeds, and power.
  4. matter / affair (5)
    A case, matter, or issue taken up as a topic. Appears widely, from legal disputes to everyday topics.
  5. reckoning / account / accountability (10)
    A reckoning, settlement, or report of money or deeds, required as "accountability." Appears in the form "to render an account (λόγον) to someone."
  6. reason / ground (6)
    The reason, rationale, or legitimate cause given as the ground of an act or judgment.
  7. the Word (Logos / the Word of God) (6)
    The Word of God, portrayed personally as the source of creation, revelation, and salvation. In the Johannine writings, identified with the incarnate Christ.
