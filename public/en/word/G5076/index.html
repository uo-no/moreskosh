Senses (NGSD)

  1. tetrarch (4)

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 † τετραάρχης (Rec. τετράρχης, v. supr.), -ου, ὁ (< τετρα- in comp. = τέτορα, Doric for τέσσαρα, + ἄρχω), a tetrarch, i.e. prop., the governor of a fourth part of a region (Strab.); any petty ruler (Plut.); in NT, of Herod Antipas: Mt 14:1, Lk 3:19 9:7, Ac 13:1.†
