Senses (NGSD)

  1. to overshadow (5)
    To cover, to cover with shadow, to cast a shadow: “so that as Peter passed, at least his shadow might fall on some of them”, “the power of the Most High will overshadow you”, “a cloud came over them”, “a cloud appeared and overshadowed them”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 17:5

      ἔτι αὐτοῦ λαλοῦντος ἰδοὺ νεφέλη φωτεινὴ ἐπεσκίασεν αὐτούς, καὶ ἰδοὺ φωνὴ ἐκ τῆς νεφέλης λέγουσα· Οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός, ἐν ᾧ εὐδόκησα· ἀκούετε αὐτοῦ.

      While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. Behold, a voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him.”
