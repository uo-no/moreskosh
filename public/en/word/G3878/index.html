Senses (NGSD)

  1. to refuse to listen (2)
    To pay no heed, to disobey: “if he refuses to listen to the church”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 18:17

      ἐὰν δὲ παρακούσῃ αὐτῶν, εἰπὸν τῇ ἐκκλησίᾳ· ἐὰν δὲ καὶ τῆς ἐκκλησίας παρακούσῃ, ἔστω σοι ὥσπερ ὁ ἐθνικὸς καὶ ὁ τελώνης.

      If he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the assembly. If he refuses to hear the assembly also, let him be to you as a Gentile or a tax collector.

      Read Matthew 18:17 in the app

  2. to overhear, to ignore (1)
    To catch with the ear, or to let pass unheeded. It is not decided whether Mark 5:36 is to be read as “ignoring” or as “overhearing”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 5:36

      ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς παρακούσας τὸν λόγον λαλούμενον λέγει τῷ ἀρχισυναγώγῳ· Μὴ φοβοῦ, μόνον πίστευε.

      But Jesus, when he heard the message spoken, immediately said to the ruler of the synagogue, “Don’t be afraid, only believe.”
