Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be shipwrecked (2)
    To suffer shipwreck: “made shipwreck of the faith”, “three times I have been shipwrecked”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 11:25

      τρὶς ἐραβδίσθην, ἅπαξ ἐλιθάσθην, τρὶς ἐναυάγησα, νυχθήμερον ἐν τῷ βυθῷ πεποίηκα·

      Three times I was beaten with rods. Once I was stoned. Three times I suffered shipwreck. I have been a night and a day in the deep.

      Read 2 Corinthians 11:25 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 1:19

      ἔχων πίστιν καὶ ἀγαθὴν συνείδησιν, ἥν τινες ἀπωσάμενοι περὶ τὴν πίστιν ἐναυάγησαν·

      holding faith and a good conscience, which some having thrust away made a shipwreck concerning the faith,
