Senses (NGSD)

  1. west, sunset (6)
    The direction where the sun sets; the west: “came from east and west”, “when you see a cloud rising in the west”, “as the lightning flashes from east to west”, “many will come from east and west”, “three gates on the west”. Mark 16:8 has the form δύσεως and is treated as a variant form of the same word.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:11

      λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν ὅτι πολλοὶ ἀπὸ ἀνατολῶν καὶ δυσμῶν ἥξουσιν καὶ ἀνακλιθήσονται μετὰ Ἀβραὰμ καὶ Ἰσαὰκ καὶ Ἰακὼβ ἐν τῇ βασιλείᾳ τῶν οὐρανῶν·

      I tell you that many will come from the east and the west, and will sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the Kingdom of Heaven,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 δυσμή, -ῆς, ἡ (= δύσις, < δύνω), [in LXX for בּוֹא, עֲרָבָה, etc. ;] mostly in pl., opp. to ἀνατολαί; a setting, as of the sun (acc. to Thayer, s.v., so perhaps in Lk 12:54). the quarter of sunset, the west: anarth., Mt 8:11 24:27, Lk 12:54 13:29, Re 21:13.†
