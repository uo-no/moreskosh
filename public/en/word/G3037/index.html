λίθος lithos G3037

Gloss

a stone

Occurrences in the New Testament

59

Senses (IGSD)

  1. stone (51)
    A stone (including building material, a thrown stone, and the figurative "living stones").
  2. precious stone (8)
    A gem, a precious stone.
