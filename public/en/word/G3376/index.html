Senses (NGSD)

  1. month (calendar month) (18)
    A month of the calendar, or a count of months: “six months”, “three months”, “Mary's sixth month”, “forty-two months”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:24

      Μετὰ δὲ ταύτας τὰς ἡμέρας συνέλαβεν Ἐλισάβετ ἡ γυνὴ αὐτοῦ· καὶ περιέκρυβεν ἑαυτὴν μῆνας πέντε, λέγουσα

      After these days Elizabeth his wife conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying,
