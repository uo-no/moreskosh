Senses (NGSD)

  1. linen sheet (2)
    Linen cloth, a large sheet: “something like a large sheet” (twice).
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Acts 10:11

      καὶ θεωρεῖ τὸν οὐρανὸν ἀνεῳγμένον καὶ καταβαῖνον σκεῦός τι ὡς ὀθόνην μεγάλην τέσσαρσιν ἀρχαῖς καθιέμενον ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς,

      He saw heaven opened and a certain container descending to him, like a great sheet let down by four corners on the earth,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 * ὀθόνη, -ης, ἡ (of Semitic origin, cf. Heb. אֵטוּן, yarn); fine linen (Hom., al.). Later, a sheet or sail: Ac 10:11 11:5.†
