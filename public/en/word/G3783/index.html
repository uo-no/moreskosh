Senses (NGSD)

  1. debt (1)
    A debt, what is owed: “forgive us our debts”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 6:12

      καὶ ἄφες ἡμῖν τὰ ὀφειλήματα ἡμῶν, ὡς καὶ ἡμεῖς ἀφήκαμεν τοῖς ὀφειλέταις ἡμῶν·

      Forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.

      Read Matthew 6:12 in the app

  2. what is due (1)
    A payment that is due, an obligation: “the wage is not counted as a gift but as what is due”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 4:4

      τῷ δὲ ἐργαζομένῳ ὁ μισθὸς οὐ λογίζεται κατὰ χάριν ἀλλὰ κατὰ ὀφείλημα·

      Now to him who works, the reward is not counted as grace, but as something owed.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ὀφείλημα, -τος, τό (< ὀφείλω), [in LXX: De 24:10 (מַשָּׁאָה), I Es 3:20, I Mac 15:8*;] that which is owed, a debt: Ro 4:4; metaph. (as Aram. חוֺבָא, חוֺב), of sin as a debt, Mt 6:12.†
