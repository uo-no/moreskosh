Senses (NGSD)

  1. to receive as a guest / lodge (7)
    To take in as a guest, to entertain, to lodge: “he is lodging with Simon the tanner”, “whether he was staying there”, “invited them in to be his guests”, “to lodge with Mnason”, “entertained us”, “entertained angels unawares”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 10:6

      οὗτος ξενίζεται παρά τινι Σίμωνι βυρσεῖ, ᾧ ἐστιν οἰκία παρὰ θάλασσαν.

      He is staying with a tanner named Simon, whose house is by the seaside.

    • Acts 10:23

      εἰσκαλεσάμενος οὖν αὐτοὺς ἐξένισεν. Τῇ δὲ ἐπαύριον ἀναστὰς ἐξῆλθεν σὺν αὐτοῖς, καί τινες τῶν ἀδελφῶν τῶν ἀπὸ Ἰόππης συνῆλθον αὐτῷ.

      So he called them in and provided a place to stay. On the next day Peter arose and went out with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.

      Read Acts 10:23 in the app

    • Acts 21:16

      συνῆλθον δὲ καὶ τῶν μαθητῶν ἀπὸ Καισαρείας σὺν ἡμῖν, ἄγοντες παρ’ ᾧ ξενισθῶμεν Μνάσωνί τινι Κυπρίῳ, ἀρχαίῳ μαθητῇ.

      Some of the disciples from Caesarea also went with us, bringing one Mnason of Cyprus, an early disciple, with whom we would stay.

      Read Acts 21:16 in the app

  2. to surprise / seem strange (3)
    To make think strange, to astonish: “they are surprised that you do not join them in the same debauchery”, “do not be surprised at the fiery trial”, “some strange things to our ears”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 17:20

      ξενίζοντα γάρ τινα εἰσφέρεις εἰς τὰς ἀκοὰς ἡμῶν· βουλόμεθα οὖν γνῶναι τίνα θέλει ταῦτα εἶναι.

      For you bring certain strange things to our ears. We want to know therefore what these things mean.”

    • 1 Peter 4:4

      ἐν ᾧ ξενίζονται μὴ συντρεχόντων ὑμῶν εἰς τὴν αὐτὴν τῆς ἀσωτίας ἀνάχυσιν, βλασφημοῦντες·

      They think it is strange that you don’t run with them into the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you.

      Read 1 Peter 4:4 in the app

      Beloved, don’t be astonished at the fiery trial which has come upon you to test you, as though a strange thing happened to you.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 ** ξενίζω (< ξένος), [in LXX: Es 3:13, Si 29:25, II Mac 9:6, III Mac 7:3*;] to receive as a guest, entertain: c. acc. pers., Ac 10:23 28:7, He 13:2; pass., Ac 10:6, 18, 32 21:16. In late writers (Polyb., al.; II Mac 9:6), to surprise, astonish by strangeness: Ac 17:20; pass., I Pe 4:4, 12.†
