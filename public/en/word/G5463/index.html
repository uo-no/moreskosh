χαίρω chairō G5463

Gloss

to rejoice; be glad

Occurrences in the New Testament

74

Senses (IGSD)

  1. to rejoice (63)
    To feel or express joy. Includes rejoicing at a reunion or at news.
  2. (as a greeting) greetings (11)
    A greeting formula. The opening of letters (ACT 15:23, JAS 1:1), and the address "Hail" (MAT 26:49, JHN 19:3).
