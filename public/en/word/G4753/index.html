Senses (NGSD)

  1. army / soldiers (8)
    An army, a body of soldiers: “ordered the soldiers to go down”, “took soldiers with him”, “Herod with his soldiers”, “sent his troops”, “the armies of heaven”, “his army”, “twice ten thousand times ten thousand mounted troops”, “his army”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 22:7

      ὁ δὲ βασιλεὺς ὠργίσθη, καὶ πέμψας τὰ στρατεύματα αὐτοῦ ἀπώλεσεν τοὺς φονεῖς ἐκείνους καὶ τὴν πόλιν αὐτῶν ἐνέπρησεν.

      When the king heard that, he was angry, and sent his armies, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.
