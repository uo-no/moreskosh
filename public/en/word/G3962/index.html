Gloss

Senses (IGSD)

  1. Father (God) (258)
    The usage referring to God as Father. As the Father of Christ, and as the heavenly Father in relation to those who believe.
  2. father (human) (77)
    A father as a parent by flesh. In some contexts refers to both parents.
  3. ancestors / fathers (61)
    Ancestors, forefathers. Includes the lineal usage of calling Abraham "our father."
  4. source / origin (father of …) (12)
    A figurative usage calling the source of a quality or group a "father." Father of faith, father of lies, spiritual father, etc.
  5. honorific (fathers) (5)
    "Father" as a form of address or title for superiors and elders in the faith.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

413 πατήρ, πατρός, -τρί, τέρα, [in LXX chiefly and very freq. for אָב;] a father; prop., of the male parent: Mt 2:22, Lk 1:17, Jo 4:53; anarth., He 12:7 (M, Pr., 82 f.); pl., of both parents (cl.), He 11:23; οἱ π. τ. σαρκός, He 12:9; of a forefather or ancestor (in cl. usually in pl.; Hom., al.): Mt 3:9, Lk 1:73, Jo 8:39, al.; pl., Mt 23:30, 32, Lk 6:23, 26, Jo 4:20, I Co 10:1, al. Metaph., of an author, originator, or archetype (= αἴτιος, ἀρχηγός, etc.; Pind., Plat., al.): Jo 8:41-44, Ro 4:11, 12, 16. as a title of respect or honour, used of seniors, teachers and others in a position of responsible authority (Jg 17:10, II Ki 2:12, Pr 1:8, al.): Mt 23:9, Ac 7:2 22:1, I Jo 2:13. Of God (as in cl. of Zeus) as Father; of created things: τ. φώτων, Ja 1:17; of all sentient beings: Eph 3:14, 15, He 12:9; of men, esp. those in covenant relation with Him (freq in OT and later Jewish lit.; v. Abbott, JG., 96 f.); by Apostles: Jo 1:14 (anarth.; v. M, Pr., 82 f.), Ro 15:6, II Co 1:3 11:31, Eph 1:3, Col 1:3, He 1:5, I Pe 1:3, Re 1:6 (cf. Westc., Epp. Jo., 27-34).
