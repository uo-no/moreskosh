Senses (IGSD)

  1. on (place) (345)
    Being positioned on or beside something. Includes sitting, standing, and being written on. With ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "together, in one place."
  2. onto / to (direction) (103)
    Movement upward or in an approaching direction. Includes touching with the hand, embracing the neck, and writing on.
  3. to come upon (rest on) (106)
    Blessing, the Spirit, the kingdom, wrath, judgment, or calamity coming upon a person. Includes a name being called over them.
  4. against (hostility) (47)
    Moving against with hostility. Includes king against king, laying hands on to seize.
  5. over (authority) (40)
    A position of authority over persons or things: "one set over …".
  6. at / during (time) (47)
    "At the time of (an era)," and length of a period. Duration, as in "for three years" and "for a long time."
  7. before (in the presence of) (20)
    Standing before a governor, a king, or a trial. The setting of testimony and confession.
  8. on the basis of / because of (ground / purpose) (169)
    The ground, reason, or purpose of acts and feelings. Includes the object of "be amazed at" and "rejoice in," by a name, by a word, the place where hope is set, and "in truth" (ἐπ᾽ ἀληθείας).
  9. in addition to (6)
    Added on top of what already exists.
  10. about (topic) (4)
    The matter to which a written account, prophecy, or testimony is directed.
