Bible Green

οὐχί ouchi G3780

Gloss

strengthened form of; not

Occurrences in the New Testament

53

Senses (IGSD)

  1. (strong) no / not at all (53)
    A strengthened negation (rhetorical "is it not?" / "no!").

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry

54 οὐχί, strengthened form of οὐ, not; in neg. sentences, not, not at all: Lk 1:60 12:51, Jo 13:10, al.; more freq. in questions where an affirm. ans. is expected (Lat. nonne): Mt 5:46, 47, Lk 6:39, Jo 11:9, al.
