Senses (NGSD)

  1. at the point of death (1)
    In the last extremity, critically ill: “is at the point of death”. The representative examples in the LXX and the Fathers are of the adjective ἔσχατος, and the adverb has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 5:23

      καὶ παρακαλεῖ αὐτὸν πολλὰ λέγων ὅτι Τὸ θυγάτριόν μου ἐσχάτως ἔχει, ἵνα ἐλθὼν ἐπιθῇς τὰς χεῖρας αὐτῇ ἵνα σωθῇ καὶ ζήσῃ.

      and begged him much, saying, “My little daughter is at the point of death. Please come and lay your hands on her, that she may be made healthy, and live.”
