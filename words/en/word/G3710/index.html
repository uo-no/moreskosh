Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be angry / enraged (9)
    To be angry, indignant: “be angry and do not sin”, “the elder brother was angry”, “the master was angry”, “the king was angry”, “everyone who is angry with his brother”, “the dragon became furious with the woman”, “the nations raged”, “Jesus was moved with anger”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 18:34

      καὶ ὀργισθεὶς ὁ κύριος αὐτοῦ παρέδωκεν αὐτὸν τοῖς βασανισταῖς ἕως οὗ ἀποδῷ πᾶν τὸ ὀφειλόμενον.

      His lord was angry, and delivered him to the tormentors until he should pay all that was due to him.

    • Mark 1:41

      καὶ ὀργισθεὶς ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα αὐτοῦ ἥψατο καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ· Θέλω, καθαρίσθητι·

      Being moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand, and touched him, and said to him, “I want to. Be made clean.”

    • Ephesians 4:26

      ὀργίζεσθε καὶ μὴ ἁμαρτάνετε· ὁ ἥλιος μὴ ἐπιδυέτω ἐπὶ παροργισμῷ ὑμῶν,

      “Be angry, and don’t sin.” Don’t let the sun go down on your wrath,
