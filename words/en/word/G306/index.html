Senses (NGSD)

  1. to put off (1)
    To defer, to postpone: “adjourned the trial”. The representative examples in the LXX are the uses “to rebuff” and “to go up”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 24:22

      Ἀνεβάλετο δὲ αὐτοὺς ὁ Φῆλιξ, ἀκριβέστερον εἰδὼς τὰ περὶ τῆς ὁδοῦ, εἴπας· Ὅταν Λυσίας ὁ χιλίαρχος καταβῇ διαγνώσομαι τὰ καθ’ ὑμᾶς·

      But Felix, having more exact knowledge concerning the Way, deferred them, saying, “When Lysias, the commanding officer, comes down, I will decide your case.”
