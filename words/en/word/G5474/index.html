Senses (NGSD)

  1. burnished bronze (2)
    Polished bronze, or a similar gleaming metal: “gleaming bronze” (twice). The exact meaning of the word is not decided (there is a view that connects its second part with ‘frankincense’).
    Attested: NT only

    • Revelation 1:15

      καὶ οἱ πόδες αὐτοῦ ὅμοιοι χαλκολιβάνῳ, ὡς ἐν καμίνῳ πεπυρωμένης, καὶ ἡ φωνὴ αὐτοῦ ὡς φωνὴ ὑδάτων πολλῶν,

      His feet were like burnished brass, as if it had been refined in a furnace. His voice was like the voice of many waters.
