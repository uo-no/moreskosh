Senses (NGSD)

  1. deeply grieved (4)
    Very sorrowful, deeply distressed: “being very sorrowful”, “my soul is sorrowful to the point of death”, “was deeply grieved”, “the king was deeply grieved”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:38

      τότε λέγει αὐτοῖς· Περίλυπός ἐστιν ἡ ψυχή μου ἕως θανάτου· μείνατε ὧδε καὶ γρηγορεῖτε μετ’ ἐμοῦ.

      Then Jesus said to them, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here and watch with me.”
