Senses (NGSD)

  1. approved, genuine (7)
    Tested and approved, of proven worth: “so that those who are approved among you may become evident”, “it is the one the Lord commends who is approved”, “a worker who has presented himself to God and has no need to be ashamed”, “blessed is the one who endures, for after being tested”, “greet Apelles”, “pleasing to God and approved by people”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 14:18

      ὁ γὰρ ἐν τούτῳ δουλεύων τῷ Χριστῷ εὐάρεστος τῷ θεῷ καὶ δόκιμος τοῖς ἀνθρώποις.

      For he who serves Christ in these things is acceptable to God and approved by men.

      δεῖ γὰρ καὶ αἱρέσεις ἐν ὑμῖν εἶναι, ἵνα καὶ οἱ δόκιμοι φανεροὶ γένωνται ἐν ὑμῖν.

      For there also must be factions among you, that those who are approved may be revealed among you.

    • 2 Corinthians 13:7

      εὐχόμεθα δὲ πρὸς τὸν θεὸν μὴ ποιῆσαι ὑμᾶς κακὸν μηδέν, οὐχ ἵνα ἡμεῖς δόκιμοι φανῶμεν, ἀλλ’ ἵνα ὑμεῖς τὸ καλὸν ποιῆτε, ἡμεῖς δὲ ὡς ἀδόκιμοι ὦμεν.

      Now I pray to God that you do no evil; not that we may appear approved, but that you may do that which is honorable, though we may seem to have failed.
