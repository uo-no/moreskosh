Senses (NGSD)

  1. gold (10)
    Gold: “gold, silver, precious stones”, “do not acquire gold, silver, or copper for your belts”, “gold, frankincense, and myrrh”, “an image of gold”, “a temple adorned with gold”, “the gold, or the temple that makes the gold sacred?”, “your gold has corroded”, “like crowns of gold”.
