Senses (NGSD)

  1. eleven (6)
    The numeral eleven; in the New Testament it refers to ‘the eleven’ (the twelve apostles without Judas): “was numbered with the eleven apostles”, “Peter, standing with the eleven”, “to the eleven and their companions”, “the eleven and the others gathered together”, “the eleven disciples went to Galilee”, “while the eleven were reclining at table”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 28:16

      Οἱ δὲ ἕνδεκα μαθηταὶ ἐπορεύθησαν εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν εἰς τὸ ὄρος οὗ ἐτάξατο αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς,

      But the eleven disciples went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had sent them.
