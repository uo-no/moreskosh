Senses (NGSD)

  1. citizenship (1)
    One's nationality as a citizen, one's country: “our citizenship is in heaven”. The reading of the sense (citizenship or homeland) is not decided. The representative examples in the LXX appear to be the uses “government” and “way of life”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 3:20

      ἡμῶν γὰρ τὸ πολίτευμα ἐν οὐρανοῖς ὑπάρχει, ἐξ οὗ καὶ σωτῆρα ἀπεκδεχόμεθα κύριον Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν,

      For our citizenship is in heaven, from where we also wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,
