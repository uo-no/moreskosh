Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be anxious (1)
    To be unsettled, to fret: “do not be anxious”. The representative examples in the LXX appear to be the use “to lift high, to be haughty”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 12:29

      καὶ ὑμεῖς μὴ ζητεῖτε τί φάγητε καὶ τί πίητε, καὶ μὴ μετεωρίζεσθε,

      “Don’t seek what you will eat or what you will drink; neither be anxious.
