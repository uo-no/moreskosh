Senses (NGSD)

  1. to grumble / complain (8)
    To murmur, to complain, to mutter: “do not grumble”, “as some of them grumbled”, “heard the crowd muttering”, “the Jews grumbled”, “do not grumble among yourselves”, “knowing that his disciples were grumbling”, “they grumbled”, “grumbled at the master of the house”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 20:11

      λαβόντες δὲ ἐγόγγυζον κατὰ τοῦ οἰκοδεσπότου

      When they received it, they murmured against the master of the household,
