Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

111 ὅλος, -η, -ον, [in LXX chiefly for כֹּל;] of persons and things, whole, entire, complete; of indefinite ideas, c. subst. anarth.: Lk 5:5, Ac 11:26 28:30, Tit 1:11; ὅλον ἄνθρωπον (an entire man; v. Field, Notes, 93), Jo 7:23; ὅλη Ἰερουσαλήμ (= πᾶσα Ἰ, Mt 2:3; v. Bl., § 47, 9), Ac 21:31. Definite, c. art.; preceding subst.: Mt 4:23, 24, Lk 8:39, I Co 12:17, al.; following subst.: Mk 1:33, Lk 9:25, Jo 4:53, Ac 21:30, al.; between art. and subst., where subst. is an abstract noun (Plat., al.). Attached to adj. or verb: Mt 13:33, Lk 13:21, Jo 9:34, al.; adverbially, δι ̓ ὅλου (MM, xviii), Jo 19:23.
