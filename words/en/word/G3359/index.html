Senses (NGSD)

  1. forehead (8)
    The forehead: “to receive a mark on the right hand or the forehead”, “his name and his Father's name written on their foreheads”, “receives the mark of the beast on his forehead”, “a name written on her forehead”, “had not received its mark on their foreheads or hands”, “God's name on their foreheads”, “seal the servants of God on their foreheads”, “those without the seal of God on their foreheads”.
