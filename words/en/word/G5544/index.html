χρηστότης chrēstotēs G5544

Occurrences in the New Testament

10

Senses (NGSD)

  1. kindness / goodness (9)
    Kindness, benevolence, goodwill: “purity, knowledge, patience, kindness”, “kindness, gentleness, patience”, “kindness toward us in Christ Jesus”, “the fruit of the Spirit is kindness”, “the kindness and severity of God”, “God's kindness leads you to repentance”, “the goodness of God our Saviour”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 2:4

      ἢ τοῦ πλούτου τῆς χρηστότητος αὐτοῦ καὶ τῆς ἀνοχῆς καὶ τῆς μακροθυμίας καταφρονεῖς, ἀγνοῶν ὅτι τὸ χρηστὸν τοῦ θεοῦ εἰς μετάνοιάν σε ἄγει;

      Or do you despise the riches of his goodness, forbearance, and patience, not knowing that the goodness of God leads you to repentance?

      Read Romans 2:4 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 6:6

      ἐν ἁγνότητι, ἐν γνώσει, ἐν μακροθυμίᾳ, ἐν χρηστότητι, ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, ἐν ἀγάπῃ ἀνυποκρίτῳ,

      in pureness, in knowledge, in perseverance, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in sincere love,

      Read 2 Corinthians 6:6 in the app

    • Ephesians 2:7

      ἵνα ἐνδείξηται ἐν τοῖς αἰῶσιν τοῖς ἐπερχομένοις τὸ ὑπερβάλλον πλοῦτος τῆς χάριτος αὐτοῦ ἐν χρηστότητι ἐφ’ ἡμᾶς ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.

      that in the ages to come he might show the exceeding riches of his grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus;

      Read Ephesians 2:7 in the app

  2. good (doing good) (1)
    Good, the doing of good: “no one does good” (Rom 3:12, quoting Ps 14:3).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 3:12

      πάντες ἐξέκλιναν, ἅμα ἠχρεώθησαν· οὐκ ἔστιν ποιῶν χρηστότητα, οὐκ ἔστιν ἕως ἑνός.

      They have all turned away. They have together become unprofitable. There is no one who does good, no, not so much as one.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 χρηστότης, -ητος, ἡ (< χρηστός), [in LXX for מוֺב and cogn. forms ;] goodness, excellence, uprightness: Ro 3:12 (LXX). goodness of heart, kindness: Ro 2:4, II Co 6:6, Ga 5:22, Col 3:12, Tit 3:4; seq. ἐπί, c. acc. pers., Eph 2:7; id., opp. to ἀποτομία, Ro 11:22. SYN.: v.s. ἀγαθωσύνη.
