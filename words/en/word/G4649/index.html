Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 σκοπός, -οῦ, ὁ [in LXX chiefly for צֹפֶה ;] a watcher, watchman (Ez 3:17, al.). a mark on which to fix the eye (Wi 5:12, 21, al.). Metaph., of an aim or object: Phl 3:14.†
