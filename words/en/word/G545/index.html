Senses (NGSD)

  1. disobedient (6)
    Disobedient, not obeying: “disobedient”, “I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision”, “to turn the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous”, “disobedient to parents”, “being disobedient”, “the disobedient”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:17

      καὶ αὐτὸς προελεύσεται ἐνώπιον αὐτοῦ ἐν πνεύματι καὶ δυνάμει Ἠλίου, ἐπιστρέψαι καρδίας πατέρων ἐπὶ τέκνα καὶ ἀπειθεῖς ἐν φρονήσει δικαίων, ἑτοιμάσαι κυρίῳ λαὸν κατεσκευασμένον.

      He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, ‘to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,’ and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to prepare a people prepared for the Lord.”
