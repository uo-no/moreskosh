Senses (NGSD)

  1. the Baptist / the one who baptizes (title) (12)
    “The one who baptizes”, used as a title attached to John: “John the Baptist”, “John the Baptist preached in the wilderness”, “the head of John the Baptist”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 3:1

      Ἐν δὲ ταῖς ἡμέραις ἐκείναις παραγίνεται Ἰωάννης ὁ βαπτιστὴς κηρύσσων ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ τῆς Ἰουδαίας

      In those days, John the Baptizer came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea, saying,
