Senses (NGSD)

  1. oath (10)
    An oath, a vow: “with an oath”, “by an oath”, “do not swear”, “perform your oaths to the Lord”, “the king, because of his oaths”, “Peter with an oath”, “the oath he swore to Abraham”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “Again you have heard that it was said to the ancient ones, ‘You shall not make false vows, but shall perform to the Lord your vows,’
