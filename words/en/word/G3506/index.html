Senses (NGSD)

  1. to signal (2)
    To make a sign, to nod: “motioned to him”, “gave a sign”. The representative examples in the LXX are the use “to incline”, and the use of making a sign has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 13:24

      νεύει οὖν τούτῳ Σίμων Πέτρος πυθέσθαι τίς ἂν εἴη περὶ οὗ λέγει.

      Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him, and said to him, “Tell us who it is of whom he speaks.”
