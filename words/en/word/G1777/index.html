Senses (NGSD)

  1. liable / guilty (9)
    Guilty, deserving judgment, answerable: “will be guilty of sinning against the body and blood of the Lord”, “whoever fails in one point has become guilty of all”, “will be liable to judgment”, “guilty of sin in the temple”, “is guilty of an eternal sin”, “deserves death”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:21

      Ἠκούσατε ὅτι ἐρρέθη τοῖς ἀρχαίοις· Οὐ φονεύσεις· ὃς δ’ ἂν φονεύσῃ, ἔνοχος ἔσται τῇ κρίσει.

      “You have heard that it was said to the ancient ones, ‘You shall not murder;’ and ‘Whoever murders will be in danger of the judgment.’

      Read Matthew 5:21 in the app

    • Mark 3:29

      ὃς δ’ ἂν βλασφημήσῃ εἰς τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον, οὐκ ἔχει ἄφεσιν εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, ἀλλὰ ἔνοχός ἐστιν αἰωνίου ἁμαρτήματος.

      but whoever may blaspheme against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is subject to eternal condemnation.”

      Read Mark 3:29 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 11:27

      Ὥστε ὃς ἂν ἐσθίῃ τὸν ἄρτον ἢ πίνῃ τὸ ποτήριον τοῦ κυρίου ἀναξίως, ἔνοχος ἔσται τοῦ σώματος καὶ τοῦ αἵματος τοῦ κυρίου.

      Therefore whoever eats this bread or drinks the Lord’s cup in a way unworthy of the Lord will be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.

      Read 1 Corinthians 11:27 in the app

  2. bound / held captive (1)
    Held captive, bound: “those who were subject to slavery all their lives through fear of death” (Heb 2:15).
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 2:15

      καὶ ἀπαλλάξῃ τούτους, ὅσοι φόβῳ θανάτου διὰ παντὸς τοῦ ζῆν ἔνοχοι ἦσαν δουλείας.

      and might deliver all of them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 ἔνοχος, -ον (= ἐνεχόμενος), (in LXX for רָשַׁע hi., etc. ;] held in, bound by: c. gen. (cl. c. dat.), δουλείας, He 2:15. In law-phrases liable to a charge or action (cl. c. dat., of crime): c. dat., of the tribunal (MM, Exp., xiii), Mt 5:21, 22; seq. εἰς (Field, Notes, 4 f.), Mt 5:22 c. gen., of the punishment (Ge 26:11): θανάτου, Mt 26:66, Mk 14:64; c. gen. (cl. c. dat„ rarely c. prep.; MM, Exp., xiii), of the crime (II Mac 13:6): Mk 3:29; c. gen., of the thing injured, guilty (absol., in cl.): I Co 11:27, Ja 2:10 (cf. Is 54:17; DB, ii, 268a).†
