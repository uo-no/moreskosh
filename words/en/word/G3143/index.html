Senses (NGSD)

  1. to testify solemnly, to charge (5)
    To bear solemn witness, to declare emphatically: “we encouraged, comforted and urged you”, “I testify to you today”, “testifying”, “I urge in the Lord”, “I … testify”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 20:26

      διότι μαρτύρομαι ὑμῖν ἐν τῇ σήμερον ἡμέρᾳ ὅτι καθαρός εἰμι ἀπὸ τοῦ αἵματος πάντων,

      Therefore I testify to you today that I am clean from the blood of all men,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** μαρτύρομαι (< μάρτυρ), [in LXX: Jth 7:28, I Mac 2:56 א)*;] to summon as witness (M, Th., 25 f.; Hort., 1Pe., 53 f.; Lft., Notes, 29; Ga 203), hence, to protest, affirm solemnly: seq. ὅτι, Ac 20:26, Ga 5:3; to adjure, beseech: c. dat. pers., Ac 26:22; c. acc. et inf., Eph 4:17; seq. εἰς, I Th 2:12 (cf. δια-, προ-μαρτύρομαι).†
