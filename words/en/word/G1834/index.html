Senses (NGSD)

  1. to relate, to describe in detail (5)
    To tell of events in detail, to report: “explaining everything”, “relating the signs and wonders God had done among the Gentiles”, “told how God had visited the Gentiles”, “described … what God had done among them”, “told what had happened on the road”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 24:35

      καὶ αὐτοὶ ἐξηγοῦντο τὰ ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ καὶ ὡς ἐγνώσθη αὐτοῖς ἐν τῇ κλάσει τοῦ ἄρτου.

      They related the things that happened along the way, and how he was recognized by them in the breaking of the bread.

      Read Luke 24:35 in the app

    • Acts 10:8

      καὶ ἐξηγησάμενος ἅπαντα αὐτοῖς ἀπέστειλεν αὐτοὺς εἰς τὴν Ἰόππην.

      Having explained everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.

      Read Acts 10:8 in the app

    • Acts 15:14

      Συμεὼν ἐξηγήσατο καθὼς πρῶτον ὁ θεὸς ἐπεσκέψατο λαβεῖν ἐξ ἐθνῶν λαὸν τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ.

      Simeon has reported how God first visited the nations to take out of them a people for his name.

      Read Acts 15:14 in the app

  2. to explain, to make known (1)
    To make God known, to reveal: “the only God, who is at the Father’s side, has made God known”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 1:18

      θεὸν οὐδεὶς ἑώρακεν πώποτε· μονογενὴς θεὸς ὁ ὢν εἰς τὸν κόλπον τοῦ πατρὸς ἐκεῖνος ἐξηγήσατο.

      No one has seen God at any time. The only born Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared him.

      Read John 1:18 in the app
