Senses (NGSD)

  1. island (9)
    An island: “went through the whole island of Cyprus”, “the island was called Malta”, “Publius, the chief man of the island”, “the people of the island”, “a ship that had wintered in the island”, “run aground on some island”, “the island called Patmos”, “every island fled away”, “the islands were moved”.
