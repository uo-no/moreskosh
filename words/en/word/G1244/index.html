Senses (NGSD)

  1. to divide (2)
    To divide, to distribute: “are distributed”, “divided his property between them”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 15:12

      καὶ εἶπεν ὁ νεώτερος αὐτῶν τῷ πατρί· Πάτερ, δός μοι τὸ ἐπιβάλλον μέρος τῆς οὐσίας· ὁ δὲ διεῖλεν αὐτοῖς τὸν βίον.

      The younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me my share of your property.’ So he divided his livelihood between them.

    • 1 Corinthians 12:11

      πάντα δὲ ταῦτα ἐνεργεῖ τὸ ἓν καὶ τὸ αὐτὸ πνεῦμα, διαιροῦν ἰδίᾳ ἑκάστῳ καθὼς βούλεται.

      But the one and the same Spirit produces all of these, distributing to each one separately as he desires.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 δι-αιρέω, -ῶ [in LXX for בּתר (as Ge 15:10), חלק (as Jos 18:5), etc. ;] to divide into parts, cut asunder. to distribute: c. acc. rei, dat. pers., Lk 15:12, I Co 12:11.†
