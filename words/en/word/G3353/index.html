Senses (NGSD)

  1. partaker, sharer (4)
    One who shares in something together: “holy brothers who share in a heavenly calling”, “in the discipline that all have received”, “become partakers of the Holy Spirit”, “those who share in Christ”, “above your companions”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Hebrews 3:1

      Ὅθεν, ἀδελφοὶ ἅγιοι, κλήσεως ἐπουρανίου μέτοχοι, κατανοήσατε τὸν ἀπόστολον καὶ ἀρχιερέα τῆς ὁμολογίας ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦν,

      Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession: Jesus,

      For we have become partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence firm to the end,

      Read Hebrews 3:14 in the app

      Ἀδύνατον γὰρ τοὺς ἅπαξ φωτισθέντας γευσαμένους τε τῆς δωρεᾶς τῆς ἐπουρανίου καὶ μετόχους γενηθέντας πνεύματος ἁγίου

      For concerning those who were once enlightened and tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Spirit,

  2. companion, partner (2)
    A companion, a partner in business: “their partners in the other boat”, “the oil of gladness beyond your companions”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 5:7

      καὶ κατένευσαν τοῖς μετόχοις ἐν τῷ ἑτέρῳ πλοίῳ τοῦ ἐλθόντας συλλαβέσθαι αὐτοῖς· καὶ ἦλθον, καὶ ἔπλησαν ἀμφότερα τὰ πλοῖα ὥστε βυθίζεσθαι αὐτά.

      They beckoned to their partners in the other boat, that they should come and help them. They came and filled both boats, so that they began to sink.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 μέτοχος, -ον (< μετέχω), [in LXX chiefly for חָבֵר;] sharing in, partaking of: c. gen. rei, He 3:1 6:4 12:8; τ. Χριστοῦ, He 3:14. As subst., ὁ μ., a partner, associate: Lk 5:7, He 1:9(LXX).
