Senses (NGSD)

  1. to surpass, to be better (3)
    To excel, to be superior: “the surpassing worth of knowing”, “the peace of God that surpasses all understanding”, “count one another as better than yourselves”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Philippians 2:3

      μηδὲν κατ’ ἐριθείαν μηδὲ κατὰ κενοδοξίαν, ἀλλὰ τῇ ταπεινοφροσύνῃ ἀλλήλους ἡγούμενοι ὑπερέχοντας ἑαυτῶν,

      doing nothing through rivalry or through conceit, but in humility, each counting others better than himself;

      Read Philippians 2:3 in the app

    • Philippians 3:8

      ἀλλὰ μενοῦνγε καὶ ἡγοῦμαι πάντα ζημίαν εἶναι διὰ τὸ ὑπερέχον τῆς γνώσεως Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ κυρίου μου δι’ ὃν τὰ πάντα ἐζημιώθην, καὶ ἡγοῦμαι σκύβαλα ἵνα Χριστὸν κερδήσω

      Yes most certainly, and I count all things to be a loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus, my Lord, for whom I suffered the loss of all things, and count them nothing but refuse, that I may gain Christ

      Read Philippians 3:8 in the app

    • Philippians 4:7

      καὶ ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ θεοῦ ἡ ὑπερέχουσα πάντα νοῦν φρουρήσει τὰς καρδίας ὑμῶν καὶ τὰ νοήματα ὑμῶν ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.

      And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

      Read Philippians 4:7 in the app

  2. to be over, to govern (2)
    To be in a higher position, to rule: “the king as supreme authority”, “the governing authorities”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 13:1

      Πᾶσα ψυχὴ ἐξουσίαις ὑπερεχούσαις ὑποτασσέσθω, οὐ γὰρ ἔστιν ἐξουσία εἰ μὴ ὑπὸ θεοῦ, αἱ δὲ οὖσαι ὑπὸ θεοῦ τεταγμέναι εἰσίν.

      Let every soul be in subjection to the higher authorities, for there is no authority except from God, and those who exist are ordained by God.

      Read Romans 13:1 in the app

    • 1 Peter 2:13

      Ὑποτάγητε πάσῃ ἀνθρωπίνῃ κτίσει διὰ τὸν κύριον· εἴτε βασιλεῖ ὡς ὑπερέχοντι,

      Therefore subject yourselves to every ordinance of man for the Lord’s sake: whether to the king, as supreme,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ὑπερ-έχω [in LXX: Ge 25:23 (אָמַץ), Ex 26:13 (עֲדַף), Si 36:7, al. ;] trans., to hold over or above. Intrans. (when a noun follows, the case is governed by the prep.; v. Bl., § 34, 1; 36, 8), to rise above, overtop; metaph., to be superior in rank, etc.: Ro 13:1, I Pe 2:13 (cf. Wi 6:6); to be superior, excel, surpass: c. gen. (cl.; v. supr.), Phl 2:3; c. acc (cl.; v. supr.), Phl 4:7; as subst., τ. ὑπερέχον, the excel­lency, the surpassing worth, Phl 3:8.†
