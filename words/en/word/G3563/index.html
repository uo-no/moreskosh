Senses (NGSD)

  1. mind / faculty of thought (intellect) (17)
    The seat and capacity of a person's thinking, judging, understanding, and directing. It may be contrasted with spirit, flesh, tongue, etc., or spoken of as the object of corruption or renewal.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 24:45

      τότε διήνοιξεν αὐτῶν τὸν νοῦν τοῦ συνιέναι τὰς γραφάς,

      Then he opened their minds, that they might understand the Scriptures.

      Read Luke 24:45 in the app

    • Romans 14:5

      Ὃς μὲν κρίνει ἡμέραν παρ’ ἡμέραν, ὃς δὲ κρίνει πᾶσαν ἡμέραν· ἕκαστος ἐν τῷ ἰδίῳ νοῒ πληροφορείσθω·

      One man esteems one day as more important. Another esteems every day alike. Let each man be fully assured in his own mind.

      Read Romans 14:5 in the app

    • Ephesians 4:23

      ἀνανεοῦσθαι δὲ τῷ πνεύματι τοῦ νοὸς ὑμῶν,

      and that you be renewed in the spirit of your mind,

      Read Ephesians 4:23 in the app

  2. understanding / insight (the power that applies understanding) (3)
    The power of understanding that sees into things. Here it is placed alongside “wisdom” or stated as surpassing human knowledge.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Philippians 4:7

      καὶ ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ θεοῦ ἡ ὑπερέχουσα πάντα νοῦν φρουρήσει τὰς καρδίας ὑμῶν καὶ τὰ νοήματα ὑμῶν ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.

      And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.

      Read Philippians 4:7 in the app

    • Revelation 13:18

      ὧδε ἡ σοφία ἐστίν· ὁ ἔχων νοῦν ψηφισάτω τὸν ἀριθμὸν τοῦ θηρίου, ἀριθμὸς γὰρ ἀνθρώπου ἐστίν· καὶ ὁ ἀριθμὸς αὐτοῦ ἑξακόσιοι ἑξήκοντα ἕξ.

      Here is wisdom. He who has understanding, let him calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man. His number is six hundred sixty-six.

      Read Revelation 13:18 in the app

    • Revelation 17:9

      Ὧδε ὁ νοῦς ὁ ἔχων σοφίαν. αἱ ἑπτὰ κεφαλαὶ ἑπτὰ ὄρη εἰσίν, ὅπου ἡ γυνὴ κάθηται ἐπ’ αὐτῶν. καὶ βασιλεῖς ἑπτά εἰσιν·

      Here is the mind that has wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman sits.

      Read Revelation 17:9 in the app

  3. thought / mind (as in “the mind of the Lord” in quotations) (4)
    Someone's thought, purpose, or intention. A use in which it is named as someone's “mind”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 11:34

      Τίς γὰρ ἔγνω νοῦν κυρίου; ἢ τίς σύμβουλος αὐτοῦ ἐγένετο;

      “For who has known the mind of the Lord? Or who has been his counselor?”

      Read Romans 11:34 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 1:10

      Παρακαλῶ δὲ ὑμᾶς, ἀδελφοί, διὰ τοῦ ὀνόματος τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ ἵνα τὸ αὐτὸ λέγητε πάντες, καὶ μὴ ᾖ ἐν ὑμῖν σχίσματα, ἦτε δὲ κατηρτισμένοι ἐν τῷ αὐτῷ νοῒ καὶ ἐν τῇ αὐτῇ γνώμῃ.

      Now I beg you, brothers, through the name of our Lord, Jesus Christ, that you all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be perfected together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

      Read 1 Corinthians 1:10 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 2:16

      τίς γὰρ ἔγνω νοῦν κυρίου, ὃς συμβιβάσει αὐτόν; ἡμεῖς δὲ νοῦν Χριστοῦ ἔχομεν.

      “For who has known the mind of the Lord that he should instruct him?” But we have Christ’s mind.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

24 νοῦς (contr. from νόος),ὁ, gen., dat., νοός, νοΐ (late forms, = cl., νοῦ, νῷ; Bl., § 9, 3), acc., νοῦν, [in LXX chiefly for לֵב, לֵבָב;] prop., of the ruling faculty, mind, understanding, reason (v. Lft., Notes, 88 f.; Vaughan on Ro 7:23): Lk 24:45, Ro 1:28 7:23 12:2 14:5, Eph 4:17, 23, Phl 4:7, II Th 2:2, I Ti 6:5, II Ti 3:8, Tit 1:15, Re 13:18 17:9; ν. τ. σαρκός (ICC, in l.), Col 2:18; opp. to σάρξ, Ro 7:25; to πνεῦμα, I Co 14:14, 15; to γλῶσσα, I Co 14:19. By meton., of an act of mind, a mind, thought, purpose: Ro 11:34 = I Co 2:16 (LXX), I Co 1:10.† SYN.: v.s. πνεῦμα.
