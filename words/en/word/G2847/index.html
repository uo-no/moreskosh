Senses (NGSD)

  1. scarlet (6)
    Scarlet, crimson: “scarlet wool”, “a scarlet cloak”, “a scarlet beast”, “clothed in purple and scarlet”, “purple and scarlet cloth”, “scarlet linen”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers
