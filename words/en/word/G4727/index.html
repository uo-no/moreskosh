Senses (NGSD)

  1. to groan, to complain (6)
    To groan, to sigh, to voice complaint: “in this tent we groan”, “we groan, being burdened”, “let them do this without groaning”, “do not grumble against one another”, “looking up to heaven, he sighed deeply”, “we groan inwardly”. The ‘grumbling’ of James 5:9 and the ‘groaning’ of Hebrews 13:17 are not made into separate senses.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 7:34

      καὶ ἀναβλέψας εἰς τὸν οὐρανὸν ἐστέναξεν, καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ· Εφφαθα, ὅ ἐστιν Διανοίχθητι·

      Looking up to heaven, he sighed, and said to him, “Ephphatha!” that is, “Be opened!”
