Senses (NGSD)

  1. majesty, greatness (3)
    Greatness, majesty: “eyewitnesses of his majesty”, “great magnificence”, “at the greatness of God”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      They were all astonished at the majesty of God. But while all were marveling at all the things which Jesus did, he said to his disciples,
