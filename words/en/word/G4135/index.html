Senses (NGSD)

  1. to fulfill, to accomplish (3)
    To carry a matter through, to accomplish it fully: “fulfil your ministry”, “so that the proclamation might be fully carried out”, “the things that have been accomplished among us”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 1:1

      Ἐπειδήπερ πολλοὶ ἐπεχείρησαν ἀνατάξασθαι διήγησιν περὶ τῶν πεπληροφορημένων ἐν ἡμῖν πραγμάτων,

      Since many have undertaken to set in order a narrative concerning those matters which have been fulfilled among us,

      Read Luke 1:1 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 4:5

      σὺ δὲ νῆφε ἐν πᾶσιν, κακοπάθησον, ἔργον ποίησον εὐαγγελιστοῦ, τὴν διακονίαν σου πληροφόρησον.

      But you be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, and fulfill your ministry.

      Read 2 Timothy 4:5 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 4:17

      ὁ δὲ κύριός μοι παρέστη καὶ ἐνεδυνάμωσέν με, ἵνα δι’ ἐμοῦ τὸ κήρυγμα πληροφορηθῇ καὶ ἀκούσωσιν πάντα τὰ ἔθνη, καὶ ἐρρύσθην ἐκ στόματος λέοντος.

      But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that through me the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear. So I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.

      Read 2 Timothy 4:17 in the app

  2. to be fully convinced (3)
    To be convinced, to be fully persuaded (passive): “being fully convinced”, “let each one be fully convinced in his own mind”, “that you may stand fully assured”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 4:21

      καὶ πληροφορηθεὶς ὅτι ὃ ἐπήγγελται δυνατός ἐστιν καὶ ποιῆσαι.

      and being fully assured that what he had promised, he was also able to perform.

      Read Romans 4:21 in the app

    • Romans 14:5

      Ὃς μὲν κρίνει ἡμέραν παρ’ ἡμέραν, ὃς δὲ κρίνει πᾶσαν ἡμέραν· ἕκαστος ἐν τῷ ἰδίῳ νοῒ πληροφορείσθω·

      One man esteems one day as more important. Another esteems every day alike. Let each man be fully assured in his own mind.

      Read Romans 14:5 in the app

    • Colossians 4:12

      ἀσπάζεται ὑμᾶς Ἐπαφρᾶς ὁ ἐξ ὑμῶν, δοῦλος Χριστοῦ, πάντοτε ἀγωνιζόμενος ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν ἐν ταῖς προσευχαῖς, ἵνα σταθῆτε τέλειοι καὶ πεπληροφορημένοι ἐν παντὶ θελήματι τοῦ θεοῦ.

      Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, salutes you, always striving for you in his prayers, that you may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.

      Read Colossians 4:12 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 † πληροφορέω, -ῶ, [in LXX: Ec 8:11 (מָלֵא)*;] to bring in full measure, hence, to fulfil, accomplish: Lk 1:1, II Ti 4:5, 17. to persuade, assure or satisfy fully (so in π.; v. Deiss., LAE, 82 f.; M, Th., 9): pass., Ro 4:21 14:5, Col 4:12 (v. Lft., in l.). to fill: Ro 15:13, L, mg. (Cl. Ro., I Co 54); metaph., pass., to be filled with, hence, fully bent on (Ec 8:11).†
