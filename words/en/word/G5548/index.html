Senses (NGSD)

  1. to anoint (5)
    To anoint with oil, to anoint for an office: “it is God who has anointed us”, “God anointed Jesus of Nazareth”, “your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed”, “has anointed you”, “he has anointed me”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 χρίω to anoint (Hom., al.); [in LXX chiefly for משׁח of consecration to a sacred office: priest, Ex 28:41; prophet, III Ki 19:16; king, I Ki 10:1; of things, Ex 40:9, Le 8:10, al.]. In NT, metaph., of God's anointing, Christ: Ac 4:27; c. inf., Lk 4:18 (LXX); c. dupl. acc (v. Bl., § 34, 4), He 1:9 (LXX); πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, Ac 10:38; Christians: II Co 1:21 (cf. Westc., Epp. Jo., 73) (cf. ἐν-, ἐπι-Χριω).† SYN: v.s. ἀλείφω
