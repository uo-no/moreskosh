Senses (NGSD)

  1. winepress (5)
    A winepress where grapes are trodden (including figurative uses): “dug a winepress in it”, “the great winepress of God’s wrath”, “the winepress was trodden outside the city”, “the winepress of the fierce wrath of God the Almighty”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “Hear another parable. There was a man who was a master of a household who planted a vineyard, set a hedge about it, dug a wine press in it, built a tower, leased it out to farmers, and went into another country.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ληνός, -οῦ, ἡ (in some MSS., LXX and NT, ὁ), [in LXX chiefly for יֶקֶב, Nu 18:27, al.; also for רַהַט (Ge 30:38, 41), גַּת (Ne 13:15, al.), etc.;] a trough or vat; esp. for the treading of grapes: Mt 21:33, Re 14:20 19:16; τὴν λ. . . . τὸν μέγαν (a solecism perhaps inadvertent), Re 14:19 (cf. ὑπολήνιον).†
