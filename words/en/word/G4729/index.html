Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be restricted (2)
    To be narrowed, to be cramped: “you are cramped in your own hearts”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 6:12

      οὐ στενοχωρεῖσθε ἐν ἡμῖν, στενοχωρεῖσθε δὲ ἐν τοῖς σπλάγχνοις ὑμῶν·

      You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted by your own affections.

      Read 2 Corinthians 6:12 in the app

  2. to be hard pressed (1)
    To be driven into straits, to be distressed: “hard pressed on every side but not crushed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 4:8

      ἐν παντὶ θλιβόμενοι ἀλλ’ οὐ στενοχωρούμενοι, ἀπορούμενοι ἀλλ’ οὐκ ἐξαπορούμενοι,

      We are pressed on every side, yet not crushed; perplexed, yet not to despair;

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 στενο-χωρέω, -ῶ (< στενός + χῶρος, space) [in LXX: Jos 17:15 (אוּץ), Jg 16:16 (אלץ pi.), Is 28:19 (20) (קצר) Is 49:19 (צרר), IV Mac 11:11 * ;] to be straitened (cf. Is 49:19); metaph., to be anxious (Hipp., al.). In late writers, trans., to straiten, compress (LXX, Diod., al.; π. ap. MM, xxiii): pass., trop., II Co 4:8 6:12.†
