Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be perplexed, to be at a loss (6)
    To be puzzled, at a loss, unable to decide: “perplexed, but not driven to despair”, “being at a loss how to investigate this matter”, “I am perplexed about you”, “not knowing of whom he spoke”, “while they were perplexed about this”, “Herod … was greatly perplexed, and yet”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 6:20

      ὁ γὰρ Ἡρῴδης ἐφοβεῖτο τὸν Ἰωάννην, εἰδὼς αὐτὸν ἄνδρα δίκαιον καὶ ἅγιον, καὶ συνετήρει αὐτόν, καὶ ἀκούσας αὐτοῦ πολλὰ ἠπόρει, καὶ ἡδέως αὐτοῦ ἤκουεν.

      for Herod feared John, knowing that he was a righteous and holy man, and kept him safe. When he heard him, he did many things, and he heard him gladly.
