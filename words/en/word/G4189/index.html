Senses (NGSD)

  1. evil, wickedness (7)
    Wickedness, malice, evil: “the leaven of malice and wickedness”, “turning you from your wickedness”, “the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places”, “inside you are full of greed and wickedness”, “knowing their malice”, “evil deeds, malice …”, “all unrighteousness, evil, greed …”.
