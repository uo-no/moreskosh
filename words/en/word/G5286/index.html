Senses (NGSD)

  1. footstool (7)
    A stool for the feet, a footstool: “until I make your enemies your footstool”, “the earth is my footstool”, “footstool”, “until I make your enemies your footstool”. (Includes both the literal use and the figurative use of enemies or the earth as a footstool, but they are not separated into distinct senses.)
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:35

      μήτε ἐν τῇ γῇ, ὅτι ὑποπόδιόν ἐστιν τῶν ποδῶν αὐτοῦ· μήτε εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα, ὅτι πόλις ἐστὶν τοῦ μεγάλου βασιλέως·

      nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet; nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.
