5

Senses (NGSD)

  1. to turn aside, to turn away (4)
    To turn off the path, to depart, to avoid: “straying from these things”, “have already strayed after Satan”, “avoiding godless chatter”, “will wander off into myths”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Timothy 1:6

      ὧν τινες ἀστοχήσαντες ἐξετράπησαν εἰς ματαιολογίαν,

      from which things some, having missed the mark, have turned away to vain talking,

      Read 1 Timothy 1:6 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 5:15

      ἤδη γάρ τινες ἐξετράπησαν ὀπίσω τοῦ Σατανᾶ.

      For already some have turned away after Satan.

      Read 1 Timothy 5:15 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 6:20

      Ὦ Τιμόθεε, τὴν παραθήκην φύλαξον, ἐκτρεπόμενος τὰς βεβήλους κενοφωνίας καὶ ἀντιθέσεις τῆς ψευδωνύμου γνώσεως,

      Timothy, guard that which is committed to you, turning away from the empty chatter and oppositions of what is falsely called knowledge,

      Read 1 Timothy 6:20 in the app

  2. to be dislocated (1)
    For a joint of the foot to be put out of place: “so that what is lame may not be put out of joint”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 12:13

      καὶ τροχιὰς ὀρθὰς ποιεῖτε τοῖς ποσὶν ὑμῶν, ἵνα μὴ τὸ χωλὸν ἐκτραπῇ, ἰαθῇ δὲ μᾶλλον.

      and make straight paths for your feet, so what is lame may not be dislocated, but rather be healed.

      Read Hebrews 12:13 in the app
