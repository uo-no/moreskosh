Senses (NGSD)

  1. sharp disagreement (1)
    A sharp clash, a heated dispute: “there was a sharp disagreement”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 15:39

      ἐγένετο δὲ παροξυσμὸς ὥστε ἀποχωρισθῆναι αὐτοὺς ἀπ’ ἀλλήλων, τόν τε Βαρναβᾶν παραλαβόντα τὸν Μᾶρκον ἐκπλεῦσαι εἰς Κύπρον,

      Then the contention grew so sharp that they separated from each other. Barnabas took Mark with him and sailed away to Cyprus,

      Read Acts 15:39 in the app

  2. stirring up (1)
    A rousing, a stimulus: “to spur one another on to love and good works”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 10:24

      καὶ κατανοῶμεν ἀλλήλους εἰς παροξυσμὸν ἀγάπης καὶ καλῶν ἔργων,

      Let’s consider how to provoke one another to love and good works,

      Read Hebrews 10:24 in the app
