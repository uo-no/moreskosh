Senses (NGSD)

  1. to advance, to progress (5)
    To go forward, to make progress, to surpass: “they will not get very far”, “evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse”, “will lead to more and more ungodliness”, “I advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own age”, “increased in wisdom”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 2:52

      Καὶ Ἰησοῦς προέκοπτεν σοφίᾳ καὶ ἡλικίᾳ καὶ χάριτι παρὰ θεῷ καὶ ἀνθρώποις.

      And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.

      Read Luke 2:52 in the app

    • Galatians 1:14

      καὶ προέκοπτον ἐν τῷ Ἰουδαϊσμῷ ὑπὲρ πολλοὺς συνηλικιώτας ἐν τῷ γένει μου, περισσοτέρως ζηλωτὴς ὑπάρχων τῶν πατρικῶν μου παραδόσεων.

      I advanced in the Jews’ religion beyond many of my own age among my countrymen, being more exceedingly zealous for the traditions of my fathers.

      Read Galatians 1:14 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 3:9

      ἀλλ’ οὐ προκόψουσιν ἐπὶ πλεῖον, ἡ γὰρ ἄνοια αὐτῶν ἔκδηλος ἔσται πᾶσιν, ὡς καὶ ἡ ἐκείνων ἐγένετο.

      But they will proceed no further. For their folly will be evident to all men, as theirs also came to be.

      Read 2 Timothy 3:9 in the app

  2. (of time) to advance (1)
    For the night to wear on, for time to advance: “the night is far gone; the day is near”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 13:12

      ἡ νὺξ προέκοψεν, ἡ δὲ ἡμέρα ἤγγικεν. ἀποβαλώμεθα οὖν τὰ ἔργα τοῦ σκότους, ἐνδυσώμεθα δὲ τὰ ὅπλα τοῦ φωτός.

      The night is far gone, and the day is near. Let’s therefore throw off the deeds of darkness, and let’s put on the armor of light.

      Read Romans 13:12 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ** προ-κόπτω, [in Sm.: Ps 44 (45):5*;] to cut forward a way, forward, advance, in cl. trans. with neut. adj., as οὐδὲν π., τὰ πολλὰ π. In late writers (Polyb., al.), wholly intrans., to advance, progress: of time, Ro 13:12. Metaph., Lk 2:52, Ga 1:14, II Ti 2:16 3:9, 13.†
