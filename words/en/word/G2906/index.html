Senses (NGSD)

  1. cry, shout (6)
    A cry, a loud shout, an outcry: “the clamour grew loud”, “clamour and slander”, “with loud cries and tears”, “crying out with a loud voice”, “at midnight there was a cry”, “there will be no more crying”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      But at midnight there was a cry, ‘Behold! The bridegroom is coming! Come out to meet him!’
