Senses (NGSD)

  1. to pervert, to turn away (active) (3)
    To distort a person or thing, to turn someone away: “trying to turn the governor away from the faith”, “making crooked the straight paths of the Lord”, “leading the people astray”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 23:2

      ἤρξαντο δὲ κατηγορεῖν αὐτοῦ λέγοντες· Τοῦτον εὕραμεν διαστρέφοντα τὸ ἔθνος ἡμῶν καὶ κωλύοντα φόρους Καίσαρι διδόναι καὶ λέγοντα αὑτὸν χριστὸν βασιλέα εἶναι.

      They began to accuse him, saying, “We found this man perverting the nation, forbidding paying taxes to Caesar, and saying that he himself is Christ, a king.”

      But Elymas the sorcerer (for so is his name by interpretation) withstood them, seeking to turn the proconsul away from the faith.

  2. crooked, perverse (participial adjective) (4)
    Crooked, perverted (of a generation or of words): “O unbelieving and perverse generation”, “a crooked generation”, “speaking perverse things”, “in the midst of a crooked and perverted generation”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 17:17

      ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν· Ὦ γενεὰ ἄπιστος καὶ διεστραμμένη, ἕως πότε μεθ’ ὑμῶν ἔσομαι; ἕως πότε ἀνέξομαι ὑμῶν; φέρετέ μοι αὐτὸν ὧδε.

      Jesus answered, “Faithless and perverse generation! How long will I be with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him here to me.”
