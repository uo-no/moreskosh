      Jesus answered, “Faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you and bear with you? Bring your son here.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 προσ-άγω, [in LXX chiefly for קרב hi., also for נגשׁ etc.;] trans., to bring or lead: c. acc. et dat., Ac 16:20; metaph., τ. θεῷ, I Pe 3:18; seq. ὧδε, Lk 9:41; pass., c. dat., Mt 18:24 (προσηνέχθη, T); in forensic sense, to summon: Ac 12:6 (προαγαγεῖν, T, WH, mg., R). Intrans., to draw near, approach (Jos 3:9, Je 26 (46):3, al.): c. dat., Ac 27:27 (WH, προσαχεῖν).†
