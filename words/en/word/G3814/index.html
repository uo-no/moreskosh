Senses (NGSD)

  1. maidservant / servant girl (8)
    A maid, a servant girl, a girl keeping the door: “a servant girl came out”, “a slave girl with a spirit of divination”, “beat the maidservants”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:69

      Ὁ δὲ Πέτρος ἐκάθητο ἔξω ἐν τῇ αὐλῇ· καὶ προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ μία παιδίσκη λέγουσα· Καὶ σὺ ἦσθα μετὰ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Γαλιλαίου·

      Now Peter was sitting outside in the court, and a maid came to him, saying, “You were also with Jesus, the Galilean!”

      The maid saw him and began again to tell those who stood by, “This is one of them.”

  2. slave woman (Galatians 4) (5)
    A slave woman, a bondwoman: “a son born of the slave woman”, “cast out the slave woman and her son”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      γέγραπται γὰρ ὅτι Ἀβραὰμ δύο υἱοὺς ἔσχεν, ἕνα ἐκ τῆς παιδίσκης καὶ ἕνα ἐκ τῆς ἐλευθέρας·

      For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the servant, and one by the free woman.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

13 παιδίσκη, -ης, ἡ, dimin, of παῖς, [in LXX for שִׁפְחָה, אָמָה, etc.;] a young girl, a maiden (נַעֲרָה, Ru 4:12). Colloq., a young female slave, a maid-servant (v. Kennedy, Sources, 40 f.; Deiss., LAE, 186, 3322): Mt 26:69, Mk 14:66, 69, Lk 12:45 22:56, Jo 18:17, Ac 12:13 16:16; of Hagar, Ga 4:22, 23, 30(LXX); id., metaph., Ga 4:31.† SYN.: v.s. παῖς.
