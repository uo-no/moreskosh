Senses (NGSD)

  1. measure (metretes) (1)
    The metretes, a unit of capacity (about 39 liters): “each holding two or three metretes”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • John 2:6

      ἦσαν δὲ ἐκεῖ λίθιναι ὑδρίαι ἓξ κατὰ τὸν καθαρισμὸν τῶν Ἰουδαίων κείμεναι, χωροῦσαι ἀνὰ μετρητὰς δύο ἢ τρεῖς.

      Now there were six water pots of stone set there after the Jews’ way of purifying, containing two or three metretes apiece.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 μετρητής, -οῦ, ὁ (< μετρέω), [in LXX: III Ki 18:32 (סְאָה), II Ch 4:5, (בַּת), etc.;] a measurer (Plat.). = ἀμφορεύς, an Attic measure, = 1 1⁄2 Roman amphoræ or about 9 Eng. gallons: Jo 2:6.†
