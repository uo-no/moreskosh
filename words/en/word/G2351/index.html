Senses (NGSD)

  1. uproar, tumult (7)
    Uproar, tumult, commotion: “after the uproar had ceased”, “without any disturbance”, “lest there be an uproar”, “seeing that a riot was starting instead”, “lest there be a disturbance”, “saw people weeping and wailing in commotion”, “lest the crowd cause a riot”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 θόρυβος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX for הָמוֹן, etc.;] a noise, uproar, tumult, as of an excited mob: Mt 26:5 27:24, Mk 14:2, Ac 20:1 21:34 24:18; of mourners, = ὄχλος θορυβούμενος, Mk 5:38.†
