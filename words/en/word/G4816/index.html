Senses (NGSD)

  1. to gather / collect (8)
    To gather, pick, or collect: “are grapes picked from thorns?”, “gathering the weeds”, “gather the weeds first”, “the weeds are gathered”, “the angels will gather all causes of sin”, “gathered into containers”, “gather figs”, “grapes from brambles”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 7:16

      ἀπὸ τῶν καρπῶν αὐτῶν ἐπιγνώσεσθε αὐτούς. μήτι συλλέγουσιν ἀπὸ ἀκανθῶν σταφυλὰς ἢ ἀπὸ τριβόλων σῦκα;

      By their fruits you will know them. Do you gather grapes from thorns or figs from thistles?

      ὁ δέ φησιν· Οὔ, μήποτε συλλέγοντες τὰ ζιζάνια ἐκριζώσητε ἅμα αὐτοῖς τὸν σῖτον·

      “But he said, ‘No, lest perhaps while you gather up the darnel weeds, you root up the wheat with them.
