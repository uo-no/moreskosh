Senses (NGSD)

  1. to save (spiritual / eschatological) (62)
    To deliver people from sin and spiritual ruin. Used in any tense: past event (atonement), present experience (walking in grace), future completion (eschatological glory); the central usage of New Testament "salvation."

    • Matthew 1:21

      τέξεται δὲ υἱὸν καὶ καλέσεις τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ Ἰησοῦν, αὐτὸς γὰρ σώσει τὸν λαὸν αὐτοῦ ἀπὸ τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν αὐτῶν.

      She shall give birth to a son. You shall name him Jesus, for it is he who shall save his people from their sins.”

      Read Matthew 1:21 in the app

    • Acts 2:47

      αἰνοῦντες τὸν θεὸν καὶ ἔχοντες χάριν πρὸς ὅλον τὸν λαόν. ὁ δὲ κύριος προσετίθει τοὺς σῳζομένους καθ’ ἡμέραν ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό.

      praising God and having favor with all the people. The Lord added to the assembly day by day those who were being saved.

      Read Acts 2:47 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 9:22

      ἐγενόμην τοῖς ἀσθενέσιν ἀσθενής, ἵνα τοὺς ἀσθενεῖς κερδήσω· τοῖς πᾶσιν γέγονα πάντα, ἵνα πάντως τινὰς σώσω.

      To the weak I became as weak, that I might gain the weak. I have become all things to all men, that I may by all means save some.

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:22 in the app

  2. to rescue (from danger / hardship) (27)
    To rescue people from physical danger, hardship, or death. Refers to deliverance from imminent threats such as storm, drowning, persecution, and murder, and need not carry a spiritual implication.

    • Matthew 8:25

      καὶ προσελθόντες ἤγειραν αὐτὸν λέγοντες· Κύριε, σῶσον, ἀπολλύμεθα.

      The disciples came to him and woke him up, saying, “Save us, Lord! We are dying!”

      Read Matthew 8:25 in the app

    • Mark 8:35

      ὃς γὰρ ἐὰν θέλῃ τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ σῶσαι ἀπολέσει αὐτήν· ὃς δ’ ἂν ἀπολέσει τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ ἕνεκεν ἐμοῦ καὶ τοῦ εὐαγγελίου σώσει αὐτήν.

      For whoever wants to save his life will lose it; and whoever will lose his life for my sake and the sake of the Good News will save it.

      Read Mark 8:35 in the app

    • Luke 23:39

      Εἷς δὲ τῶν κρεμασθέντων κακούργων ἐβλασφήμει αὐτόν λέγων· Οὐχὶ σὺ εἶ ὁ χριστός; σῶσον σεαυτὸν καὶ ἡμᾶς.

      One of the criminals who was hanged insulted him, saying, “If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!”

      Read Luke 23:39 in the app

  3. to heal (from illness / suffering) (17)
    To restore from illness or bodily suffering. Used in scenes of Jesus' miraculous healing, close to modern "to cure."

    • Matthew 9:21

      ἔλεγεν γὰρ ἐν ἑαυτῇ· Ἐὰν μόνον ἅψωμαι τοῦ ἱματίου αὐτοῦ σωθήσομαι.

      for she said within herself, “If I just touch his garment, I will be made well.”

      Read Matthew 9:21 in the app

    • Mark 10:52

      καὶ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ὕπαγε, ἡ πίστις σου σέσωκέν σε. καὶ εὐθὺς ἀνέβλεψεν, καὶ ἠκολούθει αὐτῷ ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ.

      Jesus said to him, “Go your way. Your faith has made you well.” Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus on the way.

      Read Mark 10:52 in the app

    • Luke 18:42

      καὶ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ἀνάβλεψον· ἡ πίστις σου σέσωκέν σε.

      Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight. Your faith has healed you.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

106 σώζω (on the more accurate σῴζω, v. WH, Intr., § 410; Bl., §3, 1-3), [in LXX chiefly for ישׁע hi., also for מלט ni., נצל ni., etc. ;] to save from peril, injury or suffering: Mt 8:25, Mk 13:20, Lk 23:35, al.; τ. ψυχήν, Mt 16:25, al.; seq. ἐκ, Jo 12:27, He 5:7, Ju 5; of healing, restoring to health: Mt 9:22, Mk 5:34, al. In NT, esp. of salvation from spiritual disease and death, in which sense it is "spoken of in Scripture as either (1) past, (2) present, or (3) future, according as redemption, grace, or glory is the point in view. Thus (1) Ro 8:24, Eph 2:5, 8 II Ti 1:9, Tit 3:5; (2) Ac 2:47, I Co 1:18, 15:2, II Co 2:15; (3) Mt 10:22, Ro 13:11, Phl 2:12, He 9:28" (Vau. on Ro 5:9). Seq. ἀπό, Mt 1:21, Ac 2:40, Ro 5:9; ἐκ, Ja 5:20, Ju 23 (cf. Cremer, 532 ff.).
