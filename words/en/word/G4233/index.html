Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 πράκτωρ, -ορος, ὁ (< πράσσω), [in LXX: Is 3:12 (נֹגֵשׂ)*;] (poët.) one who does or accomplishes. In Athens, one who exacts payment, a collector; hence, generally (freq. in π., v. Deiss., BS, 154), a court officer: Lk 12:58.†
