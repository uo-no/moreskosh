Senses (NGSD)

  1. shore, beach (6)
    The seashore, beach: “stood on the beach”, “knelt on the beach and prayed”, “ran the boat ashore on the beach”, “headed for the beach”, “the whole crowd stood on the beach”, “drew it up on the shore”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 13:2

      καὶ συνήχθησαν πρὸς αὐτὸν ὄχλοι πολλοί, ὥστε αὐτὸν εἰς πλοῖον ἐμβάντα καθῆσθαι, καὶ πᾶς ὁ ὄχλος ἐπὶ τὸν αἰγιαλὸν εἱστήκει.

      Great multitudes gathered to him, so that he entered into a boat and sat; and all the multitude stood on the beach.

      But when day had already come, Jesus stood on the beach; yet the disciples didn’t know that it was Jesus.
