Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be convinced (1)
    To be made sure, to believe firmly: “continue in what you have become convinced of”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Timothy 3:14

      σὺ δὲ μένε ἐν οἷς ἔμαθες καὶ ἐπιστώθης, εἰδὼς παρὰ τίνων ἔμαθες,

      But you remain in the things which you have learned and have been assured of, knowing from whom you have learned them.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 πιστόω, -ῶ (< πιστός), [in LXX chiefly for אמן ni.;] to make trustworthy (Thuc., III Ki 1:36), hence, to establish (I Ch 17:14). Pass. and mid., to be assured of: c. acc. rei, II Ti 3:14.†
