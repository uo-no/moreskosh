Senses (NGSD)

  1. to become callous (1)
    To lose feeling, to become insensible: “having lost all feeling”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Ephesians 4:19

      οἵτινες ἀπηλγηκότες ἑαυτοὺς παρέδωκαν τῇ ἀσελγείᾳ εἰς ἐργασίαν ἀκαθαρσίας πάσης ἐν πλεονεξίᾳ.

      They, having become callous, gave themselves up to lust, to work all uncleanness with greediness.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * ἀπ-αλγέω, -ῶ, prop., to cease to feel pain for (Thuc., ii, 61). In late Gk. to despair (Polyb., i, 35, 5); to become callous, reckless (Polyb., xvi, 12, 7; MM, VGT, s.v.): Eph 4:19.†
