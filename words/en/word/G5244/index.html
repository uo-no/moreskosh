Senses (NGSD)

  1. proud, arrogant (5)
    Haughty, arrogant: “God opposes the proud”, “the proud”, “the arrogant”, “he has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts”, “the proud”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:51

      Ἐποίησεν κράτος ἐν βραχίονι αὐτοῦ, διεσκόρπισεν ὑπερηφάνους διανοίᾳ καρδίας αὐτῶν·

      He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.

    • Romans 1:30

      καταλάλους, θεοστυγεῖς, ὑβριστάς, ὑπερηφάνους, ἀλαζόνας, ἐφευρετὰς κακῶν, γονεῦσιν ἀπειθεῖς,

      backbiters, hateful to God, insolent, arrogant, boastful, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

      But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ὑπερήφανος, -ον (< ὑπέρ, φαίνομαι, c. η pleonast., v. Kühner3, I, 189) [in LXX for זֵד, גֵּאֶה, לוּץ, etc. ;] showing oneself above others; in good sense (Plat., al.), pre-eminent, splendid; more freq. in had sense, and so always in Scr., arrogant, haughty, disdainful (v. Westc. Epp. Jo., 65b): Ro 1:30, II Ti 3:2; διανοίᾳ καρδίας, Lk 1:51; opp. to ταπεινός (as in Pr 3:34), Ja 4:6, I Pe 5:5 (LXX).† SYN.: ἀλαζών, ὑβριστής, v. Tr., Syn., § xxix
