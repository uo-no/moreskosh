Senses (NGSD)

  1. to seize / arrest (9)
    To catch, to arrest: “trying to arrest me”, “seized Peter and put him in prison”, “sought to arrest Jesus”, “officers to arrest him”, “the beast was captured”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 7:30

      ἐζήτουν οὖν αὐτὸν πιάσαι, καὶ οὐδεὶς ἐπέβαλεν ἐπ’ αὐτὸν τὴν χεῖρα, ὅτι οὔπω ἐληλύθει ἡ ὥρα αὐτοῦ.

      They sought therefore to take him; but no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come.

    • John 7:44

      τινὲς δὲ ἤθελον ἐξ αὐτῶν πιάσαι αὐτόν, ἀλλ’ οὐδεὶς ἐπέβαλεν ἐπ’ αὐτὸν τὰς χεῖρας.

      Some of them would have arrested him, but no one laid hands on him.

      Read John 7:44 in the app

    • John 11:57

      δεδώκεισαν δὲ οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι ἐντολὰς ἵνα ἐάν τις γνῷ ποῦ ἐστιν μηνύσῃ, ὅπως πιάσωσιν αὐτόν.

      Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had commanded that if anyone knew where he was, he should report it, that they might seize him.

      Read John 11:57 in the app

  2. to take by the hand (1)
    To take hold of the hand and help: “took him by the right hand and raised him up”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 3:7

      καὶ πιάσας αὐτὸν τῆς δεξιᾶς χειρὸς ἤγειρεν αὐτόν· παραχρῆμα δὲ ἐστερεώθησαν αἱ βάσεις αὐτοῦ καὶ τὰ σφυδρά,

      He took him by the right hand and raised him up. Immediately his feet and his ankle bones received strength.

      Read Acts 3:7 in the app

  3. to catch (fish) (2)
    To catch fish: “caught nothing”, “bring some of the fish you have caught”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 21:3

      Simon Peter said to them, “I’m going fishing.” They told him, “We are also coming with you.” They immediately went out and entered into the boat. That night, they caught nothing.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

12 πιάζω (cf. MGr. πιάνω; v. Kennedy, Sources, 155), Doric and late Att. for πιέζω in its later senses; [in LXX: Ca 2:15 (אחז), Si 23:21*;] to lay hold of: Ac 3:7 (Theocr.). to take, capture, apprehend: Jo 7:30, 32, 44 8:20 10:39 11:57 21:3, 10, Ac 12:4, II Co 11:32, Re 19:20 (v. MM, xx).†
