Senses (NGSD)

  1. to muzzle (2)
    To put a muzzle on an animal: “you shall not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Corinthians 9:9

      ἐν γὰρ τῷ Μωϋσέως νόμῳ γέγραπται· Οὐ κημώσεις βοῦν ἀλοῶντα. μὴ τῶν βοῶν μέλει τῷ θεῷ,

      For it is written in the law of Moses, “You shall not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain.” Is it for the oxen that God cares,

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:9 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 5:18

      λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή· Βοῦν ἀλοῶντα οὐ φιμώσεις, καί· Ἄξιος ὁ ἐργάτης τοῦ μισθοῦ αὐτοῦ.

      For the Scripture says, “You shall not muzzle the ox when it treads out the grain.” And, “The laborer is worthy of his wages.”

      Read 1 Timothy 5:18 in the app

  2. to silence / be silent (6)
    To stop the mouth, to silence (passive: to be silent, be still): “be silent”, “Peace! Be still!”, “be still”, “he was speechless”, “had silenced the Sadducees”, “put to silence the ignorance of foolish people”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 22:12

      καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ· Ἑταῖρε, πῶς εἰσῆλθες ὧδε μὴ ἔχων ἔνδυμα γάμου; ὁ δὲ ἐφιμώθη.

      and he said to him, ‘Friend, how did you come in here not wearing wedding clothing?’ He was speechless.

      Read Matthew 22:12 in the app

    • Mark 1:25

      καὶ ἐπετίμησεν αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς λέγων· Φιμώθητι καὶ ἔξελθε ἐξ αὐτοῦ.

      Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be quiet, and come out of him!”

      Read Mark 1:25 in the app

    • Luke 4:35

      καὶ ἐπετίμησεν αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς λέγων· Φιμώθητι καὶ ἔξελθε ἀπ’ αὐτοῦ. καὶ ῥίψαν αὐτὸν τὸ δαιμόνιον εἰς τὸ μέσον ἐξῆλθεν ἀπ’ αὐτοῦ μηδὲν βλάψαν αὐτόν.

      Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent and come out of him!” When the demon had thrown him down in the middle of them, he came out of him, having done him no harm.

      Read Luke 4:35 in the app
