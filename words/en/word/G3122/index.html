Senses (NGSD)

  1. especially / above all (12)
    Especially, particularly, above all: “especially of those who believe”, “especially for members of one's household”, “especially those who labour in preaching and teaching”, “especially those who follow the desires of the flesh”, “especially the parchments”, “especially to me”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 20:38

      ὀδυνώμενοι μάλιστα ἐπὶ τῷ λόγῳ ᾧ εἰρήκει ὅτι οὐκέτι μέλλουσιν τὸ πρόσωπον αὐτοῦ θεωρεῖν. προέπεμπον δὲ αὐτὸν εἰς τὸ πλοῖον.

      sorrowing most of all because of the word which he had spoken, that they should see his face no more. Then they accompanied him to the ship.

      All the saints greet you, especially those who are of Caesar’s household.
