Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be governor (2)
    To be governor, to rule: “while he was governor” (twice).

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 * ἡγεμονεύω (< ἡγεμών), to lead the way. to lead in war, command (cf. Ramsay, Was Christ born at Bethlehem?; DCG, ii, 463 f.). to be governor of a province: c. gen. loc., Lk 2:2 3:1.†
