Senses (NGSD)

  1. gift (11)
    A gift, a present: “his inexpressible gift”, “the gift God gave”, “the gift of the Holy Spirit”, “thought the gift of God could be bought with money”, “the gift of this grace”, “if you knew the gift of God”, “the gift of righteousness”, “tasted the heavenly gift”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 4:10

      ἀπεκρίθη Ἰησοῦς καὶ εἶπεν αὐτῇ· Εἰ ᾔδεις τὴν δωρεὰν τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ τίς ἐστιν ὁ λέγων σοι· Δός μοι πεῖν, σὺ ἂν ᾔτησας αὐτὸν καὶ ἔδωκεν ἄν σοι ὕδωρ ζῶν.

      Jesus answered her, “If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who says to you, ‘Give me a drink,’ you would have asked him, and he would have given you living water.”
