Senses (NGSD)

  1. gold (12)
    Gold, gold ornaments, gold coin: “silver and gold”, “gold tested by fire”, “adornment of gold”, “I coveted no one's gold”, “I have no silver or gold”, “the city was pure gold, clear as glass”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers
