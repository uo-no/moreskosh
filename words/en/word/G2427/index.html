Senses (NGSD)

  1. to qualify (2)
    To qualify, to make sufficient: “has made us competent as ministers”, “has qualified you”. The representative examples in the LXX are the use “to be enough”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 3:6

      ὃς καὶ ἱκάνωσεν ἡμᾶς διακόνους καινῆς διαθήκης, οὐ γράμματος ἀλλὰ πνεύματος, τὸ γὰρ γράμμα ἀποκτέννει, τὸ δὲ πνεῦμα ζῳοποιεῖ.

      who also made us sufficient as servants of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.

      Read 2 Corinthians 3:6 in the app

    • Colossians 1:12

      εὐχαριστοῦντες τῷ πατρὶ τῷ ἱκανώσαντι ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν μερίδα τοῦ κλήρου τῶν ἁγίων ἐν τῷ φωτί,

      giving thanks to the Father, who made us fit to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light,
