Senses (NGSD)

  1. share, portion (4)
    A share, the portion received: “what share has a believer with an unbeliever”, “you have neither part nor lot in this matter”, “the inheritance of the saints in the light”, “has chosen the good portion”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 10:42

      ὀλίγων δέ ἐστιν χρεία ἢ ἑνός· Μαριὰμ γὰρ τὴν ἀγαθὴν μερίδα ἐξελέξατο ἥτις οὐκ ἀφαιρεθήσεται αὐτῆς.

      but one thing is needed. Mary has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her.”

      Read Luke 10:42 in the app

    • Acts 8:21

      οὐκ ἔστιν σοι μερὶς οὐδὲ κλῆρος ἐν τῷ λόγῳ τούτῳ, ἡ γὰρ καρδία σου οὐκ ἔστιν εὐθεῖα ἔναντι τοῦ θεοῦ.

      You have neither part nor lot in this matter, for your heart isn’t right before God.

      Read Acts 8:21 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 6:15

      τίς δὲ συμφώνησις Χριστοῦ πρὸς Βελιάρ, ἢ τίς μερὶς πιστῷ μετὰ ἀπίστου;

      What agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what portion does a believer have with an unbeliever?

      Read 2 Corinthians 6:15 in the app

  2. district, region (1)
    A district or region: “the leading city of that district of Macedonia”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 16:12

      κἀκεῖθεν εἰς Φιλίππους, ἥτις ἐστὶν πρώτη τῆς μερίδος Μακεδονίας πόλις, κολωνία. ἦμεν δὲ ἐν ταύτῃ τῇ πόλει διατρίβοντες ἡμέρας τινάς.

      and from there to Philippi, which is a city of Macedonia, the foremost of the district, a Roman colony. We were staying some days in this city.

      Read Acts 16:12 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 μερίς, -ίδος, ἡ, [in LXX chiefly חֵלֶק, חֶלְקָה;] (as in cl.) a part, portion: Lk 10:42, Ac 8:21, II Co 6:15, Col 1:12. In later Gk (v. MM, xvi), as geographical term, a division, district: Ac 16:12.†
