Senses (NGSD)

  1. sickle (8)
    A sickle, a reaping blade: “when the grain is ripe, at once he puts in the sickle”, “with a sharp sickle in his hand”, “put in your sickle and reap”, “gather the clusters of the vine of the earth”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 4:29

      ὅταν δὲ παραδοῖ ὁ καρπός, εὐθὺς ἀποστέλλει τὸ δρέπανον, ὅτι παρέστηκεν ὁ θερισμός.

      But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he puts in the sickle, because the harvest has come.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 δρέπανον, -ου, τό (later form of Attic δρεπάνη, < δρέπω, to pluck), [in LXX for מַזְמֵרָה, חֶרְמֵשׁ, etc. ;] a sickle, pruning-hook; Mk 4:29, Re 14:14-19.†
