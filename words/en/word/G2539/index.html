Senses (NGSD)

  1. although (5)
    A concessive particle: although. “although you … know”, “although he was a son”, “though he sought it with tears”, “though they are descended from Abraham”, “though I myself might also rely on the flesh”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Philippians 3:4

      καίπερ ἐγὼ ἔχων πεποίθησιν καὶ ἐν σαρκί. Εἴ τις δοκεῖ ἄλλος πεποιθέναι ἐν σαρκί, ἐγὼ μᾶλλον·

      though I myself might have confidence even in the flesh. If any other man thinks that he has confidence in the flesh, I yet more:

      Read Philippians 3:4 in the app

    • Hebrews 5:8

      καίπερ ὢν υἱός, ἔμαθεν ἀφ’ ὧν ἔπαθεν τὴν ὑπακοήν,

      though he was a Son, yet learned obedience by the things which he suffered.

      Read Hebrews 5:8 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:17

      ἴστε γὰρ ὅτι καὶ μετέπειτα θέλων κληρονομῆσαι τὴν εὐλογίαν ἀπεδοκιμάσθη, μετανοίας γὰρ τόπον οὐχ εὗρεν, καίπερ μετὰ δακρύων ἐκζητήσας αὐτήν.

      For you know that even when he afterward desired to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for a change of mind though he sought it diligently with tears.
