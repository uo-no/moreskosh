Senses (NGSD)

  1. sandal / footwear (10)
    Footwear, sandals: “take off the sandals from your feet”, “not worthy to untie the strap of his sandal”, “carry no moneybag, no knapsack, no sandals”, “put shoes on his feet”, “one who carries sandals”, “whose sandals I am not worthy to carry”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, but he who comes after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit.
