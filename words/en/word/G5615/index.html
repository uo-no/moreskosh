Senses (NGSD)

  1. likewise / in the same way (17)
    An adverb showing that something happens in the same manner or with the same result as what has just been stated: “likewise”, “in the same way”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 20:5

      οἱ δὲ ἀπῆλθον. πάλιν ἐξελθὼν περὶ ἕκτην καὶ ἐνάτην ὥραν ἐποίησεν ὡσαύτως.

      Again he went out about the sixth and the ninth hour, and did likewise.

      But he spoke all the more, “If I must die with you, I will not deny you.” They all said the same thing.
