Senses (NGSD)

  1. ox / cattle (8)
    An ox, a bull: “you shall not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain”, “those selling oxen, sheep, and pigeons”, “drove the sheep and oxen out of the temple”, “untie his ox or donkey”, “bought five yoke of oxen”.
