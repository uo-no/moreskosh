Senses (NGSD)

  1. silver (5)
    Silver: “gold, silver and precious stones”, “like gold, silver or stone”, “silver and copper”, “your gold and silver have rusted”, “silver …”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      Don’t take any gold, silver, or brass in your money belts.
