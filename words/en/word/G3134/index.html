Senses (NGSD)

  1. tha (in Marana tha) (2)
    Part of the Aramaic for “come” (Marana tha). The word division in 1 Corinthians 16:22 (Marana tha or Maran atha) is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 16:22

      εἴ τις οὐ φιλεῖ τὸν κύριον, ἤτω ἀνάθεμα. Μαράνα θά.

      If any man doesn’t love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be cursed. Come, Lord!
