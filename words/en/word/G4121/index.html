Senses (NGSD)

  1. to increase / abound (8)
    To increase, overflow, grow rich: “make your love increase”, “thanksgiving abounds”, “if these things are yours and abound”, “your love for one another is increasing”, “the fruit that increases to your credit”, “where sin increased, grace abounded all the more”, “are we to continue in sin?”
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 5:20

      νόμος δὲ παρεισῆλθεν ἵνα πλεονάσῃ τὸ παράπτωμα· οὗ δὲ ἐπλεόνασεν ἡ ἁμαρτία, ὑπερεπερίσσευσεν ἡ χάρις,

      The law came in that the trespass might abound; but where sin abounded, grace abounded more exceedingly,

      Read Romans 5:20 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 4:15

      τὰ γὰρ πάντα δι’ ὑμᾶς, ἵνα ἡ χάρις πλεονάσασα διὰ τῶν πλειόνων τὴν εὐχαριστίαν περισσεύσῃ εἰς τὴν δόξαν τοῦ θεοῦ.

      For all things are for your sakes, that the grace, being multiplied through the many, may cause the thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.

      Read 2 Corinthians 4:15 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 3:12

      ὑμᾶς δὲ ὁ κύριος πλεονάσαι καὶ περισσεύσαι τῇ ἀγάπῃ εἰς ἀλλήλους καὶ εἰς πάντας, καθάπερ καὶ ἡμεῖς εἰς ὑμᾶς,

      May the Lord make you to increase and abound in love toward one another and toward all men, even as we also do toward you,

      Read 1 Thessalonians 3:12 in the app

  2. to have too much (surplus) (1)
    To have an excess, something left over: “whoever gathered much had nothing left over” (2 Cor 8:15).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Corinthians 8:15

      καθὼς γέγραπται· Ὁ τὸ πολὺ οὐκ ἐπλεόνασεν, καὶ ὁ τὸ ὀλίγον οὐκ ἠλαττόνησεν.

      As it is written, “He who gathered much had nothing left over, and he who gathered little had no lack.”

      Read 2 Corinthians 8:15 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 πλεονάζω (< πλέον), [in LXX for עדף, רבה, etc.;] Intrans. Of persons; to abound in (Arist.); to superabound: II Co 8:15(LXX). Of things, to abound, superabound: Ro 5:20 6:1, II Co 4:15, Phl 4:17, II Th 1:3, II Pe 1:8. Trans. (Nu 26:54, Ps 70 (71):21; not cl, v. Lft., Notes, 48 f.), to make to abound: I Th 3:12 (cf. ὑπερ-πλεονάζω).† SYN.: περισσεύω, q.v.
