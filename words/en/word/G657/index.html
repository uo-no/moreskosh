Senses (NGSD)

  1. to say goodbye (5)
    To take leave, to bid farewell: “taking leave of them”, “said farewell”, “took leave … and set out”, “to say farewell to those at my home”, “after taking leave of them, he went up the mountain”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 6:46

      καὶ ἀποταξάμενος αὐτοῖς ἀπῆλθεν εἰς τὸ ὄρος προσεύξασθαι.

      After he had taken leave of them, he went up the mountain to pray.

      Read Mark 6:46 in the app

    • Luke 9:61

      εἶπεν δὲ καὶ ἕτερος· Ἀκολουθήσω σοι, κύριε· πρῶτον δὲ ἐπίτρεψόν μοι ἀποτάξασθαι τοῖς εἰς τὸν οἶκόν μου.

      Another also said, “I want to follow you, Lord, but first allow me to say good-bye to those who are at my house.”

      Read Luke 9:61 in the app

    • Acts 18:21

      ἀλλὰ ἀποταξάμενος καὶ εἰπών· Πάλιν ἀνακάμψω πρὸς ὑμᾶς τοῦ θεοῦ θέλοντος ἀνήχθη ἀπὸ τῆς Ἐφέσου,

      but taking his leave of them, he said, “I must by all means keep this coming feast in Jerusalem, but I will return again to you if God wills.” Then he set sail from Ephesus.

      Read Acts 18:21 in the app

  2. to renounce, to give up (1)
    To let go of one’s possessions, to give them up: “none of you can be my disciple without giving up all that you have”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 14:33

      οὕτως οὖν πᾶς ἐξ ὑμῶν ὃς οὐκ ἀποτάσσεται πᾶσιν τοῖς ἑαυτοῦ ὑπάρχουσιν οὐ δύναται εἶναί μου μαθητής.

      So therefore, whoever of you who doesn’t renounce all that he has, he can’t be my disciple.

      Read Luke 14:33 in the app
