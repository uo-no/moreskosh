Senses (NGSD)

  1. to put to shame / be ashamed (3)
    To make ashamed, to shame: “I do not write this to make you ashamed”, “that he may be ashamed”, “so that an opponent may be put to shame”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 4:14

      Οὐκ ἐντρέπων ὑμᾶς γράφω ταῦτα, ἀλλ’ ὡς τέκνα μου ἀγαπητὰ νουθετῶν·

      I don’t write these things to shame you, but to admonish you as my beloved children.

      Read 1 Corinthians 4:14 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 3:14

      Εἰ δέ τις οὐχ ὑπακούει τῷ λόγῳ ἡμῶν διὰ τῆς ἐπιστολῆς, τοῦτον σημειοῦσθε, μὴ συναναμίγνυσθαι αὐτῷ, ἵνα ἐντραπῇ·

      If any man doesn’t obey our word in this letter, note that man and have no company with him, to the end that he may be ashamed.

      Read 2 Thessalonians 3:14 in the app

    • Titus 2:8

      λόγον ὑγιῆ ἀκατάγνωστον, ἵνα ὁ ἐξ ἐναντίας ἐντραπῇ μηδὲν ἔχων λέγειν περὶ ἡμῶν φαῦλον.

      and soundness of speech that can’t be condemned, that he who opposes you may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say about us.

      Read Titus 2:8 in the app

  2. to respect / regard (6)
    To respect, esteem, reverence: “a judge who neither feared God nor respected man”, “they will respect my son”, “we had earthly fathers who disciplined us and we respected them”, “has no regard for anyone”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 21:37

      ὕστερον δὲ ἀπέστειλεν πρὸς αὐτοὺς τὸν υἱὸν αὐτοῦ λέγων· Ἐντραπήσονται τὸν υἱόν μου.

      But afterward he sent to them his son, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’

      Read Matthew 21:37 in the app

    • Luke 18:2

      λέγων· Κριτής τις ἦν ἔν τινι πόλει τὸν θεὸν μὴ φοβούμενος καὶ ἄνθρωπον μὴ ἐντρεπόμενος.

      saying, “There was a judge in a certain city who didn’t fear God and didn’t respect man.

      Read Luke 18:2 in the app

    • Luke 20:13

      εἶπεν δὲ ὁ κύριος τοῦ ἀμπελῶνος· Τί ποιήσω; πέμψω τὸν υἱόν μου τὸν ἀγαπητόν· ἴσως τοῦτον ἐντραπήσονται.

      The lord of the vineyard said, ‘What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. It may be that seeing him, they will respect him.’

      Read Luke 20:13 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 ἐν-τρέπω [in LXX for כָּנַע ni., כָּלַם ni., etc. ;] to turn about; metaph., put to shame: c. acc, I Co 4:14; pass., II Th 3:14, Tit 2:8; mid., to reverence: c. acc pers. (cl. c. gen.), Mt 21:37, Mk 12:6, Lk 18:2, 4 20:13, He 12:9 (cf. MM, Exp., iii, xiii).†
