Senses (NGSD)

  1. dust (5)
    Dust: “shook off the dust from their feet”, “flinging dust into the air”, “even the dust that clings to our feet we wipe off”, “shake off the dust from your feet”, “shake the dust off your feet”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 10:14

      καὶ ὃς ἂν μὴ δέξηται ὑμᾶς μηδὲ ἀκούσῃ τοὺς λόγους ὑμῶν, ἐξερχόμενοι ἔξω τῆς οἰκίας ἢ τῆς πόλεως ἐκείνης ἐκτινάξατε τὸν κονιορτὸν τῶν ποδῶν ὑμῶν.

      Whoever doesn’t receive you or hear your words, as you go out of that house or that city, shake the dust off your feet.

Forms in the text

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 κονιορτός, -οῦ, ὁ (< κόνις or κονία, dust, ὄρνυμι, to stir up), [in LXX for אָבָק, etc.;] in cl., dust stirred up (Ex 9:9, Is 5:24); in NT, simply dust: Mt 10:14, Lk 9:5 10:11, Ac 13:51 22:23.†
