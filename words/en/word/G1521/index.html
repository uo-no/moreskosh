Senses (NGSD)

  1. to bring in / lead in (11)
    To lead or bring inside: “brought him into the barracks”, “brought him into the temple”, “the doorkeeper brought Peter in”, “bring them here”, “brought in the child Jesus”, “when he brings the firstborn into the world”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      He came in the Spirit into the temple. When the parents brought in the child, Jesus, that they might do concerning him according to the custom of the law,
