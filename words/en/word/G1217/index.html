Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** δημιουργός, -οῦ, ὑ (< δῆμος, ἔργον), [in LXX: II Mac 4:1 * ;] one who works for the people. Univ., an author, builder, maker; the maker of the world (Xen., Mem., I, 4, 9, al.), He 11:10.† SYN.: κτίστης, creator, τεχνίτης, craftsman, designer. In He, l.c., τ. has reference to the plan, δ. to its execution.
