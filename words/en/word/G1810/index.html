Senses (NGSD)

  1. suddenly (5)
    Suddenly, unexpectedly: “suddenly a light from heaven flashed around him”, “suddenly a light from heaven”, “suddenly … a host of the heavenly army appeared”, “a spirit suddenly seizes him”, “coming back suddenly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 13:36

      μὴ ἐλθὼν ἐξαίφνης εὕρῃ ὑμᾶς καθεύδοντας·

      lest, coming suddenly, he might find you sleeping.

      Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God and saying,
