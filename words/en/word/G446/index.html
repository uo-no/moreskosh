Senses (NGSD)

  1. proconsul (5)
    The governor of a province (proconsul): “Gallio, proconsul of Achaia”, “the proconsul Sergius Paulus”, “to turn the proconsul away from the faith”, “the proconsul … believed”, “there are proconsuls”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Acts 13:7

      ὃς ἦν σὺν τῷ ἀνθυπάτῳ Σεργίῳ Παύλῳ, ἀνδρὶ συνετῷ. οὗτος προσκαλεσάμενος Βαρναβᾶν καὶ Σαῦλον ἐπεζήτησεν ἀκοῦσαι τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ·

      who was with the proconsul, Sergius Paulus, a man of understanding. This man summoned Barnabas and Saul, and sought to hear the word of God.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 * ἀνθ-ύπατος, -ου, ὁ (ἀντί, ὕπατος, altern. for ὑπέρτατος), supreme, a consul, one acting in place of a consul, a proconsul, the administrator of a senatorial province (cf. ἡγεμών, and v. MM, VGT, 44): Ac 13:7, 8, 12 18:12 19:38.†
