Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be shortened (1)
    To be drawn together, to be made short: “the time has grown short”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 7:29

      τοῦτο δέ φημι, ἀδελφοί, ὁ καιρὸς συνεσταλμένος ἐστίν· τὸ λοιπὸν ἵνα καὶ οἱ ἔχοντες γυναῖκας ὡς μὴ ἔχοντες ὦσιν,

      But I say this, brothers: the time is short. From now on, both those who have wives may be as though they had none;

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:29 in the app

  2. to wrap up (1)
    To wrap, to shroud: “wrapped up the body”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 5:6

      ἀναστάντες δὲ οἱ νεώτεροι συνέστειλαν αὐτὸν καὶ ἐξενέγκαντες ἔθαψαν.

      The young men arose and wrapped him up, and they carried him out and buried him.

      Read Acts 5:6 in the app

2 συν-στέλλω (Rec. συσ-), [in LXX: Jg 8:28 11:33 (כּנע ni.), Si 4:31, al. ;] to draw together, contract, shorten: of time, I Co 7:29. to wrap up: of enshrouding a body for burial (Eur.), c. acc., Ac 5:6.†
