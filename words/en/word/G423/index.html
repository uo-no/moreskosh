Senses (NGSD)

  1. above reproach (3)

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 * ἀν-επί-λημπτος (Rec. -λμπτος; Bl., § 6, 8), -ον (< ἀ-, ἐπιλαμβάνω), without reproach: I Ti 3:2 5:7 6:14.† SYN.: ἄμεμπτος, ἀνέγκλητος. It is stronger than these, for it implies not only that the man is of good report, but that he is deservedly so (cf. MM, VGT, s.v.).
