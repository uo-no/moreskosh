Senses (NGSD)

  1. testing, trial (2)
    Being tested, scrutiny: “even in a severe test of affliction”, “to test you and know whether you are obedient in everything”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 2:9

      εἰς τοῦτο γὰρ καὶ ἔγραψα ἵνα γνῶ τὴν δοκιμὴν ὑμῶν, εἰ εἰς πάντα ὑπήκοοί ἐστε.

      For to this end I also wrote, that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things.

      Read 2 Corinthians 2:9 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 8:2

      ὅτι ἐν πολλῇ δοκιμῇ θλίψεως ἡ περισσεία τῆς χαρᾶς αὐτῶν καὶ ἡ κατὰ βάθους πτωχεία αὐτῶν ἐπερίσσευσεν εἰς τὸ πλοῦτος τῆς ἁπλότητος αὐτῶν·

      how in a severe ordeal of affliction, the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded to the riches of their generosity.

      Read 2 Corinthians 8:2 in the app

  2. proof, evidence (2)
    Proof or evidence resulting from a test: “proof that Christ is speaking in me”, “by the proof given by this service”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 9:13

      διὰ τῆς δοκιμῆς τῆς διακονίας ταύτης δοξάζοντες τὸν θεὸν ἐπὶ τῇ ὑποταγῇ τῆς ὁμολογίας ὑμῶν εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Χριστοῦ καὶ ἁπλότητι τῆς κοινωνίας εἰς αὐτοὺς καὶ εἰς πάντας,

      seeing that through the proof given by this service, they glorify God for the obedience of your confession to the Good News of Christ and for the generosity of your contribution to them and to all,

      Read 2 Corinthians 9:13 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 13:3

      ἐπεὶ δοκιμὴν ζητεῖτε τοῦ ἐν ἐμοὶ λαλοῦντος Χριστοῦ· ὃς εἰς ὑμᾶς οὐκ ἀσθενεῖ ἀλλὰ δυνατεῖ ἐν ὑμῖν,

      seeing that you seek a proof of Christ who speaks in me who is not weak, but is powerful in you.

      Read 2 Corinthians 13:3 in the app

  3. proven character (3)
    The condition of having been tested and found sound; proven character: “endurance produces proven character, and proven character produces hope”, “you know Timothy’s proven worth”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 5:4

      ἡ δὲ ὑπομονὴ δοκιμήν, ἡ δὲ δοκιμὴ ἐλπίδα.

      and perseverance, proven character; and proven character, hope;

      Read Romans 5:4 in the app

    • Philippians 2:22

      τὴν δὲ δοκιμὴν αὐτοῦ γινώσκετε, ὅτι ὡς πατρὶ τέκνον σὺν ἐμοὶ ἐδούλευσεν εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον.

      But you know that he has proved himself. As a child serves a father, so he served with me in furtherance of the Good News.

      Read Philippians 2:22 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 *† δοκιμή, -ῆς, ἡ (< δόκιμος), [in Sm.: Ps 67 (68):31 * ;] the process of trial, proving, test: II Co 8:2 9:13. The result of trial, approval, approvedness, proof : Ro 5:4, II Co 2:9 13:3, Phl 2:22 (Cremer, 212, 701).†
