Senses (NGSD)

  1. young man (10)
    A young man, a youth: “young men, you have overcome the evil one”, “your young men shall see visions”, “the young men came in”, “took the young man”, “young man, I say to you, arise”, “the young man said”, “a young man dressed in a white robe”, “a young man followed Jesus”.
