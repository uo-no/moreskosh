Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be tormented (1)
    To be afflicted, to be troubled: “those troubled by unclean spirits”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 5:16

      συνήρχετο δὲ καὶ τὸ πλῆθος τῶν πέριξ πόλεων Ἰερουσαλήμ, φέροντες ἀσθενεῖς καὶ ὀχλουμένους ὑπὸ πνευμάτων ἀκαθάρτων, οἵτινες ἐθεραπεύοντο ἅπαντες.

      The multitude also came together from the cities around Jerusalem, bringing sick people and those who were tormented by unclean spirits; and they were all healed.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** ὀχλέω, -ῶ (< ὄχλος), [in LXX: To 6:7, III Mac 5:41*;] to move, disturb; hence, generally, to trouble, vex: pass., Ac 5:16 (act. absol., = pass., to be in a tumult, III Mac 5.41; cf. ἐν-, παρ-εν-οχλέω, and v. MM, xviii).†
