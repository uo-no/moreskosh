Senses (NGSD)

  1. to give birth to (2)
    To bring forth, to give birth: “brings forth death”, “he gave us birth by his word”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • James 1:15

      εἶτα ἡ ἐπιθυμία συλλαβοῦσα τίκτει ἁμαρτίαν, ἡ δὲ ἁμαρτία ἀποτελεσθεῖσα ἀποκύει θάνατον.

      Then the lust, when it has conceived, bears sin. The sin, when it is full grown, produces death.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ** ἀπο-κυέω (Rec. -κύω), -ῶ (< ἀπό, κυέω or κύω, to be pregnant), [in LXX: IV Mac 15:17*;] prop., "the medical word for birth as the close of pregnancy" (Hort, Ja., 26 f.). In κοινή, "an ordinary syn. of τίκτω, but definitely 'perfectivised' (M, Pr., 111 ff.; MM, s.v.) by the ἀπό, and so implying safe delivery," to bring forth, give birth to: Ja 1:15, 18.†
