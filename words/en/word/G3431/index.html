Senses (NGSD)

  1. to commit adultery (13)
    To commit adultery, to be unfaithful in marriage: “you shall not commit adultery”, “commits adultery”, “whoever looks at a woman with lust has already committed adultery with her in his heart”, “those who commit adultery”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:27

      Ἠκούσατε ὅτι ἐρρέθη· Οὐ μοιχεύσεις.

      “You have heard that it was said, ‘You shall not commit adultery;’

      Read Matthew 5:27 in the app

    • Mark 10:19

      τὰς ἐντολὰς οἶδας· Μὴ φονεύσῃς, Μὴ μοιχεύσῃς, Μὴ κλέψῃς, Μὴ ψευδομαρτυρήσῃς, Μὴ ἀποστερήσῃς, Τίμα τὸν πατέρα σου καὶ τὴν μητέρα.

      You know the commandments: ‘Do not murder,’ ‘Do not commit adultery,’ ‘Do not steal,’ ‘Do not give false testimony,’ ‘Do not defraud,’ ‘Honor your father and mother.’”

      Read Mark 10:19 in the app

    • Romans 2:22

      ὁ λέγων μὴ μοιχεύειν μοιχεύεις; ὁ βδελυσσόμενος τὰ εἴδωλα ἱεροσυλεῖς;

      You who say a man shouldn’t commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?

      Read Romans 2:22 in the app

  2. to be made an adulteress / be in adultery (passive) (2)
    A woman is placed in an adulterous relation: “caught in the act of adultery”, “makes her commit adultery”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 5:32

      ἐγὼ δὲ λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι πᾶς ὁ ἀπολύων τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ παρεκτὸς λόγου πορνείας ποιεῖ αὐτὴν μοιχευθῆναι, καὶ ὃς ἐὰν ἀπολελυμένην γαμήσῃ μοιχᾶται.

      but I tell you that whoever puts away his wife, except for the cause of sexual immorality, makes her an adulteress; and whoever marries her when she is put away commits adultery.

      Read Matthew 5:32 in the app

    • John 8:4

      λέγουσιν αὐτῷ, πειράζοντες Διδάσκαλε, αὕτη ἡ γυνὴ κατελήφθη ἐπ᾿ αὐτοφόρῳ μοιχευομένη

      they told him, “Teacher, we found this woman in adultery, in the very act.
