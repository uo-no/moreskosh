Senses (NGSD)

  1. to sleep (literal) (4)
    To sleep, to fall asleep, in the literal sense: “the sleeping disciples”, “while the soldiers were asleep”. In John 11:12 the disciples mistake it for “sleep”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 28:13

      λέγοντες· Εἴπατε ὅτι Οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ νυκτὸς ἐλθόντες ἔκλεψαν αὐτὸν ἡμῶν κοιμωμένων·

      saying, “Say that his disciples came by night and stole him away while we slept.

      Read Matthew 28:13 in the app

    • Luke 22:45

      καὶ ἀναστὰς ἀπὸ τῆς προσευχῆς ἐλθὼν πρὸς τοὺς μαθητὰς εὗρεν κοιμωμένους αὐτοὺς ἀπὸ τῆς λύπης,

      When he rose up from his prayer, he came to the disciples and found them sleeping because of grief,

      Read Luke 22:45 in the app

    • John 11:12

      εἶπαν οὖν οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτῷ· Κύριε, εἰ κεκοίμηται σωθήσεται.

      The disciples therefore said, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover.”

      Read John 11:12 in the app

  2. to fall asleep (in death) (14)
    Dying spoken of euphemistically as “falling asleep”: the death of believers, those who have died before, the death of the fathers, the death of Lazarus.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 27:52

      καὶ τὰ μνημεῖα ἀνεῴχθησαν καὶ πολλὰ σώματα τῶν κεκοιμημένων ἁγίων ἠγέρθησαν,

      The tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised;

      Read Matthew 27:52 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 7:39

      Γυνὴ δέδεται ἐφ’ ὅσον χρόνον ζῇ ὁ ἀνὴρ αὐτῆς· ἐὰν δὲ κοιμηθῇ ὁ ἀνήρ, ἐλευθέρα ἐστὶν ᾧ θέλει γαμηθῆναι, μόνον ἐν κυρίῳ·

      A wife is bound by law for as long as her husband lives; but if the husband is dead, she is free to be married to whomever she desires, only in the Lord.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:39 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 15:51

      ἰδοὺ μυστήριον ὑμῖν λέγω· πάντες οὐ κοιμηθησόμεθα πάντες δὲ ἀλλαγησόμεθα,

      Behold, I tell you a mystery. We will not all sleep, but we will all be changed,

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:51 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

18 κοιμάω, -ῶ, [in LXX chiefly for שׁכב;] to lull to sleep, put to sleep. Mid and pass., to fall asleep (M, Pr., 162; M, Th., 1, 4, 13): Mt 28:13, Lk 22:45, Jo 11:12, Ac 12:6. Metaph, of death: Mt 27:52, Jo 11:11 Ac 7:60 13:36, I Co 7:39 11:30 15:6, 18, 20, 51, I Th 4:13-15, II Pe 3:4 (cf. Is 14:8 43:17, II Mac 12:45).† SYN.: καθεύδω.
