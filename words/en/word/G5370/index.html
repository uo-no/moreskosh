Senses (NGSD)

  1. kiss (7)
    A kiss: “greet one another with a holy kiss”, “greet one another with a kiss of love”, “would you betray the Son of Man with a kiss”, “you gave me no kiss”. (Includes the kiss of greeting and the kisses of betrayal and of welcome into a house, but they are not separated into distinct senses.)
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 7:45

      φίλημά μοι οὐκ ἔδωκας· αὕτη δὲ ἀφ’ ἧς εἰσῆλθον οὐ διέλιπεν καταφιλοῦσά μου τοὺς πόδας.

      You gave me no kiss, but she, since the time I came in, has not ceased to kiss my feet.
