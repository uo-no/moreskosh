Senses (NGSD)

  1. to rejoice with (7)
    To rejoice together with: “if one part is honoured, all the parts rejoice with it”, “it does not rejoice at wrongdoing but rejoices with the truth”, “rejoice with me”, “calls the neighbours together and says, Rejoice with me”, “her relatives rejoiced with her”, “I rejoice, and rejoice with all of you”, “likewise rejoice”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:58

      καὶ ἤκουσαν οἱ περίοικοι καὶ οἱ συγγενεῖς αὐτῆς ὅτι ἐμεγάλυνεν κύριος τὸ ἔλεος αὐτοῦ μετ’ αὐτῆς, καὶ συνέχαιρον αὐτῇ.

      Her neighbors and her relatives heard that the Lord had magnified his mercy toward her, and they rejoiced with her.

      When she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the drachma which I had lost!’
