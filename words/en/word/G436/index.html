Senses (NGSD)

  1. to resist / oppose (14)
    To oppose, withstand, stand against: “do not resist the evildoer”, “resist the devil”, “unable to withstand or refute”, “who can resist his will?”, “Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses”, “strongly opposed his message”, “he who resists authority”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:39

      ἐγὼ δὲ λέγω ὑμῖν μὴ ἀντιστῆναι τῷ πονηρῷ· ἀλλ’ ὅστις σε ῥαπίζει εἰς τὴν δεξιὰν σιαγόνα, στρέψον αὐτῷ καὶ τὴν ἄλλην·

      But I tell you, don’t resist him who is evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also.

      You will say then to me, “Why does he still find fault? For who withstands his will?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 ἀνθ-ίστημι (ἀντί, ἵστημι), [in LXX for עמד, יצב, etc.;] in pres., impf., fut. and 1 aor. act., causal, to set against. In mid. and pass., also pf. and 2 aor. act., to withstand, resist, oppose: c. dat., Mt 5:39, Lk 21:15, Ac 6:10 13:8, Ro 9:19 13:2, Ga 2:11, Eph 6:13, II Ti 3:8 4:15, Ja 4:7, I Pe 5:9.†
