Senses (NGSD)

  1. rented lodging (1)
    A rented house: “his rented house”. The representative examples in the LXX are the use “price of prostitution”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 28:30

      Ἐνέμεινεν δὲ διετίαν ὅλην ἐν ἰδίῳ μισθώματι, καὶ ἀπεδέχετο πάντας τοὺς εἰσπορευομένους πρὸς αὐτόν,

      Paul stayed two whole years in his own rented house and received all who were coming to him,
