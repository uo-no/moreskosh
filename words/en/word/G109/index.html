Senses (NGSD)

  1. air (4)
    The air: “they flung dust into the air”, “meet the Lord in the air”, “the smoke rose from the pit”, “poured out his bowl into the air”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 22:23

      κραυγαζόντων τε αὐτῶν καὶ ῥιπτούντων τὰ ἱμάτια καὶ κονιορτὸν βαλλόντων εἰς τὸν ἀέρα,

      As they cried out, threw off their cloaks, and threw dust into the air,

      then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. So we will be with the Lord forever.

  2. to beat the air, to speak into the air (idiom) (2)
    Idiom: to beat the air, to speak into the air. “I do not box as one beating the air”, “you will be speaking into the air”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 9:26

      ἐγὼ τοίνυν οὕτως τρέχω ὡς οὐκ ἀδήλως, οὕτως πυκτεύω ὡς οὐκ ἀέρα δέρων·

      I therefore run like that, not aimlessly. I fight like that, not beating the air,

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:26 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 14:9

      οὕτως καὶ ὑμεῖς διὰ τῆς γλώσσης ἐὰν μὴ εὔσημον λόγον δῶτε, πῶς γνωσθήσεται τὸ λαλούμενον; ἔσεσθε γὰρ εἰς ἀέρα λαλοῦντες.

      So also you, unless you uttered by the tongue words easy to understand, how would it be known what is spoken? For you would be speaking into the air.

      Read 1 Corinthians 14:9 in the app

  3. the air (realm of a ruler) (1)
    The air as the realm over which a ruler holds authority: “the ruler who holds authority over the air”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Ephesians 2:2

      ἐν αἷς ποτε περιεπατήσατε κατὰ τὸν αἰῶνα τοῦ κόσμου τούτου, κατὰ τὸν ἄρχοντα τῆς ἐξουσίας τοῦ ἀέρος, τοῦ πνεύματος τοῦ νῦν ἐνεργοῦντος ἐν τοῖς υἱοῖς τῆς ἀπειθείας·

      in which you once walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit who now works in the children of disobedience.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 ἀήρ, ἀέρος, ὁ, [in LXX: II Ki 22:12 (= Ps 17 (18):11 שׁחק), Wi8;] in Hom., Hes., the lower air which surrounds the earth, as opp. to the purer αἰθήρ of the higher regions; generally, air (MM, VGT, s.v.): Ac 22:23, I Th 4:17, Re 9:2 16:17; of the air as the realm of demons, Eph 2:2; ἀ. δέρειν, of striving to no purpose, I Co 9:26; εἰς ἀ. λαλεῖν, of speaking without effect, not being understood, I Co 14:9.†
