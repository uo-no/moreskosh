Senses (NGSD)

  1. busy at home (1)

    Attested: NT only

    • Titus 2:5

      to be sober minded, chaste, workers at home, kind, being in subjection to their own husbands, that God’s word may not be blasphemed.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * οἰκ-ουρός, -ον (< οἶκος + οὖρος, a keeper); watching or keeping the house; as subst., ἡ οἰ., a housekeeper (Soph., Eur.; v. LS, s.v.). keeping at home: Tit 2:5, Rec. (v. Field, Notes, 220 ff.; CGT, in l., and cf. οἰκουργός).†
