Senses (NGSD)

  1. to find fault with (2)
    To blame, to censure: “so that no blame may be found”, “so that it may not be blamed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Corinthians 6:3

      μηδεμίαν ἐν μηδενὶ διδόντες προσκοπήν, ἵνα μὴ μωμηθῇ ἡ διακονία,

      We give no occasion of stumbling in anything, that our service may not be blamed,

      Read 2 Corinthians 6:3 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 8:20

      στελλόμενοι τοῦτο μή τις ἡμᾶς μωμήσηται ἐν τῇ ἁδρότητι ταύτῃ τῇ διακονουμένῃ ὑφ’ ἡμῶν,

      We are avoiding this, that any man should blame us concerning this abundance which is administered by us.
