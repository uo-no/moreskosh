Senses (NGSD)

  1. to kiss (6)
    To kiss fervently: “embraced Paul and kissed him”, “ran, fell on his neck and kissed him”, “kissing Jesus’ feet”, “has not ceased to kiss my feet”, “said Rabbi and kissed Jesus”, “kissed him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      Immediately he came to Jesus, and said, “Greetings, Rabbi!” and kissed him.
