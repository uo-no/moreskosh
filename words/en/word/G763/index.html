Senses (NGSD)

  1. ungodliness (6)
    Not revering God; ungodliness: “they will advance to more and more ungodliness”, “will banish ungodliness from the Jews”, “all their works of ungodliness”, “ungodly passions”, “against all ungodliness and unrighteousness”, “renouncing ungodliness and worldly desires”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men who suppress the truth in unrighteousness,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ἀσέβεια, -ας, ἡ (< ἀσεβής), [in LXX for פֶּשַׁע, רֶשַׁע, etc.;] ungodliness, impiety: Ro 1:18 11:26, II Ti 2:16, Tit 2:12; ἔργα ἀσεβείας, ungodly deeds, Ju 15; ἐπιθυηίαι τ. ἀσεβειῶν, desires for ungodly things or deeds, Ju 18 (DB, iv, 532; Cremer, 523; MM, s.v.).†
