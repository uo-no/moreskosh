Senses (NGSD)

  1. tabernacle, dwelling (1)
    A tent, a dwelling place: “a dwelling place for the God of Jacob”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 7:46

      ὃς εὗρεν χάριν ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ ᾐτήσατο εὑρεῖν σκήνωμα τῷ θεῷ Ἰακώβ.

      who found favor in the sight of God, and asked to find a habitation for the God of Jacob.

      Read Acts 7:46 in the app

  2. the body (as a temporary dwelling) (2)
    The word for a tent used of the body: “as long as I am in this tent”, “the time to put off my tent is near”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 2 Peter 1:13

      δίκαιον δὲ ἡγοῦμαι, ἐφ’ ὅσον εἰμὶ ἐν τούτῳ τῷ σκηνώματι, διεγείρειν ὑμᾶς ἐν ὑπομνήσει,

      I think it right, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up by reminding you,

      Read 2 Peter 1:13 in the app

    • 2 Peter 1:14

      εἰδὼς ὅτι ταχινή ἐστιν ἡ ἀπόθεσις τοῦ σκηνώματός μου, καθὼς καὶ ὁ κύριος ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦς Χριστὸς ἐδήλωσέν μοι·

      knowing that the putting off of my tent comes swiftly, even as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 σκήνωμα, -τος, τό (< σκηνόω), [in LXX chiefly for אֹהֶל ;] a tent, tabernacle (cf. σκηνή, σκῆνος): of the temple as God's dwelling (Ps 131 (132):5, al.), Ac 7:46. Metaph., of the body: II Pe 1:13, 14.†
