Senses (NGSD)

  1. white / gleaming white (25)
    White in colour, or a bright white that gives off light. States the colour or brightness of things: clothing, hair, a horse, a cloud, a throne, a small stone, and so on.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      Neither shall you swear by your head, for you can’t make one hair white or black.
