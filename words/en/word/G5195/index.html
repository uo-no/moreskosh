Senses (NGSD)

  1. to insult, to mistreat (5)
    To insult, to treat outrageously, to abuse: “were shamefully treated”, “to mistreat and stone them”, “in saying these things you insult us also”, “will be shamefully treated”, “treated them shamefully”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 22:6

      οἱ δὲ λοιποὶ κρατήσαντες τοὺς δούλους αὐτοῦ ὕβρισαν καὶ ἀπέκτειναν.

      and the rest grabbed his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them.

      Read Matthew 22:6 in the app

    • Luke 11:45

      Ἀποκριθεὶς δέ τις τῶν νομικῶν λέγει αὐτῷ· Διδάσκαλε, ταῦτα λέγων καὶ ἡμᾶς ὑβρίζεις.

      One of the lawyers answered him, “Teacher, in saying this you insult us also.”

      Read Luke 11:45 in the app

    • Acts 14:5

      ὡς δὲ ἐγένετο ὁρμὴ τῶν ἐθνῶν τε καὶ Ἰουδαίων σὺν τοῖς ἄρχουσιν αὐτῶν ὑβρίσαι καὶ λιθοβολῆσαι αὐτούς,

      When some of both the Gentiles and the Jews, with their rulers, made a violent attempt to mistreat and stone them,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ὑβρίζω (< ὕβρις), [in LXX for גֵּאֶה, etc. ;] intrans., to wax wanton, run riot. Trans., to outrage, insult, treat insolently: c. acc pers., Mt 22:6, Lk 11:45 18:32, Ac 14:5, I Th 2:2.†
