Senses (NGSD)

  1. sulfur, brimstone (7)
    Sulfur: “fire and sulfur rained down from heaven”, “tormented with fire and sulfur”, “thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur”, “the lake of fire that burns with sulfur”, “fire, smoke and sulfur coming out of their mouths”, “killed by the fire, the smoke and the sulfur”.

      but in the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from the sky and destroyed them all.
