Senses (NGSD)

  1. to embezzle (3)
    To pilfer, to misappropriate: “kept back some of the money”, “will you keep it back”, “not pilfering”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 5:2

      καὶ ἐνοσφίσατο ἀπὸ τῆς τιμῆς, συνειδυίης καὶ τῆς γυναικός, καὶ ἐνέγκας μέρος τι παρὰ τοὺς πόδας τῶν ἀποστόλων ἔθηκεν.

      and kept back part of the price, his wife also being aware of it, then brought a certain part and laid it at the apostles’ feet.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 νοσφίζω (< νόσφι, apart, aside), [in LXX: Jos 7:1 (לקח), II Mac 4:32*;] in Hom., as depon., to turn away (from), abandon. After Hom., in act., to set apart, remove. Mid., to set apart for oneself, peculate, purloin: absol., Tit 2:10 (for ex. in π., v. MM, xvii); seq. ἀπό, Ac 5:2, 3.†
