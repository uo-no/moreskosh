Senses (NGSD)

  1. still (1)
    Still, even yet: “do you still not understand”. The representative examples in the LXX are of the noun ἀκμή (prime), and the adverb has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 15:16

      ὁ δὲ εἶπεν· Ἀκμὴν καὶ ὑμεῖς ἀσύνετοί ἐστε;

      So Jesus said, “Do you also still not understand?
