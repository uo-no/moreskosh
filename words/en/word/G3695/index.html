Senses (NGSD)

  1. to arm oneself (1)
    To take up arms, to equip oneself: “arm yourselves with the same way of thinking”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Peter 4:1

      Χριστοῦ οὖν παθόντος σαρκὶ καὶ ὑμεῖς τὴν αὐτὴν ἔννοιαν ὁπλίσασθε, ὅτι ὁ παθὼν σαρκὶ πέπαυται ἁμαρτίας,

      Therefore, since Christ suffered for us in the flesh, arm yourselves also with the same mind; for he who has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** ὁπλίζω (< ὅπλον), [in Sm.: Je 52:25*;] to make ready, equip; of soldiers, to arm. Mid., to arm oneself; fig., ἔννοιαν: I Pe 4:1 (cf. θράσος ὁ., Soph., Elec., 995), (cf. καθ-οπλίζω).†
