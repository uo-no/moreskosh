Senses (NGSD)

  1. soft (clothing) (3)
    Soft (of clothing): “a man dressed in soft clothing”, “soft clothing”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 11:8

      ἀλλὰ τί ἐξήλθατε ἰδεῖν; ἄνθρωπον ἐν μαλακοῖς ἠμφιεσμένον; ἰδοὺ οἱ τὰ μαλακὰ φοροῦντες ἐν τοῖς οἴκοις τῶν βασιλέων εἰσίν.

      But what did you go out to see? A man in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in kings’ houses.

    • Luke 7:25

      ἀλλὰ τί ἐξήλθατε ἰδεῖν; ἄνθρωπον ἐν μαλακοῖς ἱματίοις ἠμφιεσμένον; ἰδοὺ οἱ ἐν ἱματισμῷ ἐνδόξῳ καὶ τρυφῇ ὑπάρχοντες ἐν τοῖς βασιλείοις εἰσίν.

      But what did you go out to see? A man clothed in soft clothing? Behold, those who are gorgeously dressed and live delicately are in kings’ courts.

      Read Luke 7:25 in the app

  2. male prostitute, passive partner (1)
    A word for a man who takes the passive side in a same-sex relation, a male prostitute. In 1 Corinthians 6:9 it forms a pair with ἀρσενοκοῖται (the active side). Some take it more broadly as “a soft, effeminate person”, but on the evidence of usage and the paired structure it is set up, as in BDAG, as a sense denoting the sexually passive party.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 6:9

      Ἢ οὐκ οἴδατε ὅτι ἄδικοι θεοῦ βασιλείαν οὐ κληρονομήσουσιν; μὴ πλανᾶσθε· οὔτε πόρνοι οὔτε εἰδωλολάτραι οὔτε μοιχοὶ οὔτε μαλακοὶ οὔτε ἀρσενοκοῖται

      Or don’t you know that the unrighteous will not inherit God’s Kingdom? Don’t be deceived. Neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor male prostitutes, nor homosexuals,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 μαλακός, -ή, -όν, [in LXX: Pr 25:15 (רַךְ) Pr 26:22 (כְּמִתְלַהֲמִים)*;] soft; prop., to the touch (opp. to σκληρός): of clothing, pl., Mt 11:8, Lk 7:25. Of persons and their mode of living; mild, gentle; soft, effeminate: I Co 6:9 (prob. in obscene sense, cf. Deiss., LAE, 1504; MM, xvi; Zorell, s.v.).†
