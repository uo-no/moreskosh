Senses (NGSD)

  1. truly / certainly (affirmation) (18)
    An adverb affirming and emphasizing that a matter is true, that it is certainly so: “truly”, “surely”, “really”. Used almost always to introduce a confession or assertion.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 14:33

      οἱ δὲ ἐν τῷ πλοίῳ προσεκύνησαν αὐτῷ λέγοντες· Ἀληθῶς θεοῦ υἱὸς εἶ.

      Those who were in the boat came and worshiped him, saying, “You are truly the Son of God!”
