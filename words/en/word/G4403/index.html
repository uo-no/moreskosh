Senses (NGSD)

  1. stern (3)
    The stern of a ship: “anchors from the stern”, “in the stern”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 4:38

      καὶ αὐτὸς ἦν ἐν τῇ πρύμνῃ ἐπὶ τὸ προσκεφάλαιον καθεύδων· καὶ ἐγείρουσιν αὐτὸν καὶ λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Διδάσκαλε, οὐ μέλει σοι ὅτι ἀπολλύμεθα;

      He himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion; and they woke him up and asked him, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are dying?”

      Fearing that we would run aground on rocky ground, they let go four anchors from the stern, and wished for daylight.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 * πρύμνα, -ης, ἡ, prop. fem. of adj. πρύμνος, -η, -ον, (sc. ναῦς), the hindmost part of a ship, the stern: Mk 4:38, Ac 27:29; opp. to πρῷρα, Ac 27:41.†
