Senses (NGSD)

  1. to afflict / be afflicted (8)
    To afflict, trouble, undergo suffering: “we were going to suffer affliction”, “the afflicted”, “afflicted in every way”, “fighting without and fear within”, “to repay with affliction those who afflict you”, “those who suffered affliction”, “to you who are afflicted”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 1:6

      εἴτε δὲ θλιβόμεθα, ὑπὲρ τῆς ὑμῶν παρακλήσεως καὶ σωτηρίας· εἴτε παρακαλούμεθα, ὑπὲρ τῆς ὑμῶν παρακλήσεως τῆς ἐνεργουμένης ἐν ὑπομονῇ τῶν αὐτῶν παθημάτων ὧν καὶ ἡμεῖς πάσχομεν,

      But if we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation. If we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which produces in you the patient enduring of the same sufferings which we also suffer.

      Read 2 Corinthians 1:6 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 7:5

      Καὶ γὰρ ἐλθόντων ἡμῶν εἰς Μακεδονίαν οὐδεμίαν ἔσχηκεν ἄνεσιν ἡ σὰρξ ἡμῶν, ἀλλ’ ἐν παντὶ θλιβόμενοι ἔξωθεν μάχαι, ἔσωθεν φόβοι

      For even when we had come into Macedonia, our flesh had no relief, but we were afflicted on every side. Fightings were outside. Fear was inside.

      Read 2 Corinthians 7:5 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 1:7

      καὶ ὑμῖν τοῖς θλιβομένοις ἄνεσιν μεθ’ ἡμῶν ἐν τῇ ἀποκαλύψει τοῦ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ ἀπ’ οὐρανοῦ μετ’ ἀγγέλων δυνάμεως αὐτοῦ

      and to give relief to you who are afflicted with us when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels in flaming fire,

      Read 2 Thessalonians 1:7 in the app

  2. to press upon (1)
    To press in, to crush: “lest the crowd should crush him” (Mark 3:9).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 3:9

      καὶ εἶπεν τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ ἵνα πλοιάριον προσκαρτερῇ αὐτῷ διὰ τὸν ὄχλον ἵνα μὴ θλίβωσιν αὐτόν·

      He spoke to his disciples that a little boat should stay near him because of the crowd, so that they wouldn’t press on him.

      Read Mark 3:9 in the app

  3. narrow (road) (1)
    Narrow, constricted: “the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life” (Matt 7:14).
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 7:14

      ὅτι στενὴ ἡ πύλη καὶ τεθλιμμένη ἡ ὁδὸς ἡ ἀπάγουσα εἰς τὴν ζωήν, καὶ ὀλίγοι εἰσὶν οἱ εὑρίσκοντες αὐτήν.

      How narrow is the gate and the way is restricted that leads to life! There are few who find it.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 θλίβω, [in LXX chiefly (ὁ θλίβων) for צוּר;] to press: c. acc. pers., Mk 3:9; ὁδὸς τεθλιμμένη, a narrow (compressed) way, Mt 7:14. Metaph. (as freq. in LXX), to oppress, afflict, distress: c. acc. pers., II Th 1:6; pass. (Vg., tribulor, tribulationem patior), II Co 1:6 4:8 7:5, I Th 3:4, II Th 1:7, I Ti 5:10, He 11:37 (cf. ἀπο-, συν-θλίβω).†
