Senses (NGSD)

  1. birth pangs (4)
    The pains of childbirth, labor, anguish: “birth pains”, “the pains of death”, “the beginning of the birth pains”, “the first of the birth pains”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 24:8

      πάντα δὲ ταῦτα ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων.

      But all these things are the beginning of birth pains.

      Read Matthew 24:8 in the app

    • Mark 13:8

      ἐγερθήσεται γὰρ ἔθνος ἐπ’ ἔθνος καὶ βασιλεία ἐπὶ βασιλείαν, ἔσονται σεισμοὶ κατὰ τόπους, ἔσονται λιμοί· ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων ταῦτα.

      For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places. There will be famines and troubles. These things are the beginning of birth pains.

      Read Mark 13:8 in the app

    • Acts 2:24

      ὃν ὁ θεὸς ἀνέστησεν λύσας τὰς ὠδῖνας τοῦ θανάτου, καθότι οὐκ ἦν δυνατὸν κρατεῖσθαι αὐτὸν ὑπ’ αὐτοῦ·

      whom God raised up, having freed him from the agony of death, because it was not possible that he should be held by it.
