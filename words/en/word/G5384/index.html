Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

29 φίλος, -η, -ον [in LXX chiefly for רֵעַ, אֹהֵב ;] pass., beloved, dear (Hom., Eur., al.). Act., loving, friendly (in cl. less freq. and only in poets): Ac 19:31. As subst., a friend; masc., ὁ φ.: Lk 7:6 11:5 14:10 15:6 16:9 21:16 23:12, Ac 27:3, III Jn 15; opp. to δοῦλος, Jo 15:15; φ. ἀναγκαῖοι, Ac 10:24; c. gen. subj., Mt 11:19, Lk 7:34 11:6, 8 12:4 14:12 15:29, Jo 11:11 15:13, 14; ὁ φ. τοῦ νυμφίου, Jo 3:29; τ. Καίσαρος (v. Deiss., BS 167; LAE, 382 f.), Jo 19:12; θεοῦ (v. Hort, in l.), Ja 2:23; c. gen. rei, τ, κόσμου, Ja 4:4; fem., ἡ φ., Lk 15:9,†
