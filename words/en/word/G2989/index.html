Senses (NGSD)

  1. to shine, to give light (7)
    To give off light, to shine, to illuminate: “let light shine out of darkness”, “God has shone in our hearts”, “a light shone and lit up the prison”, “as the lightning … flashes”, “his face shone like the sun”, “gives light to all in the house”, “let your light shine before others”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:15

      οὐδὲ καίουσιν λύχνον καὶ τιθέασιν αὐτὸν ὑπὸ τὸν μόδιον ἀλλ’ ἐπὶ τὴν λυχνίαν, καὶ λάμπει πᾶσιν τοῖς ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ.

      Neither do you light a lamp and put it under a measuring basket, but on a stand; and it shines to all who are in the house.

      He was changed before them. His face shone like the sun, and his garments became as white as the light.
