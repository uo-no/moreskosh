Senses (NGSD)

  1. mute (9)
    Unable to speak: “a mute man”, “the mute spoke”, “the mute speaking”, “unable to utter words”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 9:32

      Αὐτῶν δὲ ἐξερχομένων ἰδοὺ προσήνεγκαν αὐτῷ ἄνθρωπον κωφὸν δαιμονιζόμενον·

      As they went out, behold, a mute man who was demon possessed was brought to him.

    • Matthew 12:22

      Then one possessed by a demon, blind and mute, was brought to him; and he healed him, so that the blind and mute man both spoke and saw.

      so that the multitude wondered when they saw the mute speaking, the injured healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing—and they glorified the God of Israel.

      the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 κωφός, -ή, -όν (< κόπτω), [in LXX chiefly (Ex 4:11, al.) for אִלֵּם; for חֵרֵשׁ, Hb 2:18;] blunt, dull. Metaph., of the senses, esp. of speech, dumb: Mt 9:32, 33 12:22 15:30, 31, Lk 1:22 11:14; of hearing, deaf: Mt 11:5, Mk 7:32, 37 9:25, Lk 7:22.†
