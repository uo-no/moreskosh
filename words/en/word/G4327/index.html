Senses (NGSD)

  1. to welcome / accept (6)
    To receive, accept, welcome: “this man receives sinners”, “welcome him in the Lord with all joy”, “welcome her in a way worthy of the saints”, “joyfully accepted the plundering of your property”, “refusing to accept release”, “having a hope that they themselves accept”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 15:2

      καὶ διεγόγγυζον οἵ τε Φαρισαῖοι καὶ οἱ γραμματεῖς λέγοντες ὅτι Οὗτος ἁμαρτωλοὺς προσδέχεται καὶ συνεσθίει αὐτοῖς.

      The Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying, “This man welcomes sinners, and eats with them.”

  2. to wait for / await (8)
    To await, to wait in expectation: “waiting for the consolation of Israel”, “people waiting for their master to return”, “was waiting for the kingdom of God”, “waiting for our blessed hope, the appearing of the glory”, “wait for the mercy of the Lord that leads to eternal life”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 προσ-δέχομαι, [in LXX chiefly for רצה;] to receive to oneself, receive favourably, admit, accept: c. acc. pers., Lk 15:2, Ro 16:2, Phl 2:29; c. acc. rei, Ac 24:15 (R, mg.), He 10:34 11:35. to expect, look for, wait for: c. acc. pers., Lk 12:36; c. acc. rei, Mk 15:43, Lk 2:25, 38 23:51, Ac 23:21, Tit 2:13, Ju 21 (cf. δέχομαι).†
