Senses (NGSD)

  1. reason / cause (10)
    The reason, cause, or ground of a state of affairs: “for what reason”, “for this reason”, “how the matter stands”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 19:3

      Καὶ προσῆλθον αὐτῷ Φαρισαῖοι πειράζοντες αὐτὸν καὶ λέγοντες· Εἰ ἔξεστιν ἀνθρώπῳ ἀπολῦσαι τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ κατὰ πᾶσαν αἰτίαν;

      Pharisees came to him, testing him and saying, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?”

      Peter went down to the men, and said, “Behold, I am he whom you seek. Why have you come?”

  2. charge / ground of accusation (guilt) (10)
    A charge or ground of accusation brought in court, guilt: “a charge deserving death”, “the written charge”, “I find no guilt in this man”.
    Attested: NT only

      καὶ ἐπέθηκαν ἐπάνω τῆς κεφαλῆς αὐτοῦ τὴν αἰτίαν αὐτοῦ γεγραμμένην· Οὗτός ἐστιν Ἰησοῦς ὁ βασιλεὺς τῶν Ἰουδαίων.

      They set up over his head the accusation against him written, “THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

20 αἰτία, -ας, ἡ, [in LXX: Ge 4:13 (עָוֹן), Pr 28:17 (עָשַׁק), and freq. in Wi, II, III Mac;] cause, reason, occasion, case: Mt 19:3, Lk 8:47, Ac 10:21 22:24 28:20, II Ti 1:6, 12, Tit 1:13, He 2:11 ; εἰ οὕτως ἐστιν ἡ αἰ. (cf. Lat. si ita res se habet, and v. MM. VGT, s.v.), Mt 19:10. In forensic sense, accusation: Ac 25:18, 27; cause for punishment, crime: Mt 27:37, Mk 15:26, Jo 18:38 19:4, 6, Ac 13:28 23:28 28:18.† SYN.: ἔλεγχος, a charge, whether moral or judicial, which has been proven, αἰ. is an accusation simply, false or true.
