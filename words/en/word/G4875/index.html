Senses (NGSD)

  1. to rest with (1)
    To rest together, to find repose together: “find rest together”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 15:32

      ἵνα ἐν χαρᾷ ἐλθὼν πρὸς ὑμᾶς διὰ θελήματος θεοῦ συναναπαύσωμαι ὑμῖν.

      that I may come to you in joy through the will of God, and together with you, find rest.

1 συν-ανα-παύομαι [in LXX: Is 11:6 (רבץ) * ;] c. dat. pers., to lie down to rest with, sleep with. Metaph., to be refreshed in spirit with: Ro 15:32 (L, om.).†
