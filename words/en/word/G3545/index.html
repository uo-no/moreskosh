Senses (NGSD)

  1. lawfully (2)
    According to law, according to the rules: “if one uses it lawfully”, “unless one competes according to the rules”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Timothy 1:8

      Οἴδαμεν δὲ ὅτι καλὸς ὁ νόμος ἐάν τις αὐτῷ νομίμως χρῆται,

      But we know that the law is good if a person uses it lawfully,

      Read 1 Timothy 1:8 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 2:5

      ἐὰν δὲ καὶ ἀθλῇ τις, οὐ στεφανοῦται ἐὰν μὴ νομίμως ἀθλήσῃ·

      Also, if anyone competes in athletics, he isn’t crowned unless he has competed by the rules.
