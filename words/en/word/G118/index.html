Senses (NGSD)

  1. to compete in games (2)
    To compete in an athletic contest, striving for the prize: “even in competing as an athlete” (twice).
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 2 Timothy 2:5

      ἐὰν δὲ καὶ ἀθλῇ τις, οὐ στεφανοῦται ἐὰν μὴ νομίμως ἀθλήσῃ·

      Also, if anyone competes in athletics, he isn’t crowned unless he has competed by the rules.
