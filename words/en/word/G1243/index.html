Senses (NGSD)

  1. distribution, variety (3)
    A division, a kind, an apportionment: “there are different kinds of gifts”, “there are different kinds of service”, “there are different kinds of working”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 12:4

      Διαιρέσεις δὲ χαρισμάτων εἰσίν, τὸ δὲ αὐτὸ πνεῦμα·

      Now there are various kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit.

    • 1 Corinthians 12:5

      καὶ διαιρέσεις διακονιῶν εἰσιν, καὶ ὁ αὐτὸς κύριος·

      There are various kinds of service, and the same Lord.

      Read 1 Corinthians 12:5 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 12:6

      καὶ διαιρέσεις ἐνεργημάτων εἰσίν, ὁ δὲ αὐτὸς θεός, ὁ ἐνεργῶν τὰ πάντα ἐν πᾶσιν.

      There are various kinds of workings, but the same God who works all things in all.
