Senses (NGSD)

  1. incense, burning of incense (6)
    Incense; the burning of incense: “at the hour of incense”, “standing at the right side of the altar of incense”, “the smoke of the incense”, “golden bowls full of incense”, “much incense was given to him”, “incense, myrrh and frankincense”.
