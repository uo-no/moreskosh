Senses (NGSD)

  1. firstfruits (6)
    Firstfruits, what ripens first (as an offering): “raised as the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep”, “Christ the firstfruits”, “a kind of firstfruits of his creatures”, “offered as firstfruits to the Lamb”, “if the dough offered as firstfruits is holy”, “we who have the firstfruits of the Spirit”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 8:23

      οὐ μόνον δέ, ἀλλὰ καὶ αὐτοὶ τὴν ἀπαρχὴν τοῦ πνεύματος ἔχοντες ἡμεῖς καὶ αὐτοὶ ἐν ἑαυτοῖς στενάζομεν, υἱοθεσίαν ἀπεκδεχόμενοι τὴν ἀπολύτρωσιν τοῦ σώματος ἡμῶν.

      Not only so, but ourselves also, who have the first fruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for adoption, the redemption of our body.

      Read Romans 8:23 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 15:20

      Νυνὶ δὲ Χριστὸς ἐγήγερται ἐκ νεκρῶν, ἀπαρχὴ τῶν κεκοιμημένων.

      But now Christ has been raised from the dead. He became the first fruit of those who are asleep.

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:20 in the app

    • James 1:18

      βουληθεὶς ἀπεκύησεν ἡμᾶς λόγῳ ἀληθείας, εἰς τὸ εἶναι ἡμᾶς ἀπαρχήν τινα τῶν αὐτοῦ κτισμάτων.

      Of his own will he gave birth to us by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures.

      Read James 1:18 in the app

  2. first converts (firstfruits) (2)
    Those who were the first to believe in a region: “the household of Stephanas, the firstfruits of Achaia”, “the first in Asia to believe in Christ”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 16:5

      καὶ τὴν κατ’ οἶκον αὐτῶν ἐκκλησίαν. ἀσπάσασθε Ἐπαίνετον τὸν ἀγαπητόν μου, ὅς ἐστιν ἀπαρχὴ τῆς Ἀσίας εἰς Χριστόν.

      Greet the assembly that is in their house. Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the first fruits of Achaia to Christ.

      Read Romans 16:5 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 16:15

      Παρακαλῶ δὲ ὑμᾶς, ἀδελφοί· οἴδατε τὴν οἰκίαν Στεφανᾶ, ὅτι ἐστὶν ἀπαρχὴ τῆς Ἀχαΐας καὶ εἰς διακονίαν τοῖς ἁγίοις ἔταξαν ἑαυτούς·

      Now I beg you, brothers—you know the house of Stephanas, that it is the first fruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to serve the saints—

      Read 1 Corinthians 16:15 in the app

  3. beginning (including a variant reading) (1)
    From the beginning, at first: “God chose you from the beginning for salvation” (2 Thess 2:13).
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Thessalonians 2:13

      Ἡμεῖς δὲ ὀφείλομεν εὐχαριστεῖν τῷ θεῷ πάντοτε περὶ ὑμῶν, ἀδελφοὶ ἠγαπημένοι ὑπὸ κυρίου, ὅτι εἵλατο ὑμᾶς ὁ θεὸς ἀπαρχὴν εἰς σωτηρίαν ἐν ἁγιασμῷ πνεύματος καὶ πίστει ἀληθείας,

      But we are bound to always give thanks to God for you, brothers loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation through sanctification of the Spirit and belief in the truth,

      Read 2 Thessalonians 2:13 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 ἀπ-αρχή, -ῆς, ἡ (< ἀπάρχομαι, to make a beginning in sacrifice, offer first fruits), [in LXX chiefly for תְּרוּמָה, רֵאשִׁית;] the beginning of a sacrifice. first fruits: τοῦ φυράματος (cf. Nu 15:20), Ro 11:16. Metaph., ἀ. τοῦ πνεύματος: Ro 8:23; of Christians: Ro 16:5, I Co 16:15, II Th 2:13 (WH, mg., R, mg., txt., ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς; v. Lft., Notes, 119 f.), Ja 1:18, Re 14:4; of Christ: I Co 15:20, 23 (Cremer, 117; MM, VGT, s.v.).†
