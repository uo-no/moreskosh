Senses (NGSD)

  1. to happen (8)
    A thing happens, comes about: “these things happened to them as examples”, “the trial that comes upon you”, “what the proverb says has happened to them”, “trials that happened to me through the plots of the Jews”, “a disturbance arose”, “at what had happened to him”, “about all these things that had happened”, “what was to happen to him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 24:14

      καὶ αὐτοὶ ὡμίλουν πρὸς ἀλλήλους περὶ πάντων τῶν συμβεβηκότων τούτων.

      They talked with each other about all of these things which had happened.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 συμ-βαίνω [in LXX for עשׂה, קרא, קרה, etc. ;] to stand with the feet together. to come together, come to terms. Of events, to come to pass, happen: c. dat. pers., Mk 10:32, Ac 3:10 20:19, I Co 10:11, I Pe 4:12, II Pe 2:22; absol., τὰ συμβεβηκότα, Lk 24:14 (cf. I Mac 4:26); c. acc. et inf., Ac 21:35.†
