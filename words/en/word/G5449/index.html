Senses (NGSD)

  1. nature / by birth (11)
    Nature, one's inborn constitution: “does not nature itself teach you?”, “by nature children of wrath”, “those who by nature are not gods”, “exchanged natural relations for unnatural ones”, “natural branches”, “Gentiles by birth”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 1:26

      Διὰ τοῦτο παρέδωκεν αὐτοὺς ὁ θεὸς εἰς πάθη ἀτιμίας· αἵ τε γὰρ θήλειαι αὐτῶν μετήλλαξαν τὴν φυσικὴν χρῆσιν εἰς τὴν παρὰ φύσιν,

      For this reason, God gave them up to vile passions. For their women changed the natural function into that which is against nature.

      Read Romans 1:26 in the app

    • Romans 11:21

      εἰ γὰρ ὁ θεὸς τῶν κατὰ φύσιν κλάδων οὐκ ἐφείσατο, οὐδὲ σοῦ φείσεται.

      for if God didn’t spare the natural branches, neither will he spare you.

      Read Romans 11:21 in the app

    • Galatians 2:15

      Ἡμεῖς φύσει Ἰουδαῖοι καὶ οὐκ ἐξ ἐθνῶν ἁμαρτωλοί,

      “We, being Jews by nature and not Gentile sinners,

      Read Galatians 2:15 in the app

  2. species / kind (2)
    A kind or class of living beings: “every kind of beast”, “tamed by humankind”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • James 3:7

      πᾶσα γὰρ φύσις θηρίων τε καὶ πετεινῶν ἑρπετῶν τε καὶ ἐναλίων δαμάζεται καὶ δεδάμασται τῇ φύσει τῇ ἀνθρωπίνῃ·

      For every kind of animal, bird, creeping thing, and sea creature is tamed, and has been tamed by mankind;

      Read James 3:7 in the app

  3. (divine) nature (1)
    The nature of God: “become partakers of the divine nature”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Peter 1:4

      δι’ ὧν τὰ τίμια καὶ μέγιστα ἡμῖν ἐπαγγέλματα δεδώρηται, ἵνα διὰ τούτων γένησθε θείας κοινωνοὶ φύσεως, ἀποφυγόντες τῆς ἐν τῷ κόσμῳ ἐν ἐπιθυμίᾳ φθορᾶς.

      by which he has granted to us his precious and exceedingly great promises; that through these you may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world by lust.

      Read 2 Peter 1:4 in the app
