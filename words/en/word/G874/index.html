Senses (NGSD)

  1. opportunity, occasion (7)
    Opportunity, foothold, occasion: “give no occasion for slander”, “in order to cut off the opportunity”, “giving you an occasion to boast”, “not as an opportunity for the flesh”, “sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment”, “sin, taking its opportunity through the commandment”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 7:8

      ἀφορμὴν δὲ λαβοῦσα ἡ ἁμαρτία διὰ τῆς ἐντολῆς κατειργάσατο ἐν ἐμοὶ πᾶσαν ἐπιθυμίαν, χωρὶς γὰρ νόμου ἁμαρτία νεκρά.

      But sin, finding occasion through the commandment, produced in me all kinds of coveting. For apart from the law, sin is dead.

      For we are not commending ourselves to you again, but speak as giving you occasion of boasting on our behalf, that you may have something to answer those who boast in appearance and not in heart.

7 ἀφ-ορμή, -ῆς, ἡ, [in LXX: Ez 5:7 (הָמַן), Pr 9:9, III Mac 3:2*;] prop., a starting point; in war, a base of operations; metaph., an occasion, incentive, opportunity (MM, s.v.): II Co 11:12, Ga 5:13; ἀφ. λαμβάνειν, Lk 11:24, WH, mg., Ro 7:8, 11; ἀφ. διδόναι, II Co 5:12, I Ti 5:14.†
