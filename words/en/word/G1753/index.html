Senses (NGSD)

  1. working / operative power (8)
    Working, activity, the manifestation of power (of God or Satan): “by the activity of Satan”, “a strong delusion”, “faith in the powerful working of God”, “the working of God's power”, “according to the working of his might”, “when each part is working”, “by the power that enables him to subject all things”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Ephesians 1:19

      καὶ τί τὸ ὑπερβάλλον μέγεθος τῆς δυνάμεως αὐτοῦ εἰς ἡμᾶς τοὺς πιστεύοντας κατὰ τὴν ἐνέργειαν τοῦ κράτους τῆς ἰσχύος αὐτοῦ

      and what is the exceeding greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to that working of the strength of his might
