Senses (NGSD)

  1. very / exceedingly (11)
    Very much, extremely, terribly: “the disciples multiplied greatly”, “was exceedingly sorrowful”, “were terrified”, “they were greatly distressed”, “greatly astonished”, “a plague of very great hail”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 2:10

      ἰδόντες δὲ τὸν ἀστέρα ἐχάρησαν χαρὰν μεγάλην σφόδρα.

      When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.
