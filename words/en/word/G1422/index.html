Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 δύσκολος, -ον (< κόλον, food), [in LXX for אֵיד, Je 29:9 (Je 49:8) (of. δυσκολία, Jb 34:30; εὔκολος, II Ki 15:3) * ;] properly, of persons, hard to satisfy with food, hence, generally, hard to please (Eur., Plat.). Of things, difficult, hard (Arist.): Mk 10:24.†
