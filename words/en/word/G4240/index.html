Senses (NGSD)

  1. gentleness / meekness (11)
    Gentleness, meekness, mildness: “in a spirit of gentleness”, “with all humility and gentleness”, “by the meekness and gentleness of Christ”, “the fruit of the Spirit is gentleness”, “with a gentle heart”, “show perfect courtesy toward all people”, “give a defence with gentleness”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 4:21

      τί θέλετε; ἐν ῥάβδῳ ἔλθω πρὸς ὑμᾶς, ἢ ἐν ἀγάπῃ πνεύματί τε πραΰτητος;

      What do you want? Shall I come to you with a rod, or in love and a spirit of gentleness?

      Brothers, even if a man is caught in some fault, you who are spiritual must restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness, looking to yourself so that you also aren’t tempted.
