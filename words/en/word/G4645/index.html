Senses (NGSD)

  1. to harden (6)
    To harden the heart, to make stubborn (passive: to be hardened). “became stubborn and would not believe”, “do not harden your hearts”, “so that none may be hardened”, “God hardens”. The implication of ‘hardening’ in Romans 9:18, where God is the subject, is not decided.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      ὡς δέ τινες ἐσκληρύνοντο καὶ ἠπείθουν κακολογοῦντες τὴν ὁδὸν ἐνώπιον τοῦ πλήθους, ἀποστὰς ἀπ’ αὐτῶν ἀφώρισεν τοὺς μαθητάς, καθ’ ἡμέραν διαλεγόμενος ἐν τῇ σχολῇ Τυράννου.

      But when some were hardened and disobedient, speaking evil of the Way before the multitude, he departed from them and separated the disciples, reasoning daily in the school of Tyrannus.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 σκληρύνω (< σκληρός), [in LXX chiefly for קשׁה hi., also for חזק ;] to harden. Metaph., to harden, make stubborn: c. acc. pers., Ro 9:18; τ. καρδίας (Ps 94 (95):8), He 3:8, 15 4:7. Pass., to become hardened: Ac 19:9, He 3:13.†
