Senses (NGSD)

  1. on the other side of (with genitive) (13)
    With a genitive (the Jordan, the sea, a valley stream), indicates position or direction on its far side (used as a preposition).

  2. the other side (τὸ πέραν) (10)
    With the article, used as a noun for the far shore of a lake or sea; often in the form “to the other side” (εἰς τὸ πέραν).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:18

      Ἰδὼν δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς πολλοὺς ὄχλους περὶ αὐτὸν ἐκέλευσεν ἀπελθεῖν εἰς τὸ πέραν.

      Now when Jesus saw great multitudes around him, he gave the order to depart to the other side.

      Read Matthew 8:18 in the app

    • Matthew 16:5

      Καὶ ἐλθόντες οἱ μαθηταὶ εἰς τὸ πέραν ἐπελάθοντο ἄρτους λαβεῖν.

      The disciples came to the other side and had forgotten to take bread.

      Read Matthew 16:5 in the app

    • Mark 5:21

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

23 πέραν, adv., [in LXX for עֵבֶר and cognate forms;] on the other side, across (usually with the idea of water lying between); as in the older poets, as prep. c. gen.: τ. θαλάσσης, Jo 6:1, 17, 22, 25; τ. Ἰορδάνου, Mt 4:15(LXX), 25 19:1, Mk 3:8 10:1, Jo 1:28 3:26 10:40; τ. χειμάρρου τ. Κέδρων, Jo 18:1; τὸ π., the region beyond, the other side: Mt 8:18, 28 14:22 16:5, Mk 4:35 5:21 6:45 8:13; τ. θαλάσσης, Mk 5:1; τ. λίμνης, Lk 8:22.†
