Senses (NGSD)

  1. force, violence (3)
    Force, violence, impetus: “because of the violence of the crowd”, “by the force of the waves”, “not by force”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      τότε ἀπελθὼν ὁ στρατηγὸς σὺν τοῖς ὑπηρέταις ἤγαγεν αὐτούς, οὐ μετὰ βίας, ἐφοβοῦντο γὰρ τὸν λαόν, μὴ λιθασθῶσιν.

      Then the captain went with the officers, and brought them without violence, for they were afraid that the people might stone them.
