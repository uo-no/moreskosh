Senses (NGSD)

  1. to flood (1)
    To drench, to cover with a deluge: “being flooded with water”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Peter 3:6

      δι’ ὧν ὁ τότε κόσμος ὕδατι κατακλυσθεὶς ἀπώλετο·

      by which means the world that existed then, being overflowed with water, perished.
