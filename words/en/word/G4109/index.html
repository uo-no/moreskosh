Senses (NGSD)

  1. tablet (3)
    A tablet, a flat slab: “tablets of stone”, “tablets of the human heart”, “the tablets of the covenant”. The distinction between stone tablets and the tablets of the heart does not divide the sense.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 3:3

      φανερούμενοι ὅτι ἐστὲ ἐπιστολὴ Χριστοῦ διακονηθεῖσα ὑφ’ ἡμῶν, ἐγγεγραμμένη οὐ μέλανι ἀλλὰ πνεύματι θεοῦ ζῶντος, οὐκ ἐν πλαξὶν λιθίναις ἀλλ’ ἐν πλαξὶν καρδίαις σαρκίναις.

      being revealed that you are a letter of Christ, served by us, written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tablets of stone, but in tablets that are hearts of flesh.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 πλάξ, -ακός, ἡ, [in LXX for לוּחַ;] anything flat and broad. a plain (poët.). In late writers (Luc., al.), a flat stone, a tablet: II Co 3:3, He 9:4.†
