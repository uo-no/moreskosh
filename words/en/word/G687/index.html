Senses (NGSD)

  1. (interrogative particle) (3)
    A particle that introduces a question: “do you understand what you are reading”, “is Christ then a servant of sin”, “will he find faith”. The interpretation of the usage is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 18:8

      λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι ποιήσει τὴν ἐκδίκησιν αὐτῶν ἐν τάχει. πλὴν ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ἐλθὼν ἆρα εὑρήσει τὴν πίστιν ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς;

      I tell you that he will avenge them quickly. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?”

      Philip ran to him, and heard him reading Isaiah the prophet, and said, “Do you understand what you are reading?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

18 ἆρα, interrog. particle, implying anxiety or impatience, "quite rare and only in Luke and Paul, therefore a literary word" (Bl., §77, 2). (num igitur) expecting a neg. reply, Lk 18:8; ἆρά γε, Ac 8:30. (ergone) in apodosis, expecting an affirm. reply, Ga 2:17 (Bl., §77, 2; Lft., Ga., in l.; MM, s.v.).†
