Senses (NGSD)

  1. sand (5)
    Sand (including figurative uses): “the sand on the seashore”, “built his house on the sand”, “stood on the sand of the sea”, “as many as the sand of the sea”, “like sand”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 7:26

      Everyone who hears these words of mine and doesn’t do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.
