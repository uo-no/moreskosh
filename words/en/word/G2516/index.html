Senses (NGSD)

  1. to sit, to sit down (7)
    To sit, to be seated: “was sitting in a window”, “those who were sitting in the council”, “was sitting in the house”, “two angels in white were sitting”, “was sitting by the well”, “sitting among the teachers”, “was sitting in the temple teaching”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:55

      ἐν ἐκείνῃ τῇ ὥρᾳ εἶπεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς τοῖς ὄχλοις· Ὡς ἐπὶ λῃστὴν ἐξήλθατε μετὰ μαχαιρῶν καὶ ξύλων συλλαβεῖν με; καθ’ ἡμέραν ἐν τῷ ἱερῷ ἐκαθεζόμην διδάσκων καὶ οὐκ ἐκρατήσατέ με.

      In that hour Jesus said to the multitudes, “Have you come out as against a robber with swords and clubs to seize me? I sat daily in the temple teaching, and you didn’t arrest me.
