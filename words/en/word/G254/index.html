Senses (NGSD)

  1. chain (11)
    A chain, a bond: “not ashamed of my chain”, “bound with two chains”, “the chains fell off Peter's hands”, “because of this chain”, “an ambassador in chains”, “broke the chains and the shackles”, “an angel holding a great chain”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 5:3

      ὃς τὴν κατοίκησιν εἶχεν ἐν τοῖς μνήμασιν, καὶ οὐδὲ ἁλύσει οὐκέτι οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο αὐτὸν δῆσαι

      He lived in the tombs. Nobody could bind him any more, not even with chains,

      And behold, an angel of the Lord stood by him, and a light shone in the cell. He struck Peter on the side and woke him up, saying, “Stand up quickly!” His chains fell off his hands.
