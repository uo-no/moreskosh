Senses (NGSD)

  1. debauchery / sensuality (10)
    Licentiousness, lust, indecent conduct: “sensuality, passions, drunkenness”, “impurity, sexual immorality, and sensuality”, “have given themselves up to sensuality”, “their sensuality”, “lusts of the flesh, sensuality”, “their sensual conduct”, “indecency, sensuality”.
