Senses (NGSD)

  1. redemption (3)
    Redemption, deliverance: “obtaining an eternal redemption”, “he has redeemed his people”, “waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has visited and redeemed his people;
