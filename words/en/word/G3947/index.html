Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be provoked (2)
    To be irritated, to be indignant: “is not easily angered”, “his spirit was provoked”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 17:16

      Ἐν δὲ ταῖς Ἀθήναις ἐκδεχομένου αὐτοὺς τοῦ Παύλου, παρωξύνετο τὸ πνεῦμα αὐτοῦ ἐν αὐτῷ θεωροῦντος κατείδωλον οὖσαν τὴν πόλιν.

      Now while Paul waited for them at Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he saw the city full of idols.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 παρ-οξύνω, [in LXX for נאץ, קצף, רגז, etc.;] primarily, but never so in cl., to sharpen (μάχαιραν, De 32:41, שׁנן). Metaph., as always in cl., to spur on, stimulate (Arist., Xen., al.); to provoke, rouse to anger (De 9:7, 18, Ps 105 (106):29, al.): pass., Ac 17:16, I Co 13:5.†
