Senses (NGSD)

  1. grief, mourning (5)
    Grief, mourning: “let laughter be turned to mourning”, “mourning …”, “mourning and famine”, “no more mourning”, “I will never see mourning”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • James 4:9

      ταλαιπωρήσατε καὶ πενθήσατε καὶ κλαύσατε· ὁ γέλως ὑμῶν εἰς πένθος μετατραπήτω καὶ ἡ χαρὰ εἰς κατήφειαν·

      Lament, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

      However much she glorified herself and grew wanton, so much give her of torment and mourning. For she says in her heart, ‘I sit a queen, and am no widow, and will in no way see mourning.’
