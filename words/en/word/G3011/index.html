Senses (NGSD)

  1. minister, servant (5)
    A servant, one who serves in an office: “who makes his angels winds and his ministers a flame of fire”, “a minister of the sanctuary and …”, “Epaphroditus, who ministered to my need”, “ministers of God”, “a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 13:6

      διὰ τοῦτο γὰρ καὶ φόρους τελεῖτε, λειτουργοὶ γὰρ θεοῦ εἰσιν εἰς αὐτὸ τοῦτο προσκαρτεροῦντες.

      For this reason you also pay taxes, for they are servants of God’s service, continually doing this very thing.

      that I should be a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving as a priest of the Good News of God, that the offering up of the Gentiles might be made acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 λειτουργός, -οῦ, ὁ (< λιός, ἔργον), [in LXX chiefly for מְשָׁרֵת, Jos 1:1 A, III Ki 10:5, Ps 102 (103):21, Si 7:30, al.;] in cl., one who discharges a public office at his own expense, then, generally, a public servant, a minister, servant: τ. ἁγίων λ., He 8:2 (cf. Ne 10:39, Si 7:30); Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, Ro 15:16; pl., τ. θεοῦ, Ro 13:6, He 1:7(LXX); λ. ὑμῶν τ. χρείας μου, Phl 2:25.† SYN.: v.s. διάκονος, λειτουργέω.
