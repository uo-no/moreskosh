Senses (NGSD)

  1. to go ahead / precede (walk in front) (12)
    To go ahead of people, to lead the way: “I will go before you to Galilee”, “made them go ahead to the other side”, “walked at the head”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 2:9

      οἱ δὲ ἀκούσαντες τοῦ βασιλέως ἐπορεύθησαν, καὶ ἰδοὺ ὁ ἀστὴρ ὃν εἶδον ἐν τῇ ἀνατολῇ προῆγεν αὐτούς, ἕως ἐλθὼν ἐστάθη ἐπάνω οὗ ἦν τὸ παιδίον.

      They, having heard the king, went their way; and behold, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them until it came and stood over where the young child was.

      Which of the two did the will of his father?” They said to him, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Most certainly I tell you that the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering into God’s Kingdom before you.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

20 προ-άγω, [in LXX: I Ki 17:16 (נגשׁ), Wi 19:11, al.;] prop. trans., to lead on, lead forth or forward: c. acc. pers., Ac 16:30 17:5; of bringing forth to trial, Ac 12:6 (WH, txt., προσάγ-); seq. ἐπί, c. gen. pers., Ac 25:26. Intrans. (Plat., Polyb., and later writers; v. Bl., § 53, 1; MM, xxi); to lead the way, I Ti 1:18 (R, mg.), hence, to go before, precede: Lk 18:39; opp. to ἀκολουθέω, Mk 11:9; seq. εἰς, Mt 14:22, Mk 6:45, I Ti 5:24, He 7:18; c. acc. pers., Mt 2:9 21:9, Mk 10:32; seq. εἰς, Mt 21:31 26:32 28:7, Mk 14:28 16:7; to go on, advance (Si 20:27): II Jn 9.†
