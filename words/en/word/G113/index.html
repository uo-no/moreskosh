Senses (NGSD)

  1. lawless (2)
    Without regard for law, disorderly: “lawless people”, “people without law”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Peter 2:7

      καὶ δίκαιον Λὼτ καταπονούμενον ὑπὸ τῆς τῶν ἀθέσμων ἐν ἀσελγείᾳ ἀναστροφῆς ἐρρύσατο

      and delivered righteous Lot, who was very distressed by the lustful life of the wicked

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 **† ἄθεσμος, -ον (< θεσμός, law, custom), [in LXX: III Mac 5:12 6:26*;] lawless, esp. of those who violate the law of nature and conscience (cf. MM, VGT, s.v.): II Pe 2:7 3:17.† SYN.: ἀθέμιτος, ἄνομος, κακός, πονηρός, φαῦλος (v. Tr., Syn., §lxxiv; DCG, ii, 821b).
