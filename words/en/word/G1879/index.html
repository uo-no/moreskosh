Senses (NGSD)

  1. to rest upon (1)
    To rest on, to settle upon: “your peace will rest …”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 10:6

      καὶ ἐὰν ᾖ ἐκεῖ υἱὸς εἰρήνης, ἐπαναπαήσεται ἐπ’ αὐτὸν ἡ εἰρήνη ὑμῶν· εἰ δὲ μήγε, ἐφ’ ὑμᾶς ἀνακάμψει.

      If a son of peace is there, your peace will rest on him; but if not, it will return to you.

      Read Luke 10:6 in the app

  2. to rely on (1)
    To rely on, to lean upon: “rely on the law”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 2:17

      Εἰ δὲ σὺ Ἰουδαῖος ἐπονομάζῃ καὶ ἐπαναπαύῃ νόμῳ καὶ καυχᾶσαι ἐν θεῷ

      Indeed you bear the name of a Jew, rest on the law, glory in God,

      Read Romans 2:17 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ἐπ-ανα-παύω [in LXX, mid., for נוּחַ, שָׁעַן ni ;] to refresh, cause to rest (upon); mid., to rest upon: metaph., c. dat., Ro 2:17; seq. ἐπί, c. acc, Lk 10:6 (Cremer, 827).†
