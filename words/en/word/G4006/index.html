Senses (NGSD)

  1. confidence, trust (6)
    Confidence, trust, assurance: “with that confidence”, “with the confidence … that I have”, “with great confidence”, “having great confidence”, “approach boldly with confidence”, “if one would rely on the flesh”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 1:15

      Καὶ ταύτῃ τῇ πεποιθήσει ἐβουλόμην πρότερον πρὸς ὑμᾶς ἐλθεῖν, ἵνα δευτέραν χάριν σχῆτε,

      In this confidence, I was determined to come first to you, that you might have a second benefit,

      We have sent with them our brother whom we have many times proved earnest in many things, but now much more earnest, by reason of the great confidence which he has in you.
