Senses (NGSD)

  1. broad street (6)
    A wide street of a town: “carried the sick out into the streets”, “go out into its streets”, “go out into the streets and lanes of the city”, “in the street of the great city”, “flowing through the middle of the street of the city”, “the street of the city”.
