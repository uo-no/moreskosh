Senses (NGSD)

  1. to signify, to make known (6)
    To show, to make known, to intimate: “foretold by the Spirit that there would be a great famine”, “to send a prisoner without indicating the charges against him”, “Jesus … indicating by what kind of death he was to die”, “… indicating what kind of death he would die”, “in order to show …”, “he sent his angel and made it known”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 12:33

      τοῦτο δὲ ἔλεγεν σημαίνων ποίῳ θανάτῳ ἤμελλεν ἀποθνῄσκειν.

      But he said this, signifying by what kind of death he should die.

      Now he said this, signifying by what kind of death he would glorify God. When he had said this, he said to him, “Follow me.”

    • Acts 25:27

      ἄλογον γάρ μοι δοκεῖ πέμποντα δέσμιον μὴ καὶ τὰς κατ’ αὐτοῦ αἰτίας σημᾶναι.

      For it seems to me unreasonable, in sending a prisoner, not to also specify the charges against him.”
