Senses (NGSD)

  1. to commit sexual immorality (8)
    To commit immorality, to fornicate: “we must not indulge in sexual immorality as some of them did”, “the sexually immoral person sins against their own body”, “the kings of the earth committed immorality with her”, “committed immorality”, “so that they practised immorality”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      φεύγετε τὴν πορνείαν· πᾶν ἁμάρτημα ὃ ἐὰν ποιήσῃ ἄνθρωπος ἐκτὸς τοῦ σώματός ἐστιν, ὁ δὲ πορνεύων εἰς τὸ ἴδιον σῶμα ἁμαρτάνει.

      Flee sexual immorality! “Every sin that a man does is outside the body,” but he who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 πορνεύω, [in LXX for זנה;] to prostitute the body for hire. to commit fornication: Mk 10:19 (WH, mg), I Co 6:18 10:8, Re 2:14, 20. Metaph. (as in LXX: I Ch 5:25, Je 3:6, Ho 9:1, al.), of idolatry (but v. Swete, Ap., 180 f.), Re 17:2 18:3, 9 (cf. ἐκ-πορνεύω).†
