Senses (NGSD)

  1. ungodly (person) (10)
    Not revering God, ungodly: “the ungodly and the sinner”, “Christ died for the ungodly”, “who justifies the ungodly”, “the destruction of the ungodly”, “a flood upon the world of the ungodly”, “what will become of the ungodly?”, “judgment on the ungodly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      But to him who doesn’t work, but believes in him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is accounted for righteousness.
