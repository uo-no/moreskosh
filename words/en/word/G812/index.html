Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be disorderly (1)
    To disturb order, to be idle: “we were not idle”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Thessalonians 3:7

      αὐτοὶ γὰρ οἴδατε πῶς δεῖ μιμεῖσθαι ἡμᾶς, ὅτι οὐκ ἠτακτήσαμεν ἐν ὑμῖν

      For you know how you ought to imitate us. For we didn’t behave ourselves rebelliously among you,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * ἀτακτέω, -ῶ (< ἄτακτος), primarily, of soldiers marching, to be out of order, to quit the ranks; hence, metaph., to be remiss, fail in the performance of duty (in π., of truancy on the part of an apprentice): II Th 3:7 (on ἀ. and its cognates, v. M, Th., 152 ff.; MM, s.vv.).†
