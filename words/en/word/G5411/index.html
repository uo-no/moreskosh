Senses (NGSD)

  1. tribute, tax (5)
    Tribute, tax: “is it lawful to pay tribute to Caesar”, “for us to pay tribute to Caesar”, “pay tribute”, “taxes to whom taxes are due”, “you pay taxes”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 20:22

      ἔξεστιν ἡμᾶς Καίσαρι φόρον δοῦναι ἢ οὔ;

      Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 φόρος, -ου, ὁ (< φέρω), [in LXX chiefly for מַס, also for מִדָּה, etc. ;] tribute paid by a subject nation (cf. I Mac 10:33): φ. δοῦναι (I Mac 8:4, 7), Lk 20:22 23:2; ἀποδοῦναι, Ro 13:7; τελεῖν, Ro 13:6 (Hdt., al.).† SYN.: κῆνσος, τέλος (q.v.)
