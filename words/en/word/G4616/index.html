Senses (NGSD)

  1. linen cloth (for burial) (4)
    Linen cloth for wrapping a body: “wrapped it in a linen cloth”, “wrapped it in a clean linen cloth”, “bought a linen cloth, took Jesus down and wrapped him in the linen”, “wrapped it in linen”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 27:59

      καὶ λαβὼν τὸ σῶμα ὁ Ἰωσὴφ ἐνετύλιξεν αὐτὸ σινδόνι καθαρᾷ,

      Joseph took the body and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth

      Read Matthew 27:59 in the app

    • Mark 15:46

      καὶ ἀγοράσας σινδόνα καθελὼν αὐτὸν ἐνείλησεν τῇ σινδόνι καὶ ἔθηκεν αὐτὸν ἐν μνημείῳ ὃ ἦν λελατομημένον ἐκ πέτρας, καὶ προσεκύλισεν λίθον ἐπὶ τὴν θύραν τοῦ μνημείου.

      He bought a linen cloth, and taking him down, wound him in the linen cloth and laid him in a tomb which had been cut out of a rock. He rolled a stone against the door of the tomb.

      Read Mark 15:46 in the app

    • Luke 23:53

      καὶ καθελὼν ἐνετύλιξεν αὐτὸ σινδόνι, καὶ ἔθηκεν αὐτὸν ἐν μνήματι λαξευτῷ οὗ οὐκ ἦν οὐδεὶς οὔπω κείμενος.

      He took it down and wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb that was cut in stone, where no one had ever been laid.

      Read Luke 23:53 in the app

  2. linen garment (2)
    A garment or covering made of linen: “a young man wearing a linen cloth”, “left the linen cloth and ran away naked”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 14:51

      Καὶ νεανίσκος τις συνηκολούθει αὐτῷ περιβεβλημένος σινδόνα ἐπὶ γυμνοῦ, καὶ κρατοῦσιν αὐτόν,

      A certain young man followed him, having a linen cloth thrown around himself over his naked body. The young men grabbed him,

      Read Mark 14:51 in the app

    • Mark 14:52

      ὁ δὲ καταλιπὼν τὴν σινδόνα γυμνὸς ἔφυγεν.

      but he left the linen cloth and fled from them naked.

      Read Mark 14:52 in the app

6 σινδών, -όνος, ἡ [in LXX: Jg 14:12, 13A, Pr 31:24 (סָדִין), I Mac 10:64 A * ;] fine linen cloth; as used for swathing dead bodies (cf. Hdt., ii, 86): Mt 27:59, Mk 15:46, Lk 23:53; a garment or wrap of this material (cf. ICC, Jg, l.c.): Mk 14:51, 52.†
