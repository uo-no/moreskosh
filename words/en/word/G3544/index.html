Senses (NGSD)

  1. lawyer / expert in the law (8)
    One versed in the law, an expert in the law: “a lawyer stood up”, “the Pharisees and the lawyers”, “the lawyers also”, “woe to you lawyers”, “the lawyers have taken away the key of knowledge”, “Pharisees and lawyers”, “Zenas the lawyer”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 22:35

      καὶ ἐπηρώτησεν εἷς ἐξ αὐτῶν νομικὸς πειράζων αὐτόν·

      One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him.

      Read Matthew 22:35 in the app

    • Luke 10:25

      Καὶ ἰδοὺ νομικός τις ἀνέστη ἐκπειράζων αὐτὸν λέγων· Διδάσκαλε, τί ποιήσας ζωὴν αἰώνιον κληρονομήσω;

      Behold, a certain lawyer stood up and tested him, saying, “Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?”

      Read Luke 10:25 in the app

    • Luke 11:52

      οὐαὶ ὑμῖν τοῖς νομικοῖς, ὅτι ἤρατε τὴν κλεῖδα τῆς γνώσεως· αὐτοὶ οὐκ εἰσήλθατε καὶ τοὺς εἰσερχομένους ἐκωλύσατε.

      Woe to you lawyers! For you took away the key of knowledge. You didn’t enter in yourselves, and those who were entering in, you hindered.”

      Read Luke 11:52 in the app

  2. about the law (adjective) (1)
    Concerning the law, about the law: “quarrels about the law” (Titus 3:9).
    Attested: NT only

    • Titus 3:9

      μωρὰς δὲ ζητήσεις καὶ γενεαλογίας καὶ ἔρεις καὶ μάχας νομικὰς περιΐστασο, εἰσὶν γὰρ ἀνωφελεῖς καὶ μάταιοι.

      but shun foolish questionings, genealogies, strife, and disputes about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.

      Read Titus 3:9 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 ** νομικός, -ή, -όν (< νόμος), [in LXX: IV Mac 5:4*;] relating to law: μάχαι, Tit 3:9. learned in the law; as subst., ὁ ν. (EV, lawyer): Mt 22:35, Lk 10:25, Tit 3:13; pl., Lk 7:30 11:45, 46, 52 14:3 (cf. MM, xvii).† SYN.: γραμματεύς, q.v.
