Senses (NGSD)

  1. to finish, to be over (4)
    For something to come to an end; to finish, to expire: “when the seven days were nearly completed”, “when the period was ended”, “having ended every temptation”, “when all these things are about to be fulfilled”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 13:4

      Εἰπὸν ἡμῖν πότε ταῦτα ἔσται, καὶ τί τὸ σημεῖον ὅταν μέλλῃ ταῦτα συντελεῖσθαι πάντα.

      “Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign that these things are all about to be fulfilled?”

      Read Mark 13:4 in the app

    • Luke 4:2

      ἡμέρας τεσσεράκοντα πειραζόμενος ὑπὸ τοῦ διαβόλου. καὶ οὐκ ἔφαγεν οὐδὲν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ἐκείναις, καὶ συντελεσθεισῶν αὐτῶν ἐπείνασεν.

      for forty days, being tempted by the devil. He ate nothing in those days. Afterward, when they were completed, he was hungry.

      Read Luke 4:2 in the app

    • Luke 4:13

      καὶ συντελέσας πάντα πειρασμὸν ὁ διάβολος ἀπέστη ἀπ’ αὐτοῦ ἄχρι καιροῦ.

      When the devil had completed every temptation, he departed from him until another time.

      Read Luke 4:13 in the app

  2. to accomplish, to conclude (2)
    To accomplish, to bring about; to conclude a covenant: “I will conclude a new covenant”, “the Lord … will carry it out without delay”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 9:28

      λόγον γὰρ συντελῶν καὶ συντέμνων ποιήσει κύριος ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς.

      for he will finish the work and cut it short in righteousness, because the Lord will make a short work upon the earth.”

      Read Romans 9:28 in the app

    • Hebrews 8:8

      μεμφόμενος γὰρ αὐτοὺς λέγει· Ἰδοὺ ἡμέραι ἔρχονται, λέγει κύριος, καὶ συντελέσω ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον Ἰσραὴλ καὶ ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον Ἰούδα διαθήκην καινήν,

      For finding fault with them, he said, “Behold, the days are coming”, says the Lord, “that I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah;

      Read Hebrews 8:8 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 συν-τελέω, -ῶ [in LXX chiefly for כּלה pi., also for עשׂה, תּמם, etc. ;] "perfective" of τελέω (cf. M, Pr., 118), to complete, finish, bring to an end: Lk 4:13; pass., ib. 2, Jo 2:3, WH, mg., Ac 21:27. to effect, accomplish, bring to fulfilment (cf. MM, xxiv): Mk 13:4, Ro 9:28, He 8:8.†
