Senses (NGSD)

  1. head of a scroll (1)
    The end of a scroll, a roll: “the scroll”. The representative examples in the LXX are uses such as “capital of a column”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 10:7

      τότε εἶπον· Ἰδοὺ ἥκω, ἐν κεφαλίδι βιβλίου γέγραπται περὶ ἐμοῦ, τοῦ ποιῆσαι, ὁ θεός, τὸ θέλημά σου.

      Then I said, ‘Behold, I have come (in the scroll of the book it is written of me) to do your will, O God.’”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 κεφαλίς, -ίδος, ἡ (dimin. of κεφαλή), [in LXX for מְגִלָּה, וָו, etc.;] a little head. an extremity, capital (of a column). (As in Ez 2:9, Ps 39 (40):8, II Es 6:2) a roll: κ. βιβλίου, He 10:7(LXX).†
