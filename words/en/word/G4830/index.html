Senses (NGSD)

  1. fellow partaker (2)
    One who shares with others, a partner: “sharers together”, “their partners”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Ephesians 3:6

      εἶναι τὰ ἔθνη συγκληρονόμα καὶ σύσσωμα καὶ συμμέτοχα τῆς ἐπαγγελίας ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ διὰ τοῦ εὐαγγελίου,

      that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the body, and fellow partakers of his promise in Christ Jesus through the Good News,

      Read Ephesians 3:6 in the app

    • Ephesians 5:7

      μὴ οὖν γίνεσθε συμμέτοχοι αὐτῶν·

      Therefore don’t be partakers with them.
