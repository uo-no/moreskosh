2 ** εὔσπλαγχνος, -ον (εὖ, σπλάγχνον, q.v.), [in Pr Ma 7 (Camb. Manual LXX, iii, 825) ;] in Hippocr., as medical term (LS, s.v.). Metaph. (cf. εὐσιλαγχνία, Eurip., Rhes., 192), in NT, tenderhearted, compassionate: Eph 4:32, I Pe 3:8.†
