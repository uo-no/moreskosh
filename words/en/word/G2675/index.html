Senses (NGSD)

  1. to mend / repair (2)
    To mend or repair nets and the like: “mending their nets”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 4:21

      Καὶ προβὰς ἐκεῖθεν εἶδεν ἄλλους δύο ἀδελφούς, Ἰάκωβον τὸν τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου καὶ Ἰωάννην τὸν ἀδελφὸν αὐτοῦ, ἐν τῷ πλοίῳ μετὰ Ζεβεδαίου τοῦ πατρὸς αὐτῶν καταρτίζοντας τὰ δίκτυα αὐτῶν, καὶ ἐκάλεσεν αὐτούς.

      Going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. He called them.

    • Mark 1:19

      καὶ προβὰς ὀλίγον εἶδεν Ἰάκωβον τὸν τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου καὶ Ἰωάννην τὸν ἀδελφὸν αὐτοῦ, καὶ αὐτοὺς ἐν τῷ πλοίῳ καταρτίζοντας τὰ δίκτυα,

      Going on a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who were also in the boat mending the nets.

  2. to set right / restore (people) (5)

      Now I beg you, brothers, through the name of our Lord, Jesus Christ, that you all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be perfected together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

  3. to prepare / make ready (equip) (5)
    To equip, prepare, make ready, be made ready: “equip you with everything good”, “a body you have prepared for me”, “you have prepared praise”, “vessels of wrath prepared for destruction”, “one fully trained”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

13 κατ-αρτίζω, [in LXX chiefly in Pss (8:2 al.; כּוּן, etc.) and II Es (כְּלַל);] to render ἄρτιος, i.e. fit, complete; to mend, repair: Mt 4:21, Mk 1:19; to furnish completely, complete, equip, prepare: pass., Lk 6:40, Ro 9:22, He 11:3; mid., Mt 21:16(LXX), He 10:5(LXX); in ethical sense, to prepare, complete, perfect: Ga 6:1 (EV, restore), I Pe 5:10; pass., I Co 1:10 (Field, Notes, 167), II Co 13:11, He 13:21 (cf. προ-καταρτίζω).†
