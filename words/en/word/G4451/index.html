Senses (NGSD)

  1. burning, fiery trial (3)
    Burning, being on fire: “the fiery trial”, “a burning fire”. The word for the “fiery trial” in 1 Peter 4:12 is placed within the same sense.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Peter 4:12

      Ἀγαπητοί, μὴ ξενίζεσθε τῇ ἐν ὑμῖν πυρώσει πρὸς πειρασμὸν ὑμῖν γινομένῃ ὡς ξένου ὑμῖν συμβαίνοντος,

      Beloved, don’t be astonished at the fiery trial which has come upon you to test you, as though a strange thing happened to you.

      The kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived wantonly with her will weep and wail over her, when they look at the smoke of her burning,
