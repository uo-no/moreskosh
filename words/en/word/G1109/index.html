Senses (NGSD)

  1. one well acquainted (1)
    One who is expert in, one who knows: “one who is expert”. The representative examples in the LXX are the use “soothsayer”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 26:3

      μάλιστα γνώστην ὄντα σε πάντων τῶν κατὰ Ἰουδαίους ἐθῶν τε καὶ ζητημάτων· διὸ δέομαι μακροθύμως ἀκοῦσαί μου.

      especially because you are expert in all customs and questions which are among the Jews. Therefore I beg you to hear me patiently.
