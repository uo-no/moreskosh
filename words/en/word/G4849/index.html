Senses (NGSD)

  1. group (2)
    A party, a company: “in groups” (twice). The representative examples in the LXX are the use “banquet”, and this distributive use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 6:39

      καὶ ἐπέταξεν αὐτοῖς ἀνακλῖναι πάντας συμπόσια συμπόσια ἐπὶ τῷ χλωρῷ χόρτῳ.

      He commanded them that everyone should sit down in groups on the green grass.
