Senses (NGSD)

  1. to look upon (2)
    To take note of, to regard: “look upon their threats”, “the Lord … has looked upon”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:25

      ὅτι Οὕτως μοι πεποίηκεν κύριος ἐν ἡμέραις αἷς ἐπεῖδεν ἀφελεῖν ὄνειδός μου ἐν ἀνθρώποις.

      “Thus has the Lord done to me in the days in which he looked at me, to take away my reproach among men.”

      Read Luke 1:25 in the app

    • Acts 4:29

      καὶ τὰ νῦν, κύριε, ἔπιδε ἐπὶ τὰς ἀπειλὰς αὐτῶν καὶ δὸς τοῖς δούλοις σου μετὰ παρρησίας πάσης λαλεῖν τὸν λόγον σου,

      Now, Lord, look at their threats, and grant to your servants to speak your word with all boldness,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 * ἐπ-εῖδον 2 aor. without pres. in use; [in LXX chiefly for רָאָה ;] to regard with attention, look upon (in cl., of the gods) with a view to bless: c. inf., Lk 1:25 (cf. DB, 136 b). To punish: seq. ἐπί, Ac 4:29.†
