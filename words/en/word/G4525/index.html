Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be shaken (1)
    To be disturbed, to be moved: “so that no one would be shaken”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Thessalonians 3:3

      τὸ μηδένα σαίνεσθαι ἐν ταῖς θλίψεσιν ταύταις. αὐτοὶ γὰρ οἴδατε ὅτι εἰς τοῦτο κείμεθα·

      that no one would be moved by these afflictions. For you know that we are appointed to this task.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * σαίνω prop. (Hom., al.), of dogs, to wag the tail, fawn. Metaph., of persons, c. acc., to fawn upon, flatter, beguile: pass., I Th 3:3 (for conjectural emendations of the text, v. ICC, and M, Th., in l.).†
