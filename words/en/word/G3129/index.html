Senses (NGSD)

  1. to learn / acquire by being taught (18)
    To receive teaching, knowledge, or content from people or through observation and make it one's own. What is learned is shown by an object (a teaching, a parable, a song, Christ) or a clause; the one learned from may be shown with “from”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 9:13

      πορευθέντες δὲ μάθετε τί ἐστιν· Ἔλεος θέλω καὶ οὐ θυσίαν· οὐ γὰρ ἦλθον καλέσαι δικαίους ἀλλὰ ἁμαρτωλούς.

      But you go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, and not sacrifice,’ for I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”

      Read Matthew 9:13 in the app

    • John 7:15

      ἐθαύμαζον οὖν οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι λέγοντες· Πῶς οὗτος γράμματα οἶδεν μὴ μεμαθηκώς;

      The Jews therefore marveled, saying, “How does this man know letters, having never been educated?”

      Read John 7:15 in the app

    • Ephesians 4:20

      ὑμεῖς δὲ οὐχ οὕτως ἐμάθετε τὸν Χριστόν,

      But you didn’t learn Christ that way,

      Read Ephesians 4:20 in the app

  2. to find out / be informed (2)
    To come to know a fact by hearing it or by inquiring. The focus is not on a process of learning but on grasping the fact.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 23:27

      τὸν ἄνδρα τοῦτον συλλημφθέντα ὑπὸ τῶν Ἰουδαίων καὶ μέλλοντα ἀναιρεῖσθαι ὑπ’ αὐτῶν ἐπιστὰς σὺν τῷ στρατεύματι ἐξειλάμην, μαθὼν ὅτι Ῥωμαῖός ἐστιν,

      “This man was seized by the Jews, and was about to be killed by them when I came with the soldiers and rescued him, having learned that he was a Roman.

      Read Acts 23:27 in the app

    • Galatians 3:2

      τοῦτο μόνον θέλω μαθεῖν ἀφ’ ὑμῶν, ἐξ ἔργων νόμου τὸ πνεῦμα ἐλάβετε ἢ ἐξ ἀκοῆς πίστεως;

      I just want to learn this from you: Did you receive the Spirit by the works of the law, or by hearing of faith?

      Read Galatians 3:2 in the app

  3. to learn to do / acquire as a habit (5)
    To make an attitude or action one's own habit through experience or training. Used with an infinitive or with words expressing an attitude or state.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Philippians 4:11

      οὐχ ὅτι καθ’ ὑστέρησιν λέγω, ἐγὼ γὰρ ἔμαθον ἐν οἷς εἰμι αὐτάρκης εἶναι·

      Not that I speak because of lack, for I have learned in whatever state I am, to be content in it.

      Read Philippians 4:11 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 5:4

      εἰ δέ τις χήρα τέκνα ἢ ἔκγονα ἔχει, μανθανέτωσαν πρῶτον τὸν ἴδιον οἶκον εὐσεβεῖν καὶ ἀμοιβὰς ἀποδιδόναι τοῖς προγόνοις, τοῦτο γάρ ἐστιν ἀπόδεκτον ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ.

      But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them learn first to show piety toward their own family and to repay their parents, for this is acceptable in the sight of God.

      Let our people also learn to maintain good works to meet necessary needs, that they may not be unfruitful.
