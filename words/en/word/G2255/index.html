Senses (NGSD)

  1. half (5)
    Half: “half of my goods”, “up to half of my kingdom”, “a time, times and half a time”, “three and a half days”, “after three and a half days”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 6:23

      He swore to her, “Whatever you ask of me, I will give you, up to half of my kingdom.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ἥμισυς, -εια, -υ, gen., -ους (late, as in π., for cl., -εος; Bl., §8, 4; Mayser, 294), n. p1., ἡμίσια (Rec., cl., -εια), [in LXX for חֲצִי, מַחֲצִית;] half; as adj., agreeing in gender and number with the substantive following, τὰ ἡμίσια τ. ὑταρχόντων, Lk 19:8. As neut. subst., τὸ ἥμισυ, the half; ἥ. (anarth.), a half: c. gen., ἥ. καιροῦ, Re 12:14; ἕως ἡμίσους τ. βασιλείας μου, Mk 6:23; after a cardinal number, ἡμέρας τρεῖς κ. ἥμισυ, Re 11:9, 11.†
