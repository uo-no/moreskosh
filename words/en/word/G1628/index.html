Senses (NGSD)

  1. to escape (8)
    To escape, to flee away: “they will not escape”, “escaped his hands”, “supposing that the prisoners had escaped”, “they fled out of that house naked and wounded”, “how shall we escape if we neglect such a great salvation?”, “if they did not escape who refused him who warned them on earth”, “to escape all these things”, “do you suppose you will escape the judgment of God?”
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 21:36

      ἀγρυπνεῖτε δὲ ἐν παντὶ καιρῷ δεόμενοι ἵνα κατισχύσητε ἐκφυγεῖν ταῦτα πάντα τὰ μέλλοντα γίνεσθαι, καὶ σταθῆναι ἔμπροσθεν τοῦ υἱοῦ τοῦ ἀνθρώπου.

      Therefore be watchful all the time, praying that you may be counted worthy to escape all these things that will happen, and to stand before the Son of Man.”
