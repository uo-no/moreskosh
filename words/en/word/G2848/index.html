Senses (NGSD)

  1. grain, seed (7)
    A grain or seed of a crop: “it is like a grain of mustard seed”, “faith like a grain of mustard seed”, “unless a grain of wheat falls into the ground and dies”, “what you sow is merely a bare grain, of wheat or of some other crop”, “like a mustard seed”.
