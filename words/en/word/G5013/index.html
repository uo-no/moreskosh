Senses (NGSD)

  1. to humble oneself / be humbled (9)
    To humble oneself, become humble, be brought low: “whoever humbles himself will be exalted”, “humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God”, “humble yourselves before the Lord”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 18:4

      ὅστις οὖν ταπεινώσει ἑαυτὸν ὡς τὸ παιδίον τοῦτο, οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ μείζων ἐν τῇ βασιλείᾳ τῶν οὐρανῶν·

      Whoever therefore humbles himself as this little child is the greatest in the Kingdom of Heaven.

      Read Matthew 18:4 in the app

    • Luke 14:11

      ὅτι πᾶς ὁ ὑψῶν ἑαυτὸν ταπεινωθήσεται καὶ ὁ ταπεινῶν ἑαυτὸν ὑψωθήσεται.

      For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.”

      Read Luke 14:11 in the app

    • James 4:10

      ταπεινώθητε ἐνώπιον κυρίου, καὶ ὑψώσει ὑμᾶς.

      Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he will exalt you.

      Read James 4:10 in the app

  2. to make oneself lowly (2)
    To bring oneself low, to abase oneself: “he humbled himself”, “becoming obedient to the point of death”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 11:7

      Ἢ ἁμαρτίαν ἐποίησα ἐμαυτὸν ταπεινῶν ἵνα ὑμεῖς ὑψωθῆτε, ὅτι δωρεὰν τὸ τοῦ θεοῦ εὐαγγέλιον εὐηγγελισάμην ὑμῖν;

      Or did I commit a sin in humbling myself that you might be exalted, because I preached to you God’s Good News free of charge?

      Read 2 Corinthians 11:7 in the app

    • Philippians 2:8

      ἐταπείνωσεν ἑαυτὸν γενόμενος ὑπήκοος μέχρι θανάτου, θανάτου δὲ σταυροῦ·

      And being found in human form, he humbled himself, becoming obedient to the point of death, yes, the death of the cross.

      Read Philippians 2:8 in the app

  3. to humiliate (a person) (1)
    To shame or humble someone: “lest my God humble me before you”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 12:21

      μὴ πάλιν ἐλθόντος μου ταπεινώσῃ με ὁ θεός μου πρὸς ὑμᾶς, καὶ πενθήσω πολλοὺς τῶν προημαρτηκότων καὶ μὴ μετανοησάντων ἐπὶ τῇ ἀκαθαρσίᾳ καὶ πορνείᾳ καὶ ἀσελγείᾳ ᾗ ἔπραξαν.

      that again when I come my God would humble me before you, and I would mourn for many of those who have sinned before now, and not repented of the uncleanness, sexual immorality, and lustfulness which they committed.

      Read 2 Corinthians 12:21 in the app

  4. to make low (terrain) (1)
    To lower the land, make it level: “every mountain and hill shall be made low”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 3:5

      πᾶσα φάραγξ πληρωθήσεται καὶ πᾶν ὄρος καὶ βουνὸς ταπεινωθήσεται, καὶ ἔσται τὰ σκολιὰ εἰς εὐθείαν καὶ αἱ τραχεῖαι εἰς ὁδοὺς λείας·

      Every valley will be filled. Every mountain and hill will be brought low. The crooked will become straight, and the rough ways smooth.

      Read Luke 3:5 in the app

  5. to endure want (1)
    To accept hardship, to be in straitened circumstances: “I know how to be brought low”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 4:12

      οἶδα καὶ ταπεινοῦσθαι, οἶδα καὶ περισσεύειν· ἐν παντὶ καὶ ἐν πᾶσιν μεμύημαι, καὶ χορτάζεσθαι καὶ πεινᾶν, καὶ περισσεύειν καὶ ὑστερεῖσθαι·

      I know how to be humbled, and I also know how to abound. In any and all circumstances I have learned the secret both to be filled and to be hungry, both to abound and to be in need.

      Read Philippians 4:12 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 ταπεινόω, -ῶ (< ταπεινός), [in LXX chiefly for עָנָה, also for שָׁפֵל, כָּנַע, etc. ;] to make low: ὄρος (βουνόν), Lk 3:5 (LXX). Metaph., to humble, abase: Mt 18:4 23:12, Lk 14:11 18:14, II Co 11:7 12:21, Phl 2:8; pass., Mt 23:12, Lk 14:11 18:14, Phl 4:12; id. with mid. sense, Ja 4:10, I Pe 5:6.†
