Senses (NGSD)

  1. long robe / fine garment (9)
    A long robe, a fine garment, festal dress: “bring the best robe”, “walk around in long robes”, “dressed in a white robe”, “a white robe was given them”, “clothed in white robes”, “who are these clothed in white robes?”, “have washed their robes”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 στολή, -ῆς, ἡ (< στέλλω), [in LXX chiefly for בֶּגֶד, also for לְבֻשׁ, etc. ;] an equipment, an armament (Æsch.). Equipment in clothes, apparel, esp. flowing raiment, a festal robe: (cf. Jh 3:6, Es 8:15, I Mac 6:15): Mk 12:38 16:5, Lk 15:22 20:46, Re 6:11 7:9, 13, 14 22:14.†
