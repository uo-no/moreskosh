Senses (NGSD)

  1. relative / kinsman (12)
    A relative, kin, one of the same people: “invite relatives and friends”, “Elizabeth your relative”, “not without honour except among his relatives”, “my kinsmen”, “Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 6:4

      καὶ ἔλεγεν αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς ὅτι Οὐκ ἔστιν προφήτης ἄτιμος εἰ μὴ ἐν τῇ πατρίδι αὐτοῦ καὶ ἐν τοῖς συγγενεῦσιν αὐτοῦ καὶ ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ αὐτοῦ.

      Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor, except in his own country, and among his own relatives, and in his own house.”

      but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day’s journey; and they looked for him among their relatives and acquaintances.
