Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be a concern / care (μέλει) (10)
    To be of concern or interest, to care: “is it for oxen that God is concerned?”, “do not be concerned about it”, “he cares for you”, “Gallio paid no attention to any of this”, “cares nothing for the sheep”, “not because he cared about the poor”, “do you not care?”, “you are not swayed by appearances”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 4:38

      καὶ αὐτὸς ἦν ἐν τῇ πρύμνῃ ἐπὶ τὸ προσκεφάλαιον καθεύδων· καὶ ἐγείρουσιν αὐτὸν καὶ λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Διδάσκαλε, οὐ μέλει σοι ὅτι ἀπολλύμεθα;

      He himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion; and they woke him up and asked him, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are dying?”

    • John 10:13

      ὅτι μισθωτός ἐστιν καὶ οὐ μέλει αὐτῷ περὶ τῶν προβάτων.

      The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand and doesn’t care for the sheep.

      Read John 10:13 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 7:21

      Δοῦλος ἐκλήθης; μή σοι μελέτω· ἀλλ’ εἰ καὶ δύνασαι ἐλεύθερος γενέσθαι, μᾶλλον χρῆσαι.

      Were you called being a bondservant? Don’t let that bother you, but if you get an opportunity to become free, use it.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 μέλω, [in LXX: Jb 22:3 (חֵפֶץ), To 10:5, Wi 12:13, I Mac 14:42, 43*;] intrans., to be an object of care, be a care; commonly in third pers.: c. dat. pers., Ac 18:17; very freq. impers., I Co 7:21; seq. ὅτι, Mk 4:38, Lk 10:40; c. gen. rei (as freq. in Att.), I Co 9:9; seq. περί, Mt 22:16, Mk 12:14, Jo 10:13 12:6, I Pe 5:7. Trans., in act. and mid., to care for (not in LXX or NT).†
