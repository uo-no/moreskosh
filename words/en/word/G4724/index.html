Senses (NGSD)

  1. to avoid, to keep away (2)
    To avoid, to draw back from: “seeking to avoid”, “keep away”. The representative examples in the LXX are the uses “to equip” and “to sail”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 8:20

      στελλόμενοι τοῦτο μή τις ἡμᾶς μωμήσηται ἐν τῇ ἁδρότητι ταύτῃ τῇ διακονουμένῃ ὑφ’ ἡμῶν,

      We are avoiding this, that any man should blame us concerning this abundance which is administered by us.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 στέλλω [in LXX (mid.): Ma 2:5 (חתת ni.), Pr 31:24 (26), Wi 7:14 14:1, II Mac 5:1, III Mac 1:19 4:11 * ;] prop., to set, place, arrange, fit out; hence, mid., to set oneself for, prepare (Wi, II Mac, ll. c.). to bring together, gather up (in Hom. of furling sails), hence to restrain, check; mid., to restrain or withdraw oneself, hold aloof, avoid: II Co 8:20; seq. ἀπό, II Th 3:6 (cf. ἀπο-, ἐξ-απο-, συν-απο-, δια-, ἐπι-, κατα-, συ(ν)-, ὑπο-στέλλω).†
