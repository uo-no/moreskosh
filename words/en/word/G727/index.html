Senses (NGSD)

  1. swindler, robber (4)
    One who seizes by force, a plunderer: “the greedy and plunderers”, “one who seizes by force”, “the greedy and plunderers”, “the plunderer”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Luke 18:11

      ὁ Φαρισαῖος σταθεὶς πρὸς ἑαυτὸν ταῦτα προσηύχετο· Ὁ θεός, εὐχαριστῶ σοι ὅτι οὐκ εἰμὶ ὥσπερ οἱ λοιποὶ τῶν ἀνθρώπων, ἅρπαγες, ἄδικοι, μοιχοί, ἢ καὶ ὡς οὗτος ὁ τελώνης·

      The Pharisee stood and prayed by himself like this: ‘God, I thank you that I am not like the rest of men: extortionists, unrighteous, adulterers, or even like this tax collector.

      yet not at all meaning with the sexual sinners of this world, or with the covetous and extortionists, or with idolaters, for then you would have to leave the world.

    Rapacious, grasping: “inwardly they are ravenous wolves”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      “Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves.
