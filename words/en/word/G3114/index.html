Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be patient / forbearing (8)
    To be patient, to be forbearing, to wait: “love is patient”, “be patient with them all”, “God is patient”, “be patient until the coming of the Lord”, “have patience with me, and I will pay you”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 18:26

      πεσὼν οὖν ὁ δοῦλος προσεκύνει αὐτῷ λέγων· Μακροθύμησον ἐπ’ ἐμοί, καὶ πάντα ἀποδώσω σοι.

      The servant therefore fell down and knelt before him, saying, ‘Lord, have patience with me, and I will repay you all!’

      Read Matthew 18:26 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 13:4

      Ἡ ἀγάπη μακροθυμεῖ, χρηστεύεται ἡ ἀγάπη, οὐ ζηλοῖ ἡ ἀγάπη, οὐ περπερεύεται, οὐ φυσιοῦται,

      Love is patient and is kind. Love doesn’t envy. Love doesn’t brag, is not proud,

      Read 1 Corinthians 13:4 in the app

    • James 5:7

      Μακροθυμήσατε οὖν, ἀδελφοί, ἕως τῆς παρουσίας τοῦ κυρίου. ἰδοὺ ὁ γεωργὸς ἐκδέχεται τὸν τίμιον καρπὸν τῆς γῆς, μακροθυμῶν ἐπ’ αὐτῷ ἕως λάβῃ πρόϊμον καὶ ὄψιμον.

      Be patient therefore, brothers, until the coming of the Lord. Behold, the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the earth, being patient over it, until it receives the early and late rain.

      Read James 5:7 in the app

  2. to wait patiently (1)
    To wait quietly with endurance: “Abraham, having patiently waited” (Heb 6:15).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Hebrews 6:15

      καὶ οὕτως μακροθυμήσας ἐπέτυχεν τῆς ἐπαγγελίας.

      Thus, having patiently endured, he obtained the promise.

      Read Hebrews 6:15 in the app

  3. to keep waiting / delay (1)
    To make wait, to be long about: “will he delay long over his chosen ones?” (Luke 18:7).
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 18:7

      ὁ δὲ θεὸς οὐ μὴ ποιήσῃ τὴν ἐκδίκησιν τῶν ἐκλεκτῶν αὐτοῦ τῶν βοώντων αὐτῷ ἡμέρας καὶ νυκτός, καὶ μακροθυμεῖ ἐπ’ αὐτοῖς;

      Won’t God avenge his chosen ones who are crying out to him day and night, and yet he exercises patience with them?

      Read Luke 18:7 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 † μακροθυμέω, -ῶ (< μακρό-θυμος, long-tempered; v.s. -ία), [in LXX: Ec 8:12 א2, Pr 19:11 (ארךְ, אַף הֶאֱרִיךְ), Jb 7:16, Si 2:4, al.;] actively = καρτερέω, to persevere (Plut., 2, 593 F). Passively, to be patient, long-suffering: absol., I Co 13:4, He 6:15, Ja 5:8; seq. ἕως, c. gen., Ja 5:7; ἐπί, c. dat., Ja 5:7. (Si 2:4); πρός, c. acc., I Th 5:14; ἐπί, c. dat., Mt 18:26, 29, Lk 18:7; εἰς, II Pe 3:9.† SYN.: ὑπομένω, q.v.
