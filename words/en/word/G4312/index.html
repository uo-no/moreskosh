Senses (NGSD)

  1. rash (2)
    Reckless, hasty: “reckless”, “do nothing rash”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 19:36

      Seeing then that these things can’t be denied, you ought to be quiet and to do nothing rash.
