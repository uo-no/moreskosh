Senses (NGSD)

  1. to know, to be aware (2)
    To be conscious of something against oneself, to be aware, to know: “I am not aware of anything against myself”, “with his wife also knowing of it”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 5:2

      καὶ ἐνοσφίσατο ἀπὸ τῆς τιμῆς, συνειδυίης καὶ τῆς γυναικός, καὶ ἐνέγκας μέρος τι παρὰ τοὺς πόδας τῶν ἀποστόλων ἔθηκεν.

      and kept back part of the price, his wife also being aware of it, then brought a certain part and laid it at the apostles’ feet.

      Read Acts 5:2 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 4:4

      οὐδὲν γὰρ ἐμαυτῷ σύνοιδα, ἀλλ’ οὐκ ἐν τούτῳ δεδικαίωμαι, ὁ δὲ ἀνακρίνων με κύριός ἐστιν.

      For I know nothing against myself. Yet I am not justified by this, but he who judges me is the Lord.

      Read 1 Corinthians 4:4 in the app

  2. to realize, to notice (2)
    To become aware of a matter (aorist participle): “when he realized this”, “becoming aware of this”. The forms in the source packet (συνιδών, συνιδόντες) could also be seen as forms of συνοράω, but they are assigned as standing under the same number.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 12:12

      Συνιδών τε ἦλθεν ἐπὶ τὴν οἰκίαν τῆς Μαρίας τῆς μητρὸς Ἰωάννου τοῦ ἐπικαλουμένου Μάρκου, οὗ ἦσαν ἱκανοὶ συνηθροισμένοι καὶ προσευχόμενοι.

      Thinking about that, he came to the house of Mary, the mother of John who was called Mark, where many were gathered together and were praying.

      Read Acts 12:12 in the app

    • Acts 14:6

      συνιδόντες κατέφυγον εἰς τὰς πόλεις τῆς Λυκαονίας Λύστραν καὶ Δέρβην καὶ τὴν περίχωρον,

      they became aware of it and fled to the cities of Lycaonia, Lystra, Derbe, and the surrounding region.

2 ** συν-εῖδον irreg. aor. of συνοράω (v.s. εἶδον), [in LXX: I Mac 4:21א, II Mac 2:24 4:4, 41 R 5:17 7:4, 20 8:8, III Mac 2:8 A * ;] to see together or at the same time (Arist., Xen.). to see in one view, hence, of mental vision, to comprehend, understand (LXX, Polyb., Plut., al.) : Ac 12:12 14:6 (on the related σύνοιδα, v. s.v.).†
