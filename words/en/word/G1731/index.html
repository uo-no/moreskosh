Senses (NGSD)

  1. to show / demonstrate (11)
    To show, make clear, prove: “might display his perfect patience”, “give proof of your love”, “show that the work of the law is written on their hearts”, “to show his wrath and make known his power”, “showing all good faith”, “might show the immeasurable riches of his grace”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 2:15

      οἵτινες ἐνδείκνυνται τὸ ἔργον τοῦ νόμου γραπτὸν ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις αὐτῶν, συμμαρτυρούσης αὐτῶν τῆς συνειδήσεως καὶ μεταξὺ ἀλλήλων τῶν λογισμῶν κατηγορούντων ἢ καὶ ἀπολογουμένων,

      in that they show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience testifying with them, and their thoughts among themselves accusing or else excusing them)

      Therefore show the proof of your love to them before the assemblies, and of our boasting on your behalf.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 ἐν-δείκνυμι [in LXX for לָכַד, גָּמַל, רָאָה hi. ;] to mark, point out. Mid„ to show forth, prove: c. acc rei, Ro 2:15 9:22, Eph 2:7, Tit 2:10 3:2, He 6:11; seq. ἐν, c. dat. pers., Ro 9:17 (LXX), I Ti 1:16; seq. εἰς, He 6:10 (c. cogn. acc), II Co 8:24. to manifest (by act): c. acc rei et dat. pers., II Ti 4:14 (cf. Ge 50:15, 17, and v. MM, Exp., xiii).†
