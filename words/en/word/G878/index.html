Senses (NGSD)

  1. foolish (person) (11)
    Foolish, senseless: “You foolish one! What you sow …”, “the ignorance of foolish people”, “accept me as a fool”, “do not be foolish”, “Fool! This night …”, “you foolish ones”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 11:40

      ἄφρονες, οὐχ ὁ ποιήσας τὸ ἔξωθεν καὶ τὸ ἔσωθεν ἐποίησεν;

      You foolish ones, didn’t he who made the outside make the inside also?

      Read Luke 11:40 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 15:36

      ἄφρων, σὺ ὃ σπείρεις, οὐ ζῳοποιεῖται ἐὰν μὴ ἀποθάνῃ·

      You foolish one, that which you yourself sow is not made alive unless it dies.

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:36 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 12:6

      ἐὰν γὰρ θελήσω καυχήσασθαι, οὐκ ἔσομαι ἄφρων, ἀλήθειαν γὰρ ἐρῶ· φείδομαι δέ, μή τις εἰς ἐμὲ λογίσηται ὑπὲρ ὃ βλέπει με ἢ ἀκούει τι ἐξ ἐμοῦ,

      For if I would desire to boast, I will not be foolish; for I will speak the truth. But I refrain, so that no man may think more of me than that which he sees in me or hears from me.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 ἄφρων, -ον gen. -ονος (< φρήν), [in LXX for כְּסִיל, נָבָל, etc.;] without reason, senseless, foolish, expressing "want of mental sanity and sobriety, a reckless and inconsiderate habit of mind" (Hort; cf. MM, s.v.): Lk 11:40 12:20, Ro 2:20, I Co 15:36, II Co 11:16 12:6, 11, I Pe 2:15; opp. to φρόνιμος, II Co 11:19; to συνιέντες, Eph 2:17.†
