Senses (NGSD)

  1. unceasing (2)
    Constant, never stopping: “constantly”, “unceasing anguish”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 9:2

      ὅτι λύπη μοί ἐστιν μεγάλη καὶ ἀδιάλειπτος ὀδύνη τῇ καρδίᾳ μου·

      that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.
