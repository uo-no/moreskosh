Senses (NGSD)

  1. place of honor (5)
    The chief seat at a banquet or in a synagogue: “choosing the places of honour”, “do not sit down in the place of honour”, “the place of honour at feasts”, “the best seats in the synagogues”, “love the place of honour”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 23:6

      φιλοῦσι δὲ τὴν πρωτοκλισίαν ἐν τοῖς δείπνοις καὶ τὰς πρωτοκαθεδρίας ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς

      and love the place of honor at feasts, the best seats in the synagogues,
