Senses (NGSD)

  1. to sing praise (5)
    To sing, to praise (including with instruments): “pray with the spirit and sing with the spirit”, “singing praise to the Lord with your heart”, “let him sing psalms of praise”, “I will sing praise to your name”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 15:9

      τὰ δὲ ἔθνη ὑπὲρ ἐλέους δοξάσαι τὸν θεόν· καθὼς γέγραπται· Διὰ τοῦτο ἐξομολογήσομαί σοι ἐν ἔθνεσι, καὶ τῷ ὀνόματί σου ψαλῶ.

      and that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy. As it is written, “Therefore I will give praise to you among the Gentiles and sing to your name.”

    • 1 Corinthians 14:15

      τί οὖν ἐστιν; προσεύξομαι τῷ πνεύματι, προσεύξομαι δὲ καὶ τῷ νοΐ· ψαλῶ τῷ πνεύματι, ψαλῶ δὲ καὶ τῷ νοΐ·

      What should I do? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also. I will sing with the spirit, and I will sing with the understanding also.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ψάλλω [in LXX chiefly for זמר pi. (Jg 5:3, Ps 7:17, al.), also for נגן pi. (I Ki 16:16ff., al.) ;] to pull, twitch, twang (as a bowstring, etc.; Æsch., Eur., al.), hence, absol., to play a stringed instrument with the fingers (Hdt., Plat., al.); later, to sing to a harp, sing psalms (LXX); in NT, to sing a hymn, sing praise: Ja 5:13; c. dat. pers., Ro 15:9 (LXX), Eph 5:19; dat. instr., I Co 14:15.†
