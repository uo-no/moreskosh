Senses (NGSD)

  1. to work together (5)
    To work together, to cooperate: “every fellow worker and labourer”, “working together with God”, “faith was working together with his works”, “the Lord worked with them”, “all things work together for good”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 16:20

      ἐκεῖνοι δὲ ἐξελθόντες ἐκήρυξαν πανταχοῦ, τοῦ κυρίου συνεργοῦντος καὶ τὸν λόγον βεβαιοῦντος διὰ τῶν ἐπακολουθούντων σημείων.

      They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed. Amen.

      Read Mark 16:20 in the app

    • Romans 8:28

      Οἴδαμεν δὲ ὅτι τοῖς ἀγαπῶσι τὸν θεὸν πάντα συνεργεῖ εἰς ἀγαθόν, τοῖς κατὰ πρόθεσιν κλητοῖς οὖσιν.

      We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to his purpose.

      Read Romans 8:28 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 6:1

      Συνεργοῦντες δὲ καὶ παρακαλοῦμεν μὴ εἰς κενὸν τὴν χάριν τοῦ θεοῦ δέξασθαι ὑμᾶς·

      Working together, we entreat also that you do not receive the grace of God in vain.

5 ** συνεργέω, -ῶ [in LXX: I Es 7:2, I Mac 12:1 * ;] prop., to work together (with): absol., Mk 16:[20], I Co 16:16, II Co 6:1; c. dat., Ja 2:22; dat. commod., Ro 8:28, T, R, txt. (but v. infr.). In Hellenistic writers (M, Pr., 65), trans., to cause to work together (cf. ICC, in l.): c. acc. rei, Ro 8:28 [WH] R, mg. (but v. supr.).†
