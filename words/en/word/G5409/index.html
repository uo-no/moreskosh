Senses (NGSD)

  1. to wear, to bear (4)
    To wear or carry clothing, weapons and the like: “the man wearing the fine clothing”, “those who wear soft clothing”, “wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe”, “he does not bear the sword in vain”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 11:8

      ἀλλὰ τί ἐξήλθατε ἰδεῖν; ἄνθρωπον ἐν μαλακοῖς ἠμφιεσμένον; ἰδοὺ οἱ τὰ μαλακὰ φοροῦντες ἐν τοῖς οἴκοις τῶν βασιλέων εἰσίν.

      But what did you go out to see? A man in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in kings’ houses.

      Read Matthew 11:8 in the app

    • John 19:5

      ἐξῆλθεν οὖν ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἔξω, φορῶν τὸν ἀκάνθινον στέφανον καὶ τὸ πορφυροῦν ἱμάτιον. καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς· Ἰδοὺ ὁ ἄνθρωπος.

      Jesus therefore came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple garment. Pilate said to them, “Behold, the man!”

      Read John 19:5 in the app

    • Romans 13:4

      θεοῦ γὰρ διάκονός ἐστιν σοὶ εἰς τὸ ἀγαθόν. ἐὰν δὲ τὸ κακὸν ποιῇς, φοβοῦ· οὐ γὰρ εἰκῇ τὴν μάχαιραν φορεῖ· θεοῦ γὰρ διάκονός ἐστιν, ἔκδικος εἰς ὀργὴν τῷ τὸ κακὸν πράσσοντι.

      for he is a servant of God to you for good. But if you do that which is evil, be afraid, for he doesn’t bear the sword in vain; for he is a servant of God, an avenger for wrath to him who does evil.

      Read Romans 13:4 in the app

  2. to bear the image (2)
    To bear the likeness of a person or form: “just as we have borne the image of the man of dust”, “we shall also bear the image of the man from heaven”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 15:49

      καὶ καθὼς ἐφορέσαμεν τὴν εἰκόνα τοῦ χοϊκοῦ, φορέσομεν καὶ τὴν εἰκόνα τοῦ ἐπουρανίου.

      As we have borne the image of those made of dust, let’s also bear the image of the heavenly.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 φορέω, -ῶ [in LXX: Pr 16:23 (יָסַף hi.), Si 11:5, al. ;] frequent. of φέρω, denoting repeated or habitual action (cf. Tr., Syn., § lviii), most commonly used of clothing, weapons, etc., to bear constantly, wear: Mt 11:8, Jo 19:5, Ro 13:4, I Co 15:49, Ja 2:3.†
