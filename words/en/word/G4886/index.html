Senses (NGSD)

  1. bond, tie (2)
    A knot, a bond: “bound together in the bond of peace”, “love, … the perfect bond”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Ephesians 4:3

      σπουδάζοντες τηρεῖν τὴν ἑνότητα τοῦ πνεύματος ἐν τῷ συνδέσμῳ τῆς εἰρήνης·

      being eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

      Read Ephesians 4:3 in the app

    • Colossians 3:14

      ἐπὶ πᾶσιν δὲ τούτοις τὴν ἀγάπην, ὅ ἐστιν σύνδεσμος τῆς τελειότητος.

      Above all these things, walk in love, which is the bond of perfection.

      Read Colossians 3:14 in the app

  2. ligament (1)
    The ligaments that join the body together: “through joints and ligaments”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Colossians 2:19

      καὶ οὐ κρατῶν τὴν κεφαλήν, ἐξ οὗ πᾶν τὸ σῶμα διὰ τῶν ἁφῶν καὶ συνδέσμων ἐπιχορηγούμενον καὶ συμβιβαζόμενον αὔξει τὴν αὔξησιν τοῦ θεοῦ.

      and not holding firmly to the Head, from whom all the body, being supplied and knit together through the joints and ligaments, grows with God’s growth.

      Read Colossians 2:19 in the app

  3. fetter, chain (1)
    That which binds; bondage: “the bonds of unrighteousness”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Acts 8:23

      εἰς γὰρ χολὴν πικρίας καὶ σύνδεσμον ἀδικίας ὁρῶ σε ὄντα.

      For I see that you are in the poison of bitterness and in the bondage of iniquity.”

      Read Acts 8:23 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 σύν-δεσμος, -ου, ὁ (< συνδέω), [in LXX for קשׁר, קטר, etc. ;] that which binds together, a bond: of the ligaments of the body, Col 2:19. Metaph., σ. ἀδικίας, Ac 8:23; τ. τελειότητος, Col 3:14; τ. εἰρήνης, Eph 4:3 (v. Lft., Col., ll. c.).†
