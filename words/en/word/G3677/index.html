Senses (NGSD)

  1. dream (6)
    A dream; used chiefly in κατ’ ὄναρ (in a dream): “an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream”, “being warned in a dream”, “was told … in a dream”, “appeared in a dream”, “being warned in a dream”, “I have suffered … in a dream”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 1:20

      ταῦτα δὲ αὐτοῦ ἐνθυμηθέντος ἰδοὺ ἄγγελος κυρίου κατ’ ὄναρ ἐφάνη αὐτῷ λέγων· Ἰωσὴφ υἱὸς Δαυίδ, μὴ φοβηθῇς παραλαβεῖν Μαρίαν τὴν γυναῖκά σου, τὸ γὰρ ἐν αὐτῇ γεννηθὲν ἐκ πνεύματός ἐστιν ἁγίου·

      But when he thought about these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, “Joseph, son of David, don’t be afraid to take to yourself Mary as your wife, for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Spirit.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 * ὄναρ, τό, indecl., used only in nom, and acc. sing. (the other cases are supplied by ὄνειρος), a dream: κατ’ ὄ. (in later writers only), in a dream, Mt 1:20 2:12, 13, 19, 22 27:19.†
