Senses (NGSD)

  1. to make foolish, to become foolish (2)
    To make foolish; to become a fool: “has he not made the wisdom of this world foolish”, “became fools”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 1:22

      φάσκοντες εἶναι σοφοὶ ἐμωράνθησαν,

      Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,

      Read Romans 1:22 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 1:20

      ποῦ σοφός; ποῦ γραμματεύς; ποῦ συζητητὴς τοῦ αἰῶνος τούτου; οὐχὶ ἐμώρανεν ὁ θεὸς τὴν σοφίαν τοῦ κόσμου;

      Where is the wise? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Hasn’t God made foolish the wisdom of this world?

      Read 1 Corinthians 1:20 in the app

  2. to lose its taste (2)
    For salt to lose its saltiness: “if the salt loses its strength”, “if it loses its saltiness”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 5:13

      Ὑμεῖς ἐστε τὸ ἅλας τῆς γῆς· ἐὰν δὲ τὸ ἅλας μωρανθῇ, ἐν τίνι ἁλισθήσεται; εἰς οὐδὲν ἰσχύει ἔτι εἰ μὴ βληθὲν ἔξω καταπατεῖσθαι ὑπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων.

      “You are the salt of the earth, but if the salt has lost its flavor, with what will it be salted? It is then good for nothing, but to be cast out and trodden under the feet of men.

      Read Matthew 5:13 in the app

    • Luke 14:34

      Καλὸν οὖν τὸ ἅλας· ἐὰν δὲ καὶ τὸ ἅλας μωρανθῇ, ἐν τίνι ἀρτυθήσεται;

      “Salt is good, but if the salt becomes flat and tasteless, with what do you season it?

      Read Luke 14:34 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 μωραίνω (< μωρός), [in LXX: Is 19:11, Je 10:14 28 (51):17 (בּער ni.), II Ki 24:10, Is 44:25 R (סכל ni., pi.)*;] cl., to be foolish, play the fool. LXX and NT, causal, to make foolish: I Co 1:20; pass., to become foolish: Ro 1:22; of salt that has lost its flavour, become tasteless: Mt 5:13, Lk 14:34.†
