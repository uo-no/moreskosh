Senses (NGSD)

  1. pure, sincere (2)
    Pure, unmixed: “a sincere mind”, “being pure”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Philippians 1:10

      εἰς τὸ δοκιμάζειν ὑμᾶς τὰ διαφέροντα, ἵνα ἦτε εἰλικρινεῖς καὶ ἀπρόσκοποι εἰς ἡμέραν Χριστοῦ,

      so that you may approve the things that are excellent, that you may be sincere and without offense to the day of Christ,

2 ** εἰλικρινής, -ές, [in LXX: Wi 7:25 אB * ;] unalloyed, pure (Lat. sincerus; v. DCG, ii, 635a); of unmixed substances; of abstract ideas; of ethical purity: Phl 1:10, II Pe 3:1.† SYN.: ἁγνός (q.v.), καθαρός, cf. Tr., Syn.: §lxxxv; DB, iv, 176a; Cremer, 378; Westc. on I Jo 3:3.
