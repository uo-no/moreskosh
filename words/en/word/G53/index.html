Senses (NGSD)

  1. pure / clean (8)
    Pure, clean, undefiled: “purifies himself as he is pure”, “pure conduct”, “keep yourself pure”, “as a pure virgin”, “proved yourselves innocent at every point”, “first pure”, “whatever is pure”, “pure women”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 ἁγνός, -ή, -όν (< ἅγος, v.s. ἅγιος), [in LXX chiefly for טָהוֹר (Pss, and Pr. only), also II Mac 13:8, IV Mac 18:7, al.;] free from ceremonial defilement , in a condition prepared for worship (for exx. of pagan usage, V. MM, VGT, s.v.). holy, sacred, venerable (II Mac 13:8). As in OT (cl.), pure, chaste, undefiled, guiltless; of persons: II Co 7:11 11:12, I Ti 5:22, Tit 2:5, I Jo 3:3; of things: Phl 4:8, Ja 3:17, I Pe 3:2.† SYN.: εἰλικρινής (q.v.), pure, primarily as winnowed, purged, first found in ethical sense in NT (sincere). On the equivalence of ἁ. and καθαρός (q.v.), v. DCG, ii, 459a though Westc. (Epp. Jo., 101) notes a distinction between them.
