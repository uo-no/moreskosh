1 **† ἀρχι-ποίμην, -ενος, ὁ, [in Sm.: IV Ki 3:4*;] found on an Egyptian mummy label (Deiss., LAE, 97 ff.; cf. MM, s.v.); used by modem Greeks of tribal chiefs; chief shepherd. of Christ, I Pe 5:4.†
