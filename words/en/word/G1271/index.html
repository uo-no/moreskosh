Senses (NGSD)

  1. mind / understanding (7)
    Thought, mind, power of understanding: “preparing your minds”, “your sincere minds”, “hostile in mind”, “darkened in their understanding”, “the desires of the body and the mind”, “the thoughts of their hearts” (Luke 1:51), “understanding to know him who is true”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 1:51

      Ἐποίησεν κράτος ἐν βραχίονι αὐτοῦ, διεσκόρπισεν ὑπερηφάνους διανοίᾳ καρδίας αὐτῶν·

      He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.

      Read Luke 1:51 in the app

    • Ephesians 4:18

      ἐσκοτωμένοι τῇ διανοίᾳ ὄντες, ἀπηλλοτριωμένοι τῆς ζωῆς τοῦ θεοῦ, διὰ τὴν ἄγνοιαν τὴν οὖσαν ἐν αὐτοῖς, διὰ τὴν πώρωσιν τῆς καρδίας αὐτῶν,

      being darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardening of their hearts.

      Read Ephesians 4:18 in the app

    • 1 Peter 1:13

      Διὸ ἀναζωσάμενοι τὰς ὀσφύας τῆς διανοίας ὑμῶν, νήφοντες τελείως, ἐλπίσατε ἐπὶ τὴν φερομένην ὑμῖν χάριν ἐν ἀποκαλύψει Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ.

      Therefore prepare your minds for action. Be sober, and set your hope fully on the grace that will be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ—

      Jesus said to him, “‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’

      Read Matthew 22:37 in the app
