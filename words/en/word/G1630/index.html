Senses (NGSD)

  1. terrified (2)
    Greatly afraid: “I am trembling with fear”, “was terrified”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 9:6

      οὐ γὰρ ᾔδει τί ἀποκριθῇ, ἔκφοβοι γὰρ ἐγένοντο.

      For he didn’t know what to say, for they were very afraid.
