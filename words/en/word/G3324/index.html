Senses (NGSD)

  1. full (of things) (4)
    Things are full: “a jar full of sour wine”, “a sponge full of sour wine”, “a net full of 153 large fish”, “full of deadly poison”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 19:29

      σκεῦος ἔκειτο ὄξους μεστόν· σπόγγον οὖν μεστὸν τοῦ ὄξους ὑσσώπῳ περιθέντες προσήνεγκαν αὐτοῦ τῷ στόματι.

      Now a vessel full of vinegar was set there; so they put a sponge full of the vinegar on hyssop, and held it at his mouth.

    • John 21:11

      ἀνέβη οὖν Σίμων Πέτρος καὶ εἵλκυσεν τὸ δίκτυον εἰς τὴν γῆν μεστὸν ἰχθύων μεγάλων ἑκατὸν πεντήκοντα τριῶν· καὶ τοσούτων ὄντων οὐκ ἐσχίσθη τὸ δίκτυον.

      Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land, full of one hundred fifty-three great fish. Even though there were so many, the net wasn’t torn.

      Read John 21:11 in the app

      but nobody can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.

  2. full (of quality, emotion) (5)
    Full of a quality or emotion: “full of hypocrisy and lawlessness”, “full of envy, murder, strife”, “full of goodness”, “full of curses and lawlessness”, “full of mercy and good fruits”, “eyes full of adultery”.

      Even so you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.
