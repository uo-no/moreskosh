Senses (NGSD)

  1. to die (11)
    To die: “David died and was buried”, “when Herod died”, “my daughter has just died”, “my brother died”, “whoever reviles father or mother must surely die”, “Martha, the sister of the dead man”, “married and died”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 τελευτάω, -ῶ (< τελευτή), [in LXX chiefly for מוּת ;] trans., to complete, finish; esp. τ. τ. αἰῶνα, τ. βίον, to complete life, to die (Æsch., Hdt., al.). Intrans., to come to an end, hence, to die (Hdt., al.): Mt 2:19 9:18 22:25, Mk 9:48, Lk 7:2, Jo 11:39, Ac 2:29 7:15, He 11:22; Hebraistically, θανάτῳ τελευτάτω (מוּת יוּמָת, Ex 21:17), Mt 15:4, Mk 7:10 (LXX).†
