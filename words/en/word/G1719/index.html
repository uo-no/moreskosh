Senses (NGSD)

  1. afraid, terrified (5)
    Filled with fear, frightened: “gazed at him in fear”, “became terrified”, “as they bowed their faces to the ground in fright”, “seized with terror”, “filled with fear”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 24:5

      ἐμφόβων δὲ γενομένων αὐτῶν καὶ κλινουσῶν τὰ πρόσωπα εἰς τὴν γῆν εἶπαν πρὸς αὐτάς· Τί ζητεῖτε τὸν ζῶντα μετὰ τῶν νεκρῶν;

      Becoming terrified, they bowed their faces down to the earth. The men said to them, “Why do you seek the living among the dead?
