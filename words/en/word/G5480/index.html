Senses (NGSD)

  1. mark / stamp (7)
    An engraved sign, a stamp: “receive a mark on the right hand or the forehead”, “unless one has the mark”, “those who bear the mark of the beast”, “those who receive the mark of the beast”, “had not received its mark on their foreheads or hands”, “whoever receives the mark”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Revelation 13:17

      καὶ ἵνα μή τις δύνηται ἀγοράσαι ἢ πωλῆσαι εἰ μὴ ὁ ἔχων τὸ χάραγμα, τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ θηρίου ἢ τὸν ἀριθμὸν τοῦ ὀνόματος αὐτοῦ.

      and that no one would be able to buy or to sell unless he has that mark, which is the name of the beast or the number of his name.

  2. carved image (1)
    Something engraved, an image: “like gold or silver or stone, an image formed by human art and imagination”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 17:29

      Being then the offspring of God, we ought not to think that the Divine Nature is like gold, or silver, or stone, engraved by art and design of man.
