Senses (NGSD)

  1. to envy / be jealous (5)
    To envy, to be jealous, to burn with passion: “love does not envy”, “the patriarchs, jealous of Joseph”, “the Jews became jealous”, “you covet and cannot obtain”, “I am jealous for you with a godly jealousy”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 7:9

      Καὶ οἱ πατριάρχαι ζηλώσαντες τὸν Ἰωσὴφ ἀπέδοντο εἰς Αἴγυπτον· καὶ ἦν ὁ θεὸς μετ’ αὐτοῦ,

      “The patriarchs, moved with jealousy against Joseph, sold him into Egypt. God was with him

      Read Acts 7:9 in the app

    • Acts 17:5

      ζηλώσαντες δὲ οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι καὶ προσλαβόμενοι τῶν ἀγοραίων ἄνδρας τινὰς πονηροὺς καὶ ὀχλοποιήσαντες ἐθορύβουν τὴν πόλιν, καὶ ἐπιστάντες τῇ οἰκίᾳ Ἰάσονος ἐζήτουν αὐτοὺς προαγαγεῖν εἰς τὸν δῆμον·

      But the unpersuaded Jews took along some wicked men from the marketplace and gathering a crowd, set the city in an uproar. Assaulting the house of Jason, they sought to bring them out to the people.

      Read Acts 17:5 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 11:2

      ζηλῶ γὰρ ὑμᾶς θεοῦ ζήλῳ, ἡρμοσάμην γὰρ ὑμᾶς ἑνὶ ἀνδρὶ παρθένον ἁγνὴν παραστῆσαι τῷ Χριστῷ·

      For I am jealous over you with a godly jealousy. For I promised you in marriage to one husband, that I might present you as a pure virgin to Christ.

      Read 2 Corinthians 11:2 in the app

  2. to seek earnestly / desire eagerly (3)
    To strive eagerly for, to long for: “earnestly desire the higher gifts”, “pursue love and earnestly desire the spiritual gifts”, “earnestly desire to prophesy”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Corinthians 12:31

      ζηλοῦτε δὲ τὰ χαρίσματα τὰ μείζονα. καὶ ἔτι καθ’ ὑπερβολὴν ὁδὸν ὑμῖν δείκνυμι.

      But earnestly desire the best gifts. Moreover, I show a most excellent way to you.

      Read 1 Corinthians 12:31 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 14:1

      Διώκετε τὴν ἀγάπην, ζηλοῦτε δὲ τὰ πνευματικά, μᾶλλον δὲ ἵνα προφητεύητε.

      Follow after love and earnestly desire spiritual gifts, but especially that you may prophesy.

      Read 1 Corinthians 14:1 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 14:39

      ὥστε, ἀδελφοί μου, ζηλοῦτε τὸ προφητεύειν, καὶ τὸ λαλεῖν μὴ κωλύετε γλώσσαις·

      Therefore, brothers, desire earnestly to prophesy, and don’t forbid speaking with other languages.

      Read 1 Corinthians 14:39 in the app

  3. to be zealous / court someone (4)
    To be zealous, to set one's heart on, to try to win someone over: “they make much of you”, “it is good to be zealous in a good cause”, “be zealous and repent”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Galatians 4:17

      ζηλοῦσιν ὑμᾶς οὐ καλῶς, ἀλλὰ ἐκκλεῖσαι ὑμᾶς θέλουσιν, ἵνα αὐτοὺς ζηλοῦτε.

      They zealously seek you in no good way. No, they desire to alienate you, that you may seek them.

      But it is always good to be zealous in a good cause, and not only when I am present with you.

      Read Galatians 4:18 in the app

    • Revelation 3:19

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 ζηλόω, -ῶ, [in LXX chiefly for קנא pi.;] to burn with envy or jealousy, to be jealous: absol., Ac 7:9 17:5, I Co 13:4, Ja 4:2 (R, mg., cf. Mayor, in l.). to seek or desire eagerly: c. acc. rei, I Co 12:31 14:1, 39 (cf. Si 50:18, Wi 1:12); c. acc. pers., II Co 11:2, Ga 4:17; pass., Ga 4:18.†
