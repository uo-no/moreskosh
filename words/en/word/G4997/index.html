Senses (NGSD)

  1. good sense, self-restraint (3)
    Modesty, moderation, sound judgment: “with modesty”, “sensibly”, “sober talk”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 26:25

      ὁ δὲ Παῦλος· Οὐ μαίνομαι, φησίν, κράτιστε Φῆστε, ἀλλὰ ἀληθείας καὶ σωφροσύνης ῥήματα ἀποφθέγγομαι.

      But he said, “I am not crazy, most excellent Festus, but boldly declare words of truth and reasonableness.
