Senses (NGSD)

  1. terror (1)
    Trembling fear, dread: “without fearing any terror”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Peter 3:6

      ὡς Σάρρα ὑπήκουσεν τῷ Ἀβραάμ, κύριον αὐτὸν καλοῦσα· ἧς ἐγενήθητε τέκνα ἀγαθοποιοῦσαι καὶ μὴ φοβούμεναι μηδεμίαν πτόησιν.

      So Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord, whose children you now are if you do well and are not put in fear by any terror.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 πτόησις, -εως, ἡ (< πτοέω), [in LXX: Pr 3:25 (פַּחַד), Sir 50:4 א1, I Mac 3:25 R*;] a fluttering, excitement, caused by any emotion, but esp. by fear, hence, terror: φοβεῖσθαι πτόησιν, to be afraid with (cogn. acc.) or of any terror (v. ICC, in l.): I Pe 3:6.†
