Senses (NGSD)

  1. to arbitrate; to rule (1)
    To decide like an umpire in a contest, to rule: “let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts”. The representative examples in the LXX are the use “to award the victory”, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Colossians 3:15

      καὶ ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ Χριστοῦ βραβευέτω ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις ὑμῶν, εἰς ἣν καὶ ἐκλήθητε ἐν ἑνὶ σώματι· καὶ εὐχάριστοι γίνεσθε.

      And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body, and be thankful.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** βραβεύω (< βραβεύς, an umpire), [in LXX: Wi 10:12 * ;] prop., to act as umpire; hence, generally, to arbitrate, decide (Isocr., Dem.; Lft., Col., l.c.; MM, s.v.): Col 3:15; in some late writers, to direct, rule, control (so in Col., l.c., acc. to Thayer, s.v., Meyer, in l.), (cf. κατα-βραβεύω).†
