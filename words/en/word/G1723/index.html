Senses (NGSD)

  1. to take in one’s arms (2)
    To embrace, to take up in the arms: “drawing him into his arms”, “taking in his arms”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 9:36

      He took a little child and set him in the middle of them. Taking him in his arms, he said to them,
