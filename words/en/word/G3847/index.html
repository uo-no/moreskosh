Senses (NGSD)

  1. transgression (7)
    Stepping over a law or commandment; violation: “the woman was deceived and fell into transgression”, “it was added to show transgressions”, “for every transgression”, “you dishonour God by breaking the law”, “where there is no law there is no transgression”, “sin like the transgression of Adam”, “to redeem the transgressions under the first covenant”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 2:23

      ὃς ἐν νόμῳ καυχᾶσαι, διὰ τῆς παραβάσεως τοῦ νόμου τὸν θεὸν ἀτιμάζεις;

      You who glory in the law, do you dishonor God by disobeying the law?

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 παρά-βασις, -εως, ἡ (< παραβαίνω), [in LXX: Ps 100 (101):3 (סֵטִים), IV Ki 2:24 A, Wi 14:31, II Mac 15:10*;] a going aside, a deviation (Arist.). In later writers, an overstepping; metaph., transgression (Plut., al.): Ro 4:15 5:14, He 2:2 9:15; τ. νόμου, Ro 2:23; ἐν π., I Ti 2:14; τ. παραβάσεων χάριν, Ga 3:19. SYN.: v.s. ἁμαρτία.
