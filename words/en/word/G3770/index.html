Senses (NGSD)

  1. heavenly (of the Father) (7)
    The “heavenly” in the phrase “heavenly Father”: “every plant my heavenly Father has not planted”, “so also my heavenly Father will do to you”, “your Father in heaven”, “your heavenly Father will forgive you”, “the heavenly Father feeds the birds”, “your heavenly Father knows that you need them”, “as your heavenly Father is perfect”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 5:48

      Ἔσεσθε οὖν ὑμεῖς τέλειοι ὡς ὁ πατὴρ ὑμῶν ὁ οὐράνιος τέλειός ἐστιν.

      Therefore you shall be perfect, just as your Father in heaven is perfect.

      Read Matthew 5:48 in the app

    • Matthew 6:26

      ἐμβλέψατε εἰς τὰ πετεινὰ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ ὅτι οὐ σπείρουσιν οὐδὲ θερίζουσιν οὐδὲ συνάγουσιν εἰς ἀποθήκας, καὶ ὁ πατὴρ ὑμῶν ὁ οὐράνιος τρέφει αὐτά· οὐχ ὑμεῖς μᾶλλον διαφέρετε αὐτῶν;

      See the birds of the sky, that they don’t sow, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns. Your heavenly Father feeds them. Aren’t you of much more value than they?

      Read Matthew 6:26 in the app

    • Matthew 15:13

      ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν· Πᾶσα φυτεία ἣν οὐκ ἐφύτευσεν ὁ πατήρ μου ὁ οὐράνιος ἐκριζωθήσεται.

      But he answered, “Every plant which my heavenly Father didn’t plant will be uprooted.

      Read Matthew 15:13 in the app

  2. heavenly (host, vision) (2)
    Of heaven, from heaven: “the heavenly host”, “the heavenly vision”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 2:13

      “Therefore, King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision,
