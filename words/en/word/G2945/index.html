Senses (NGSD)

  1. around (adverb) (5)
    Round about, around: “the surrounding villages and farms”, “looking about at those who sat around him”, “went about among the villages”, “into the surrounding countryside and villages”, “from Jerusalem and round about”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 3:34

      καὶ περιβλεψάμενος τοὺς περὶ αὐτὸν κύκλῳ καθημένους λέγει· Ἴδε ἡ μήτηρ μου καὶ οἱ ἀδελφοί μου·

      Looking around at those who sat around him, he said, “Behold, my mother and my brothers!

    • Luke 9:12

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 κύκλος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for סָבִיב;] a ring, circle. Dat., κύκλῳ, as adverb., round about, around: Mk 3:34 6:6, 36, Lk 9:12, Ro 15:19; c. gen., Re 4:6 5:11 7:11.†
