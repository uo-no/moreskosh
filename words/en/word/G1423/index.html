Senses (NGSD)

  1. with difficulty (3)
    With difficulty, hardly: “it is hard for a rich person … to enter the kingdom of God”, “it is hard”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 19:23

      Ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ· Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι πλούσιος δυσκόλως εἰσελεύσεται εἰς τὴν βασιλείαν τῶν οὐρανῶν·

      Jesus said to his disciples, “Most certainly I say to you, a rich man will enter into the Kingdom of Heaven with difficulty.

      Jesus looked around and said to his disciples, “How difficult it is for those who have riches to enter into God’s Kingdom!”

      Jesus, seeing that he became very sad, said, “How hard it is for those who have riches to enter into God’s Kingdom!
