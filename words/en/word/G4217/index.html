Senses (NGSD)

  1. what sort of, what manner of (7)
    An interrogative asking about quality: of what sort. “what kind of love the Father has given us”, “what sort of people ought you to be”, “wondering what sort of greeting this might be”, “what sort of woman she is”, “what sort of man is this”, “what stones and what buildings”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:27

      οἱ δὲ ἄνθρωποι ἐθαύμασαν λέγοντες· Ποταπός ἐστιν οὗτος ὅτι καὶ οἱ ἄνεμοι καὶ ἡ θάλασσα αὐτῷ ὑπακούουσιν;

      The men marveled, saying, “What kind of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”

7 ** ποταπός (late form of cl. ποδ-), -ή, -όν, [in LXX: Da LXX Su 54*;] (= ποδαπός) from what country? In late writers, = ποῖος, of what sort?: Mt 8:27, Mk 13:1, Lk 1:29 7:39, II Pe 3:11, I Jn 3:1.†
