Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 φέγγος, -ους, τό [in LXX chiefly for נֹגַהּ ;] light, brightness, usually c. gen. of something that shines or reflects: of the moon, Mt 24:29, Mk 13:24; of a lamp, Lk 11:33 (WH, RV, φῶς).† SYN.: αὐγή, q.v.
