Senses (NGSD)

  1. to confirm / make firm (8)
    To make sure, to make firm, to confirm: “the testimony about Christ was confirmed among you”, “who will sustain you to the end”, “establishes us”, “established in the faith”, “confirmed by the Lord”, “good for the heart to be strengthened by grace”, “confirmed the word by signs”, “to confirm the promises given to the patriarchs”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 16:20

      ἐκεῖνοι δὲ ἐξελθόντες ἐκήρυξαν πανταχοῦ, τοῦ κυρίου συνεργοῦντος καὶ τὸν λόγον βεβαιοῦντος διὰ τῶν ἐπακολουθούντων σημείων.

      They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed. Amen.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 βεβαιόω, -ῶ (< βέβαιος), [in LXX: Ps 40 (41):12 (נצב hi.), 118 (119):28 (קוּם pi.), III Mac 5:42*;] to confirm, establish, secure, of things (cl.): λόγον, Mk 16:[20]; ἐπαγγελίας, Ro 15:8; of persons (DCG, ii, 605): I Co 1:8, II Co 1:21. Pass., I Co 1:6, Col 2:7, He 2:3 13:9 (as an Attic legal term, to guarantee the validity of a purchase, establish or confirm a title; v. next word, Cremer, 139; cf. δια-β.).†
