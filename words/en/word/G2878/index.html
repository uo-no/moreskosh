Senses (NGSD)

  1. temple treasury (1)
    The treasury of the temple, its offering chest: “the temple treasury”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 27:6

      οἱ δὲ ἀρχιερεῖς λαβόντες τὰ ἀργύρια εἶπαν· Οὐκ ἔξεστιν βαλεῖν αὐτὰ εἰς τὸν κορβανᾶν, ἐπεὶ τιμὴ αἵματός ἐστιν·

      The chief priests took the pieces of silver and said, “It’s not lawful to put them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood.”

      Read Matthew 27:6 in the app

  2. a gift devoted to God (1)
    A transliteration of the Aramaic word for what is devoted to God: “Corban” (once). Used where support owed to one's parents is declared a gift to God instead.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 7:11

      ὑμεῖς δὲ λέγετε· Ἐὰν εἴπῃ ἄνθρωπος τῷ πατρὶ ἢ τῇ μητρί· Κορβᾶν, ὅ ἐστιν Δῶρον, ὃ ἐὰν ἐξ ἐμοῦ ὠφεληθῇς,

      But you say, ‘If a man tells his father or his mother, “Whatever profit you might have received from me is Corban,”’” that is to say, given to God,

      Read Mark 7:11 in the app
