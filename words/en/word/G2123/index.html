Senses (NGSD)

  1. easier (7)
    Comparative: to be easier. “it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one stroke of the law to fall”, “it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God”, “which is easier, to say your sins are forgiven or to say get up and walk”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 9:5

      τί γάρ ἐστιν εὐκοπώτερον, εἰπεῖν· Ἀφίενταί σου αἱ ἁμαρτίαι, ἢ εἰπεῖν· Ἔγειρε καὶ περιπάτει;

      For which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Get up, and walk’?
