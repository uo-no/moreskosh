Senses (NGSD)

  1. sumptuously (1)
    Splendidly, lavishly: “lived in luxury and merriment”. The representative examples in the LXX and the Fathers are of the adjective λαμπρός, and the adverb has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 16:19

      Ἄνθρωπος δέ τις ἦν πλούσιος, καὶ ἐνεδιδύσκετο πορφύραν καὶ βύσσον εὐφραινόμενος καθ’ ἡμέραν λαμπρῶς.

      “Now there was a certain rich man, and he was clothed in purple and fine linen, living in luxury every day.
