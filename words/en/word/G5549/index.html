Senses (NGSD)

  1. to delay (5)
    To be late, to linger, to take a long time: “will not delay”, “my master is delayed in coming”, “his lingering in the temple”, “as the bridegroom was delayed”, “my master is delaying”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 24:48

      ἐὰν δὲ εἴπῃ ὁ κακὸς δοῦλος ἐκεῖνος ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ αὐτοῦ· Χρονίζει μου ὁ κύριος,

      But if that evil servant should say in his heart, ‘My lord is delaying his coming,’
