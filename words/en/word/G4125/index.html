Senses (NGSD)

  1. side (5)
    The side of the body: “struck Peter on the side”, “pierced his side with a spear”, “showed his hands and his side”, “put my hand into his side”, “put your hand into my side”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 19:34

      ἀλλ’ εἷς τῶν στρατιωτῶν λόγχῃ αὐτοῦ τὴν πλευρὰν ἔνυξεν, καὶ ἐξῆλθεν εὐθὺς αἷμα καὶ ὕδωρ.

      However, one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out.
