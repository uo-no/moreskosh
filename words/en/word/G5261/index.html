1 **† ὑπογραμμός, -οῦ, ὁ (< ὑπογράφω, (a) to write under; (b) to trace letters for copying), [in LXX: II Mac 2:28 * ;] a writing-copy, hence, an example: I Pe 2:11 (Philo).†
