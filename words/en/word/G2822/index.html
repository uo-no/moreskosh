Senses (NGSD)

  1. called (as apostle) (2)
    Called: “Paul, called to be an apostle”, “Paul, a servant of Christ Jesus, called to be an apostle”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 1:1

      Παῦλος δοῦλος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ, κλητὸς ἀπόστολος, ἀφωρισμένος εἰς εὐαγγέλιον θεοῦ

      Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, called to be an apostle, set apart for the Good News of God,

      Read Romans 1:1 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 1:1

      Παῦλος κλητὸς ἀπόστολος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ διὰ θελήματος θεοῦ καὶ Σωσθένης ὁ ἀδελφὸς

      Paul, called to be an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,

      Read 1 Corinthians 1:1 in the app

  2. those called (to salvation) (7)
    Those called by God: “those called to be saints”, “to those who are called, Christ is the power of God”, “called, beloved, and kept”, “among whom you also are”, “those called by God”, “those with the Lamb are called”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 1:6

      ἐν οἷς ἐστε καὶ ὑμεῖς κλητοὶ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ,

      among whom you are also called to belong to Jesus Christ;

      Read Romans 1:6 in the app

    • Romans 8:28

      Οἴδαμεν δὲ ὅτι τοῖς ἀγαπῶσι τὸν θεὸν πάντα συνεργεῖ εἰς ἀγαθόν, τοῖς κατὰ πρόθεσιν κλητοῖς οὖσιν.

      We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, for those who are called according to his purpose.

      Read Romans 8:28 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 1:24

      αὐτοῖς δὲ τοῖς κλητοῖς, Ἰουδαίοις τε καὶ Ἕλλησιν, Χριστὸν θεοῦ δύναμιν καὶ θεοῦ σοφίαν.

      but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God;

      Read 1 Corinthians 1:24 in the app

  3. invited ones (1)
    Those invited: “many are called, but few are chosen” (Matt 22:14).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 22:14

      πολλοὶ γάρ εἰσιν κλητοὶ ὀλίγοι δὲ ἐκλεκτοί.

      For many are called, but few chosen.”

      Read Matthew 22:14 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 κλητός, -ή, -όν (< καλέω), [in LXX for קרא, מִקְרָא, Ex 12:16, al.;] called, invited (as to a banquet, Æsch., I Ki 1:41, III Mac 5:14); in NT, always of the Divine call; to some office: κ. ἀπόστολος, Ro 1:1, I Co 1:1; to salvation: Ro 8:28, I Co 1:24, Ju 1; κ. καὶ ἐκλεκτοὶ κ. πιστοί, Re 17:14; in gosp. (not in pl., v.s. καλέω) disting. fr.ἐκλεκτός, Mt 20:16 (WH, txt., R, omit), 22:14; κ. Ἰησοῦ Χρ., Ro 1:6; κ. ἅγιοι, saints by calling, Ro 1:7, I Co 1:2.†
