Senses (NGSD)

  1. to discipline / train by chastening (6)
    To chasten and train, to correct: “when we are judged by the Lord we are disciplined so that we may not be condemned with the world”, “the Lord disciplines the one he loves”, “what son is there whom his father does not discipline?”, “those whom I love I reprove and discipline”, “punished and yet not killed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 11:32

      κρινόμενοι δὲ ὑπὸ κυρίου παιδευόμεθα, ἵνα μὴ σὺν τῷ κόσμῳ κατακριθῶμεν.

      But when we are judged, we are disciplined by the Lord, that we may not be condemned with the world.

      Read 1 Corinthians 11:32 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:6

      ὃν γὰρ ἀγαπᾷ κύριος παιδεύει, μαστιγοῖ δὲ πάντα υἱὸν ὃν παραδέχεται.

      for whom the Lord loves, he disciplines, and chastises every son whom he receives.”

      Read Hebrews 12:6 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:10

      οἱ μὲν γὰρ πρὸς ὀλίγας ἡμέρας κατὰ τὸ δοκοῦν αὐτοῖς ἐπαίδευον, ὁ δὲ ἐπὶ τὸ συμφέρον εἰς τὸ μεταλαβεῖν τῆς ἁγιότητος αὐτοῦ.

      For they indeed for a few days disciplined us as seemed good to them, but he for our profit, that we may be partakers of his holiness.

      Read Hebrews 12:10 in the app

  2. to flog (punish) (2)
    To chastise as a punishment, to flog: “I will have him punished and release him”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 23:16

      παιδεύσας οὖν αὐτὸν ἀπολύσω.

      I will therefore chastise him and release him.”

      Read Luke 23:16 in the app

    • Luke 23:22

      ὁ δὲ τρίτον εἶπεν πρὸς αὐτούς· Τί γὰρ κακὸν ἐποίησεν οὗτος; οὐδὲν αἴτιον θανάτου εὗρον ἐν αὐτῷ· παιδεύσας οὖν αὐτὸν ἀπολύσω.

      He said to them the third time, “Why? What evil has this man done? I have found no capital crime in him. I will therefore chastise him and release him.”

      Read Luke 23:22 in the app

  3. to educate / instruct (3)
    To instruct and bring up, to educate: “strictly educated according to the law”, “instructed in all the wisdom of the Egyptians”, “teaching us to renounce ungodliness and worldly passions”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 7:22

      καὶ ἐπαιδεύθη Μωϋσῆς πάσῃ σοφίᾳ Αἰγυπτίων, ἦν δὲ δυνατὸς ἐν λόγοις καὶ ἔργοις αὐτοῦ.

      Moses was instructed in all the wisdom of the Egyptians. He was mighty in his words and works.

      Read Acts 7:22 in the app

    • Acts 22:3

      Ἐγώ εἰμι ἀνὴρ Ἰουδαῖος, γεγεννημένος ἐν Ταρσῷ τῆς Κιλικίας, ἀνατεθραμμένος δὲ ἐν τῇ πόλει ταύτῃ παρὰ τοὺς πόδας Γαμαλιήλ, πεπαιδευμένος κατὰ ἀκρίβειαν τοῦ πατρῴου νόμου, ζηλωτὴς ὑπάρχων τοῦ θεοῦ καθὼς πάντες ὑμεῖς ἐστε σήμερον,

      “I am indeed a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, but brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, instructed according to the strict tradition of the law of our fathers, being zealous for God, even as you all are today.

    • Titus 2:12

      instructing us to the intent that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we would live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present age;

  4. to correct (opponents) (2)
    To correct, to admonish: “that they may learn not to blaspheme”, “correcting opponents with gentleness”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Timothy 1:20

      ὧν ἐστιν Ὑμέναιος καὶ Ἀλέξανδρος, οὓς παρέδωκα τῷ Σατανᾷ ἵνα παιδευθῶσι μὴ βλασφημεῖν.

      of whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I delivered to Satan that they might be taught not to blaspheme.

      in gentleness correcting those who oppose him. Perhaps God may give them repentance leading to a full knowledge of the truth,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

13 παιδεύω (< παίς), [in LXX chiefly for יסר;] as in cl., to train children, hence, generally, to teach, instruct: Ac 7:22 22:3, I Ti 1:20, Tit 2:12. As in LXX (Ps 6:2, Pr 19:18, Wi 3:5, al., and for prob. ex. from π., v. MM, xviii), to chasten, correct, chastise: Lk 23:16, 22, II Ti 2:25, He 12:7, 10; of divine chastening, I Co 11:32, II Co 6:9, He 12:6, Re 3:19.† SYN.: διδάσκω, q.v.
