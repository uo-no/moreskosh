Senses (NGSD)

  1. confession (of faith) (6)
    Confession, open declaration: “made the good confession”, “made a good confession”, “the obedience that accompanies your confession”, “let us hold fast our confession”, “the high priest of our confession”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 9:13

      διὰ τῆς δοκιμῆς τῆς διακονίας ταύτης δοξάζοντες τὸν θεὸν ἐπὶ τῇ ὑποταγῇ τῆς ὁμολογίας ὑμῶν εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Χριστοῦ καὶ ἁπλότητι τῆς κοινωνίας εἰς αὐτοὺς καὶ εἰς πάντας,

      seeing that through the proof given by this service, they glorify God for the obedience of your confession to the Good News of Christ and for the generosity of your contribution to them and to all,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ὁμολογία, -ας, ἡ (< ὁμολογέω), [in LXX: De 12:6, 17, Am 4:5, Ez 46:12 (נְדָבָה), Le 22:18, Je 51 (44):25 (נֶדֶר), I Es 9:8*;] in cl., an agreement, assent, compact (in π., of a contract; Deiss., BS, 249). confession (prob. always in an objective sense): II Co 9:13, I Ti 6:12, 13, He 3:1 4:14 10:13.†
