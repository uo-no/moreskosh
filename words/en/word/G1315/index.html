Senses (NGSD)

  1. to kill (2)
    To kill, to do away with: “tried to kill”, “killed”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 5:30

      ὁ θεὸς τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν ἤγειρεν Ἰησοῦν, ὃν ὑμεῖς διεχειρίσασθε κρεμάσαντες ἐπὶ ξύλου·

      The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you killed, hanging him on a tree.

    • Acts 26:21

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 * δια-χειρίζω (< χείρ), to have in hand, conduct, manage. Mid. = act.; in late writers (Polyb., FlJ, al.), to lay hands on, kill: c. acc, Ac 5:30 26:21.†
