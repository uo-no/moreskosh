Senses (NGSD)

  1. governor's residence / praetorium (7)
    The governor's residence, headquarters: “the governor's soldiers took Jesus into the praetorium”, “the soldiers … into the courtyard of the residence”, “they led Jesus to the governor's headquarters”, “the governor entered the headquarters”, “entered the headquarters again”, “in Herod's praetorium”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 27:27

      Τότε οἱ στρατιῶται τοῦ ἡγεμόνος παραλαβόντες τὸν Ἰησοῦν εἰς τὸ πραιτώριον συνήγαγον ἐπ’ αὐτὸν ὅλην τὴν σπεῖραν.

      Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium, and gathered the whole garrison together against him.

      Read Matthew 27:27 in the app

    • John 18:28

      Ἄγουσιν οὖν τὸν Ἰησοῦν ἀπὸ τοῦ Καϊάφα εἰς τὸ πραιτώριον· ἦν δὲ πρωΐ· καὶ αὐτοὶ οὐκ εἰσῆλθον εἰς τὸ πραιτώριον, ἵνα μὴ μιανθῶσιν ἀλλὰ φάγωσιν τὸ πάσχα.

      They led Jesus therefore from Caiaphas into the Praetorium. It was early, and they themselves didn’t enter into the Praetorium, that they might not be defiled, but might eat the Passover.

      Read John 18:28 in the app

    • John 19:9

      καὶ εἰσῆλθεν εἰς τὸ πραιτώριον πάλιν καὶ λέγει τῷ Ἰησοῦ· Πόθεν εἶ σύ; ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς ἀπόκρισιν οὐκ ἔδωκεν αὐτῷ.

      He entered into the Praetorium again, and said to Jesus, “Where are you from?” But Jesus gave him no answer.

      Read John 19:9 in the app

  2. imperial guard (or residence) (disputed) (1)
    “It has become known throughout the whole praetorian guard that my imprisonment is for Christ.”
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 1:13

      ὥστε τοὺς δεσμούς μου φανεροὺς ἐν Χριστῷ γενέσθαι ἐν ὅλῳ τῷ πραιτωρίῳ καὶ τοῖς λοιποῖς πᾶσιν,

      so that it became evident to the whole palace guard, and to all the rest, that my bonds are in Christ,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 *† πραιτώριον, -ου, τό, (Lat. prætorium), headquarters in a Roman camp. The palace or official residence of the Governor of a province: Mt 27:27, Mk 15:16 (v. Swete, in l.), Jo 18:28, 33 19:9; τ. π. τ. Ἡρῴδου, Ac 23:35. the Prætorian Guard: Phl 1:13 (v. Lft., in l.; ICC, 51 f.).†
