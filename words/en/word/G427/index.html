Senses (NGSD)

  1. without (3)
    Without, apart from: “without grumbling”, “without a word”, “without your Father’s permission”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 10:29

      οὐχὶ δύο στρουθία ἀσσαρίου πωλεῖται; καὶ ἓν ἐξ αὐτῶν οὐ πεσεῖται ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν ἄνευ τοῦ πατρὸς ὑμῶν.

      “Aren’t two sparrows sold for an assarion coin? Not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father’s will.

      In the same way, wives, be in subjection to your own husbands, so that, even if any don’t obey the Word, they may be won by the behavior of their wives without a word,
