Senses (NGSD)

  1. calling (of God) (10)
    God's call, invitation: “consider your calling”, “the hope to which he has called you”, “make your calling sure”, “the prize of the upward call of God”, “the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable”, “walk worthily of the calling to which you have been called”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Romans 11:29

      ἀμεταμέλητα γὰρ τὰ χαρίσματα καὶ ἡ κλῆσις τοῦ θεοῦ.

      For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.

      Read Romans 11:29 in the app

    • Ephesians 4:1

      Παρακαλῶ οὖν ὑμᾶς ἐγὼ ὁ δέσμιος ἐν κυρίῳ ἀξίως περιπατῆσαι τῆς κλήσεως ἧς ἐκλήθητε,

      I therefore, the prisoner in the Lord, beg you to walk worthily of the calling with which you were called,

      Read Ephesians 4:1 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 1:11

      εἰς ὃ καὶ προσευχόμεθα πάντοτε περὶ ὑμῶν, ἵνα ὑμᾶς ἀξιώσῃ τῆς κλήσεως ὁ θεὸς ἡμῶν καὶ πληρώσῃ πᾶσαν εὐδοκίαν ἀγαθωσύνης καὶ ἔργον πίστεως ἐν δυνάμει,

      To this end we also pray always for you that our God may count you worthy of your calling, and fulfill every desire of goodness and work of faith with power,

      Read 2 Thessalonians 1:11 in the app

  2. station (at the time of calling) (1)
    The state or station one was in when called: “remain in the calling in which you were called” (1 Cor 7:20).
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 7:20

      ἕκαστος ἐν τῇ κλήσει ᾗ ἐκλήθη ἐν ταύτῃ μενέτω.

      Let each man stay in that calling in which he was called.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:20 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 κλῆσις, -εως, ἡ (καλέω), [in LXX: Je 38 (31):6 (קרא), Jth 12:10 A, III Mac 5:14*;] a calling, call; in NT, always of the Divine call to salvation: Ro 11:29, I Co 1:26 7:20, Eph 1:18 4:1, 4, Phl 3:14, II Th 1:11, II Ti 1:9, He 3:1, II Pe 1:10 (Cremer, 332).†
