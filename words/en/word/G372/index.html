Senses (NGSD)

  1. rest, repose (5)
    Rest, ease: “seeking a resting place”, “you will find rest for your souls”, “looking for a place to rest and …”, “without a moment’s rest”, “without rest day and night”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 11:29

      ἄρατε τὸν ζυγόν μου ἐφ’ ὑμᾶς καὶ μάθετε ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ, ὅτι πραΰς εἰμι καὶ ταπεινὸς τῇ καρδίᾳ, καὶ εὑρήσετε ἀνάπαυσιν ταῖς ψυχαῖς ὑμῶν·

      Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ἀνά-παυσις, -εως, ή (ἀνάπαύω), [in LXX chiefly for נוּחַ and its derivatives, שַׁבָּת and its cognates (Ex, Le);] cessation, rest, refreshment: Mt 11:26 12:43, Lk 11:24, Re 4:8 14:11.† SYN.: ἄνεσις (lit, the relaxation of the strings of a lyre), prop. signifies the rest or ease which comes from the relaxation of unfavourable conditions, as, e.g. affliction: ἀνάπ., the rest which comes from the temporary cessation of labour (v. Tr., Syn., § xl; Cremer, 827; MM, VGT, s.v.)
