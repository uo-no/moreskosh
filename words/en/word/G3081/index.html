Senses (NGSD)

  1. it is profitable (1)
    It is of advantage, it is better: “it would be better to have a millstone hung around his neck”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 17:2

      λυσιτελεῖ αὐτῷ εἰ λίθος μυλικὸς περίκειται περὶ τὸν τράχηλον αὐτοῦ καὶ ἔρριπται εἰς τὴν θάλασσαν ἢ ἵνα σκανδαλίσῃ τῶν μικρῶν τούτων ἕνα.

      It would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck, and he were thrown into the sea, rather than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** λυσιτελέω, -ῶ (< λυσιτελής, useful, prop., τὰ τέλη λύων), [in LXX: To 3:6, Si 20:10, 14 29:11*;] prop., to indemnify, pay expenses. to be useful, to profit; usually impers., λυσιτελεῖ, it profits: c, dat. pers., seq. εἰ . . . ἤ, Lk 17:2.†
