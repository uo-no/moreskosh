Senses (NGSD)

  1. to betroth (1)
    To join together, to give in marriage (middle voice): “I betrothed you”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 2 Corinthians 11:2

      ζηλῶ γὰρ ὑμᾶς θεοῦ ζήλῳ, ἡρμοσάμην γὰρ ὑμᾶς ἑνὶ ἀνδρὶ παρθένον ἁγνὴν παραστῆσαι τῷ Χριστῷ·

      For I am jealous over you with a godly jealousy. For I promised you in marriage to one husband, that I might present you as a pure virgin to Christ.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ἁρμόζω (< ἁρμός), [in LXX for אמן, etc.;] to fit, join. of marriage, to betroth. Mid., to join to oneself, marry, take to wife; to give in marriage: II Co 11:2 (for this there is no direct parallel. But v. M, Pr., 160; MM, s.v.).†
