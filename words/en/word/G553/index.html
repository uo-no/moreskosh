Occurrences in the New Testament

Senses (NGSD)

  1. to await eagerly (8)
    To wait for, to long for: “as you wait for the revealing of our Lord Jesus Christ”, “God's patience waited in the days of Noah”, “we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness”, “will appear a second time to save”, “we await a Saviour”, “the creation waits for the revealing of the sons of God”, “we wait for the redemption of our bodies”, “we wait for it with patience”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 8:19

      ἡ γὰρ ἀποκαραδοκία τῆς κτίσεως τὴν ἀποκάλυψιν τῶν υἱῶν τοῦ θεοῦ ἀπεκδέχεται·

      For the creation waits with eager expectation for the children of God to be revealed.

    • Romans 8:25

      εἰ δὲ ὃ οὐ βλέπομεν ἐλπίζομεν, δι’ ὑπομονῆς ἀπεκδεχόμεθα.

      But if we hope for that which we don’t see, we wait for it with patience.

      Read Romans 8:25 in the app

    • Philippians 3:20

      ἡμῶν γὰρ τὸ πολίτευμα ἐν οὐρανοῖς ὑπάρχει, ἐξ οὗ καὶ σωτῆρα ἀπεκδεχόμεθα κύριον Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν,

      For our citizenship is in heaven, from where we also wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,
