Senses (NGSD)

  1. to choke, to drown (3)
    To strangle, to drown, to throttle: “choked it”, “seizing him by the throat”, “drowned in the lake”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:7

      ἄλλα δὲ ἔπεσεν ἐπὶ τὰς ἀκάνθας, καὶ ἀνέβησαν αἱ ἄκανθαι καὶ ἔπνιξαν αὐτά.

      Others fell among thorns. The thorns grew up and choked them.

      “But that servant went out and found one of his fellow servants who owed him one hundred denarii, and he grabbed him and took him by the throat, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’
