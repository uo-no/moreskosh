Senses (NGSD)

  1. opposite / ahead (place) (5)
    Opposite, facing: “go into the village over there”, “go into the village in front of you”, “sat down opposite the treasury”, “as he sat opposite the temple”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 21:2

      λέγων αὐτοῖς· Πορεύεσθε εἰς τὴν κώμην τὴν κατέναντι ὑμῶν, καὶ εὐθέως εὑρήσετε ὄνον δεδεμένην καὶ πῶλον μετ’ αὐτῆς· λύσαντες ἀγάγετέ μοι.

      saying to them, “Go into the village that is opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied, and a colt with her. Untie them and bring them to me.

      and said to them, “Go your way into the village that is opposite you. Immediately as you enter into it, you will find a young donkey tied, on which no one has sat. Untie him and bring him.

    Attested: NT only

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 † κατέναντι, adv., [in LXX chiefly for נֶגֶד(לְ), לִפְנֵי, etc.;] over against, opposite, before: ἡ, κ. κώμη, Lk 19:30; as prep., c. gen., Mt 21:2 27:24 (ἀπ-, WH, mg.), Mk 11:2 12:41 (ἀπ-, WH, mg.) 13:3; metaph., before, seq. θεοῦ, Ro 4:17, II Co 2:17 12:19.†
