Senses (NGSD)

  1. tear (10)
    Tears: “with many tears”, “I remember your tears”, “with tears”, “offered up prayers and supplications with loud cries and tears”, “though he sought it with tears”, “wet his feet with her tears”, “God will wipe away every tear from their eyes”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 7:38

      καὶ στᾶσα ὀπίσω παρὰ τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ κλαίουσα, τοῖς δάκρυσιν ἤρξατο βρέχειν τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ καὶ ταῖς θριξὶν τῆς κεφαλῆς αὐτῆς ἐξέμασσεν, καὶ κατεφίλει τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ καὶ ἤλειφεν τῷ μύρῳ.

      Standing behind at his feet weeping, she began to wet his feet with her tears, and she wiped them with the hair of her head, kissed his feet, and anointed them with the ointment.

      For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you with many tears, not that you should be made to grieve, but that you might know the love that I have so abundantly for you.
