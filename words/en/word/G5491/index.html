Senses (NGSD)

  1. lips (6)
    The lips (as organs of speech): “by the lips of speakers of other tongues”, “keep his lips from speaking deceit”, “the fruit of lips that praise God’s name”, “this people honours me with their lips”, “honours me with their lips”, “the venom of vipers is under their lips”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 15:8

      Ὁ λαὸς οὗτος τοῖς χείλεσίν με τιμᾷ, ἡ δὲ καρδία αὐτῶν πόρρω ἀπέχει ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ·

      ‘These people draw near to me with their mouth, and honor me with their lips; but their heart is far from me.

    • Romans 3:13

      τάφος ἀνεῳγμένος ὁ λάρυγξ αὐτῶν, ταῖς γλώσσαις αὐτῶν ἐδολιοῦσαν, ἰὸς ἀσπίδων ὑπὸ τὰ χείλη αὐτῶν,

      “Their throat is an open tomb. With their tongues they have used deceit.” “The poison of vipers is under their lips.”

      Read Romans 3:13 in the app

    • Hebrews 13:15

      δι’ αὐτοῦ οὖν ἀναφέρωμεν θυσίαν αἰνέσεως διὰ παντὸς τῷ θεῷ, τοῦτ’ ἔστιν καρπὸν χειλέων ὁμολογούντων τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ.

      Through him, then, let’s offer up a sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of lips which proclaim allegiance to his name.

      Read Hebrews 13:15 in the app

  2. shore, edge (1)
    The shore or edge of the sea: “as the innumerable sand by the seashore”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 11:12

      διὸ καὶ ἀφ’ ἑνὸς ἐγεννήθησαν, καὶ ταῦτα νενεκρωμένου, καθὼς τὰ ἄστρα τοῦ οὐρανοῦ τῷ πλήθει καὶ ὡς ἡ ἄμμος ἡ παρὰ τὸ χεῖλος τῆς θαλάσσης ἡ ἀναρίθμητος.

      Therefore as many as the stars of the sky in multitude, and as innumerable as the sand which is by the sea shore, were fathered by one man, and him as good as dead.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 χεῖλος, -ους, τό, gen. pl., -εων (v. Bl., § 8, 4; Thackeray, Gr., I, 151), [in LXX chiefly for שָׂפָה ;] a lip (as in Heb., of the lip as the organ of speech): Mt 15:8, Mk 7:6, Ro 3:13, I Co 14:21, He 13:15, I Pe 3:10 (LXX). Metaph., of things, an edge, brink, etc. (Hom., Hdt., al.): of the sea­shore, He 11:12.†
