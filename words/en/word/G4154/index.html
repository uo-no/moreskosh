Senses (NGSD)

  1. to blow (7)
    For the wind to blow: “the wind blew”, “the wind blows where it wishes”, “the wind was against them”, “when the south wind blows”, “the winds blew and beat against that house”, “the four winds of the earth … that no wind should blow on land or sea or any tree”, “hoisting the sail to the wind”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 7:25

      καὶ κατέβη ἡ βροχὴ καὶ ἦλθον οἱ ποταμοὶ καὶ ἔπνευσαν οἱ ἄνεμοι καὶ προσέπεσαν τῇ οἰκίᾳ ἐκείνῃ, καὶ οὐκ ἔπεσεν, τεθεμελίωτο γὰρ ἐπὶ τὴν πέτραν.

      The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house; and it didn’t fall, for it was founded on the rock.

      Read Matthew 7:25 in the app

    • Luke 12:55

      καὶ ὅταν νότον πνέοντα, λέγετε ὅτι Καύσων ἔσται, καὶ γίνεται.

      When a south wind blows, you say, ‘There will be a scorching heat,’ and it happens.

      Read Luke 12:55 in the app

    • John 6:18

      ἥ τε θάλασσα ἀνέμου μεγάλου πνέοντος διεγείρετο.

      The sea was tossed by a great wind blowing.

      Read John 6:18 in the app
