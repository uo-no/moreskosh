Senses (NGSD)

  1. figurehead (1)
    The ensign at a ship's prow, the mark of a vessel: “with the figurehead of the Twin Gods”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Acts 28:11

      Μετὰ δὲ τρεῖς μῆνας ἀνήχθημεν ἐν πλοίῳ παρακεχειμακότι ἐν τῇ νήσῳ Ἀλεξανδρίνῳ, παρασήμῳ Διοσκούροις.

      After three months, we set sail in a ship of Alexandria which had wintered in the island, whose figurehead was “The Twin Brothers.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ** παράσημος, -ον (< σῆμα, a mark), [in LXX: III Mac 2:29*;] in cl., marked amiss, spurious, counterfeit. Later, marked at the side, annotated (Plut.). marked with a sign (LXX); as subst., a figurehead: Ac 28:11 (v. MM, xix).†
