Senses (NGSD)

  1. to surpass, to exceed (5)
    To surpass by far, to overflow (often as a participle): “the far surpassing glory”, “the surpassing grace God has given you”, “immeasurably great grace”, “the immeasurable greatness of his power”, “the love that far surpasses knowledge”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 3:10

      καὶ γὰρ οὐ δεδόξασται τὸ δεδοξασμένον ἐν τούτῳ τῷ μέρει εἵνεκεν τῆς ὑπερβαλλούσης δόξης·

      For most certainly that which has been made glorious has not been made glorious in this respect, by reason of the glory that surpasses.

      while they themselves also, with supplication on your behalf, yearn for you by reason of the exceeding grace of God in you.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ὑπερ-βάλλω [in LXX: Jb 15:11 A (†), Si 5:7 25:11, al. ;] trans., to throw over or beyond. Intrans., to run beyond. In both senses, metaph., to exceed, surpass, transcend: II Co 3:10 9:14, Eph 1:19 2:7; c. gen. obj., Eph 3:19.†
