Senses (NGSD)

  1. myth, fable (5)
    An invented tale, a myth: “avoid godless and silly myths”, “not to devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies”, “cleverly devised myths”, “will wander off into myths”, “Jewish myths”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Timothy 1:4

      μηδὲ προσέχειν μύθοις καὶ γενεαλογίαις ἀπεράντοις, αἵτινες ἐκζητήσεις παρέχουσι μᾶλλον ἢ οἰκονομίαν θεοῦ τὴν ἐν πίστει

      and not to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which cause disputes rather than God’s stewardship, which is in faith.

    • 1 Timothy 4:7

      τοὺς δὲ βεβήλους καὶ γραώδεις μύθους παραιτοῦ. γύμναζε δὲ σεαυτὸν πρὸς εὐσέβειαν·

      But refuse profane and old wives’ fables. Exercise yourself toward godliness.

      Read 1 Timothy 4:7 in the app

    • Titus 1:14

      μὴ προσέχοντες Ἰουδαϊκοῖς μύθοις καὶ ἐντολαῖς ἀνθρώπων ἀποστρεφομένων τὴν ἀλήθειαν.

      not paying attention to Jewish fables and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** μῦθος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX: Wi 17:4 A, Si 20:19*;] speech, conversation. a story, narrative (Hom.); later, opp. to λόγος (a true narrative) = Lat. fabula, a myth, fable, fiction: I Ti 1:4 4:7, II Ti 4:4, Tit 1:14, II Pe 1:16.† SYN.: λόγος, q.v.
