Senses (NGSD)

  1. linen cloth (5)
    Linen cloth (for wrapping a body): “wrapped it in linen cloths”, “saw the linen cloths lying there”, “saw the linen cloths lying”, “only the linen cloths”, “the linen cloths …”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping and looking in, he saw the strips of linen lying by themselves, and he departed to his home, wondering what had happened.
