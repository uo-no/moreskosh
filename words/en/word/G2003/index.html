Senses (NGSD)

  1. command, instruction (5)
    A command, an instruction: “as a concession, not a command”, “I have no command of the Lord”, “I do not say this as a command”, “an apostle by the command of God”, “according to the command of the eternal God”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Corinthians 7:6

      τοῦτο δὲ λέγω κατὰ συγγνώμην, οὐ κατ’ ἐπιταγήν.

      But this I say by way of concession, not of commandment.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:6 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 7:25

      Περὶ δὲ τῶν παρθένων ἐπιταγὴν κυρίου οὐκ ἔχω, γνώμην δὲ δίδωμι ὡς ἠλεημένος ὑπὸ κυρίου πιστὸς εἶναι.

      Now concerning virgins, I have no commandment from the Lord, but I give my judgment as one who has obtained mercy from the Lord to be trustworthy.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:25 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 1:1

      Παῦλος ἀπόστολος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ κατ’ ἐπιταγὴν θεοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν καὶ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τῆς ἐλπίδος ἡμῶν

      Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ according to the commandment of God our Savior and the Lord Jesus Christ our hope,

      Read 1 Timothy 1:1 in the app

  2. authority (1)
    With authority, with the force of command: “with all authority”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Titus 2:15

      Ταῦτα λάλει καὶ παρακάλει καὶ ἔλεγχε μετὰ πάσης ἐπιταγῆς· μηδείς σου περιφρονείτω.

      Say these things and exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no one despise you.

      Read Titus 2:15 in the app
