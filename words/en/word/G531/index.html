Senses (NGSD)

  1. unchangeable (1)
    Not changing, not passing away: “a priesthood that does not change”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 7:24

      ὁ δὲ διὰ τὸ μένειν αὐτὸν εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα ἀπαράβατον ἔχει τὴν ἱερωσύνην·

      But he, because he lives forever, has his priesthood unchangeable.
