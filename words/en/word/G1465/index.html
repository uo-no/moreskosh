ἐγκόπτω enkoptō G1465

Extended collation (NT only) — no LXX or Fathers occurrences; senses rest on NT usage alone

Occurrences in the New Testament

5

Senses (NGSD)

  1. to hinder (4)
    To hinder, to obstruct: “so that your prayers may not be hindered”, “Satan hindered us”, “who hindered you from obeying the truth”, “I have been hindered from coming to you”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 15:22

      Διὸ καὶ ἐνεκοπτόμην τὰ πολλὰ τοῦ ἐλθεῖν πρὸς ὑμᾶς·

      Therefore also I was hindered these many times from coming to you,

      Read Romans 15:22 in the app

    • Galatians 5:7

      Ἐτρέχετε καλῶς· τίς ὑμᾶς ἐνέκοψεν τῇ ἀληθείᾳ μὴ πείθεσθαι;

      You were running well! Who interfered with you that you should not obey the truth?

      Read Galatians 5:7 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 2:18

      διότι ἠθελήσαμεν ἐλθεῖν πρὸς ὑμᾶς, ἐγὼ μὲν Παῦλος καὶ ἅπαξ καὶ δίς, καὶ ἐνέκοψεν ἡμᾶς ὁ Σατανᾶς.

      because we wanted to come to you—indeed, I, Paul, once and again—but Satan hindered us.

      Read 1 Thessalonians 2:18 in the app

  2. to detain, to weary (1)
    To trouble someone at length, to take up their time: “so as not to detain you any further”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 24:4

      ἵνα δὲ μὴ ἐπὶ πλεῖόν σε ἐγκόπτω, παρακαλῶ ἀκοῦσαί σε ἡμῶν συντόμως τῇ σῇ ἐπιεικείᾳ.

      But that I don’t delay you, I entreat you to bear with us and hear a few words.

      Read Acts 24:4 in the app
