Senses (NGSD)

  1. grace (God's saving and transforming grace) (108)
    Saving grace given by God unilaterally, not on the basis of human merit. Refers to God's transforming work flowing out through Christ's redemptive work, and holds the center of New Testament theology as the ground and power of salvation.

    • Luke 2:40

      Τὸ δὲ παιδίον ηὔξανεν καὶ ἐκραταιοῦτο πληρούμενον σοφίᾳ, καὶ χάρις θεοῦ ἦν ἐπ’ αὐτό.

      The child was growing, and was becoming strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom, and the grace of God was upon him.

      Read Luke 2:40 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 1:2

      χάρις ὑμῖν καὶ εἰρήνη ἀπὸ θεοῦ πατρὸς ἡμῶν καὶ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ.

      Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

      Read 2 Corinthians 1:2 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 1:2

      χάρις ὑμῖν καὶ εἰρήνη ἀπὸ θεοῦ πατρὸς καὶ κυρίου Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ.

      Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

      Read 2 Thessalonians 1:2 in the app

  2. favor (33)
    Goodwill and protective care directed toward a particular person or group. Used both of patronage in human relations (political consideration, social kindness) and of the special care God shows a particular person, carrying on the Old Testament usage "to find favor in the eyes" (חֵן).

    • Luke 1:30

      καὶ εἶπεν ὁ ἄγγελος αὐτῇ· Μὴ φοβοῦ, Μαριάμ, εὗρες γὰρ χάριν παρὰ τῷ θεῷ·

      The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

      Read Luke 1:30 in the app

    • Acts 25:3

      αἰτούμενοι χάριν κατ’ αὐτοῦ ὅπως μεταπέμψηται αὐτὸν εἰς Ἰερουσαλήμ, ἐνέδραν ποιοῦντες ἀνελεῖν αὐτὸν κατὰ τὴν ὁδόν.

      asking a favor against him, that he would summon him to Jerusalem, plotting to kill him on the way.

      Read Acts 25:3 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 8:7

      ἀλλ’ ὥσπερ ἐν παντὶ περισσεύετε, πίστει καὶ λόγῳ καὶ γνώσει καὶ πάσῃ σπουδῇ καὶ τῇ ἐξ ἡμῶν ἐν ὑμῖν ἀγάπῃ, ἵνα καὶ ἐν ταύτῃ τῇ χάριτι περισσεύητε.

      But as you abound in everything—in faith, utterance, knowledge, all earnestness, and in your love to us—see that you also abound in this grace.

  3. thanks / gratitude (12)
    Gratitude for grace or favor received, or its expression. Appears mostly in the fixed expression χάριν ἔχω (to give thanks).

    • Luke 17:9

      μὴ ἔχει χάριν τῷ δούλῳ ὅτι ἐποίησεν τὰ διαταχθέντα;

      Does he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded? I think not.

      But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

      Read 1 Corinthians 15:57 in the app

      Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly; in all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your heart to the Lord.

  4. grace / charm (of speech) (2)
    The graceful and attractive quality of speech or manner of speaking. Refers to the dignity of words that draws the hearer.

    • Luke 4:22

      All testified about him and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth; and they said, “Isn’t this Joseph’s son?”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

155 χάρις, -ιτος, ἡ Abbott-Smith does not include the article here., acc., χάριν (χάριτα in Ac 24:27, Ju 4; v. WH, App., 157), [in LXX chiefly for חֵן ;] objectively, that which causes favourable regard, gracefulness, grace, loveliness of form, graciousness of speech (cl.; Ec 10:12, Si 21:16, al.): Col 4:6; λόγοι τ. χάριτος (gen. qual.), Lk 4:22. Subjectively, on the part of the giver, grace, graciousness, kindness, goodwill, favour: Lk 2:52, Ac 7:10, al.; esp. in NT of the divine favour, grace, with emphasis on its freeness and universality: Lk 1:30, Ac 14:26, Ro 1:7, I Co 1:3, al.; opp. to ὀφείλημα, Ro 4:4, 16; to ἔργα, Ro 11:6; on the part of the receiver, a sense of favour received, thanks, gratitude: Ro 6:17 7:25, al.; χ. ἔχειν, to be thankful, Lk 17:9, I Ti 1:12, al. Objectively, of the effect of grace, a state of grace: Ro 5:2, II Ti 2:1, I Pe 5:12, II Pe 3:18; a proof or gift of grace (cl., a favour): Jo 1:16, Ac 6:8, Ro 1:5, I Co 3:10, II Co 9:8, Ga 2:9, Eph 3:2, I Pe 5:5, 10 al. (For fuller treatment of the NT usage, v. AR, Eph., 221 ff.; DB, ii, 254 ff.; DCG, i, 686 ff.; Cremer, s.v.).
