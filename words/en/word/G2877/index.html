Senses (NGSD)

  1. girl (8)
    A girl, a young girl, a maiden: “little girl, arise”, “the girl got up and began walking”, “the head was brought on a platter and given to the girl”, “the girl went out and said to her mother”, “she came in immediately with haste to the king”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      he said to them, “Make room, because the girl isn’t dead, but sleeping.” They were ridiculing him.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 κοράσιον, -ου, τό, (dimin. of κόρη), [in LXX chiefly for נַעֲרָה, Ru 2:8, al.; in Jl 3 (4):3, Za 8:5 for יַלְדָּה;] a colloquial word which survives in MGr. (Kennedy, Sourcs, 154), girl, maiden: Mt 9:24, 25 14:11, Mk 5:41, 42 6:22, 28.†
