Senses (NGSD)

  1. darnel / weeds (tares) (8)
    A weed resembling wheat, darnel: “an enemy came and sowed weeds among the wheat”, “the weeds appeared also”, “where have the weeds come from?”, “wanting to gather the weeds”, “explain to us the parable of the weeds”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 13:25

      ἐν δὲ τῷ καθεύδειν τοὺς ἀνθρώπους ἦλθεν αὐτοῦ ὁ ἐχθρὸς καὶ ἐπέσπειρεν ζιζάνια ἀνὰ μέσον τοῦ σίτου καὶ ἀπῆλθεν.

      but while people slept, his enemy came and sowed darnel weeds also among the wheat, and went away.

    • Matthew 13:27

      The servants of the householder came and said to him, ‘Sir, didn’t you sow good seed in your field? Where did these darnel weeds come from?’
