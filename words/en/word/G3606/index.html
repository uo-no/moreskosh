Senses (NGSD)

  1. from where / from which (source, place) (8)
    “From there”, “from which”, indicating source or place: “to my house from which I came”, “from there we sailed”, “by this we know it is the last hour”, “reaping where you did not sow”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 12:44

      τότε λέγει· Εἰς τὸν οἶκόν μου ἐπιστρέψω ὅθεν ἐξῆλθον· καὶ ἐλθὸν εὑρίσκει σχολάζοντα σεσαρωμένον καὶ κεκοσμημένον.

      Then he says, ‘I will return into my house from which I came;’ and when he has come back, he finds it empty, swept, and put in order.

      “But his lord answered him, ‘You wicked and slothful servant. You knew that I reap where I didn’t sow, and gather where I didn’t scatter.

      From there we circled around and arrived at Rhegium. After one day, a south wind sprang up, and on the second day we came to Puteoli,
