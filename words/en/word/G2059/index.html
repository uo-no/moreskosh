Senses (NGSD)

  1. to translate, to mean (3)
    To translate, to mean, to interpret (passive: to be translated). “when its name is translated”, “meaning ‘one who is sent’”, “when translated …”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 1:42

      ἤγαγεν αὐτὸν πρὸς τὸν Ἰησοῦν. ἐμβλέψας αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν· Σὺ εἶ Σίμων ὁ υἱὸς Ἰωάννου, σὺ κληθήσῃ Κηφᾶς ὃ ἑρμηνεύεται Πέτρος.

      He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him and said, “You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas” (which is by interpretation, Peter).
