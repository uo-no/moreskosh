Senses (NGSD)

  1. to charge to account (2)
    To reckon, to impute: “charge that to my account”, “sin is not reckoned”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 5:13

      ἄχρι γὰρ νόμου ἁμαρτία ἦν ἐν κόσμῳ, ἁμαρτία δὲ οὐκ ἐλλογεῖται μὴ ὄντος νόμου,

      For until the law, sin was in the world; but sin is not charged when there is no law.

      Read Romans 5:13 in the app

    • Philemon 1:18

      εἰ δέ τι ἠδίκησέν σε ἢ ὀφείλει, τοῦτο ἐμοὶ ἐλλόγα·

      But if he has wronged you at all or owes you anything, put that to my account.

2 *† ἐλλογάω, -ῶ (a κοινή word, elsewhere usually -έω; cf. Bl., § 22, 2), to charge to one's account, impute: Phm 18 (on parallels, cf. Deiss., LAE, 79 f., 335 f.; Milligan, NTD, 73; MM, Exp., xii); of sin, Ro 5:13 (Cremer, 400).†
