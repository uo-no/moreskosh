Senses (NGSD)

  1. to speak the truth (2)
    To tell the truth, to hold to the truth: “speaking the truth in love”, “because I told the truth”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Galatians 4:16

      ὥστε ἐχθρὸς ὑμῶν γέγονα ἀληθεύων ὑμῖν;

      So then, have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?

      Read Galatians 4:16 in the app

    • Ephesians 4:15

      ἀληθεύοντες δὲ ἐν ἀγάπῃ αὐξήσωμεν εἰς αὐτὸν τὰ πάντα, ὅς ἐστιν ἡ κεφαλή, Χριστός,

      but speaking truth in love, we may grow up in all things into him who is the head, Christ,
