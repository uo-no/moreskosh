Senses (NGSD)

  1. council of elders (3)
    The assembly of elders: “the laying on of hands by the council of elders”, “the whole council of elders”, “the elders of the people”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

      As soon as it was day, the assembly of the elders of the people were gathered together, both chief priests and scribes, and they led him away into their council, saying,

3 **† πρεσβυτέριον, -ου, τό (< πρεσβύτερος), [in LXX: Da TH Su 50 A*;] a body of elders, presbytery: of the Sanhedrin (cf. συνέδριον), Lk 22:66, Ac 22:5; of Christian presbyters, I Ti 4:14.†
