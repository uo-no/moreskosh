Senses (NGSD)

  1. distress, hardship (4)
    Distress, straits, an impasse: “tribulation, hardship”, “hemmed in”, “affliction and anguish”, “distresses and persecutions”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 2:9

      θλῖψις καὶ στενοχωρία, ἐπὶ πᾶσαν ψυχὴν ἀνθρώπου τοῦ κατεργαζομένου τὸ κακόν, Ἰουδαίου τε πρῶτον καὶ Ἕλληνος·

      oppression, and anguish on every soul of man who does evil, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 στενοχωρία, -ας, ἡ (v. supr.) [in LXX for צוּקָה, etc. ;] narrowness of space, want of room (Thuc., al.). Metaph. (Xen., Polyb., De 28:53, Wi 5:3, al.), difficulty, distress: θλίψις καὶ (ἢ) σ., Ro 2:9 8:35 (EV, anguish); pl. (cf. Bl., § 32, 6; WM, 220), II Co 6:4 12:10.† SYN.: θλίψις, q.v
