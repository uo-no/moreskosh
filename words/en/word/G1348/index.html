Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 δικαστής, -οῦ, ὁ (< δικάζω), [in LXX for שֹׁפֵט ;] a judge: Ac 7:27, 35 (LXX).† SYN.: κριτής, wh. "gives prominence to the mental process" (Thayer). δ. is the forensic term. In Attic law, the δικασταί were jurors, with a κριτής as presiding officer (LS, s.v.; Enc. Brit.11, xii, 504 f.).
