Senses (NGSD)

  1. to spare / refrain from harshness (9)
    To spare, to show leniency: “to spare you”, “out of consideration for you”, “I will not spare”, “God did not spare the angels who sinned”, “did not spare the ancient world”, “fierce wolves not sparing the flock”, “did not spare the natural branches”, “did not spare his own Son”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 20:29

      ἐγὼ οἶδα ὅτι εἰσελεύσονται μετὰ τὴν ἄφιξίν μου λύκοι βαρεῖς εἰς ὑμᾶς μὴ φειδόμενοι τοῦ ποιμνίου,

      For I know that after my departure, vicious wolves will enter in among you, not sparing the flock.

      Read Acts 20:29 in the app

    • Romans 11:21

      εἰ γὰρ ὁ θεὸς τῶν κατὰ φύσιν κλάδων οὐκ ἐφείσατο, οὐδὲ σοῦ φείσεται.

      for if God didn’t spare the natural branches, neither will he spare you.

      Read Romans 11:21 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 13:2

      προείρηκα καὶ προλέγω ὡς παρὼν τὸ δεύτερον καὶ ἀπὼν νῦν, τοῖς προημαρτηκόσιν καὶ τοῖς λοιποῖς πᾶσιν, ὅτι ἐὰν ἔλθω εἰς τὸ πάλιν οὐ φείσομαι,

      I have warned previously, and I warn again, as when I was present the second time, so now, being absent, I write to those who have sinned before now and to all the rest that if I come again, I will not spare,

      Read 2 Corinthians 13:2 in the app

  2. to refrain (1)
    To hold back from: “though I could boast, I refrain” (2 Cor 12:6).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Corinthians 12:6

      ἐὰν γὰρ θελήσω καυχήσασθαι, οὐκ ἔσομαι ἄφρων, ἀλήθειαν γὰρ ἐρῶ· φείδομαι δέ, μή τις εἰς ἐμὲ λογίσηται ὑπὲρ ὃ βλέπει με ἢ ἀκούει τι ἐξ ἐμοῦ,

      For if I would desire to boast, I will not be foolish; for I will speak the truth. But I refrain, so that no man may think more of me than that which he sees in me or hears from me.

      Read 2 Corinthians 12:6 in the app
