Senses (NGSD)

  1. to give in marriage (8)
    To give in marriage (passive: to be married off): “marrying and giving in marriage”, “are given in marriage”, “they were marrying and being given in marriage”, “neither marry nor are given in marriage”, “he who gives his daughter in marriage does well”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 22:30

      ἐν γὰρ τῇ ἀναστάσει οὔτε γαμοῦσιν οὔτε γαμίζονται, ἀλλ’ ὡς ἄγγελοι θεοῦ ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ εἰσιν·

      For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like God’s angels in heaven.

      For when they will rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.
