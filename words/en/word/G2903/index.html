Senses (NGSD)

  1. most excellent (honorific) (4)
    An honorific form of address: ‘most excellent’. “most excellent lord Felix”, “your excellency Festus”, “most excellent Theophilus”, “most excellent Diognetus”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Luke 1:3

      ἔδοξε κἀμοὶ παρηκολουθηκότι ἄνωθεν πᾶσιν ἀκριβῶς καθεξῆς σοι γράψαι, κράτιστε Θεόφιλε,

      it seemed good to me also, having traced the course of all things accurately from the first, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus;

      Read Luke 1:3 in the app

    • Acts 23:26

      Κλαύδιος Λυσίας τῷ κρατίστῳ ἡγεμόνι Φήλικι χαίρειν.

      “Claudius Lysias to the most excellent governor Felix: Greetings.

      Read Acts 23:26 in the app

    • Acts 24:3

      πάντῃ τε καὶ πανταχοῦ ἀποδεχόμεθα, κράτιστε Φῆλιξ, μετὰ πάσης εὐχαριστίας.

      we accept it in all ways and in all places, most excellent Felix, with all thankfulness.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 κράτιστος, -η, -ον, superl. of κρατύς (Hom.), strongest, mightiest (Hom.). noblest, best (cf. κρείσσων), most excellent (Pind., Soph., al.): voc., κρατίστε, as title of honour and respect (DCG, ii, 727a), Lk 1:3, Ac 23:26 24:3 26:25.†
