Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be contained, to stand written (1)
    To be contained, to be written: “it stands written in Scripture”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Peter 2:6

      διότι περιέχει ἐν γραφῇ· Ἰδοὺ τίθημι ἐν Σιὼν λίθον ἀκρογωνιαῖον ἐκλεκτὸν ἔντιμον, καὶ ὁ πιστεύων ἐπ’ αὐτῷ οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ.

      Because it is contained in Scripture, “Behold, I lay in Zion a chief cornerstone, chosen and precious. He who believes in him will not be disappointed.”

      Read 1 Peter 2:6 in the app

  2. to seize, to surround (1)
    To encompass, to come over: “astonishment came over him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 5:9

      θάμβος γὰρ περιέσχεν αὐτὸν καὶ πάντας τοὺς σὺν αὐτῷ ἐπὶ τῇ ἄγρᾳ τῶν ἰχθύων ὧν συνέλαβον,

      For he was amazed, and all who were with him, at the catch of fish which they had caught;

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 περι-έχω, [in LXX for אפף, נקף hi., צפה pi., etc.;] to surround, encompass: Lk 5:9 (cf. MM, xix). to comprehend, include, contain, esp. of books: c. acc., Ac 23:25, Rec.; acc. to a late usage, intrans. (Bl., § 53, 13; MM, xix; Hort, in l.), it stands written, I Pe 2:6.†
