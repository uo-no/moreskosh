Senses (NGSD)

  1. to turn away from / desert (5)
    To turn one's back, leave, repel: “all have turned away from me”, “turn their ears from the truth”, “do not reject him who speaks”, “people who turn away from the truth”, “do not turn away from the one who would borrow”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:42

      τῷ αἰτοῦντί σε δός, καὶ τὸν θέλοντα ἀπὸ σοῦ δανίσασθαι μὴ ἀποστραφῇς.

      Give to him who asks you, and don’t turn away him who desires to borrow from you.

      Read Matthew 5:42 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 1:15

      Οἶδας τοῦτο ὅτι ἀπεστράφησάν με πάντες οἱ ἐν τῇ Ἀσίᾳ, ὧν ἐστιν Φύγελος καὶ Ἑρμογένης.

      This you know, that all who are in Asia turned away from me, of whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.

      Read 2 Timothy 1:15 in the app

    • Titus 1:14

      μὴ προσέχοντες Ἰουδαϊκοῖς μύθοις καὶ ἐντολαῖς ἀνθρώπων ἀποστρεφομένων τὴν ἀλήθειαν.

      not paying attention to Jewish fables and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.

      Read Titus 1:14 in the app

  2. to put back (1)
    To return something to its place: “put your sword back into its place” (Matt 26:52).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 26:52

      τότε λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Ἀπόστρεψον τὴν μάχαιράν σου εἰς τὸν τόπον αὐτῆς, πάντες γὰρ οἱ λαβόντες μάχαιραν ἐν μαχαίρῃ ἀπολοῦνται·

      Then Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back into its place, for all those who take the sword will die by the sword.

      Read Matthew 26:52 in the app

  3. to turn (people) away from sin (2)
    To draw people away from sin and iniquity: “to bless you by turning you from your wickedness”, “he will banish ungodliness from Jacob”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 3:26

      ὑμῖν πρῶτον ἀναστήσας ὁ θεὸς τὸν παῖδα αὐτοῦ ἀπέστειλεν αὐτὸν εὐλογοῦντα ὑμᾶς ἐν τῷ ἀποστρέφειν ἕκαστον ἀπὸ τῶν πονηριῶν ὑμῶν.

      God, having raised up his servant Jesus, sent him to you first to bless you, in turning away every one of you from your wickedness.”

      Read Acts 3:26 in the app

    • Romans 11:26

      καὶ οὕτως πᾶς Ἰσραὴλ σωθήσεται· καθὼς γέγραπται· Ἥξει ἐκ Σιὼν ὁ ῥυόμενος, ἀποστρέψει ἀσεβείας ἀπὸ Ἰακώβ.

      and so all Israel will be saved. Even as it is written, “There will come out of Zion the Deliverer, and he will turn away ungodliness from Jacob.

      Read Romans 11:26 in the app

  4. to mislead / turn aside (1)
    To lead the people astray, to turn them away: “as one who was misleading the people” (Luke 23:14).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 23:14

      εἶπεν πρὸς αὐτούς· Προσηνέγκατέ μοι τὸν ἄνθρωπον τοῦτον ὡς ἀποστρέφοντα τὸν λαόν, καὶ ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ ἐνώπιον ὑμῶν ἀνακρίνας οὐθὲν εὗρον ἐν τῷ ἀνθρώπῳ τούτῳ αἴτιον ὧν κατηγορεῖτε κατ’ αὐτοῦ.

      and said to them, “You brought this man to me as one that perverts the people, and behold, having examined him before you, I found no basis for a charge against this man concerning those things of which you accuse him.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

44 ἀπο-στρέφω, [in LXX chiefly for שׁוּב;] trans., c. acc., to turn away, remove: Ro 11:26, II Ti 4:4; metaph., to turn away from allegiance, pervert: Lk 23:14; to turn back, return: μάχαιραν, Mt 26:52. Pass., reflex., to turn oneself away from: c. acc., Mt 5:42, II Ti 1:15, Tit 1:14, He 12:25; so act., absol., Ac 3:26 (cf. Si 8:5; Bl., § 53, 1; Cremer, 880).†
