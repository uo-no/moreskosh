Senses (NGSD)

  1. ever, at any time (6)
    Ever, at any time (with a negative: ‘never’). “a colt on which no one has ever sat”, “no one has ever yet seen God”, “you have never heard his voice”, “we have never been slaves to anyone”, “whoever comes to me shall never hunger”, “no one has ever seen God”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      saying, “Go your way into the village on the other side, in which, as you enter, you will find a colt tied, which no man has ever sat upon. Untie it and bring it.
