Senses (NGSD)

  1. to endure, to bear (4)
    To bear, to put up with, to cover: “bears all things”, “we endure everything”, “when we could bear it no longer”, “when I could bear it no longer”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 9:12

      εἰ ἄλλοι τῆς ὑμῶν ἐξουσίας μετέχουσιν, οὐ μᾶλλον ἡμεῖς; Ἀλλ’ οὐκ ἐχρησάμεθα τῇ ἐξουσίᾳ ταύτῃ, ἀλλὰ πάντα στέγομεν ἵνα μή τινα ἐγκοπὴν δῶμεν τῷ εὐαγγελίῳ τοῦ Χριστοῦ.

      If others partake of this right over you, don’t we yet more? Nevertheless we didn’t use this right, but we bear all things, that we may cause no hindrance to the Good News of Christ.

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:12 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 13:7

      πάντα στέγει, πάντα πιστεύει, πάντα ἐλπίζει, πάντα ὑπομένει.

      bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, and endures all things.

      Read 1 Corinthians 13:7 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 3:1

      Διὸ μηκέτι στέγοντες εὐδοκήσαμεν καταλειφθῆναι ἐν Ἀθήναις μόνοι,

      Therefore when we couldn’t stand it any longer, we thought it good to be left behind at Athens alone,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 ** στέγω (cf. Lat. tego), [in LXX: Si 8:17 * ;] prop., to cover closely, to protect by covering, esp. to keep water in or out (Soph., Plat., al.). to cover, keep secret, conceal (Si, l.c., Polyb., al.): I Co 13:7, R, mg. By covering to ward of, bear up under, endure (for exx., v., Lft., Notes, 40; M, Th., 36): I Co 9:12 13:7, I Th 3:1, 5.†
