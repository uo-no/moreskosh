Senses (NGSD)

  1. poor person / poor (31)
    An economically poor person, a beggar, one with no possessions.

    • Matthew 11:5

      τυφλοὶ ἀναβλέπουσιν καὶ χωλοὶ περιπατοῦσιν, λεπροὶ καθαρίζονται καὶ κωφοὶ ἀκούουσιν, καὶ νεκροὶ ἐγείρονται καὶ πτωχοὶ εὐαγγελίζονται·

      the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.

    A figurative expression for things and basic principles that are powerless and lacking.

      But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, why do you turn back again to the weak and miserable elemental principles, to which you desire to be in bondage all over again?

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

34 πτωχός, -ή, -όν (< πτώσσω, to crouch, cower), [in LXX for עָנִי, דַּל, רָשׁ, etc.;] of one who crouches and cowers, hence, as subst., a beggar: Lk 14:13, 21 16:20, 22. As adj., prop., beggarly: metaph., στοιχεῖα, Ga 4:9 (v. Lft., in l.); in broader sense (opp. to πλούσιος), poor: Mt 11:5 19:21 26:9, 11, Mk 10:21 12:42, 43 14:5, 7, Lk 4:18 7:22 18:22 19:8 21:3, Jo 12:5, 6, 8 13:29, Ro 15:26, II Co 6:10, Ga 2:10, Ja 2:2, 3, 6, Re 13:16; π. τ. κόσμῳ, Ja 2:5; metaph., Lk 6:20, Re 3:17; π. τ. πνεύματι, Mt 5:3.† SYN.: πένης, q.v.
