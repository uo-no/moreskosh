Senses (NGSD)

  1. orphan (2)
    An orphan, a fatherless child: “orphans and widows”, “I will not leave you as orphans”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 14:18

      Οὐκ ἀφήσω ὑμᾶς ὀρφανούς, ἔρχομαι πρὸς ὑμᾶς.

      I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you.

      Read John 14:18 in the app

    • James 1:27

      θρησκεία καθαρὰ καὶ ἀμίαντος παρὰ τῷ θεῷ καὶ πατρὶ αὕτη ἐστίν, ἐπισκέπτεσθαι ὀρφανοὺς καὶ χήρας ἐν τῇ θλίψει αὐτῶν, ἄσπιλον ἑαυτὸν τηρεῖν ἀπὸ τοῦ κόσμου.

      Pure religion and undefiled before our God and Father is this: to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained by the world.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ὀρφανός, -ή, -όν, [in LXX for יָתוֹם;] prop., orphan, fatherless: Mk 12:40, WH, mg, Ja 1:27. In a general sense (as also in cl.; v. LS, s.v.; and cf. MM, xviii), bereft, friendless, desolate: Jo 14:18.†
