Senses (NGSD)

  1. little children (address) (8)
    A little child; used as an affectionate address: “little children, yet a little while I am with you”, “little children, let no one deceive you”, “little children, let us not love in word or talk”, “I am writing these things to you, little children”, “little children, you are from God”, “little children, keep yourselves from idols”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 13:33

      τεκνία, ἔτι μικρὸν μεθ’ ὑμῶν εἰμι· ζητήσετέ με, καὶ καθὼς εἶπον τοῖς Ἰουδαίοις ὅτι Ὅπου ἐγὼ ὑπάγω ὑμεῖς οὐ δύνασθε ἐλθεῖν, καὶ ὑμῖν λέγω ἄρτι.

      Little children, I will be with you a little while longer. You will seek me, and as I said to the Jews, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come,’ so now I tell you.

    • 1 John 2:12

      Γράφω ὑμῖν, τεκνία, ὅτι ἀφέωνται ὑμῖν αἱ ἁμαρτίαι διὰ τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ·

      I write to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven you for his name’s sake.

      Read 1 John 2:12 in the app

    • 1 John 3:18

      Τεκνία, μὴ ἀγαπῶμεν λόγῳ μηδὲ τῇ γλώσσῃ ἀλλὰ ἐν ἔργῳ καὶ ἀληθείᾳ.

      My little children, let’s not love in word only, or with the tongue only, but in deed and truth.
