Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 ἄστρον, -ου, τό, [in LXX chiefly for כּוֹכָב;] mostly in pl. (as in cl.), the stars: Lk 21:25, Ac 27:20, He 11:12; in sing. (Xen., al.), only of some noted star; the symbol or image of a star, Ac 7:43 (cf. ἀστήρ, and v. MM, s.v.).†
