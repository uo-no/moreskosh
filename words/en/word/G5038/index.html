Senses (NGSD)

  1. wall (of a city) (9)
    A city wall: “let him down in a basket through the wall”, “the walls of Jericho fell down”, “the city had a great, high wall”, “the wall of the city had twelve foundations”, “its wall, 144 cubits”, “the wall was built of jasper”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      but his disciples took him by night and let him down through the wall, lowering him in a basket.
