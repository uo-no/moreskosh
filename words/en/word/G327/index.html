Senses (NGSD)

  1. to search for (3)
    To seek, to look for: “to look for Saul”, “searched all over”, “as they were searching”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 2:44

      νομίσαντες δὲ αὐτὸν εἶναι ἐν τῇ συνοδίᾳ ἦλθον ἡμέρας ὁδὸν καὶ ἀνεζήτουν αὐτὸν ἐν τοῖς συγγενεῦσιν καὶ τοῖς γνωστοῖς,

      but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day’s journey; and they looked for him among their relatives and acquaintances.

      Read Luke 2:44 in the app

    • Luke 2:45

      καὶ μὴ εὑρόντες ὑπέστρεψαν εἰς Ἰερουσαλὴμ ἀναζητοῦντες αὐτόν.

      When they didn’t find him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for him.

      Read Luke 2:45 in the app

    • Acts 11:25

      ἐξῆλθεν δὲ εἰς Ταρσὸν ἀναζητῆσαι Σαῦλον,

      Barnabas went out to Tarsus to look for Saul.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 ἀνα-ζητέω, -ῶ [in LXX: Jb 3:4 (דרשׁ), Jb 10:6 (בּקשׁ pi.), II Mac 13:21*;] to look for or seek carefully ("specially of searching for human beings, with an implication of difficulty": MM, VGT, s.v.): Lk 2:44, 45, Ac 11:25.†
