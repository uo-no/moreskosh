Senses (NGSD)

  1. possession, property (4)
    Possessions, property, land: “property and belongings”, “sold land”, “because they had many possessions”, “had great possessions”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 19:22

      ἀκούσας δὲ ὁ νεανίσκος τὸν λόγον ἀπῆλθεν λυπούμενος, ἦν γὰρ ἔχων κτήματα πολλά.

      But when the young man heard this, he went away sad, for he was one who had great possessions.
