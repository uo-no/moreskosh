Senses (NGSD)

  1. to gather (3)
    To gather, to assemble: “many people had gathered”, “gathering those of the same trade”, “those who were gathered together”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 24:33

      καὶ ἀναστάντες αὐτῇ τῇ ὥρᾳ ὑπέστρεψαν εἰς Ἰερουσαλήμ, καὶ εὗρον ἠθροισμένους τοὺς ἕνδεκα καὶ τοὺς σὺν αὐτοῖς,

      They rose up that very hour, returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and those who were with them,
