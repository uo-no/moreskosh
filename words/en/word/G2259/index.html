Senses (NGSD)

  1. when (2)
    When, whenever: “to this day”, “when one turns to the Lord”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 2 Corinthians 3:15

      But to this day, when Moses is read, a veil lies on their heart.
