Senses (NGSD)

  1. to supply, to fill up (4)
    To make good what is lacking, to fill up: “they made up for what you lacked”, “one who is in the seat of someone without knowledge”, “filling up their sins”, “to supply what was lacking in your service to me”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 14:16

      ἐπεὶ ἐὰν εὐλογῇς πνεύματι, ὁ ἀναπληρῶν τὸν τόπον τοῦ ἰδιώτου πῶς ἐρεῖ τὸ Ἀμήν ἐπὶ τῇ σῇ εὐχαριστίᾳ; ἐπειδὴ τί λέγεις οὐκ οἶδεν·

      Otherwise, if you bless with the spirit, how will he who fills the place of the unlearned say the “Amen” at your giving of thanks, seeing he doesn’t know what you say?

      Read 1 Corinthians 14:16 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 16:17

      χαίρω δὲ ἐπὶ τῇ παρουσίᾳ Στεφανᾶ καὶ Φορτουνάτου καὶ Ἀχαϊκοῦ, ὅτι τὸ ὑμέτερον ὑστέρημα οὗτοι ἀνεπλήρωσαν,

      I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus; for that which was lacking on your part, they supplied.

      Read 1 Corinthians 16:17 in the app

    • Philippians 2:30

      ὅτι διὰ τὸ ἔργον Χριστοῦ μέχρι θανάτου ἤγγισεν, παραβολευσάμενος τῇ ψυχῇ ἵνα ἀναπληρώσῃ τὸ ὑμῶν ὑστέρημα τῆς πρός με λειτουργίας.

      because for the work of Christ he came near to death, risking his life to supply that which was lacking in your service toward me.

      Read Philippians 2:30 in the app

  2. to fulfill (the law) (1)
    To fulfill the law: “fulfil the law of Christ”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Galatians 6:2

      ἀλλήλων τὰ βάρη βαστάζετε, καὶ οὕτως ἀναπληρώσετε τὸν νόμον τοῦ Χριστοῦ.

      Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.

      Read Galatians 6:2 in the app

  3. to be fulfilled (prophecy) (1)
    For a prophecy to be fulfilled: “Isaiah’s prophecy … is being fulfilled”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 13:14

      καὶ ἀναπληροῦται αὐτοῖς ἡ προφητεία Ἠσαΐου ἡ λέγουσα· Ἀκοῇ ἀκούσετε καὶ οὐ μὴ συνῆτε, καὶ βλέποντες βλέψετε καὶ οὐ μὴ ἴδητε.

      In them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled, which says, ‘By hearing you will hear, and will in no way understand; Seeing you will see, and will in no way perceive;

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ἀνα-πληρόω, -ῶ, [in LXX chiefly for מלא, Le 12:6, al.; also שׁלם (Ge 15:16, III Ki 7:51, Is 60:20), etc.;] to fill up, make full (in π. of completing contracts and making up rent; cf. MM, VGT, s.v.): τόπον, take one's place (cf. Heb. מָקוֹם מִלֵּא), I Co 14:16; άμαρτίας, complete the number, I Th 2:16; τ. νόμον, observe perfectly, Ga 6:2; pass., προφητεία, fulfilled, Mt 13:14. to supply: τὸ ὑστέρημα, I Co 16:17, Phl 2:30 (Cremer, 838).†
