Senses (NGSD)

  1. fragment / broken piece (9)
    A broken piece, a fragment of bread: “gather up the fragments”, “twelve baskets of broken pieces left over”, “took up seven baskets full of the pieces left”, “the leftover fragments”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 14:20

      καὶ ἔφαγον πάντες καὶ ἐχορτάσθησαν, καὶ ἦραν τὸ περισσεῦον τῶν κλασμάτων δώδεκα κοφίνους πλήρεις.

      They all ate and were filled. They took up twelve baskets full of that which remained left over from the broken pieces.
