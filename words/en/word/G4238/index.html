Senses (NGSD)

  1. to do / practice (16)
    To do or carry out an act or task (neutral, whether good or bad): τὰ ἴδια πράσσειν, ἔργα πράσσοντας, τοῦτο πράσσω, νόμον πράσσῃς, τί μέλλετε πράσσειν, etc.

    • Luke 22:23

      καὶ αὐτοὶ ἤρξαντο συζητεῖν πρὸς ἑαυτοὺς τὸ τίς ἄρα εἴη ἐξ αὐτῶν ὁ τοῦτο μέλλων πράσσειν.

      They began to question among themselves which of them it was who would do this thing.

    • Acts 19:36

      ἀναντιρρήτων οὖν ὄντων τούτων δέον ἐστὶν ὑμᾶς κατεσταλμένους ὑπάρχειν καὶ μηδὲν προπετὲς πράσσειν.

      Seeing then that these things can’t be denied, you ought to be quiet and to do nothing rash.

      Read Acts 19:36 in the app

    • Romans 7:15

      ὃ γὰρ κατεργάζομαι οὐ γινώσκω· οὐ γὰρ ὃ θέλω τοῦτο πράσσω, ἀλλ’ ὃ μισῶ τοῦτο ποιῶ.

      For I don’t understand what I am doing. For I don’t practice what I desire to do; but what I hate, that I do.

      Read Romans 7:15 in the app

  2. to do (evil / sin) (18)

      Neither has Herod, for I sent you to him, and see, nothing worthy of death has been done by him.

    • Acts 25:11

      For if I have done wrong and have committed anything worthy of death, I don’t refuse to die; but if none of those things is true that they accuse me of, no one can give me up to them. I appeal to Caesar!”

  3. to fare (be in a condition) (2)
    In the forms εὖ πράσσειν／τί πράσσω, states a person's condition and circumstances: εὖ πράξετε (you will fare well), τί πράσσω (how I am doing).

    • Acts 15:29

      ἀπέχεσθαι εἰδωλοθύτων καὶ αἵματος καὶ πνικτῶν καὶ πορνείας· ἐξ ὧν διατηροῦντες ἑαυτοὺς εὖ πράξετε. ἔρρωσθε.

      that you abstain from things sacrificed to idols, from blood, from things strangled, and from sexual immorality, from which if you keep yourselves, it will be well with you. Farewell.”

      But that you also may know my affairs, how I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make known to you all things.

  4. to exact / collect (2)
    To exact tax or interest: τὸ διατεταγμένον πράσσετε (do not collect more than what was appointed), σὺν τόκῳ ἔπραξα.

    • Luke 3:13

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

39 πράσσω, (Att. -ττω, and so Ac 17:7 Rec.; cf. M, Pr., 25, 45), [in LXX chiefly for עשׂה, פָּעַל;] = Lat agere, as ποιέω (q.v.) = facere, to do, practise, be engaged in: Ac 19:19, 36, I Co 9:17; τ. ἴδια π., to mind one's own business (τὰ ἑαυτοῦ, Soph., Plat.), I Th 4:11; intrans., to act, Ac 17:7. to achieve, effect, accomplish, perform: Ac 26:20, 26, Ro 7:15 9:11, II Co 5:10, Phl 4:9; νόμον (ICC, in l.), Ro 2:25; of unworthy acts (for wh. usually ποιέω in cl.), to commit, do: Lk 22:23 23:41, Jo 3:20 5:29, Ac 3:17 5:35 16:28 25:11, 25 26:9, 31, Ro 1:32 (ICC, in l.), 2:1-3 7:19 13:4, I Co 5:2, II Co 12:21, Ga 5:21. to transact, manage, hence, of payment, to exact (cl.): Lk 3:13 19:23. Reflexively, of state or condition, to do or fare (Æsch., Hdt., al.): Eph 6:21; εὖ π. (v. M, Pr., 228 f.), Ac 15:29.† SYN.: v.s. ποιέω.
