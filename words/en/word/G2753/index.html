Senses (NGSD)

  1. to command / order (25)
    To direct someone by word of mouth that some act be done. The one commanding is in most cases someone with authority (governor, tribune, king, master, etc.), and the act is expressed by an infinitive (often passive).
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 8:18

      Ἰδὼν δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς πολλοὺς ὄχλους περὶ αὐτὸν ἐκέλευσεν ἀπελθεῖν εἰς τὸ πέραν.

      Now when Jesus saw great multitudes around him, he gave the order to depart to the other side.
