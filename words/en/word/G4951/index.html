Senses (NGSD)

  1. to drag (5)
    To drag, to haul: “dragged out”, “dragging”, “dragging off men and women”, “hauling the net”, “his tail swept a third of the stars of heaven”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • John 21:8

      οἱ δὲ ἄλλοι μαθηταὶ τῷ πλοιαρίῳ ἦλθον, οὐ γὰρ ἦσαν μακρὰν ἀπὸ τῆς γῆς ἀλλὰ ὡς ἀπὸ πηχῶν διακοσίων, σύροντες τὸ δίκτυον τῶν ἰχθύων.

      But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits away), dragging the net full of fish.
