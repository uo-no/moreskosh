Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 * πιθανολογία, -ας, ἡ (< πιθανός, persuasive, plausible), in cl. (Plat), the use of probable arguments, as opp. to demonstration (ἀπόδειξις); hence, persuasive speech: Col 2:4 (v. ICC, in l.).†
