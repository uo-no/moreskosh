Senses (NGSD)

  1. to receive / get back (9)
    To receive, to be recompensed, to get back: “you will receive the unfading crown of glory”, “receiving the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls”, “receive what is due for good or evil”, “the wrongdoer will be paid back for the wrong”, “will receive back from the Lord”, “receive what is promised”, “received him back from the dead”, “received back with interest”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 25:27

      ἔδει σε οὖν βαλεῖν τὰ ἀργύριά μου τοῖς τραπεζίταις, καὶ ἐλθὼν ἐγὼ ἐκομισάμην ἂν τὸ ἐμὸν σὺν τόκῳ.

      You ought therefore to have deposited my money with the bankers, and at my coming I should have received back my own with interest.

      Read Matthew 25:27 in the app

    • Colossians 3:25

      ὁ γὰρ ἀδικῶν κομίσεται ὃ ἠδίκησεν, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν προσωπολημψία.

      But he who does wrong will receive again for the wrong that he has done, and there is no partiality.

      Read Colossians 3:25 in the app

    • Hebrews 11:39

      καὶ πάντες μαρτυρηθέντες διὰ τῆς πίστεως οὐκ ἐκομίσαντο τὴν ἐπαγγελίαν,

      These all, having been commended for their faith, didn’t receive the promise,

      Read Hebrews 11:39 in the app

  2. to bring (1)
    To bring, to carry: “brought an alabaster flask of ointment” (Luke 7:37).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 7:37

      καὶ ἰδοὺ γυνὴ ἥτις ἦν ἐν τῇ πόλει ἁμαρτωλός, καὶ ἐπιγνοῦσα ὅτι κατάκειται ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ τοῦ Φαρισαίου, κομίσασα ἀλάβαστρον μύρου

      Behold, a woman in the city who was a sinner, when she knew that he was reclining in the Pharisee’s house, brought an alabaster jar of ointment.

      Read Luke 7:37 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 κομίζω, [in LXX for נשׂא, etc.;] to take care of. to carry off safe. to carry off as booty. to bear or carry: Lk 7:37. Mid., to bear for oneself, hence, to receive: He 10:36 11:13, 39, I Pe 1:9 5:4, II Pe 2:13; to receive back, recover (in cl. so also act.): Mt 25:27, He 11:19; metaph., of requital, II Co 5:10, Col 3:25; παρὰ Κυρίου, Eph 6:8 (cf. ἐκ-, συν-κομίζω).†
