Senses (NGSD)

  1. to dwell in a tent, to tabernacle (5)
    To live in a tent, to dwell: “dwelt among us”, “you who dwell in them”, “those who dwell in heaven”, “he will dwell with them”, “will spread his tent over them”. The overtone of “pitching a tent” and the range of “dwelling” are not decided.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • John 1:14

      Καὶ ὁ λόγος σὰρξ ἐγένετο καὶ ἐσκήνωσεν ἐν ἡμῖν, καὶ ἐθεασάμεθα τὴν δόξαν αὐτοῦ, δόξαν ὡς μονογενοῦς παρὰ πατρός, πλήρης χάριτος καὶ ἀληθείας·

      The Word became flesh and lived among us. We saw his glory, such glory as of the only born Son of the Father, full of grace and truth.
