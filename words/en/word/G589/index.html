Senses (NGSD)

  1. to go abroad, to go on a journey (6)
    To travel far away, to go to another country: “went to a distant country”, “went on a journey”, “let out the vineyard and went abroad”, “as when a man going on a journey”, “went on a journey”, “went on a journey”.
    Attested: NT only
