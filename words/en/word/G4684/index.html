Senses (NGSD)

  1. to live in luxury (2)
    To live luxuriously, to indulge in pleasure: “she who lives in self-indulgence”, “lived in luxury”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Timothy 5:6

      ἡ δὲ σπαταλῶσα ζῶσα τέθνηκεν.

      But she who gives herself to pleasure is dead while she lives.

      Read 1 Timothy 5:6 in the app

    • James 5:5

      ἐτρυφήσατε ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς καὶ ἐσπαταλήσατε, ἐθρέψατε τὰς καρδίας ὑμῶν ἐν ἡμέρᾳ σφαγῆς.

      You have lived in luxury on the earth, and taken your pleasure. You have nourished your hearts as in a day of slaughter.
