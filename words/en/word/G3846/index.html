Senses (NGSD)

  1. to cross over (1)
    To pass across, to draw near: “arrived at Samos”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 20:15

      κἀκεῖθεν ἀποπλεύσαντες τῇ ἐπιούσῃ κατηντήσαμεν ἄντικρυς Χίου, τῇ δὲ ἑτέρᾳ παρεβάλομεν εἰς Σάμον, τῇ δὲ ἐχομένῃ ἤλθομεν εἰς Μίλητον·

      Sailing from there, we came the following day opposite Chios. The next day we touched at Samos and stayed at Trogyllium, and the day after we came to Miletus.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 παρα-βάλλω, [in LXX: Pr 2:2 4:20 5:1, 13 22:17 (נטה hi.), Ru 2:16 (שׁלל), II Mac 14:38 (elsewhere as v.l.4)*;] to throw to or beside, as fodder to horses (Hom., al.). to lay beside, compare (Hdt., Plat., al.): Mk 4:30, Rec. Reflexive, to betake oneself, come near; of seamen, to cross over: εἰς Σάμον, Ac 20:15. In mid. = παρα-βολεύομαι, q.v.†
