Senses (NGSD)

  1. to recline at table, to seat (5)
    To take one’s place at table; to have people sit down to eat: “go and sit in the lowest place”, “when he had reclined at table”, “took his place at the table”, “have them sit down in groups of fifty”, “made everyone sit down”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 7:36

      Ἠρώτα δέ τις αὐτὸν τῶν Φαρισαίων ἵνα φάγῃ μετ’ αὐτοῦ· καὶ εἰσελθὼν εἰς τὸν οἶκον τοῦ Φαρισαίου κατεκλίθη.

      One of the Pharisees invited him to eat with him. He entered into the Pharisee’s house and sat at the table.

5 κατα-κλίνω, [in LXX: Ex 21:18 (נפל), Nu 24:9, Jg 5:27, (כּרע), I Ki 16:11 (סבב), Jth 12:15, III Mac 1:3*;] to lay down, make to lie down, esp. for meals: c. acc. pers., Lk 9:14, 15; mid., to recline at meals: Lk 7:36 24:30; seq. εἰς, Lk 14:8 (cf. Jth 12:15).†
