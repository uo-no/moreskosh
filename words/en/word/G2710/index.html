Senses (NGSD)

  1. to make full use of (2)
    To use to the full, to use up: “not making full use of my right in the gospel”, “those who use the things of the world”. The representative examples in the LXX appear to be the use to misuse, and this use has not been confirmed.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 7:31

      καὶ οἱ χρώμενοι τὸν κόσμον ὡς μὴ καταχρώμενοι· παράγει γὰρ τὸ σχῆμα τοῦ κόσμου τούτου.

      and those who use the world, as not using it to the fullest. For the mode of this world passes away.

    • 1 Corinthians 9:18

      τίς οὖν μού ἐστιν ὁ μισθός; ἵνα εὐαγγελιζόμενος ἀδάπανον θήσω τὸ εὐαγγέλιον, εἰς τὸ μὴ καταχρήσασθαι τῇ ἐξουσίᾳ μου ἐν τῷ εὐαγγελίῳ.

      What then is my reward? That when I preach the Good News, I may present the Good News of Christ without charge, so as not to abuse my authority in the Good News.
