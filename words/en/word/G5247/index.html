Senses (NGSD)

  1. excellence (1)
    Superiority, being outstanding: “with superior words or wisdom”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Corinthians 2:1

      Κἀγὼ ἐλθὼν πρὸς ὑμᾶς, ἀδελφοί, ἦλθον οὐ καθ’ ὑπεροχὴν λόγου ἢ σοφίας καταγγέλλων ὑμῖν τὸ μαρτύριον τοῦ θεοῦ.

      When I came to you, brothers, I didn’t come with excellence of speech or of wisdom, proclaiming to you the testimony of God.

      Read 1 Corinthians 2:1 in the app

  2. high position (1)
    High rank, authority: “all who are in high position”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Timothy 2:2

      ὑπὲρ βασιλέων καὶ πάντων τῶν ἐν ὑπεροχῇ ὄντων, ἵνα ἤρεμον καὶ ἡσύχιον βίον διάγωμεν ἐν πάσῃ εὐσεβείᾳ καὶ σεμνότητι.

      for kings and all who are in high places, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and reverence.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ὑπεροχή, -ῆς, ἡ (< ὑπερέχω) [in LXX: Je 52:22 (קוֹמָה), II Mac 3:11, al. ;] a projection, eminence, as the peak of a mountain. Metaph. (Arist.), excellence, pre-eminence: λόγου ἢ σοφίας, I Co 2:1; οἱ ἐν ὑ. (for a parallel to this phrase, v. Deiss., BS, 255, and cf. II Mac, l.c.), I Ti 2:2.†
