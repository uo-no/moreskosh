Senses (NGSD)

  1. to strike / hit (8)
    To strike, to smite, to strike down: “struck Peter on the side”, “an angel of the Lord struck Herod”, “struck down the Egyptian”, “shall we strike with the sword?”, “struck the servant of the high priest”, “I will strike the shepherd”, “drew his sword and struck”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 26:31

      Τότε λέγει αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Πάντες ὑμεῖς σκανδαλισθήσεσθε ἐν ἐμοὶ ἐν τῇ νυκτὶ ταύτῃ, γέγραπται γάρ· Πατάξω τὸν ποιμένα, καὶ διασκορπισθήσονται τὰ πρόβατα τῆς ποίμνης·

      Then Jesus said to them, “All of you will be made to stumble because of me tonight, for it is written, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 πατάσσω, [in LXX chiefly and very freq. for נכה hi., also for נגף, etc.;] in Hom., intrans., to beat (of the heart). Trans. = πλήσσω, to strike, smite: absol., ἐν μαχαίρᾳ, Lk 22:49; c. acc. pers., Mt 26:51, Lk 22:50; c. acc. rei, Ac 12:7; of a deadly blow, Mt 26:31, Mk 14:27, Ac 7:24. Metaph., of disease: Ac 12:23, Re 11:6 19:15.†
