Senses (NGSD)

  1. fellow servant (10)
    A fellow servant of the same master, a fellow slave: “fellow servants”, “our beloved fellow servant”, “his fellow servant”, “I am a fellow servant with you and your brothers”, “until the number of their fellow servants who were to be killed should be complete”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 18:28

      ἐξελθὼν δὲ ὁ δοῦλος ἐκεῖνος εὗρεν ἕνα τῶν συνδούλων αὐτοῦ ὃς ὤφειλεν αὐτῷ ἑκατὸν δηνάρια, καὶ κρατήσας αὐτὸν ἔπνιγεν λέγων· Ἀπόδος εἴ τι ὀφείλεις.

      “But that servant went out and found one of his fellow servants who owed him one hundred denarii, and he grabbed him and took him by the throat, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’
