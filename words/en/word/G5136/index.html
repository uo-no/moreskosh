Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be laid bare (1)
    To be exposed, to have the throat bared: “laid bare”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Hebrews 4:13

      καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν κτίσις ἀφανὴς ἐνώπιον αὐτοῦ, πάντα δὲ γυμνὰ καὶ τετραχηλισμένα τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς αὐτοῦ, πρὸς ὃν ἡμῖν ὁ λόγος.

      There is no creature that is hidden from his sight, but all things are naked and laid open before the eyes of him to whom we must give an account.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 † τραχηλίζω (< τράχηλος), prop., of wrestlers, to take by the throat, hence (Philo), to prostrate, overthrow. Metaph., but in what sense and from what age it is doubtful (v. Westc., Rendall, on He, l.c.; DB, iii, 625n), τραχηλισμένα, laid open (RV, Westc.; downcast, Rendall): He 4:13.†
