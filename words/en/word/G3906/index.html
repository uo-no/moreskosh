Senses (NGSD)

  1. to watch closely (5)
    To watch attentively, to keep an eye on: “they watched the gates day and night”, “were watching Jesus closely”, “watching for an opportunity”, “were watching Jesus”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 3:2

      καὶ παρετήρουν αὐτὸν εἰ τοῖς σάββασιν θεραπεύσει αὐτόν, ἵνα κατηγορήσωσιν αὐτοῦ.

      They watched him, whether he would heal him on the Sabbath day, that they might accuse him.

      Read Mark 3:2 in the app

    • Luke 6:7

      παρετηροῦντο δὲ οἱ γραμματεῖς καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι εἰ ἐν τῷ σαββάτῳ θεραπεύει, ἵνα εὕρωσιν κατηγορεῖν αὐτοῦ.

      The scribes and the Pharisees watched him, to see whether he would heal on the Sabbath, that they might find an accusation against him.

      Read Luke 6:7 in the app

    • Luke 20:20

      Καὶ παρατηρήσαντες ἀπέστειλαν ἐγκαθέτους ὑποκρινομένους ἑαυτοὺς δικαίους εἶναι, ἵνα ἐπιλάβωνται αὐτοῦ λόγου, ὥστε παραδοῦναι αὐτὸν τῇ ἀρχῇ καὶ τῇ ἐξουσίᾳ τοῦ ἡγεμόνος.

      They watched him and sent out spies, who pretended to be righteous, that they might trap him in something he said, so as to deliver him up to the power and authority of the governor.

      Read Luke 20:20 in the app

  2. to observe (days, seasons) (1)
    To keep or attach importance to days, months, seasons and years: “you observe days and months and seasons and years”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Galatians 4:10

      ἡμέρας παρατηρεῖσθε καὶ μῆνας καὶ καιροὺς καὶ ἐνιαυτούς.

      You observe days, months, seasons, and years.

      Read Galatians 4:10 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 παρα-τηρέω, -ῶ, [in LXX: Ps 36 (37):12 (זמם), 129 (130):8 (שׁמר), Da TH 6:11 (12), Su 1:12, 15, 16*;] to watch closely, observe narrowly: so mid., τ. πύλας, Ac 9:24; with evil intent, Mk 3:2, Lk 20:20 (absol., v. Field, Notes, 74); so mid., Lk 6:7 14:1. to observe scrupulously (of days and seasons; cf. Ex 12:42, Sm.): mid., Ga 4:10.†
