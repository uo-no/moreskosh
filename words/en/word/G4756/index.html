Senses (NGSD)

  1. host, army (2)
    An army, a great multitude: “all the host of heaven”, “the heavenly host”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 2:13

      καὶ ἐξαίφνης ἐγένετο σὺν τῷ ἀγγέλῳ πλῆθος στρατιᾶς οὐρανίου αἰνούντων τὸν θεὸν καὶ λεγόντων·

      Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God and saying,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 στρατιά, -ᾶς, ἡ [in LXX chiefly for צָבָא ;] = στρατός, an army, a host: of angels (III Ki 22:19, al.), Lk 2:13; of the stars of heaven (Je 8:2, II Ch 33:3), Ac 7:42. As sometimes in cl. (poët.), = στρατεία: II Co 10:4, T (WH, RV, στρατεία).†
