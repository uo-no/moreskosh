Senses (NGSD)

  1. inscription (5)
    The legend on a coin or on the cross: “whose image and inscription is this”, “the inscription of the charge”, “the inscription of the charge against him read …”, “the inscription of the charge”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 22:20

      καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς· Τίνος ἡ εἰκὼν αὕτη καὶ ἡ ἐπιγραφή;

      He asked them, “Whose is this image and inscription?”

      They brought it. He said to them, “Whose is this image and inscription?” They said to him, “Caesar’s.”
