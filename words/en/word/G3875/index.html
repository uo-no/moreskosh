Senses (NGSD)

  1. advocate, intercessor (1)
    An advocate, one who pleads for another: “we have an advocate with the Father”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 John 2:1

      Τεκνία μου, ταῦτα γράφω ὑμῖν ἵνα μὴ ἁμάρτητε. καὶ ἐάν τις ἁμάρτῃ, παράκλητον ἔχομεν πρὸς τὸν πατέρα Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν δίκαιον,

      My little children, I write these things to you so that you may not sin. If anyone sins, we have a Counselor with the Father, Jesus Christ, the righteous.

      Read 1 John 2:1 in the app

  2. Helper, Paraclete (4)
    The Helper (the Holy Spirit): “another Helper”, “the Helper, that is, the Spirit of truth”, “when the Helper comes”, “the Helper … will not come to you”. The range of the meaning of ‘Helper’ (advocate, comforter, helper) is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 14:16

      κἀγὼ ἐρωτήσω τὸν πατέρα καὶ ἄλλον παράκλητον δώσει ὑμῖν ἵνα ᾖ μεθ’ ὑμῶν εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα,

      I will pray to the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, that he may be with you forever:

      Read John 14:16 in the app

    • John 14:26

      ὁ δὲ παράκλητος, τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον ὃ πέμψει ὁ πατὴρ ἐν τῷ ὀνόματί μου, ἐκεῖνος ὑμᾶς διδάξει πάντα καὶ ὑπομνήσει ὑμᾶς πάντα ἃ εἶπον ὑμῖν.

      But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things, and will remind you of all that I said to you.

      “When the Counselor has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will testify about me.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** παρά-κλητος, -ον (< παρακαλέω), [in Aq., Th.: Jb 16:2*;] called to one's aid in a judicial cause (Dion. Cass.); hence, most freq. as subst., ὁ π., an advocate, pleader, intercessor (Dem., al.; so in Rabbinic lit., פְּרַקְלִיטָא), "a friend of the accused person, called to speak to his character, or otherwise enlist sympathy in his favour" (Field, Notes, 102). In NT, specif., ὁ π., of Christ, I Jn 2:1 (v. ICC, Jo. Epp., 23 ff.); of the Holy Spirit (AV, comforter; but v. opp. c.), Jo 14:16, 26 15:26 16:7 (cf. also Abbott, Essays, 86, 97; Deiss., LAE, 339 f.; MM, xviii; Westc., Jo., ii, 188 ff.).
