Senses (NGSD)

  1. order (1)
    An order, a division of troops: “each in its own order”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • 1 Corinthians 15:23

      ἕκαστος δὲ ἐν τῷ ἰδίῳ τάγματι· ἀπαρχὴ Χριστός, ἔπειτα οἱ τοῦ Χριστοῦ ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ αὐτοῦ·

      But each in his own order: Christ the first fruits, then those who are Christ’s at his coming.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 τάγμα, -τος, τό (< τάσσω), [in LXX chiefly for דֶּגֶל, also for חַיָּה, רַגְלִי ;] that which has been arranged or placed in order; esp. as military term, a company, troop, division, rank: metaph., I Co 15:23 (v. ICC, in l.).†
