Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be amazed / astounded (middle, passive, intransitive) (13)
    To be astonished at an event, sign, or teaching, to marvel beside oneself: “all were amazed”, “astonished and wondering”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 12:23

      καὶ ἐξίσταντο πάντες οἱ ὄχλοι καὶ ἔλεγον· Μήτι οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ υἱὸς Δαυίδ;

      All the multitudes were amazed, and said, “Can this be the son of David?”

      Read Matthew 12:23 in the app

    • Luke 2:47

      ἐξίσταντο δὲ πάντες οἱ ἀκούοντες αὐτοῦ ἐπὶ τῇ συνέσει καὶ ταῖς ἀποκρίσεσιν αὐτοῦ.

      All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

      Read Luke 2:47 in the app

    • Acts 2:12

      ἐξίσταντο δὲ πάντες καὶ διηπόρουν, ἄλλος πρὸς ἄλλον λέγοντες· Τί θέλει τοῦτο εἶναι;

      They were all amazed and were perplexed, saying to one another, “What does this mean?”

      Read Acts 2:12 in the app

  2. to amaze / astonish (transitive) (2)
    To astonish people, to amaze them by magic and the like.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 8:9

      Ἀνὴρ δέ τις ὀνόματι Σίμων προϋπῆρχεν ἐν τῇ πόλει μαγεύων καὶ ἐξιστάνων τὸ ἔθνος τῆς Σαμαρείας, λέγων εἶναί τινα ἑαυτὸν μέγαν,

      But there was a certain man, Simon by name, who used to practice sorcery in the city and amazed the people of Samaria, making himself out to be some great one,

      Read Acts 8:9 in the app

    • Acts 8:11

      προσεῖχον δὲ αὐτῷ διὰ τὸ ἱκανῷ χρόνῳ ταῖς μαγείαις ἐξεστακέναι αὐτούς.

      They listened to him because for a long time he had amazed them with his sorceries.

      Read Acts 8:11 in the app

  3. to be out of one's mind (2)
    To lose one's senses, lose judgment, act beyond the norm.
    Attested: NT only

    • Mark 3:21

      καὶ ἀκούσαντες οἱ παρ’ αὐτοῦ ἐξῆλθον κρατῆσαι αὐτόν, ἔλεγον γὰρ ὅτι ἐξέστη.

      When his friends heard it, they went out to seize him; for they said, “He is insane.”

      Read Mark 3:21 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 5:13

      εἴτε γὰρ ἐξέστημεν, θεῷ· εἴτε σωφρονοῦμεν, ὑμῖν.

      For if we are beside ourselves, it is for God. Or if we are of sober mind, it is for you.

      Read 2 Corinthians 5:13 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

17 † ἐξ-ίστημι (also in Hellenistic -ιστάνω, Act 8:9), [in LXX forחרד , etc. (29 words in all) ;], causal in pres., impf., fut., 1 aor., to put out of its place; metaph., ἐ. τινά φρενῶν (Eur.), to drive one out of his senses, hence, absol., to confound, amaze: c. acc pers., Lk 24:22, Ac 8:9, 11. Intr. in pass. and mid., also in 2 aor., pf., plpf. act., seq. ἐκ or c. gen., to stand aside from, retire from; esp. τ. φρενῶν,to lose one's senses (Eur.), hence, absol.; to be beside oneself, be mad: Mk 3:21, II Co 5:13 (opp. to σωφρονεῖν); to be amazed, confounded: Mt 12:23, Mk 2:12 5:42 6:51, Lk 2:47 8:56, Ac 2:7, 12 8:13 9:21 10:45 12:16 (Cremer, 309).†
