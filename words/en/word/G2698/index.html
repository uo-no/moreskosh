Senses (NGSD)

  1. to do a favor (idiom) (2)
    Used in the idiom “to lay down a favor”, that is, to curry favor: “wishing to do the Jews a favour” (twice). This idiom has not been confirmed in the representative examples in the LXX.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 24:27

      Διετίας δὲ πληρωθείσης ἔλαβεν διάδοχον ὁ Φῆλιξ Πόρκιον Φῆστον· θέλων τε χάριτα καταθέσθαι τοῖς Ἰουδαίοις ὁ Φῆλιξ κατέλιπε τὸν Παῦλον δεδεμένον.

      But when two years were fulfilled, Felix was succeeded by Porcius Festus, and desiring to gain favor with the Jews, Felix left Paul in bonds.

    • Acts 25:9
