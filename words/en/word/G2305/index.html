Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 **† θειότης, -ητος, ἡ (< θεῖος) [in LXX: Wi 18:9*;] divine nature, divinity: Ro 1:20 (for ex. from π., v. MM, Exp., xv).† SYN.: θεότης, deity, godhead, divine personality. θει. = divine nature and properties, a summary term for the attributes of deity, differing from θεότης as quality or attribute from essence.
