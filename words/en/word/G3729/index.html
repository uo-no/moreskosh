Senses (NGSD)

  1. to rush (5)
    To rush, to move headlong toward: “they all … rushed into the theatre”, “rushed at him together”, “the herd of pigs rushed down the steep bank”, “the whole herd rushed”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:32

      καὶ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Ὑπάγετε. οἱ δὲ ἐξελθόντες ἀπῆλθον εἰς τοὺς χοίρους· καὶ ἰδοὺ ὥρμησεν πᾶσα ἡ ἀγέλη κατὰ τοῦ κρημνοῦ εἰς τὴν θάλασσαν, καὶ ἀπέθανον ἐν τοῖς ὕδασιν.

      He said to them, “Go!” They came out and went into the herd of pigs; and behold, the whole herd of pigs rushed down the cliff into the sea and died in the water.

5 ὁρμάω, -ῶ (< ὁρμή), [in LXX for שׁוּב, etc.;] causal, to set in motion, urge on. Intrans., to hasten on, rush: seq. εἰς, Mt 8:32, Mk 5:13, Lk 8:33, Ac 19:29; ἐπί, c. acc., Ac 7:57.†
