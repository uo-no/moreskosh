Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

22 πληγή, ῆς, ἡ (< πλήσσω), [in LXX chiefly for מַכָּה, also for מַגֶּפָה etc.;] a blow, stripe, wound: pl., Lk 10:30 12:48, Ac 16:23, 33, II Co 6:5 11:23; ἡ π. τ. θανάτου (RV, death-stroke), Re 13:3, 12; τ. μαχαίρας, Re 13:14. Metaph., a calamity, plague: Re 9:18, 20 11:6 15:1, 6, 8 16:9, 21 18:4, 8 21:9 22:18.†
