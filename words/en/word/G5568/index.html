Senses (NGSD)

  1. psalm (7)
    A psalm, a hymn of praise to God: “when you come together, one has a hymn”, “it is written in the Psalms”, “in the second psalm”, “with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs”, “in the Psalms”, “everything written about me in the Psalms”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 ψαλμός, -οῦ, ὁ (< ψάλλω), [in LXX chiefly for מִזְמוֹר ;] a striking, twitching with the fingers (Eur., al.), hence, a striking of musical strings (Æsch., al.), and hence in later writers, a sacred song sung to musical accompaniment, a psalm (LXX): I Co 14:26, Eph 5:19, Col 3:16; of OT psalms, Lk 24:44, Ac 13:33; βίβλος ψαλμῶν, Lk 20:42, Ac 1:20.† SYN.: v.s. ὕμνος
