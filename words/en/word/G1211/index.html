Senses (NGSD)

  1. come now, indeed (emphatic particle) (5)
    A particle expressing urging or emphasis: “come, let us go”, “sharing …”, “indeed”, “truly”, “now then”. Depending on its position in the word order, renderings range over ‘come’, ‘truly’, ‘so then’, but it is not divided into separate senses.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 δή consecutive co-ordinating particle with no exact equiv. in Eng., giving greater exactness and emphasis to the word or words to which it is attached; sometimes translatable as now therefore, then, verily, certainly. With verbs: imperat., Ac 6:3 (WH, mg.) 13:2, I Co 6:20; hort. subjc., Lk 2:15, Ac 15:36; indic., δή που (T, δήπου, q.v.), He 2:16. With pronouns: ὃ δή, now this is he who, Mt 13:23.†
