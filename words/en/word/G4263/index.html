Senses (NGSD)

  1. sheep (as an animal) (9)
    The sheep as an animal: πρόβατα καὶ βόες (the temple traders), the sheep that falls into a pit (on the Sabbath), the parable of the man with a hundred sheep, ἐν ἐνδύμασι προβάτων (sheep's clothing), the sheep among the goods in Revelation, etc.

      “Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves.

  2. sheep (figurative for people / disciples / believers) (24)
    In the image of shepherd and sheep, refers to God's people, Jesus' disciples, believers, Israel: τὰ πρόβατά μου (JHN 10 and 21), τὰ πρόβατα τὰ ἀπολωλότα οἴκου Ἰσραήλ, the separation of sheep and goats, sheep being scattered, ποιμήν and sheep, etc.

  3. ὡς πρόβατα (like sheep) (6)
    In the comparative form ὡς πρόβατα, people are likened to sheep: ὡς πρόβατα ἐν μέσῳ λύκων, ὡς πρόβατα μὴ ἔχοντα ποιμένα, ὡς πρόβατον ἐπὶ σφαγήν, etc.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

39 πρόβατον, -ου, τό (< προβαίνω), [in LXX chiefly for צֹאן, also for שֶׂה, more rarely for כֶּבֶשׂ (כֶּשֶׂב), רָחֵל;] in Hom., Hdt., cattle, esp. of small cattle, sheep and goats. In NT, as in Attic writers generally (cf. MM, xxi), a sheep: Mt 7:15, Mk 6:34, al.; πρόβατα σφαγῆς, Ro 8:36(LXX). Metaph. (in cl. of timidity, stupidity or idleness), of the followers of a leader or master, esp. of those who are subject to the care of the Good Shepherd: Mt 10:6 15:24 26:31(LXX), Mk 14:27(LXX), Jo 10:7, 8, 15, 16, 26, 27 21:16, 17 (WH txt., προβάτια), He 13:20; opp. to ἐρίφια, Mt 25:33.
