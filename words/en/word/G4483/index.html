Senses (NGSD)

  1. it was said (ἐρρέθη) (10)
    “It was said”, “it was told”: in the aorist passive form ἐρρέθη, introduces what was spoken or written: “it was said to those of old”, “she was told …”, “as it was said”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 5:21

      Ἠκούσατε ὅτι ἐρρέθη τοῖς ἀρχαίοις· Οὐ φονεύσεις· ὃς δ’ ἂν φονεύσῃ, ἔνοχος ἔσται τῇ κρίσει.

      “You have heard that it was said to the ancient ones, ‘You shall not murder;’ and ‘Whoever murders will be in danger of the judgment.’

      “Again you have heard that it was said to the ancient ones, ‘You shall not make false vows, but shall perform to the Lord your vows,’
