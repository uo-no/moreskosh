Senses (NGSD)

  1. to stir up, to provoke (2)
    To provoke, to rouse: “your zeal has stirred up most of them”, “do not provoke your children”. The difference between positive and negative does not divide the sense.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Corinthians 9:2

      οἶδα γὰρ τὴν προθυμίαν ὑμῶν ἣν ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν καυχῶμαι Μακεδόσιν ὅτι Ἀχαΐα παρεσκεύασται ἀπὸ πέρυσι, καὶ τὸ ὑμῶν ζῆλος ἠρέθισε τοὺς πλείονας.

      for I know your readiness, of which I boast on your behalf to those of Macedonia, that Achaia has been prepared for the past year. Your zeal has stirred up very many of them.

      Read 2 Corinthians 9:2 in the app

    • Colossians 3:21

      οἱ πατέρες, μὴ ἐρεθίζετε τὰ τέκνα ὑμῶν, ἵνα μὴ ἀθυμῶσιν.

      Fathers, don’t provoke your children, so that they won’t be discouraged.
