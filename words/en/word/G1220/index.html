Senses (NGSD)

  1. denarius (Roman silver coin) (16)
    A Roman silver coin, one denarius: used for a day labourer's daily wage, the coin of the tax, a unit of debt, the price of costly ointment, the cost of bread, and so on.
    Attested: NT only

      “But that servant went out and found one of his fellow servants who owed him one hundred denarii, and he grabbed him and took him by the throat, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’
