Senses (NGSD)

  1. merciful (1)
    Merciful, forbearing: “I will be merciful toward their wrongdoings”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Hebrews 8:12

      ὅτι ἵλεως ἔσομαι ταῖς ἀδικίαις αὐτῶν, καὶ τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν αὐτῶν οὐ μὴ μνησθῶ ἔτι.

      For I will be merciful to their unrighteousness. I will remember their sins and lawless deeds no more.”

      Read Hebrews 8:12 in the app

  2. God forbid (1)
    “Far from it”, “may it never be”: “Lord, may such a thing never happen”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 16:22

      καὶ προσλαβόμενος αὐτὸν ὁ Πέτρος ἤρξατο ἐπιτιμᾶν αὐτῷ λέγων· Ἵλεώς σοι, κύριε· οὐ μὴ ἔσται σοι τοῦτο.

      Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him, saying, “Far be it from you, Lord! This will never be done to you.”
