Senses (NGSD)

  1. more quickly, sooner (4)
    Sooner, quickly (comparative): “to you the sooner”, “sooner”, “very soon”, “ran ahead faster than Peter”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 13:27

      καὶ μετὰ τὸ ψωμίον τότε εἰσῆλθεν εἰς ἐκεῖνον ὁ Σατανᾶς. λέγει οὖν αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Ὃ ποιεῖς ποίησον τάχιον.

      After the piece of bread, then Satan entered into him. Then Jesus said to him, “What you do, do quickly.”
