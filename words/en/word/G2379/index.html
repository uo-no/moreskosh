Senses (NGSD)

  1. altar (of burnt offering in the temple) (12)
    The altar belonging to the temple or tabernacle in Jerusalem on which offerings and sacrifices are presented. Appears in expressions such as “place the gift on it”, “swear by it”, “serve at the altar”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 5:23

      ἐὰν οὖν προσφέρῃς τὸ δῶρόν σου ἐπὶ τὸ θυσιαστήριον κἀκεῖ μνησθῇς ὅτι ὁ ἀδελφός σου ἔχει τι κατὰ σοῦ,

      “If therefore you are offering your gift at the altar, and there remember that your brother has anything against you,

      Read Matthew 5:23 in the app

    • Matthew 23:19

      τυφλοί, τί γὰρ μεῖζον, τὸ δῶρον ἢ τὸ θυσιαστήριον τὸ ἁγιάζον τὸ δῶρον;

      You blind fools! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift?

      Read Matthew 23:19 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 9:13

      οὐκ οἴδατε ὅτι οἱ τὰ ἱερὰ ἐργαζόμενοι τὰ ἐκ τοῦ ἱεροῦ ἐσθίουσιν, οἱ τῷ θυσιαστηρίῳ παρεδρεύοντες τῷ θυσιαστηρίῳ συμμερίζονται;

      Don’t you know that those who serve around sacred things eat from the things of the temple, and those who wait on the altar have their portion with the altar?

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:13 in the app

  2. altar of incense (in the holy place) (1)
    The altar in the holy place on which incense is burned.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 1:11

      ὤφθη δὲ αὐτῷ ἄγγελος κυρίου ἑστὼς ἐκ δεξιῶν τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου τοῦ θυμιάματος.

      An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.

      Read Luke 1:11 in the app

  3. altar (in general, plural) (2)
    Not limited to a particular temple: an altar built for offering sacrifices. Abraham's altar for offering Isaac, the prophets' altars (plural).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 11:3

      Κύριε, τοὺς προφήτας σου ἀπέκτειναν, τὰ θυσιαστήριά σου κατέσκαψαν, κἀγὼ ὑπελείφθην μόνος, καὶ ζητοῦσιν τὴν ψυχήν μου.

      “Lord, they have killed your prophets. They have broken down your altars. I am left alone, and they seek my life.”

      Read Romans 11:3 in the app

    • James 2:21

      Ἀβραὰμ ὁ πατὴρ ἡμῶν οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων ἐδικαιώθη, ἀνενέγκας Ἰσαὰκ τὸν υἱὸν αὐτοῦ ἐπὶ τὸ θυσιαστήριον;

      Wasn’t Abraham our father justified by works, in that he offered up Isaac his son on the altar?

      Read James 2:21 in the app

  4. altar (as metaphor) (1)
    “We have an altar” (Heb 13:10): an altar from which those who serve the tabernacle have no right to eat.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Hebrews 13:10

      ἔχομεν θυσιαστήριον ἐξ οὗ φαγεῖν οὐκ ἔχουσιν ἐξουσίαν οἱ τῇ σκηνῇ λατρεύοντες.

      We have an altar from which those who serve the holy tabernacle have no right to eat.

      Read Hebrews 13:10 in the app

  5. altar in heaven (7)
    The altar before the heavenly temple and throne that appears in the visions of Revelation: incense is offered on it, the souls of the martyrs are under it, and the altar speaks.
    Attested: NT only

    • Revelation 6:9

      Καὶ ὅτε ἤνοιξεν τὴν πέμπτην σφραγῖδα, εἶδον ὑποκάτω τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου τὰς ψυχὰς τῶν ἐσφαγμένων διὰ τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ διὰ τὴν μαρτυρίαν ἣν εἶχον.

      When he opened the fifth seal, I saw underneath the altar the souls of those who had been killed for the Word of God, and for the testimony of the Lamb which they had.

      Read Revelation 6:9 in the app

    • Revelation 8:5

      καὶ εἴληφεν ὁ ἄγγελος τὸν λιβανωτόν, καὶ ἐγέμισεν αὐτὸν ἐκ τοῦ πυρὸς τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου, καὶ ἔβαλεν εἰς τὴν γῆν· καὶ ἐγένοντο βρονταὶ καὶ φωναὶ καὶ ἀστραπαὶ καὶ σεισμός.

      The angel took the censer, and he filled it with the fire of the altar, then threw it on the earth. Thunders, sounds, lightnings, and an earthquake followed.

      Read Revelation 8:5 in the app

    • Revelation 9:13

      Καὶ ὁ ἕκτος ἄγγελος ἐσάλπισεν· καὶ ἤκουσα φωνὴν μίαν ἐκ τῶν κεράτων τοῦ θυσιαστηρίου τοῦ χρυσοῦ τοῦ ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ,

      The sixth angel sounded. I heard a voice from the horns of the golden altar which is before God,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

23 † θυσιαστήριον, -ου, τό (< θυσιάζω, to sacrifice), [in LXX (where the word first appears) very freq., nearly always for מִזְבֵּחַ;] an altar: generally, Ja 2:21; pl., Ro 11:3(LXX); metaph., He 13:10 (v. Westc., in l., and esp. his add. note on the history of the word, 455 ff.); of the altar of burnt-offering in the Temple, Mt 5:23, 24 23:18-20, 35, Lk 11:51, I Co 9:13 10:18, He 7:13, Re 11:1; of the altar of incense in the sanctuary (Ex 30:1, al.), Lk 1:11; symbolically in Heaven, Re 6:9 8:3, 5 9:13 14:18 16:7 (Cremer, 292).†
