Senses (NGSD)

  1. outlet; fork of a road (1)
    An exit, a point where highways branch: “to the crossroads of the highways”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 22:9

      πορεύεσθε οὖν ἐπὶ τὰς διεξόδους τῶν ὁδῶν, καὶ ὅσους ἐὰν εὕρητε καλέσατε εἰς τοὺς γάμους.

      Go therefore to the intersections of the highways, and as many as you may find, invite to the wedding feast.’
