Senses (NGSD)

  1. to praise / extol (8)
    To praise, to extol: “praising God”, “walking about and praising God”, “a people praising God”, “a multitude of the heavenly host praising God”, “returned praising God”, “praise our God”, “praise the Lord, all you Gentiles”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 2:13

      καὶ ἐξαίφνης ἐγένετο σὺν τῷ ἀγγέλῳ πλῆθος στρατιᾶς οὐρανίου αἰνούντων τὸν θεὸν καὶ λεγόντων·

      Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God and saying,
