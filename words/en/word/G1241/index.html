Senses (NGSD)

  1. to gird, to tie around (3)
    To wrap around the waist, to fasten as a belt: “tied a towel around his waist”, “with the towel … that was wrapped around him”, “put on his outer garment”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 13:4

      ἐγείρεται ἐκ τοῦ δείπνου καὶ τίθησιν τὰ ἱμάτια καὶ λαβὼν λέντιον διέζωσεν ἑαυτόν·

      arose from supper, and laid aside his outer garments. He took a towel and wrapped a towel around his waist.

      Then he poured water into the basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him.
