Senses (NGSD)

  1. leather strap, thong (4)
    A leather strap, a whip: “as they were about to flog him”, “to untie the strap of the sandal”, “the sandal strap”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 1:7

      καὶ ἐκήρυσσεν λέγων· Ἔρχεται ὁ ἰσχυρότερός μου ὀπίσω μου, οὗ οὐκ εἰμὶ ἱκανὸς κύψας λῦσαι τὸν ἱμάντα τῶν ὑποδημάτων αὐτοῦ·

      He preached, saying, “After me comes he who is mightier than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and loosen.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 ἱμάς, -άντος, ὁ, [in LXX: Is 5:27 (שְׂרוֹךְ), Si 30:35 (33:26), IV Mac 9:11, al;] a thong, strap: for binding prisoners (IV Mac 9:11, Si 30:35 (33:26)), Ac 22:25; for fastening sandals, Mk 1:7, Lk 3:16, Jo 1:27.†
