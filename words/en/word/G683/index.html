Senses (NGSD)

  1. to push away, to reject (middle) (6)
    In the middle voice: to thrust away, to push off, to refuse. “having rejected a good conscience”, “you thrust it aside”, “pushed him aside”, “our fathers … thrust him away”, “has God rejected his people”, “God … has not rejected”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 7:27

      ὁ δὲ ἀδικῶν τὸν πλησίον ἀπώσατο αὐτὸν εἰπών· Τίς σε κατέστησεν ἄρχοντα καὶ δικαστὴν ἐφ’ ἡμῶν;

      But he who did his neighbor wrong pushed him away, saying, ‘Who made you a ruler and a judge over us?

      to whom our fathers wouldn’t be obedient, but rejected him and turned back in their hearts to Egypt,

6 ἀπ-ωθέω, -ῶ, [in LXX for זנח, מאס, etc.;] to thrust away. Mid., to thrust away from oneself, refuse, reject: c. acc. pers. (MM, s.v.), Ac 7:27, 39 13:46, Ro 11:1, 2, I Ti 1:19.†
