Senses (NGSD)

  1. to sing hymns (4)
    To sing a hymn, to sing praise: “singing hymns”, “I will sing your praise”, “after singing a hymn”, “when they had sung a hymn”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 26:30

      καὶ ὑμνήσαντες ἐξῆλθον εἰς τὸ Ὄρος τῶν Ἐλαιῶν.

      When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 (KJV) ὑμνέω, -ῶ (< ὕμνος) [in LXX for הָלַל pi., שִׁיר, יָדָה hi., etc. ;] trans., c. acc pers. (in cl. also c. acc. rei), to sing to, laud, sing to the praise of: Ac 16:25, He 2:12. Intrans., to sing: in LXX and NT, of singing hymns and praises to God (Ps 64 (65):13, al.), Mt 26:30, Mk 14:26 (v. Swete, in l.).†
