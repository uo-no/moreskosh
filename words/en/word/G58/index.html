Senses (NGSD)

  1. square / marketplace (11)
    A square, a marketplace, a public place: “children playing in the marketplaces”, “standing in the marketplace”, “greetings in the marketplaces”, “they do not eat what comes from the market unless they wash”, “dragged them into the marketplace”, “reasoned in the marketplace every day”.

      “But to what shall I compare this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces, who call to their companions
