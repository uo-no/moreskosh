Senses (NGSD)

  1. creeping thing, reptile (4)
    What creeps; a reptile: “creeping things of the earth”, “things that creep”, “reptiles”, “the likeness of creeping things”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 10:12

      ἐν ᾧ ὑπῆρχεν πάντα τὰ τετράποδα καὶ ἑρπετὰ τῆς γῆς καὶ πετεινὰ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ.

      in which were all kinds of four-footed animals of the earth, wild animals, reptiles, and birds of the sky.
