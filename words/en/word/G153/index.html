Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be ashamed (5)
    To be ashamed, to be put to shame: “so that we may not be ashamed at the Lord’s coming”, “if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed”, “I will not be put to shame”, “I am ashamed”, “that I may not be ashamed in anything”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 16:3

      εἶπεν δὲ ἐν ἑαυτῷ ὁ οἰκονόμος· Τί ποιήσω ὅτι ὁ κύριός μου ἀφαιρεῖται τὴν οἰκονομίαν ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ; σκάπτειν οὐκ ἰσχύω, ἐπαιτεῖν αἰσχύνομαι·

      “The manager said within himself, ‘What will I do, seeing that my lord is taking away the management position from me? I don’t have strength to dig. I am ashamed to beg.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 αἰσχύνω (< αἶσχος, shame), [in LXX chiefly for בּוּשׁ;] to disfigure (Hom.). to dishonour (Pr 29:15). to make ashamed (Si 13:7). Pass., to be put to shame, be ashamed: II Co 10:8; Phl 1:20, I Pe 4:16, I Jo 2:28; c. inf. (M, Pr., 205), Lk 16:3 (cf. ἐπ- (-ομαι), κατ-αἰσχύνω).†
