Senses (NGSD)

  1. pressure, mob gathering (2)
    A pressure bearing down; a crowding in upon: “the pressure that weighs on me”, “stirred up a crowd”. 2 Corinthians 11:28 has a textual variant, and the reading is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 24:12

      καὶ οὔτε ἐν τῷ ἱερῷ εὗρόν με πρός τινα διαλεγόμενον ἢ ἐπίστασιν ποιοῦντα ὄχλου οὔτε ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς οὔτε κατὰ τὴν πόλιν,

      In the temple they didn’t find me disputing with anyone or stirring up a crowd, either in the synagogues or in the city.

      Read Acts 24:12 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 11:28

      χωρὶς τῶν παρεκτὸς ἡ ἐπίστασίς μοι ἡ καθ’ ἡμέραν, ἡ μέριμνα πασῶν τῶν ἐκκλησιῶν.

      Besides those things that are outside, there is that which presses on me daily: anxiety for all the assemblies.
