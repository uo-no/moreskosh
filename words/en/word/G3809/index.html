Senses (NGSD)

  1. discipline, chastening (by God) (4)
    God’s chastening and training of God’s children: “do not despise the discipline of the Lord”, “endure it as discipline”, “the discipline in which all have shared”, “all discipline …”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Hebrews 12:5

      καὶ ἐκλέλησθε τῆς παρακλήσεως, ἥτις ὑμῖν ὡς υἱοῖς διαλέγεται, Υἱέ μου, μὴ ὀλιγώρει παιδείας κυρίου, μηδὲ ἐκλύου ὑπ’ αὐτοῦ ἐλεγχόμενος·

      You have forgotten the exhortation which reasons with you as with children, “My son, don’t take lightly the chastening of the Lord, nor faint when you are reproved by him;

      Read Hebrews 12:5 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:7

      εἰς παιδείαν ὑπομένετε· ὡς υἱοῖς ὑμῖν προσφέρεται ὁ θεός· τίς γὰρ υἱὸς ὃν οὐ παιδεύει πατήρ;

      It is for discipline that you endure. God deals with you as with children, for what son is there whom his father doesn’t discipline?

      Read Hebrews 12:7 in the app

    • Hebrews 12:8

      εἰ δὲ χωρίς ἐστε παιδείας ἧς μέτοχοι γεγόνασι πάντες, ἄρα νόθοι καὶ οὐχ υἱοί ἐστε.

      But if you are without discipline, of which all have been made partakers, then you are illegitimate, and not children.

      Read Hebrews 12:8 in the app

  2. instruction, upbringing (2)
    Education, upbringing, guidance by teaching: “training in righteousness”, “bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Ephesians 6:4

      Καὶ οἱ πατέρες, μὴ παροργίζετε τὰ τέκνα ὑμῶν, ἀλλὰ ἐκτρέφετε αὐτὰ ἐν παιδείᾳ καὶ νουθεσίᾳ κυρίου.

      You fathers, don’t provoke your children to wrath, but nurture them in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

      Read Ephesians 6:4 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 3:16

      πᾶσα γραφὴ θεόπνευστος καὶ ὠφέλιμος πρὸς διδασκαλίαν, πρὸς ἐλεγμόν, πρὸς ἐπανόρθωσιν, πρὸς παιδείαν τὴν ἐν δικαιοσύνῃ,

      Every Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 παιδεία (-ία, T), -ας, ἡ (< παιδεύω), [in LXX chiefly for מוּסָר;] the rearing of a child (Æsch.). training, learning, instruction (Plat., al.): Eph 6:4, II Ti 3:16. As in LXX (Pr 3:11 15:5, al.), chastening, discipline: He 12:5(LXX), 7, 8, 11.†
