Senses (NGSD)

  1. if (condition) (272)
    Marks a hypothetical condition. Also used in the form "unless (if not)."

  2. whoever (indefinite) (61)
    Combined with a relative, introduces a generalizing conditional clause meaning "whoever, whatever, whenever."

    • Matthew 5:32

      ἐγὼ δὲ λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι πᾶς ὁ ἀπολύων τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ παρεκτὸς λόγου πορνείας ποιεῖ αὐτὴν μοιχευθῆναι, καὶ ὃς ἐὰν ἀπολελυμένην γαμήσῃ μοιχᾶται.

      but I tell you that whoever puts away his wife, except for the cause of sexual immorality, makes her an adulteress; and whoever marries her when she is put away commits adultery.

(Bl., §65, 4; cf. Jo 13:17, I Co 7:36), if haply, if; c. subjc. (cl.); pres.: Mt 6:22, Lk 10:6, Jo 7:17, Ro 2:25, 26 al.; aor.
