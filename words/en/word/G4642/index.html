Senses (NGSD)

  1. hard, harsh (5)
    Hard, harsh, severe: “it is hard for you to kick against the goads”, “fierce winds”, “this is a hard saying”, “harsh words”, “a hard man”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “He also who had received the one talent came and said, ‘Lord, I knew you that you are a hard man, reaping where you didn’t sow, and gathering where you didn’t scatter.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 σκληρός, -ά, -όν (< σκέλλω, to dry), [in LXX chiefly for קָשָׁה ;] hard to the touch, rough, harsh, (opp. to μαλακός); metaph., in various uses; of men, hard, stern, severe: Mt 25:24; of things, hard, rough, violent: σκληρόν σοι (ἐστι), Ac 26:14; λόγος, Jo 6:60; ἄνεμος, Ja 3:4; σκληρὰ λαλεῖν, Ju 15.† SYN.: αὐστηρός, q.v
