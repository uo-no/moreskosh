Senses (NGSD)

  1. pretext, pretence (6)
    A pretext, show or evasion: “nor did we ever use a pretext for greed”, “on the pretext of letting down anchors”, “they have no excuse for their sin”, “long prayers for show”, “make long prayers for show”, “whether in pretence or in truth”. (The distinction between pretext or show and excuse is a continuum and is not made into separate senses.)
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 12:40

      οἱ κατεσθίοντες τὰς οἰκίας τῶν χηρῶν καὶ προφάσει μακρὰ προσευχόμενοι· οὗτοι λήμψονται περισσότερον κρίμα.

      those who devour widows’ houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”

      εἰ μὴ ἦλθον καὶ ἐλάλησα αὐτοῖς, ἁμαρτίαν οὐκ εἴχοσαν· νῦν δὲ πρόφασιν οὐκ ἔχουσιν περὶ τῆς ἁμαρτίας αὐτῶν.

      If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.
