Senses (NGSD)

  1. disobedience (7)
    Disobedience, refusal to obey: “sons of disobedience” (υἱοὶ τῆς ἀπειθείας), “because of disobedience the wrath of God comes”, “they could not enter the rest because of disobedience”, “lest anyone fall by the same example of disobedience”, “you received mercy through their disobedience”, “God has shut up all people in disobedience”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Romans 11:30

      ὥσπερ γὰρ ὑμεῖς ποτε ἠπειθήσατε τῷ θεῷ, νῦν δὲ ἠλεήθητε τῇ τούτων ἀπειθείᾳ,

      For as you in time past were disobedient to God, but now have obtained mercy by their disobedience,

    • Ephesians 2:2

      ἐν αἷς ποτε περιεπατήσατε κατὰ τὸν αἰῶνα τοῦ κόσμου τούτου, κατὰ τὸν ἄρχοντα τῆς ἐξουσίας τοῦ ἀέρος, τοῦ πνεύματος τοῦ νῦν ἐνεργοῦντος ἐν τοῖς υἱοῖς τῆς ἀπειθείας·

      in which you once walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit who now works in the children of disobedience.
