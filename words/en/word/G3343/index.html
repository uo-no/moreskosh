Senses (NGSD)

  1. to summon / send for (9)
    To send for a person, to have them brought: “send and call for Simon”, “to have you come to his house”, “sent for Paul”, “often sent for Paul”, “to summon Paul to Jerusalem”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 10:5

      καὶ νῦν πέμψον ἄνδρας εἰς Ἰόππην καὶ μετάπεμψαι Σίμωνά τινα ὃς ἐπικαλεῖται Πέτρος·

      Now send men to Joppa, and get Simon, who is also called Peter.
