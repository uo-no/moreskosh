Senses (NGSD)

  1. to grow dull (2)
    To become dull, to lose sensitivity: “this people’s heart has grown dull” (twice). The representative examples in the LXX and the Fathers are the literal use “to grow fat”, which is not the same use.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 13:15

      ἐπαχύνθη γὰρ ἡ καρδία τοῦ λαοῦ τούτου, καὶ τοῖς ὠσὶν βαρέως ἤκουσαν, καὶ τοὺς ὀφθαλμοὺς αὐτῶν ἐκάμμυσαν· μήποτε ἴδωσιν τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς καὶ τοῖς ὠσὶν ἀκούσωσιν καὶ τῇ καρδίᾳ συνῶσιν καὶ ἐπιστρέψωσιν, καὶ ἰάσομαι αὐτούς.

      for this people’s heart has grown callous, their ears are dull of hearing, and they have closed their eyes; or else perhaps they might perceive with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their heart, and would turn again, and I would heal them.’

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 παχύνω (< παχύς, thick), [in LXX: De 32:15, Is 6:10 (שָׁמֵן), al.;] to thicken, fatten; pass., to grow fat. Metaph., to make dull or stupid (τ. ψυχάς, Plut.); pass., to wax gross: ἡ καρδία, Mt 13:15, Ac 28:27 (LXX).†
