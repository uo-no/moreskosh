Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be indignant, to be angry (7)
    To be indignant or angry: “the ruler of the synagogue was indignant”, “when the ten heard this, they were indignant at the two brothers”, “they saw the children crying out in the temple and were indignant”, “when the disciples saw it, they were indignant”, “Jesus saw it and was indignant”, “the other ten heard it and were indignant”, “some were indignant”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 20:24

      Καὶ ἀκούσαντες οἱ δέκα ἠγανάκτησαν περὶ τῶν δύο ἀδελφῶν.

      When the ten heard it, they were indignant with the two brothers.
