Senses (NGSD)

  1. stadion (a distance) (6)
    A unit of distance, the stadion (about 185 meters): “Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia away”, “when they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia”, “sixty stadia from Jerusalem”, “several stadia from land”, “one thousand six hundred stadia”, “twelve thousand stadia”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 14:24

      τὸ δὲ πλοῖον ἤδη σταδίους πολλοὺς ἀπὸ τῆς γῆς ἀπεῖχεν, βασανιζόμενον ὑπὸ τῶν κυμάτων, ἦν γὰρ ἐναντίος ὁ ἄνεμος.

      But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, distressed by the waves, for the wind was contrary.

      Read Matthew 14:24 in the app

    • John 6:19

      ἐληλακότες οὖν ὡς σταδίους εἴκοσι πέντε ἢ τριάκοντα θεωροῦσιν τὸν Ἰησοῦν περιπατοῦντα ἐπὶ τῆς θαλάσσης καὶ ἐγγὺς τοῦ πλοίου γινόμενον, καὶ ἐφοβήθησαν.

      When therefore they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea and drawing near to the boat; and they were afraid.

      Read John 6:19 in the app

    • Revelation 14:20

      καὶ ἐπατήθη ἡ ληνὸς ἔξωθεν τῆς πόλεως, καὶ ἐξῆλθεν αἷμα ἐκ τῆς ληνοῦ ἄχρι τῶν χαλινῶν τῶν ἵππων ἀπὸ σταδίων χιλίων ἑξακοσίων.

      The wine press was trodden outside of the city, and blood came out of the wine press, up to the bridles of the horses, as far as one thousand six hundred stadia.

      Read Revelation 14:20 in the app

  2. stadium (1)
    The place where races are run, a stadium: “all the runners in the stadium run, but”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 9:24

      Οὐκ οἴδατε ὅτι οἱ ἐν σταδίῳ τρέχοντες πάντες μὲν τρέχουσιν, εἷς δὲ λαμβάνει τὸ βραβεῖον; οὕτως τρέχετε ἵνα καταλάβητε.

      Don’t you know that those who run in a race all run, but one receives the prize? Run like that, so that you may win.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 στάδιον, -ου, τό pl., στάδια (Jo 6:19, T), and metapl., στάδιοι (v. B1., § 9, 1), [in LXX: Da LXX 4:9, Su 37, II Mac 11:5 12:9 ff. * ;] a stadium, i.e. a measure of length = 600 Greek feet or 1⁄8 of a Roman mile Mt 14:24 (Rec., WH, txt., R, mg.), Lk 24:13, Jo 6:19 11:18, Re 14:20 21:16, and, this being the length of the Olympic course, a race-course: I Co 9:24.†
