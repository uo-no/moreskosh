Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be darkened (5)
    To become dark, to be darkened (including figurative uses): “the sun will be darkened”, “the sun is darkened”, “a third of the light of day was darkened”, “their foolish hearts were darkened”, “let their eyes be darkened”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 24:29

      Εὐθέως δὲ μετὰ τὴν θλῖψιν τῶν ἡμερῶν ἐκείνων ὁ ἥλιος σκοτισθήσεται, καὶ ἡ σελήνη οὐ δώσει τὸ φέγγος αὐτῆς, καὶ οἱ ἀστέρες πεσοῦνται ἀπὸ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ, καὶ αἱ δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν σαλευθήσονται.

      “But immediately after the suffering of those days, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light, the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken;

      But in those days, after that oppression, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light,
