Senses (NGSD)

  1. to adjure (2)
    To put under oath, to charge solemnly: “I adjure you by God”, “I charge you in the Lord”, “I implore you before God”. 1 Thessalonians 5:27 has the form ἐνορκίζω, which may be a separate lemma, and is left unassigned.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 5:7

      καὶ κράξας φωνῇ μεγάλῃ λέγει· Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί, Ἰησοῦ υἱὲ τοῦ θεοῦ τοῦ ὑψίστου; ὁρκίζω σε τὸν θεόν, μή με βασανίσῃς.

      and crying out with a loud voice, he said, “What have I to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I adjure you by God, don’t torment me.”
