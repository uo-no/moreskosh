Senses (NGSD)

  1. to lead out / bring out (12)
    To lead out, to bring out: “brought them out of Egypt”, “led them out of prison”, “leads out the sheep”, “led them out as far as Bethany”, “how the Lord had brought him out of prison”, “having brought them out”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 15:20

      καὶ ὅτε ἐνέπαιξαν αὐτῷ, ἐξέδυσαν αὐτὸν τὴν πορφύραν καὶ ἐνέδυσαν αὐτὸν τὰ ἱμάτια τὰ ἴδια. καὶ ἐξάγουσιν αὐτὸν ἵνα σταυρώσωσιν αὐτόν.

      When they had mocked him, they took the purple cloak off him, and put his own garments on him. They led him out to crucify him.

      But an angel of the Lord opened the prison doors by night, and brought them out and said,
