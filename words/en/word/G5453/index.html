Senses (NGSD)

  1. to grow, to spring up (3)
    To grow, to sprout: “sprang up”, “grew up”, “as it grew”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 8:6

      καὶ ἕτερον κατέπεσεν ἐπὶ τὴν πέτραν, καὶ φυὲν ἐξηράνθη διὰ τὸ μὴ ἔχειν ἰκμάδα.

      Some seed fell on the rock, and as soon as it grew, it withered away, because it had no moisture.

      Some fell into the good ground and grew and produced one hundred times as much fruit.” As he said these things, he called out, “He who has ears to hear, let him hear!”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 φύω [in LXX: Pr 26:9, Ez 37:8 (עלה), etc. ;] trans., to bring forth, produce (of men, to beget); pass., to spring up, grow: Lk 8:6, 8. Intrans., in 2 aor., pf., plpf., and rarely in pres. (v. LS, s.v., A, II.), to spring up: He 12:15.†
