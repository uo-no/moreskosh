Senses (NGSD)

  1. overflow, abundance (4)
    Overflowing, abundance, excess: “still more abundantly … according to our measure”, “extreme poverty … overflowed”, “rampant evil”, “abundant grace”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 2 Corinthians 8:2

      ὅτι ἐν πολλῇ δοκιμῇ θλίψεως ἡ περισσεία τῆς χαρᾶς αὐτῶν καὶ ἡ κατὰ βάθους πτωχεία αὐτῶν ἐπερίσσευσεν εἰς τὸ πλοῦτος τῆς ἁπλότητος αὐτῶν·

      how in a severe ordeal of affliction, the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded to the riches of their generosity.

4 † περισσεία, -ας, ἡ (< περισσεύω), [in LXX: Ec12 (יִתְרוֹן and cogn. forms, 1:3, al.)*;] abundance, superfluity: Ro 5:17, II Co 8:2 10:15, Ja 1:21 (so also Inscr., v. LAE, 80). superiority (Ec, v. supr.); profit (ib.).†
