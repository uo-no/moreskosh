Senses (NGSD)

  1. to bear with (1)
    To put up with someone's ways, to take care of: “bore with them”. The reading of the sense (to bear with or to care for) is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 13:18

      καί, ὡς τεσσερακονταετῆ χρόνον ἐτροποφόρησεν αὐτοὺς ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ,

      For a period of about forty years he put up with them in the wilderness.
