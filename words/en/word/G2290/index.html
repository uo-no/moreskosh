Senses (NGSD)

  1. to bury (11)
    To bury, to inter: “that he was buried”, “was buried, and his tomb is here to this day”, “let the dead bury their dead”, “let me first go and bury my father”, “buried him”, “the rich man also died and was buried”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:21

      ἕτερος δὲ τῶν μαθητῶν εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Κύριε, ἐπίτρεψόν μοι πρῶτον ἀπελθεῖν καὶ θάψαι τὸν πατέρα μου.

      Another of his disciples said to him, “Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father.”
