Senses (NGSD)

  1. between / among (7)
    Between, among: “between two soldiers”, “between us and you”, “between the sanctuary and the altar”, “between you and him alone”, “a great chasm has been fixed”, “their thoughts accusing or excusing between themselves”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      “If your brother sins against you, go, show him his fault between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained back your brother.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 μεταξύ (< μετά + ξύν = σύν), [in LXX: Ge 31:50, Jg 5:27, III Ki 15:6; 15:32, Wi 4:10 16:19 18:23*;] adv. of place and time (in NT time only); between: ἐν τῷ μ. (s.c. χρόνῳ), Jo 4:31; in late writers (FlJ, Plut., al.), like μετά (adv.), after, afterwards: τὸ μ. σάββατον, Ac 13:42 (cf. Cl., Ro., I Co., 44, 2). Prep. c. gen., between: of place, Mt 23:35, Lk 11:51 16:26, Ac 12:6; of persons, as to mutual relation, Mt 18:15, Ac 15:9, Ro 2:15.†
