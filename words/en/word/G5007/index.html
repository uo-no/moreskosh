Senses (NGSD)

  1. talent (unit of weight and money) (14)
    A large unit of weight or money, the talent: “a debt of ten thousand talents”, “five talents, two talents, one talent”, “take the talent from him”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 18:24

      ἀρξαμένου δὲ αὐτοῦ συναίρειν προσηνέχθη αὐτῷ εἷς ὀφειλέτης μυρίων ταλάντων.

      When he had begun to settle, one was brought to him who owed him ten thousand talents.

      Immediately he who received the five talents went and traded with them, and made another five talents.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

14 τάλαντον, -ου, τό [in LXX for כִּכָּר ;] a balance (Hom.). that which is weighed, a talent; a talent in weight (in Horn. always of gold); a sum of money, whether gold or silver, equivalent to a talent in weight (v. DB, iii, 418 ff.): Mt 18:24 25:15ff.†
