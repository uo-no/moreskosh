Senses (NGSD)

  1. selfish ambition, factionalism (7)
    A mind that seeks its own interest; factional spirit: “strife, jealousy, anger, rivalries”, “rivalries, slander”, “envy, greed and selfish ambition”, “but rather out of selfish ambition”, “do nothing from selfish ambition or vain conceit”, “for those driven by selfish ambition there will be wrath and anger”.
