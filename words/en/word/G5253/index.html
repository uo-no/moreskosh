Senses (NGSD)

  1. upper room (4)
    An upper room, a room upstairs: “went up to the upper room”, “a room on the third storey”, “carried her to the upper room”, “the upper room”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      In those days, she became sick and died. When they had washed her, they laid her in an upper room.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 ὑπερῷον, -ου, τό (neut. of ὑπερῷος, above, < ὑπέρ) [in LXX for עֲלִיָּה and cogn. forms ;] in cl., the upper story or upper rooms where the women resided (Hom., a1.). In LXX and NT, an upper chamber, roof-chamber, built on the flat roof of the house (v. DB, iii, 674a): Ac 1:13 9:37, 39 20:8 (cf. IV Ki 23:22).†
