Senses (NGSD)

  1. to compel / force (9)
    To compel, to force, to constrain: “you forced me to it”, “tried to make them blaspheme”, “I was compelled”, “would force you to be circumcised”, “even Titus, a Greek, was not forced”, “force the Gentiles to live like Jews”, “compel people to come in”, “made the disciples get into the boat”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 14:22

      Καὶ εὐθέως ἠνάγκασεν τοὺς μαθητὰς ἐμβῆναι εἰς πλοῖον καὶ προάγειν αὐτὸν εἰς τὸ πέραν, ἕως οὗ ἀπολύσῃ τοὺς ὄχλους.

      Immediately Jesus made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of him to the other side, while he sent the multitudes away.

    • Acts 26:11

      καὶ κατὰ πάσας τὰς συναγωγὰς πολλάκις τιμωρῶν αὐτοὺς ἠνάγκαζον βλασφημεῖν, περισσῶς τε ἐμμαινόμενος αὐτοῖς ἐδίωκον ἕως καὶ εἰς τὰς ἔξω πόλεις.

      Punishing them often in all the synagogues, I tried to make them blaspheme. Being exceedingly enraged against them, I persecuted them even to foreign cities.

      Read Acts 26:11 in the app

    • Galatians 2:3

      But not even Titus, who was with me, being a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised.
