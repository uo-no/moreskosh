Senses (NGSD)

  1. to rise early (1)
    To get up early in the morning: “came to him early in the morning”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 21:38

      καὶ πᾶς ὁ λαὸς ὤρθριζεν πρὸς αὐτὸν ἐν τῷ ἱερῷ ἀκούειν αὐτοῦ.

      All the people came early in the morning to him in the temple to hear him.
