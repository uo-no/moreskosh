Senses (NGSD)

  1. to stone to death (8)
    To kill by stoning, to hurl stones at: “who kills the prophets and stones those sent to it”, “they stoned Stephen”, “the law commanded us to stone such women”, “even a beast that touches the mountain shall be stoned”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

      The farmers took his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned another.
