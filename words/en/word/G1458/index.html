Senses (NGSD)

  1. to accuse, to bring charges (7)
    To bring a charge, to accuse, to blame: “let them bring charges against one another”, “we are in danger of being charged”, “accused over questions about the Jewish law”, “the one who is accused”, “accused by the Jews”, “O King, it is for this that I am accused”, “who will bring a charge against God’s elect”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 19:38

      εἰ μὲν οὖν Δημήτριος καὶ οἱ σὺν αὐτῷ τεχνῖται ἔχουσιν πρός τινα λόγον, ἀγοραῖοι ἄγονται καὶ ἀνθύπατοί εἰσιν, ἐγκαλείτωσαν ἀλλήλοις.

      If therefore Demetrius and the craftsmen who are with him have a matter against anyone, the courts are open and there are proconsuls. Let them press charges against one another.

      Desiring to know the cause why they accused him, I brought him down to their council.

7 ἐγκαλέω, -ῶ, [in LXX for אמר, etc.; c. dat., Za 1:4 (קָרָא אֶל), Wi 12:12, Si 46:19 ;] to call in, demand. to bring a charge against, accuse: c. dat. pers. (as in cl.), Ac 19:38 23:28; seq. κατά, c. gen. pers., Ro 8:33.
