Senses (NGSD)

  1. Master (form of address) (7)
    Used in address, to call Jesus ‘Master’: “Master, we toiled all night”, “Master, we are perishing”, “Master, have mercy on us”, “Master, they are pushing one another”, “Master, it is good for us to be here”, “Master, in your name”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 5:5

      καὶ ἀποκριθεὶς Σίμων εἶπεν· Ἐπιστάτα, δι’ ὅλης νυκτὸς κοπιάσαντες οὐδὲν ἐλάβομεν, ἐπὶ δὲ τῷ ῥήματί σου χαλάσω τὰ δίκτυα.

      Simon answered him, “Master, we worked all night and caught nothing; but at your word I will let down the net.”
