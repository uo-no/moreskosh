Senses (NGSD)

  1. to pay attention, to notice (3)
    To give heed, to fix one’s attention, to notice: “keep a close watch on yourself and on the teaching”, “fixed his attention on them”, “noticing how they chose the places of honour”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Luke 14:7

      Ἔλεγεν δὲ πρὸς τοὺς κεκλημένους παραβολήν, ἐπέχων πῶς τὰς πρωτοκλισίας ἐξελέγοντο, λέγων πρὸς αὐτούς·

      He spoke a parable to those who were invited, when he noticed how they chose the best seats, and said to them,

      Read Luke 14:7 in the app

    • Acts 3:5

      ὁ δὲ ἐπεῖχεν αὐτοῖς προσδοκῶν τι παρ’ αὐτῶν λαβεῖν.

      He listened to them, expecting to receive something from them.

      Read Acts 3:5 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 4:16

      ἔπεχε σεαυτῷ καὶ τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ· ἐπίμενε αὐτοῖς· τοῦτο γὰρ ποιῶν καὶ σεαυτὸν σώσεις καὶ τοὺς ἀκούοντάς σου.

      Pay attention to yourself and to your teaching. Continue in these things, for in doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.

      Read 1 Timothy 4:16 in the app

  2. to stay on (1)
    To remain in a place: “he himself stayed for a while longer in Asia”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 19:22

      ἀποστείλας δὲ εἰς τὴν Μακεδονίαν δύο τῶν διακονούντων αὐτῷ, Τιμόθεον καὶ Ἔραστον, αὐτὸς ἐπέσχεν χρόνον εἰς τὴν Ἀσίαν.

      Having sent into Macedonia two of those who served him, Timothy and Erastus, he himself stayed in Asia for a while.

      Read Acts 19:22 in the app

  3. to hold fast or to hold forth (disputed) (1)
    Holding fast, or holding out, the word of life: “holding fast to the word of life”, “holding out the word of life”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 2:16

      λόγον ζωῆς ἐπέχοντες, εἰς καύχημα ἐμοὶ εἰς ἡμέραν Χριστοῦ, ὅτι οὐκ εἰς κενὸν ἔδραμον οὐδὲ εἰς κενὸν ἐκοπίασα.

      holding up the word of life, that I may have something to boast in the day of Christ that I didn’t run in vain nor labor in vain.

      Read Philippians 2:16 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ἐπ-έχω [in LXX for חָדַל, etc.; also Si 8:1 31(34):2, II Mac 5:25 9:25, al. ;] to hold upon. Like παρέχω (as in Hom., al.), to hold out, offer: λόγον ζωῆς, Phl 2:16. to hold or direct towards, Sc.νοῦν; absol., to intend, purpose; to observe, give attention to (v. MM, Exp., xiv): seq. πῶς, Lk 14:7; c. dat. pers., Ac 3:5, I Ti 4:16. to stay, wait: Ac 19:22 (in legal phrase, MM, Exp., l.c.).†
