Senses (NGSD)

  1. branch (6)
    A branch of a tree: “the birds of the air make nests in its branches”, “cut branches from the trees”, “when its branch becomes tender”, “puts out branches”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:32

      ὃ μικρότερον μέν ἐστιν πάντων τῶν σπερμάτων, ὅταν δὲ αὐξηθῇ μεῖζον τῶν λαχάνων ἐστὶν καὶ γίνεται δένδρον, ὥστε ἐλθεῖν τὰ πετεινὰ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ καὶ κατασκηνοῦν ἐν τοῖς κλάδοις αὐτοῦ.

      which indeed is smaller than all seeds. But when it is grown, it is greater than the herbs and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in its branches.”

  2. branch (grafting, olive figure) (5)
    A grafted branch, a branch as a figure for people: “if the root is holy, so are the branches”, “some of the branches were broken off”, “do not boast over the branches”, “did not spare the natural branches”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 11:16

      εἰ δὲ ἡ ἀπαρχὴ ἁγία, καὶ τὸ φύραμα· καὶ εἰ ἡ ῥίζα ἁγία, καὶ οἱ κλάδοι.

      If the first fruit is holy, so is the lump. If the root is holy, so are the branches.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

11 κλάδος, -ου, ὁ (< κλάω), [in LXX for דָּלִית, כַּף, etc.;] a young tender shoot broken off for grafting; then, a branch: Mt 13:32 21:8 24:32, Mk 4:32 13:28, Lk 13:19; metaph., of descendants (cf. Si 40:15), Ro 11:16-19, 21.†
