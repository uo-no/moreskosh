Senses (NGSD)

  1. decently, with propriety (3)
    With dignity, in a proper manner: “all things … decently”, “properly toward outsiders”, “let us walk decently, as in the daytime”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 13:13

      ὡς ἐν ἡμέρᾳ εὐσχημόνως περιπατήσωμεν, μὴ κώμοις καὶ μέθαις, μὴ κοίταις καὶ ἀσελγείαις, μὴ ἔριδι καὶ ζήλῳ,

      Let’s walk properly, as in the day; not in reveling and drunkenness, not in sexual promiscuity and lustful acts, and not in strife and jealousy.

      Read Romans 13:13 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 14:40

      πάντα δὲ εὐσχημόνως καὶ κατὰ τάξιν γινέσθω.

      Let all things be done decently and in order.

      Read 1 Corinthians 14:40 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 4:12

      ἵνα περιπατῆτε εὐσχημόνως πρὸς τοὺς ἔξω καὶ μηδενὸς χρείαν ἔχητε.

      that you may walk properly toward those who are outside, and may have need of nothing.

      Read 1 Thessalonians 4:12 in the app
