Senses (NGSD)

  1. fifteen (3)
    The numeral fifteen: “fifteen fathoms”, “fifteen days”, “fifteen stadia”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • John 11:18

      ἦν δὲ ἡ Βηθανία ἐγγὺς τῶν Ἱεροσολύμων ὡς ἀπὸ σταδίων δεκαπέντε.

      Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia away.
