Senses (NGSD)

  1. interest (2)
    Interest on money: “with interest” (twice).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 25:27

      ἔδει σε οὖν βαλεῖν τὰ ἀργύριά μου τοῖς τραπεζίταις, καὶ ἐλθὼν ἐγὼ ἐκομισάμην ἂν τὸ ἐμὸν σὺν τόκῳ.

      You ought therefore to have deposited my money with the bankers, and at my coming I should have received back my own with interest.

      Read Matthew 25:27 in the app

    • Luke 19:23

      καὶ διὰ τί οὐκ ἔδωκάς μου τὸ ἀργύριον ἐπὶ τράπεζαν; κἀγὼ ἐλθὼν σὺν τόκῳ ἂν αὐτὸ ἔπραξα.

      Then why didn’t you deposit my money in the bank, and at my coming, I might have earned interest on it?’

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 τόκος, -ου, ὁ (< τίκτω), [in LXX for נֶשֶׁךְ, etc. ;] a bringing forth, birth; offspring. Metaph., of the produce of money lent out, interest, usury (cf. Merch. of Venice, I, iii, "a breed of barren metal "): Mt 25:27, Lk 19:23 (Soph., Plat., al.).†
