Senses (NGSD)

  1. lawless (person) (5)
    Transgressing the law, lawless: “the lawless and disobedient”, “distressed by the conduct of the lawless”, “the lawless one will be revealed”, “crucified by the hands of lawless men”, “was numbered with the transgressors”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 22:37

      λέγω γὰρ ὑμῖν ὅτι τοῦτο τὸ γεγραμμένον δεῖ τελεσθῆναι ἐν ἐμοί, τό· Καὶ μετὰ ἀνόμων ἐλογίσθη· καὶ γὰρ τὸ περὶ ἐμοῦ τέλος ἔχει.

      For I tell you that this which is written must still be fulfilled in me: ‘He was counted with transgressors.’ For that which concerns me is being fulfilled.”

      Read Luke 22:37 in the app

    • Acts 2:23

      τοῦτον τῇ ὡρισμένῃ βουλῇ καὶ προγνώσει τοῦ θεοῦ ἔκδοτον διὰ χειρὸς ἀνόμων προσπήξαντες ἀνείλατε,

      him, being delivered up by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of God, you have taken by the hand of lawless men, crucified and killed;

      Read Acts 2:23 in the app

    • 1 Timothy 1:9

      εἰδὼς τοῦτο, ὅτι δικαίῳ νόμος οὐ κεῖται, ἀνόμοις δὲ καὶ ἀνυποτάκτοις, ἀσεβέσι καὶ ἁμαρτωλοῖς, ἀνοσίοις καὶ βεβήλοις, πατρολῴαις καὶ μητρολῴαις, ἀνδροφόνοις,

      as knowing this, that law is not made for a righteous person, but for the lawless and insubordinate, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, for manslayers,

      Read 1 Timothy 1:9 in the app

  2. without the law (person) (4)
    Having no law, being outside the law: “to those outside the law as one outside the law”, “to win those outside the law”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • 1 Corinthians 9:21

      τοῖς ἀνόμοις ὡς ἄνομος, μὴ ὢν ἄνομος θεοῦ ἀλλ’ ἔννομος Χριστοῦ, ἵνα κερδάνω τοὺς ἀνόμους·

      to those who are without law, as without law (not being without law toward God, but under law toward Christ), that I might win those who are without law.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

9 ἄ-νομος, -ον (ἀ. neg., νόμος), [in LXX for עָוֺן, פֶּשַׁע, רֶשַׁע, etc.;] lawless, wicked: Mk 15:28, Lk 22:37, Ac 2:23, I Ti 1:9, II Pe 2:8; ὁ ἄ., II Th 2:8 (= ὁ ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας, II Th 2:3). without law (= οἱ μὴ ὑπὸ νόμον, Ro 2:14): I Co 9:21 (MM, VGT, s.v.).† SYN.: v.s. ἄθεσμος.
