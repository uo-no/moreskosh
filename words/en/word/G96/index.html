Senses (NGSD)

  1. disqualified / failing the test (8)
    Having failed the test, disqualified: “lest I myself should be disqualified”, “unless you fail to meet the test?”, “that you will find we have not failed”, “disqualified regarding the faith”, “a debased mind”, “land that bears thorns and thistles is worthless”, “unfit for any good work”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 1:28

      Καὶ καθὼς οὐκ ἐδοκίμασαν τὸν θεὸν ἔχειν ἐν ἐπιγνώσει, παρέδωκεν αὐτοὺς ὁ θεὸς εἰς ἀδόκιμον νοῦν, ποιεῖν τὰ μὴ καθήκοντα,

      Even as they refused to have God in their knowledge, God gave them up to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not fitting;

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

8 ἀ-δόκιμος, -ον, [in LXX: Pr 25:4, Is 1:22 (סִיג)*;] of things (prop. of metals: LXX, Pr 25:4, Is 1:22), not standing the test, rejected: γῆ, He 6:8. Of persons, rejected after testing, reprobate: Ro 1:28, I Co 9:27, II Co 13:5-7, II Ti 3:8, Tit 1:16 (Cremer, 212).†
