Senses (NGSD)

  1. next / then (order) (12)
    Marks “next” in an enumeration, followed by “then”: “first …, next …”, “Christ the firstfruits, then those who belong to Christ”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 16:7

      ἔπειτα ἑτέρῳ εἶπεν· Σὺ δὲ πόσον ὀφείλεις; ὁ δὲ εἶπεν· Ἑκατὸν κόρους σίτου· λέγει αὐτῷ· Δέξαι σου τὰ γράμματα καὶ γράψον ὀγδοήκοντα.

      Then he said to another, ‘How much do you owe?’ He said, ‘A hundred cors of wheat.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and write eighty.’

      to whom also Abraham divided a tenth part of all (being first, by interpretation, “king of righteousness”, and then also “king of Salem”, which means “king of peace”,

  2. afterwards (after a lapse of time) (4)
    After a certain time has passed, thereafter: “after three years”, “fourteen years later”, “then I went up to Jerusalem”.
    Attested: NT only

    • John 11:7

      ἔπειτα μετὰ τοῦτο λέγει τοῖς μαθηταῖς· Ἄγωμεν εἰς τὴν Ἰουδαίαν πάλιν.

      Then after this he said to the disciples, “Let’s go into Judea again.”

      Ἔπειτα μετὰ ἔτη τρία ἀνῆλθον εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα ἱστορῆσαι Κηφᾶν, καὶ ἐπέμεινα πρὸς αὐτὸν ἡμέρας δεκαπέντε·

      Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Peter, and stayed with him fifteen days.
