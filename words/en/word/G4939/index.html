Senses (NGSD)

  1. one brought up with (1)
    One reared together with another, a foster brother: “a friend from childhood”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 13:1

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 σύν-τροφος, -ου, ὁ (< συντρέφομαι, to be brought up together) [in LXX: III Ki 12:24, I Mac 1:6 R, II Mac 9:29 * ;] prop., one nourished or brought up with, a foster-brother: Ac 13:1 EV. In Hellenistic usage, as a court term, an intimate friend of a king (v. Deiss., BS, 305, 310 f.): Ac 13:1 (cf. also MM, xxiv).†
