Senses (NGSD)

  1. calf (6)
    A calf or young bull (including sacrificial, food and symbolic uses): “not by the blood of goats and calves”, “took the blood of calves and goats”, “bring the fattened calf and kill it”, “has killed the fattened calf”, “the second living creature was like a calf”.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 μόσχος, -ου, ὁ, [in LXX chiefly for פָּר, also for שׁוֹר, עֵגֶל. etc.;] a young shoot or twig. ὁ, ἡ, μ., offspring; of men; of animals; most freq. (as always in LXX), a calf, bullock, heifer: Lk 15:23, 27, 30, He 9:12, 19, Re 4:7.†
