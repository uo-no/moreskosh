Senses (NGSD)

  1. to mount, to board (3)
    To mount a horse or donkey; to board a ship: “riding on a donkey, on a colt”, “boarded a ship”, “got on board and set sail”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 21:5

      Εἴπατε τῇ θυγατρὶ Σιών· Ἰδοὺ ὁ βασιλεύς σου ἔρχεταί σοι πραῢς καὶ ἐπιβεβηκὼς ἐπὶ ὄνον καὶ ἐπὶ πῶλον υἱὸν ὑποζυγίου.

      “Tell the daughter of Zion, behold, your King comes to you, humble, and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.”

      Read Matthew 21:5 in the app

    • Acts 21:2

      καὶ εὑρόντες πλοῖον διαπερῶν εἰς Φοινίκην ἐπιβάντες ἀνήχθημεν.

      Having found a ship crossing over to Phoenicia, we went aboard and set sail.

      Read Acts 21:2 in the app

    • Acts 27:2

      ἐπιβάντες δὲ πλοίῳ Ἀδραμυττηνῷ μέλλοντι πλεῖν εἰς τοὺς κατὰ τὴν Ἀσίαν τόπους ἀνήχθημεν ὄντος σὺν ἡμῖν Ἀριστάρχου Μακεδόνος Θεσσαλονικέως·

      Embarking in a ship of Adramyttium, which was about to sail to places on the coast of Asia, we put to sea, Aristarchus, a Macedonian of Thessalonica being with us.

      Read Acts 27:2 in the app

  2. to set foot in, to go up (3)
    To set foot in a land or place, to go up to it: “from the first day I set foot in Asia”, “passing through Caesarea, arrived in the province”, “not to go up to Jerusalem”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 20:18

      ὡς δὲ παρεγένοντο πρὸς αὐτὸν εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Ὑμεῖς ἐπίστασθε ἀπὸ πρώτης ἡμέρας ἀφ’ ἧς ἐπέβην εἰς τὴν Ἀσίαν πῶς μεθ’ ὑμῶν τὸν πάντα χρόνον ἐγενόμην,

      When they had come to him, he said to them, “You yourselves know, from the first day that I set foot in Asia, how I was with you all the time,

      Having found disciples, we stayed there seven days. These said to Paul through the Spirit that he should not go up to Jerusalem.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

6 ἐπι-βαίνω [in LXX chiefly for רָכַב ;] to get up on, mount: seq. ἐπί, c. acc, Mt 21:5 (LXX); to embark in (a boat), go aboard: c. dat., Ac 27:2; seq. εἰς, Ac 21:6 (Rec.); absol., Ac 21:2. to go up to, go on to, enter: seq. εἰς, Ac 20:18 21:4; c. dat., Ac 25:1.†
