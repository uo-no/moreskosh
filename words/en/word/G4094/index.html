Senses (NGSD)

  1. platter, dish (5)
    A platter or large dish: “on a platter”, “John’s head on a platter”, “the head on a platter”, “the outside of the dish”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 14:8

      ἡ δὲ προβιβασθεῖσα ὑπὸ τῆς μητρὸς αὐτῆς· Δός μοι, φησίν, ὧδε ἐπὶ πίνακι τὴν κεφαλὴν Ἰωάννου τοῦ βαπτιστοῦ.

      She, being prompted by her mother, said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptizer.”

      His head was brought on a platter and given to the young lady; and she brought it to her mother.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 ** πίναξ, -ακος, ὁ, [in LXX: IV Mac 17:7 R*;] prop., a board, plank; hence, of various at wooden articles; a tablet; a disc, a dish: Mt 14:8, 11, Mk 6:25, 28, Lk 11:39.†
