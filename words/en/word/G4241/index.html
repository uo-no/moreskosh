Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be fitting, to be proper (7)
    To be suitable, proper or becoming: “is it fitting”, “as is proper for a woman”, “as befits the saints”, “it was fitting for him for whom all things exist”, “such a high priest was fitting for us”, “it is proper for us thus …”, “speak what is fitting for sound teaching”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 3:15

      ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν πρὸς αὐτόν· Ἄφες ἄρτι, οὕτως γὰρ πρέπον ἐστὶν ἡμῖν πληρῶσαι πᾶσαν δικαιοσύνην. τότε ἀφίησιν αὐτόν.

      But Jesus, answering, said to him, “Allow it now, for this is the fitting way for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then he allowed him.
