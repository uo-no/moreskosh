Senses (NGSD)

  1. defence / apology (8)
    One's own defence: “my defence to those who would examine me”, “ready to give a defence for the hope that is in you”, “my defence is”, “at my first defence”, “hear my defence”, “an opportunity to make a defence against the accusers”, “the defence and confirmation of the gospel”, “for the defence of the gospel”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 22:1

      Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοὶ καὶ πατέρες, ἀκούσατέ μου τῆς πρὸς ὑμᾶς νυνὶ ἀπολογίας.

      “Brothers and fathers, listen to the defense which I now make to you.”

    • 1 Corinthians 9:3

      Ἡ ἐμὴ ἀπολογία τοῖς ἐμὲ ἀνακρίνουσίν ἐστιν αὕτη.

      My defense to those who examine me is this:

      Read 1 Corinthians 9:3 in the app

    • 2 Timothy 4:16

      Ἐν τῇ πρώτῃ μου ἀπολογίᾳ οὐδείς μοι παρεγένετο, ἀλλὰ πάντες με ἐγκατέλιπον μὴ αὐτοῖς λογισθείη

      At my first defense, no one came to help me, but all left me. May it not be held against them.
