Senses (NGSD)

  1. herd (of pigs) (7)
    A herd of animals; in the New Testament, a herd of pigs: “a large herd of pigs was feeding a good way off”, “the herd of pigs was rooting for food”, “send us into the herd of pigs”, “the herd of pigs … rushed into the lake”, “a great herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside”, “a herd of about two thousand”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 8:30

      ἦν δὲ μακρὰν ἀπ’ αὐτῶν ἀγέλη χοίρων πολλῶν βοσκομένη.

      Now there was a herd of many pigs feeding far away from them.

      καὶ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Ὑπάγετε. οἱ δὲ ἐξελθόντες ἀπῆλθον εἰς τοὺς χοίρους· καὶ ἰδοὺ ὥρμησεν πᾶσα ἡ ἀγέλη κατὰ τοῦ κρημνοῦ εἰς τὴν θάλασσαν, καὶ ἀπέθανον ἐν τοῖς ὕδασιν.

      He said to them, “Go!” They came out and went into the herd of pigs; and behold, the whole herd of pigs rushed down the cliff into the sea and died in the water.
