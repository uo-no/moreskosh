Senses (NGSD)

  1. companion / partner (of persons) (4)
    A companion, partner, one who works jointly: “James and John, partners with Simon”, “my partner and fellow worker”, “if you count me your partner, receive him”, “became partners with those so treated”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 5:10

      ὁμοίως δὲ καὶ Ἰάκωβον καὶ Ἰωάννην υἱοὺς Ζεβεδαίου, οἳ ἦσαν κοινωνοὶ τῷ Σίμωνι. καὶ εἶπεν πρὸς τὸν Σίμωνα ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Μὴ φοβοῦ· ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν ἀνθρώπους ἔσῃ ζωγρῶν.

      and so also were James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon. Jesus said to Simon, “Don’t be afraid. From now on you will be catching people alive.”

      Read Luke 5:10 in the app

    • 2 Corinthians 8:23

      εἴτε ὑπὲρ Τίτου, κοινωνὸς ἐμὸς καὶ εἰς ὑμᾶς συνεργός· εἴτε ἀδελφοὶ ἡμῶν, ἀπόστολοι ἐκκλησιῶν, δόξα Χριστοῦ.

      As for Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker for you. As for our brothers, they are the apostles of the assemblies, the glory of Christ.

      Read 2 Corinthians 8:23 in the app

    • Philemon 1:17

      Εἰ οὖν με ἔχεις κοινωνόν, προσλαβοῦ αὐτὸν ὡς ἐμέ.

      If then you count me a partner, receive him as you would receive me.

      Read Philemon 1:17 in the app

  2. sharer / partaker (6)
    One who has a share in …, who partakes: “partakers in the altar”, “I do not want you to be partners with demons”, “witness of Christ's sufferings”, “sharers in the sufferings”, “partakers of the divine nature”, “would not have taken part in shedding the blood of the prophets”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 23:30

      καὶ λέγετε· Εἰ ἤμεθα ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν, οὐκ ἂν ἤμεθα αὐτῶν κοινωνοὶ ἐν τῷ αἵματι τῶν προφητῶν·

      and say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we wouldn’t have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.’

      Read Matthew 23:30 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 10:20

      ἀλλ’ ὅτι ἃ θύουσιν, δαιμονίοις καὶ οὐ θεῷ θύουσιν, οὐ θέλω δὲ ὑμᾶς κοινωνοὺς τῶν δαιμονίων γίνεσθαι.

      But I say that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God, and I don’t desire that you would have fellowship with demons.

      Read 1 Corinthians 10:20 in the app

    • 1 Peter 5:1

      Πρεσβυτέρους οὖν ἐν ὑμῖν παρακαλῶ ὁ συμπρεσβύτερος καὶ μάρτυς τῶν τοῦ Χριστοῦ παθημάτων, ὁ καὶ τῆς μελλούσης ἀποκαλύπτεσθαι δόξης κοινωνός,

      Therefore I exhort the elders among you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and who will also share in the glory that will be revealed:

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 κοινωνός, -ή, -όν (< κοινός), [in LXX: Pr 28:24, Is 1:23 (חָבֵר), Ma 2:14 (חֲבֶרֶת), Es 8:13, al.;] as adj., = κοινός. As subst., ὁ, ἡ κ., a partner, associate, companion: II Co 8:23, Phm 17; c. dat. pers., Lk 5:10; c. gen. pers., He 10:33; id. seq. ἐν, Mt 23:30; a partaker, sharer: c. gen. rei, I Co 10:18, 20, II Co 1:7, I Pe 5:1, II Pe 1:4 (v. Deiss., BS, 368 n2).†
