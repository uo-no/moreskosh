    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 14:13

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 πεζός, -ή, -όν (< πούς), [in LXX for רַגְלִי;] on foot: Mt 14:13 (WH, mg., R, txt.). Opp. to going by sea, by land: Mt 14:13 (WH, mg., R, mg.). As adv., πεζῇ (sc. ὁδῷ), on foot or by land: Mt 14:13 (WH, txt.), Mk 6:33.†
