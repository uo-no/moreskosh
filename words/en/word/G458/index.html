Senses (NGSD)

  1. lawlessness (state, quality) (13)
    Acting against the law, lawlessness: “you workers of lawlessness”, “because lawlessness will increase”, “sin is lawlessness”, “the mystery of lawlessness”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 7:23

      καὶ τότε ὁμολογήσω αὐτοῖς ὅτι Οὐδέποτε ἔγνων ὑμᾶς· ἀποχωρεῖτε ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ οἱ ἐργαζόμενοι τὴν ἀνομίαν.

      Then I will tell them, ‘I never knew you. Depart from me, you who work iniquity.’

      Read Matthew 7:23 in the app

    • Matthew 24:12

      καὶ διὰ τὸ πληθυνθῆναι τὴν ἀνομίαν ψυγήσεται ἡ ἀγάπη τῶν πολλῶν.

      Because iniquity will be multiplied, the love of many will grow cold.

      Read Matthew 24:12 in the app

    • 2 Thessalonians 2:7

      τὸ γὰρ μυστήριον ἤδη ἐνεργεῖται τῆς ἀνομίας· μόνον ὁ κατέχων ἄρτι ἕως ἐκ μέσου γένηται.

      For the mystery of lawlessness already works. Only there is one who restrains now, until he is taken out of the way.

      Read 2 Thessalonians 2:7 in the app

  2. lawless deeds (plural) (2)
    Individual lawless acts: “whose lawless deeds are forgiven”, “the iniquities of those whose sins are covered”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Romans 4:7

      Μακάριοι ὧν ἀφέθησαν αἱ ἀνομίαι καὶ ὧν ἐπεκαλύφθησαν αἱ ἁμαρτίαι,

      “Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, whose sins are covered.

      Read Romans 4:7 in the app

    • Hebrews 10:17

      καὶ τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν αὐτῶν καὶ τῶν ἀνομιῶν αὐτῶν οὐ μὴ μνησθήσομαι ἔτι·

      “I will remember their sins and their iniquities no more.”

      Read Hebrews 10:17 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

15 ἀνομία, -ας, ἡ (< ἄνομος), [in LXX for עָוֺן, פֶּשַׁע, תּוֹעֵבָה, רֶשַׁע, etc.;] lawlessness, iniquity: Mt 7:23 13:41 23:28 24:12, Ro 6:19, II Co 6:14, II Th 2:3, 7, Tit 2:14, He 1:9, I Jn 3:4; in pl. (as LXX, Ps 31:1, al.; v. Bl., § 32, 6; Swete, Mk., 153), of acts or manifestations of lawlessness: Ro 4:7(LXX), He 10:17.† SYN.: v.s. ἁμάρτημα, ἄνομος.
