Occurrences in the New Testament

12

Senses (NGSD)

  1. master of the house / landowner (12)
    The head or owner of a house, a landowner: “when once the master of the house has shut the door”, “if the householder had known when the thief was coming”, “the owner of the vineyard”, “say to the master of the house”, “a scribe like a householder”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Matthew 10:25

      ἀρκετὸν τῷ μαθητῇ ἵνα γένηται ὡς ὁ διδάσκαλος αὐτοῦ, καὶ ὁ δοῦλος ὡς ὁ κύριος αὐτοῦ. εἰ τὸν οἰκοδεσπότην Βεελζεβοὺλ ἐπεκάλεσαν, πόσῳ μᾶλλον τοὺς οἰκιακοὺς αὐτοῦ.

      It is enough for the disciple that he be like his teacher, and the servant like his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household!
