Senses (NGSD)

  1. to hold (capacity) (3)
    To be able to contain, to have room for: “the jars hold two or three measures”, “the world itself could not contain the books that would be written”, “there was no more room, not even at the door”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 2:2

      καὶ συνήχθησαν πολλοὶ ὥστε μηκέτι χωρεῖν μηδὲ τὰ πρὸς τὴν θύραν, καὶ ἐλάλει αὐτοῖς τὸν λόγον.

      Immediately many were gathered together, so that there was no more room, not even around the door; and he spoke the word to them.

    • John 2:6

      ἦσαν δὲ ἐκεῖ λίθιναι ὑδρίαι ἓξ κατὰ τὸν καθαρισμὸν τῶν Ἰουδαίων κείμεναι, χωροῦσαι ἀνὰ μετρητὰς δύο ἢ τρεῖς.

      Now there were six water pots of stone set there after the Jews’ way of purifying, containing two or three metretes apiece.

      Read John 2:6 in the app

    • John 21:25

      ἔστιν δὲ καὶ ἄλλα πολλὰ ἃ ἐποίησεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς, ἅτινα ἐὰν γράφηται καθ’ ἕν, οὐδ’ αὐτὸν οἶμαι τὸν κόσμον χωρήσειν τὰ γραφόμενα βιβλία.

      There are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they would all be written, I suppose that even the world itself wouldn’t have room for the books that would be written.

      Read John 21:25 in the app

  2. to receive / make room for (5)
    To accept, make room, understand: “make room for us in your hearts”, “not everyone can receive this saying”, “let the one who is able to receive it receive it”, “my word finds no place in you”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      But he said to them, “Not all men can receive this saying, but those to whom it is given.

      I know that you are Abraham’s offspring, yet you seek to kill me, because my word finds no place in you.

      Read 2 Corinthians 7:2 in the app

  3. to pass / reach (2)
    To pass through, to come to: “whatever goes into the mouth passes into the stomach”, “that all should reach repentance”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 15:17

      οὐ νοεῖτε ὅτι πᾶν τὸ εἰσπορευόμενον εἰς τὸ στόμα εἰς τὴν κοιλίαν χωρεῖ καὶ εἰς ἀφεδρῶνα ἐκβάλλεται;

      Don’t you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the belly and then out of the body?

      Read Matthew 15:17 in the app

      οὐ βραδύνει κύριος τῆς ἐπαγγελίας, ὥς τινες βραδύτητα ἡγοῦνται, ἀλλὰ μακροθυμεῖ εἰς ὑμᾶς, μὴ βουλόμενός τινας ἀπολέσθαι ἀλλὰ πάντας εἰς μετάνοιαν χωρῆσαι.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

10 χωρέω, -ῶ [in LXX: Ge 13:6 (נשׂא), III Ki 7:26, 38 (כּוּל hi.), II Ch 4:5 (חזק hi.), Wi 7:23, 24, al. ;] Intrans., to make room, give way, retire, pass: seq. εἰς, Mt 15:17; metaph. (EV, come), εἰς μετάνοιαν, II Pe 3:9. to go forward, advance, progress (Plat., Polyb., al.) ὁ λόγος ὁ ἐμὸς οὐ χωρεῖ. ἐν ὑμῖν, Jo 8:37 (R, txt., hath not free course; R, mg., hath no place, for wh. cf. Field, Notes, 94 f.). Trans., to have space for holding, to hold: Mk 2:2 (cf. Ge, l.c.), Jo 21:25; of measures (III Ki, II Ch, ll. c.), Jo 2:6. Metaph., of having or making room in mind or heart: Mt 19:11, 12 (EV, receive), II Co 7:2 (R, txt., open your hearts; mg., make room) (cf. ἀνα-, ἀπο-, ἐκ-, ὑπο-χωρέω).†
