Senses (NGSD)

  1. to give / provide (9)
    To give, provide, or show: “richly provides”, “give what is right and fair”, “show an example of good works”, “let him request it of them”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 6:29

      τῷ τύπτοντί σε ἐπὶ τὴν σιαγόνα πάρεχε καὶ τὴν ἄλλην, καὶ ἀπὸ τοῦ αἴροντός σου τὸ ἱμάτιον καὶ τὸν χιτῶνα μὴ κωλύσῃς.

      To him who strikes you on the cheek, offer also the other; and from him who takes away your cloak, don’t withhold your coat also.

      For a certain man named Demetrius, a silversmith who made silver shrines of Artemis, brought no little business to the craftsmen,

      Masters, give to your servants that which is just and equal, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

16 παρ-έχω, [in LXX: Ps 29 (30):7 (עמד hi.), etc.;] Act., to furnish, provide, supply; with ref. to incorporeal things, to afford, show, give, cause: c. acc., Ac 22:2, I Ti 1:4; c. acc. et dat., Mt 26:10, Mk 14:6, Lk 11:7 18:5, Ac 16:16 17:31 28:2, Ga 6:17, I Ti 6:17. to present, offer: c. acc. et dat., Lk 6:29; c. prop. reflex. et acc. pred., to show or present oneself (v. infr., and cf. Bl., § 55, 1). Mid., to supply, furnish or display of one's own part: c. acc. et dat., Lk 7:4, Ac 19:24, Col 4:1. In late writers, c. prop. reflex. et acc. pred. (= act. ut supr.), to show or present oneself (Xen., FlJ, al.; cf. M, Pr., 248; Deiss., BS, 254): Tit 2:7.†
