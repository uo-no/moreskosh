Senses (NGSD)

  1. to settle (1)
    To resolve, to decide a matter: “it will be settled in the lawful assembly”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 19:39

      εἰ δέ τι περαιτέρω ἐπιζητεῖτε, ἐν τῇ ἐννόμῳ ἐκκλησίᾳ ἐπιλυθήσεται.

      But if you seek anything about other matters, it will be settled in the regular assembly.

      Read Acts 19:39 in the app

  2. to explain (1)
    To expound, to make clear: “to his disciples … he explained”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Mark 4:34

      χωρὶς δὲ παραβολῆς οὐκ ἐλάλει αὐτοῖς, κατ’ ἰδίαν δὲ τοῖς ἰδίοις μαθηταῖς ἐπέλυεν πάντα.

      Without a parable he didn’t speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained everything.

      Read Mark 4:34 in the app
