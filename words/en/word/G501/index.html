Senses (NGSD)

  1. to draw (water) (4)
    To draw water: “now draw some out”, “the servants who had drawn the water”, “came to draw water”, “so that I need not come to draw”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • John 2:8

      καὶ λέγει αὐτοῖς· Ἀντλήσατε νῦν καὶ φέρετε τῷ ἀρχιτρικλίνῳ· οἱ δὲ ἤνεγκαν.

      He said to them, “Now draw some out, and take it to the ruler of the feast.” So they took it.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

4 ἀντλέω, -ῶ (< ἄντλος, bilge-water in a hold), [in LXX for שׁאב, etc.;] prop., to bale out. Generally, to draw water: absol., Jo 2:8 4:15; ὕδωρ, Jo 2:9 4:7 (on its use of the water made wine, v. DCG, ii, 815a; MM, VGT, s.v.; Field, Notes, 84 f.).†
