Senses (NGSD)

  1. to command (spirits, nature) (5)
    To command, give orders to spirits, wind, and water: “commands even the unclean spirits with authority”, “commands the winds and water”, “begged him not to command them to depart into the abyss”, commands “come out” to an unclean spirit.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Mark 1:27

      καὶ ἐθαμβήθησαν ἅπαντες, ὥστε συζητεῖν πρὸς ἑαυτοὺς λέγοντας· Τί ἐστιν τοῦτο; διδαχὴ καινή· κατ’ ἐξουσίαν καὶ τοῖς πνεύμασι τοῖς ἀκαθάρτοις ἐπιτάσσει, καὶ ὑπακούουσιν αὐτῷ.

      They were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, “What is this? What can this new doctrine be? For with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him!”

      When Jesus saw that a multitude came running together, he rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to him, “You mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him, and never enter him again!”

  2. to order (people) (5)
    To command a person, to issue an order: “ordered them to strike him”, “the servant did as commanded”, “ordered that his head be brought”, “commanded the people to sit down on the grass”, “could have ordered you”.
