Senses (NGSD)

  1. furnace (4)
    A furnace, an oven: “into the blazing furnace”, “refined in a furnace”, “the smoke from the shaft was like the smoke of a great furnace”, “the furnace of fire”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:42

      καὶ βαλοῦσιν αὐτοὺς εἰς τὴν κάμινον τοῦ πυρός· ἐκεῖ ἔσται ὁ κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὁ βρυγμὸς τῶν ὀδόντων.

      and will cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
