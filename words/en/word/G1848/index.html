Senses (NGSD)

  1. to despise / look down on (10)
    To make light of, to look down on, to scorn: “let no one despise him”, “what is despised in the world”, “those who have no standing in the church”, “do not despise prophecies”, “did not scorn or despise me”, “let not the one who eats despise the one who abstains”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 18:9

      Εἶπεν δὲ καὶ πρός τινας τοὺς πεποιθότας ἐφ’ ἑαυτοῖς ὅτι εἰσὶν δίκαιοι καὶ ἐξουθενοῦντας τοὺς λοιποὺς τὴν παραβολὴν ταύτην·

      He also spoke this parable to certain people who were convinced of their own righteousness, and who despised all others:

      Read Luke 18:9 in the app

    • Romans 14:10

      Σὺ δὲ τί κρίνεις τὸν ἀδελφόν σου; ἢ καὶ σὺ τί ἐξουθενεῖς τὸν ἀδελφόν σου; πάντες γὰρ παραστησόμεθα τῷ βήματι τοῦ θεοῦ,

      But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you again, why do you despise your brother? For we will all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.

      Read Romans 14:10 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 16:11

      μή τις οὖν αὐτὸν ἐξουθενήσῃ. προπέμψατε δὲ αὐτὸν ἐν εἰρήνῃ, ἵνα ἔλθῃ πρός με, ἐκδέχομαι γὰρ αὐτὸν μετὰ τῶν ἀδελφῶν.

      Therefore let no one despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come to me; for I expect him with the brothers.

      Read 1 Corinthians 16:11 in the app

  2. to be rejected / cast off (1)
    To be rejected, thrown aside: “the stone that was rejected by you, the builders” (Acts 4:11).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 4:11

      οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ λίθος ὁ ἐξουθενηθεὶς ὑφ’ ὑμῶν τῶν οἰκοδόμων, ὁ γενόμενος εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας.

      He is ‘the stone which was regarded as worthless by you, the builders, which has become the head of the corner.’

      Read Acts 4:11 in the app
