Senses (NGSD)

  1. fearful (3)
    Fearful, awe-inspiring: “terrifying”, “it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God”, “the sight was so terrifying”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Hebrews 10:27

      φοβερὰ δέ τις ἐκδοχὴ κρίσεως καὶ πυρὸς ζῆλος ἐσθίειν μέλλοντος τοὺς ὑπεναντίους.

      but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and a fierceness of fire which will devour the adversaries.

      So fearful was the appearance that Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

3 φοβερός, -ά, -όν (< φοβέω), [in LXX chiefly for נוֺרָא, also for דְּחַל, אָיֹם ;] fearful, whether act, or pass.; act., = δεινός, causing fear, terrible (LXX): He 10:27, 31 12:21 Pass., = δειλός, feeling fear, timid (cl. in both senses).†
