Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 κολλούριον (TTr., κολλύ-, the more usual form), -ου, τό (dimin. of κολλύρα = κόλλιξ, a coarse bread roll), [in LXX: III Ki 12:24hil B*;] a small bread roll (LXX, III Ki 12:24hil). (Usually in pl., LS, s.v.) an eye salve shaped like a roll: Re 3:18.†
