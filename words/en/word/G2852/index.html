Senses (NGSD)

  1. to strike with the fist, to buffet (5)
    To strike with the fist, to maltreat: “struck him with their fists”, “are beaten”, “beaten for doing wrong”, “a messenger of Satan … to strike me”, “struck him with their fists”. The subject and content of the ‘striking’ in 2 Corinthians 12:7 are not decided.
    Attested: NT only
