Senses (NGSD)

  1. rightly, correctly (4)
    Rightly, properly: “you have answered correctly”, “what you say is right”, “you have judged rightly”, “plainly”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Mark 7:35

      καὶ ἠνοίγησαν αὐτοῦ αἱ ἀκοαί, καὶ ἐλύθη ὁ δεσμὸς τῆς γλώσσης αὐτοῦ, καὶ ἐλάλει ὀρθῶς·

      Immediately his ears were opened, and the impediment of his tongue was released, and he spoke clearly.

      Simon answered, “He, I suppose, to whom he forgave the most.” He said to him, “You have judged correctly.”
