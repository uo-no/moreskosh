Senses (NGSD)

  1. to be anxious / worry (13)
    To be so concerned about life, tomorrow, one's defence, and so on that the heart is troubled; to fret, to be uneasy: “Do not worry about what you will eat.”
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 6:25

      Διὰ τοῦτο λέγω ὑμῖν· μὴ μεριμνᾶτε τῇ ψυχῇ ὑμῶν τί φάγητε, μηδὲ τῷ σώματι ὑμῶν τί ἐνδύσησθε· οὐχὶ ἡ ψυχὴ πλεῖόν ἐστι τῆς τροφῆς καὶ τὸ σῶμα τοῦ ἐνδύματος;

      Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life: what you will eat, or what you will drink; nor yet for your body, what you will wear. Isn’t life more than food, and the body more than clothing?

      Read Matthew 6:25 in the app

    • Matthew 6:34

      μὴ οὖν μεριμνήσητε εἰς τὴν αὔριον, ἡ γὰρ αὔριον μεριμνήσει αὑτῆς· ἀρκετὸν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ ἡ κακία αὐτῆς.

      Therefore don’t be anxious for tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Each day’s own evil is sufficient.

      Read Matthew 6:34 in the app

    • Luke 12:22

      Εἶπεν δὲ πρὸς τοὺς μαθητὰς αὐτοῦ· Διὰ τοῦτο λέγω ὑμῖν, μὴ μεριμνᾶτε τῇ ψυχῇ τί φάγητε, μηδὲ τῷ σώματι τί ἐνδύσησθε.

      He said to his disciples, “Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life, what you will eat, nor yet for your body, what you will wear.

      Read Luke 12:22 in the app

  2. to care for / be concerned (solicitude) (6)
    To give thought to and care about people, the Lord's affairs, or one another. A positive concern: “care for one another”, “concerned about the things of the Lord”.
    Attested: NT only

    • 1 Corinthians 7:32

      Θέλω δὲ ὑμᾶς ἀμερίμνους εἶναι. ὁ ἄγαμος μεριμνᾷ τὰ τοῦ κυρίου, πῶς ἀρέσῃ τῷ κυρίῳ·

      But I desire to have you to be free from cares. He who is unmarried is concerned for the things of the Lord, how he may please the Lord;

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:32 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 7:33

      ὁ δὲ γαμήσας μεριμνᾷ τὰ τοῦ κόσμου, πῶς ἀρέσῃ τῇ γυναικί,

      but he who is married is concerned about the things of the world, how he may please his wife.

      Read 1 Corinthians 7:33 in the app

    • 1 Corinthians 12:25

      ἵνα μὴ ᾖ σχίσμα ἐν τῷ σώματι, ἀλλὰ τὸ αὐτὸ ὑπὲρ ἀλλήλων μεριμνῶσι τὰ μέλη.

      that there should be no division in the body, but that the members should have the same care for one another.

      Read 1 Corinthians 12:25 in the app
