Senses (NGSD)

  1. adoption as sons (5)
    Being made a son; adoption: “to receive adoption as sons”, “predestined for adoption”, “the status of sons”, “the Spirit of adoption”, “as we wait for adoption as sons”. The overtone of the word (adoption or the status of a son) is not decided.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 8:15

      οὐ γὰρ ἐλάβετε πνεῦμα δουλείας πάλιν εἰς φόβον, ἀλλὰ ἐλάβετε πνεῦμα υἱοθεσίας ἐν ᾧ κράζομεν· Αββα ὁ πατήρ·

      For you didn’t receive the spirit of bondage again to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption, by whom we cry, “Abba! Father!”

    • Romans 8:23

      οὐ μόνον δέ, ἀλλὰ καὶ αὐτοὶ τὴν ἀπαρχὴν τοῦ πνεύματος ἔχοντες ἡμεῖς καὶ αὐτοὶ ἐν ἑαυτοῖς στενάζομεν, υἱοθεσίαν ἀπεκδεχόμενοι τὴν ἀπολύτρωσιν τοῦ σώματος ἡμῶν.

      Not only so, but ourselves also, who have the first fruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for adoption, the redemption of our body.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

5 *† υἱοθεσία, -ας, ἡ (cf. the cl. phrases, υἱὸν τίθεσθαι, θετὸς υἱός), freq. in Inscr. (v. Deiss., BS, 239), adoption of a son (or daughter); metaph., of God's relation established with Israel: Ro 9:4; with Christ­ians: Ro 8:15, Ga 4:5, Eph 1:5; of its consummation, Ro 8:23.†
