Senses (NGSD)

  1. rocky ground (4)
    Ground full of stones: “fell on rocky ground”, “what was sown on rocky ground”, “sown on rocky places”, “rocky ground”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 13:5

      ἄλλα δὲ ἔπεσεν ἐπὶ τὰ πετρώδη ὅπου οὐκ εἶχεν γῆν πολλήν, καὶ εὐθέως ἐξανέτειλεν διὰ τὸ μὴ ἔχειν βάθος γῆς,

      Others fell on rocky ground, where they didn’t have much soil, and immediately they sprang up, because they had no depth of earth.

      What was sown on the rocky places, this is he who hears the word and immediately with joy receives it;

4 * πετρώδης, -ες (< πέτρα, εἶδος), rock-like, rocky, stony: τὸ, τὰ π., of shallow soil with underlying rock, Mt 13:5, 20, Mk 4:5, 16.†
