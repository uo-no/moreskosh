Senses (NGSD)

  1. to set before, to lay out (2)
    To present, to state: “explained the case to the king”, “laid out the gospel”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 ἀνα-τίθημι, [in LXX chiefly for חרם (Cremer, 546);] to lay upon, set up, etc. Mid. -εμαι, in late writers (Plut., al.; v. also MM, VGT, s.v.), to set forth, declare: Ac 25:14, Ga 2:2.†
