Senses (NGSD)

  1. always, continually (7)
    Always, continually, on each occasion: “always be ready to give a defence”, “as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing”, “always being given over to death”, “always remind you”, “you always resist the Holy Spirit”, “they always go astray in their hearts”, “always liars”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Acts 7:51

      Σκληροτράχηλοι καὶ ἀπερίτμητοι καρδίαις καὶ τοῖς ὠσίν, ὑμεῖς ἀεὶ τῷ πνεύματι τῷ ἁγίῳ ἀντιπίπτετε, ὡς οἱ πατέρες ὑμῶν καὶ ὑμεῖς.

      “You stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit! As your fathers did, so you do.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 ἀεί, adv., [in LXX: Is 42:14 (מֵעוֹלָם) 51:13 (תָּמִיד), Ps 94 (95):10, al.;] ever; of continuous time, unceasingly, perpetually: Ac 7:51, 2 Co 4:11 6:10, Tit 1:12, He 3:10. Of successive occurrences, on every occasion (MM, VGT, s.v.): I Pe 3:15, II Pe 1:12.†
