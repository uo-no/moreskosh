Senses (NGSD)

  1. olive grove (3)
    An olive orchard; the Mount of Olives: “the mount called the Olive Grove”.
    Attested: NT only

      When he came near to Bethsphage and Bethany, at the mountain that is called Olivet, he sent two of his disciples,
