Senses (NGSD)

  1. to lord it over, to overpower (4)
    To rule over, to overpower, to domineer: “not as those who lord it over the flock”, “he overpowered them”, “the rulers of the Gentiles … lord it over them”, “those who rule the Gentiles”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 20:25

      ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς προσκαλεσάμενος αὐτοὺς εἶπεν· Οἴδατε ὅτι οἱ ἄρχοντες τῶν ἐθνῶν κατακυριεύουσιν αὐτῶν καὶ οἱ μεγάλοι κατεξουσιάζουσιν αὐτῶν.

      But Jesus summoned them, and said, “You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them.

4 κατα-κυριεύω, [in LXX: Ge 1:28 (כּבשׁ), Ps 109 (110):2 (רדה) Si 17:4, al.;] to gain or exercise dominion (over); absol. (Arist.); c. gen. (Arist., al.): Mt 20:25, Mk 10:42, Ac 19:16, I Pe 5:3.† SYN.: (κατεξουσιάζω) (v. Swete, Mk., l.c.).
