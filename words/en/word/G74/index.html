1 ** ἀγωνία, -ας, ἡ (< ἀγών), [in LXX: II Mac 3:14, 16 15:19*;] a contest, wrestling (Eur., Xen.). Of the mind, great fear, agony, anguish (Dem., Arist.): Lk 22:44 (cf . Field, Notes, 77 f. ; Abbott, Essays, 101 f. ; MM, VGT, s.v.).†
