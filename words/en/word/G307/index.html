Senses (NGSD)

  1. to haul up (1)
    To draw up, to pull upward: “drew the net up onto the shore”.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 13:48

      ἣν ὅτε ἐπληρώθη ἀναβιβάσαντες ἐπὶ τὸν αἰγιαλὸν καὶ καθίσαντες συνέλεξαν τὰ καλὰ εἰς ἄγγη, τὰ δὲ σαπρὰ ἔξω ἔβαλον.

      which, when it was filled, fishermen drew up on the beach. They sat down and gathered the good into containers, but the bad they threw away.

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

1 ἀνα-βιβάζω (causal of ἀναβαίνω), [in LXX chiefly for עלה hi., also for רכב hi., etc.;] to make go up, draw up, as a ship (Xen.): σαγήνην, Mt 13:48 (metaph., MM, VGT, s.v.).†
