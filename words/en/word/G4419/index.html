Senses (NGSD)

  1. pinnacle (2)
    A wing-like tip, the edge of the top of the temple: “the pinnacle of the temple” (twice).
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Matthew 4:5

      Τότε παραλαμβάνει αὐτὸν ὁ διάβολος εἰς τὴν ἁγίαν πόλιν, καὶ ἔστησεν αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τὸ πτερύγιον τοῦ ἱεροῦ,

      Then the devil took him into the holy city. He set him on the pinnacle of the temple,

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 πτερύγιον, -ου, -τό (dimin. of πτέρυξ), [in LXX chiefly for כָּנָף;] a little wing. Anything like a wing, as a turret, battlement: τ. ἱεροῦ, Mt 4:5, Lk 4:9.†
