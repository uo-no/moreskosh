Senses (NGSD)

  1. breast (3)
    The breast: “the breasts at which you nursed”, “breasts that never nursed”, “a golden sash around the chest”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Luke 11:27

      Ἐγένετο δὲ ἐν τῷ λέγειν αὐτὸν ταῦτα ἐπάρασά τις φωνὴν γυνὴ ἐκ τοῦ ὄχλου εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Μακαρία ἡ κοιλία ἡ βαστάσασά σε καὶ μαστοὶ οὓς ἐθήλασας·

      It came to pass, as he said these things, a certain woman out of the multitude lifted up her voice and said to him, “Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts which nursed you!”
