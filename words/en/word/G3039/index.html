Senses (NGSD)

  1. to crush (2)
    To crush, to grind to powder: “will crush that person to powder” (twice). The representative examples in the LXX are the use of winnowing (sifting), which is not the same use.
    Attested: NT only

    • Matthew 21:44

      Καὶ ὁ πεσὼν ἐπὶ τὸν λίθον τοῦτον συνθλασθήσεται· ἐφ’ ὃν δ’ ἂν πέσῃ λικμήσει αὐτόν.

      He who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, but on whomever it will fall, it will scatter him as dust.”

      Read Matthew 21:44 in the app

    • Luke 20:18

      πᾶς ὁ πεσὼν ἐπ’ ἐκεῖνον τὸν λίθον συνθλασθήσεται· ἐφ’ ὃν δ’ ἂν πέσῃ, λικμήσει αὐτόν.

      Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces, but it will crush whomever it falls on to dust.”

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

2 λικμάω, -ῶ (< λικμός = λίκνον, a winnowing-fan), [in LXX chiefly for זרה ni., pi., Ru 3:2, III Ki 14:15, Is 17:13, Je 38 (31):10, Da 2:44, al.;] in cl., to winnow (so Ru 3:2). In LXX (III Ki 14:15, Is 17:13, Je 38 (31):10, Da 2:44), to scatter (as chaff or dust): Lk 20:18 (RV, scatter as dust, Deiss., BS, 225 f., quotes ex. in π. which suggests the meaning ruin, destroy; cf. Vg. comminuet, AV, grind to powder; cf. also Kennedy, Sources, 126), Mt 21:44 [WH], R, txt.†
