Senses (NGSD)

  1. ah (1)
    An exclamation expressing surprise or rejection, “Ah!”: “Ah, leave us alone”. The representative examples in the LXX are of different words (such as the conditional ἐάν), which is not the same use.
    Attested: NT only

    • Luke 4:34

      Ἔα, τί ἡμῖν καὶ σοί, Ἰησοῦ Ναζαρηνέ; ἦλθες ἀπολέσαι ἡμᾶς; οἶδά σε τίς εἶ, ὁ ἅγιος τοῦ θεοῦ.

      saying, “Ah! what have we to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!”
