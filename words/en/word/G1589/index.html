Senses (NGSD)

  1. election (the act) (5)
    God’s act of choosing; election: “we know that you have been chosen by God”, “be diligent to make your calling and election sure”, “God’s purpose according to election”, “a remnant chosen by grace”, “loved ones as regards the election of the gospel”.
    Attested: NT · Fathers

    • Romans 9:11

      μήπω γὰρ γεννηθέντων μηδὲ πραξάντων τι ἀγαθὸν ἢ φαῦλον, ἵνα ἡ κατ’ ἐκλογὴν πρόθεσις τοῦ θεοῦ μένῃ,

      For being not yet born, neither having done anything good or bad, that the purpose of God according to election might stand, not of works, but of him who calls,

      Read Romans 9:11 in the app

    • Romans 11:5

      οὕτως οὖν καὶ ἐν τῷ νῦν καιρῷ λεῖμμα κατ’ ἐκλογὴν χάριτος γέγονεν·

      Even so too at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace.

      Read Romans 11:5 in the app

    • 1 Thessalonians 1:4

      εἰδότες, ἀδελφοὶ ἠγαπημένοι ὑπὸ θεοῦ, τὴν ἐκλογὴν ὑμῶν,

      We know, brothers loved by God, that you are chosen,

      Read 1 Thessalonians 1:4 in the app

  2. the elect, the chosen group (1)
    The body of those who have been chosen: “those who share in the election obtained it, but the rest were hardened”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Romans 11:7

      τί οὖν; ὃ ἐπιζητεῖ Ἰσραήλ, τοῦτο οὐκ ἐπέτυχεν, ἡ δὲ ἐκλογὴ ἐπέτυχεν· οἱ δὲ λοιποὶ ἐπωρώθησαν,

      What then? That which Israel seeks for, that he didn’t obtain, but the chosen ones obtained it, and the rest were hardened.

      Read Romans 11:7 in the app

  3. chosen vessel (1)
    A chosen vessel (σκεῦος ἐκλογῆς): “my chosen vessel”.
    Attested: NT only

    • Acts 9:15

      εἶπεν δὲ πρὸς αὐτὸν ὁ κύριος· Πορεύου, ὅτι σκεῦος ἐκλογῆς ἐστίν μοι οὗτος τοῦ βαστάσαι τὸ ὄνομά μου ἐνώπιον ἐθνῶν τε καὶ βασιλέων υἱῶν τε Ἰσραήλ,

      But the Lord said to him, “Go your way, for he is my chosen vessel to bear my name before the nations and kings, and the children of Israel.

      Read Acts 9:15 in the app

Abbott-Smith dictionary entry (quoted)

7 ** ἐκλογή, ῆς ἡ (< ἐκλέγω), [in Aq.: Is 22:7; Sm., Th.: Is 37:24 * ;] a choice, selection; in NT, always of the Divine choice (EV, election): σκεῦος ἐκλογῆς, gen. qual., a chosen vessel; κατ ἐ., Ro 9:11 11:5, 28; c. gen. pers., I Th 1:4, II Pe 1:10; by meton., ἡ ἐ. = οἱ ἐκλεκτοί, Ro 11:7.†
