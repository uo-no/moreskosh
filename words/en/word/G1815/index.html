Senses (NGSD)

  1. resurrection (1)
    A rising again, a getting up: “the resurrection from among the dead”. The representative examples in the LXX are not examples of this noun.
    Attested: NT only

    • Philippians 3:11

      εἴ πως καταντήσω εἰς τὴν ἐξανάστασιν τὴν ἐκ νεκρῶν.

      if by any means I may attain to the resurrection from the dead.
