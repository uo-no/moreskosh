Senses (NGSD)

  1. region / territory (plural) (12)
    Region, territory, land delimited by borders (always plural in the New Testament): “by the Sea of Galilee, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali”, “from the region of Tyre and Sidon”, “begged him to depart from their region”, “the region of Judea beyond the Jordan”, “drove them out of their district”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

      Leaving Nazareth, he came and lived in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali,
