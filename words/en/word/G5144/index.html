Senses (NGSD)

  1. thirty (numeral) (11)
    The number thirty: “thirtyfold”, “thirty years”, “thirty-eight years”, “thirty stadia”, “thirty pieces of silver”, “about thirty years of age”, “four hundred and thirty years”.
    Attested: NT · LXX · Fathers

    • Matthew 13:8

      ἄλλα δὲ ἔπεσεν ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν τὴν καλὴν καὶ ἐδίδου καρπόν, ὃ μὲν ἑκατὸν ὃ δὲ ἑξήκοντα ὃ δὲ τριάκοντα.

      Others fell on good soil and yielded fruit: some one hundred times as much, some sixty, and some thirty.

      Then Judas, who betrayed him, when he saw that Jesus was condemned, felt remorse, and brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,
