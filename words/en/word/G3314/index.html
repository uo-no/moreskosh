Senses (NGSD)

  1. noon, south (2)
    Midday; also the direction south. Acts 22:6 has “about noon” and Acts 8:26 “toward the south” (whether the latter means noon is not decided). The sense is not divided.
    Attested: NT · LXX

    • Acts 8:26

      Ἄγγελος δὲ κυρίου ἐλάλησεν πρὸς Φίλιππον λέγων· Ἀνάστηθι καὶ πορεύου κατὰ μεσημβρίαν ἐπὶ τὴν ὁδὸν τὴν καταβαίνουσαν ἀπὸ Ἰερουσαλὴμ εἰς Γάζαν· αὕτη ἐστὶν ἔρημος.

      Then an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, “Arise, and go toward the south to the way that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza. This is a desert.”

      “As I made my journey and came close to Damascus, about noon suddenly a great light shone around me from the sky.
