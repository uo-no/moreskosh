4 σάκκος also written σάκος, -ου, ὁ (cf. Heb. שַׂק, which it renders in LXX), a coarse cloth, sackcloth, usually made of hair: Re 6:12. Anything made of sackcloth; a sack (Ge 42:25, al.); a garment of sackcloth, expressive of mourning or penitence: Mt 11:21, Lk 10:13, Re 11:3.†
