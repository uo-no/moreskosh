Abbott-Smith 辞書本文（引用）

原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

1 * παρ-οψίς, -ίδος, ἡ (< παρά, ὄψον; v.s. ὀψάριον), a side-dish of dainties (Xen., al.). In Comic poets and late prose, the dish itself on which the dainties are served: Mt 23:25, 26 (In this sense it is condemned by the Atticists; v. Rutherford, NPhr., 265 f.)†
