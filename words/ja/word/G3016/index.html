原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

3 * λεπτός, -ή, -όν (λέπω, to peel), peeled. fine, thin, small, light; hence, in late Gk., as subst. τὸ λ., a small coin (one-eighth of an as, AV, mite): Mk 12:42, Lk 12:59 21:2.†
