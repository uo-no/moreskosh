5 βέβηλος, -ον (< βαίνω, whence βηλός, a threshold), [in LXX chiefly for חֹל;] permitted to be trodden, accessible (v. DCG, ii, 422b); hence, (opp. to ἱερός) unhallowed, profane: of things, I Ti 4:7 6:20, II Ti 2:16; of men, I Ti 1:9, He 12:16 (cf. κοινός; Cremer, 140).†
