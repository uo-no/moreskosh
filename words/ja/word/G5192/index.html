1 ὑακίνθος, -ου, ό [in LXX chiefly for תְּכֵלֶת ;] hyacinth; in cl., a flower, prob. the dark blue iris; in late writers, a precious stone of the same colour, perhaps the sapphire: Re 21:20 (Phil., FlJ, al.).†
