Abbott-Smith 辞書本文（引用）

原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

1 θυρεός, -οῦ, ὁ (< θύρα), [in LXX for מָגֵן, צִנָּה;] in Hom., a door-stone. In late Gk. (Polyb., Plut.), the scutum, a large oblong shield: θ. τ. πίστεως, Eph 6:16 (cf. Wi 5:20, where ὁσιότης is likened to the ἀσπίς, the clypeus or small round shield of the light-armed soldier).†
