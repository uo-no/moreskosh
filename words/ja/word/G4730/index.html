原典の英語のまま掲載しています。

4 στενοχωρία, -ας, ἡ (v. supr.) [in LXX for צוּקָה, etc. ;] narrowness of space, want of room (Thuc., al.). Metaph. (Xen., Polyb., De 28:53, Wi 5:3, al.), difficulty, distress: θλίψις καὶ (ἢ) σ., Ro 2:9 8:35 (EV, anguish); pl. (cf. Bl., § 32, 6; WM, 220), II Co 6:4 12:10.† SYN.: θλίψις, q.v
