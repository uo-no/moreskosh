1 συν-κατα-τίθημι (Rec. συγκ-), [in LXX: Ex 23:1 (יָד שִׁית), ib. 32 (כּרת), Da TH Su 1:20 * ;] to deposit together. Mid., to deposit one's vote with, hence, to agree with, assent to: c. dat., Lk 23:51.†
